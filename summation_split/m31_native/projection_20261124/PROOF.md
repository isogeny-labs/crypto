# Exact projection by section idempotents

This is an algebraic justification of the proposed native postprocessing
component. It is an application of standard commutative algebra to the
fixed diagnostic section, rather than a new general Gröbner degree bound.
It has no independently signed or formal-kernel standing. [pcc:unverified]

## Setup and theorem

Let k = F_2147483647, let R = k[x_1,...,x_r,t,u], and let I be the ideal
generated by all original equations. Include every fixed projective chart
equation in I. Assume B = (t²-t,u²-u) is contained in I. A completed candidate
G must be checked to be a Gröbner basis for I in the actual ambient grevlex
order, including both ideal containments. The implementation tests the
assumption B ⊆ I rather than silently adjoining B.

Define e_00=(1-t)(1-u), e_01=(1-t)u, e_10=t(1-u), e_11=tu. Then

    (a,b) belongs to pi_(t,u)(V_overline(k)(I))
        if and only if NF_G(e_ab) is nonzero.

All projected points lie in {0,1}² ⊂ k². The auxiliary coordinates in this
statement belong to the algebraic closure; they need not belong to k.

### Proof

The ideals (t-a,u-b), for (a,b) in {0,1}², are pairwise comaximal, and their
intersection is B. The Chinese remainder isomorphism gives

    k[t,u]/B ≅ k × k × k × k.

The four e_ab are the orthogonal idempotent identities of these factors.
They sum to 1. Therefore A=R/I decomposes as the direct product of the
four k-algebras A_ab=e_ab A; this can equally be obtained by successively
applying [Stacks, Lemma 10.24.3](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00EM).
The fiber A_ab is zero if and only if its identity e_ab is zero in A,
equivalently e_ab belongs to I. A Gröbner normal form is zero exactly for
ideal members; see the [Macaulay2 normal-form documentation](https://macaulay2.com/doc/Macaulay2/share/doc/Macaulay2/Macaulay2Doc/html/_normal_spforms.html).

Each A_ab is finite type over k. If nonzero, it has a maximal ideal whose
residue field is a finite extension of k, by the
[Hilbert Nullstellensatz](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00FS).
Embedding this field into the algebraic closure gives a geometric witness
in the fiber. Conversely, any geometric witness implies a nonzero fiber.
Thus the nonzero normal forms select exactly the projected support.
Nilpotents and positive-dimensional auxiliary fibers do not affect the
argument. [pcc:unverified]

## Exact elimination generators

Let S be the selected subset of {0,1}², and put

    Q = sum_(a,b not in S) e_ab.

Then

    I ∩ k[t,u] = (t²-t, u²-u, Q).

Indeed, the map k[t,u]/B → R/I sends each coordinate identity either to
zero or to the identity of a nonzero k-algebra. In a nonzero k-algebra,
the scalar map from the field k is injective. Hence the kernel consists
exactly of the excluded coordinate factors, and Q generates that kernel.
For full support Q=0; for empty support Q=1. These are elimination-ideal
generators, with no assertion that the returned list is a reduced Gröbner
basis. In particular this parameter elimination ideal is radical even
when I itself is not. [pcc:unverified]

Consequently any parameter polynomial h(t,u) vanishing on S belongs to I
itself. On this section, a guarded derivative row known to vanish on the
balanced baseline's projected support is an ideal consequence of that
baseline. This is conditional on correct support preservation; it is not
a new check of a future multiplicity-arm execution. Such redundant rows
can change a solver's work, and the statement does not extend unchanged to
an unsliced parameter plane. [pcc:unverified]

## Bounded postprocessing

The two section polynomials and four e_ab have total degree at most two.
Under grevlex, if the leading monomial of a divisor divides a degree-at-most-
two current term, all terms of the corresponding divisor multiple also
have degree at most two. Ordinary division therefore never increases this
degree. Every iteration either subtracts a divisor multiple or moves an
irreducible leading term to the remainder; its current leading monomial
strictly decreases. There are binomial(n+2,2) monomials of degree at most
two in n ambient variables. Each of the six divisions visits at most that
many leading monomials, for a total bound 6*binomial(n+2,2).
[pcc:unverified]

For the current geometric presentations, n=8 at m=3 and n=12 at m=4,
so the per-query bounds are 45 and 91 respectively. These bounds concern
ordinary postprocessing after the basis exists and has been checked.
They do not bound basis construction, independent Buchberger checking,
runtime, memory, or solving degree. Basis scans and coefficient arithmetic
also have a cost not counted by a leading-term iteration.

## Discriminating controls

The development test plan includes fibers with nilpotents and with no
base-field auxiliary witness. In the latter control the geometric chart
relations plus z-1, s+1 and w*t-u force w²=-1. Because p≡3 mod 4, w cannot
lie in k, but it does lie in the quadratic extension F_(p²). The only
surviving section point is (0,0): t=0 forces u=0; t=1 would force w=u in
{0,1}, incompatible with w²=-1. Adding w^p-w would incorrectly remove
this fiber. A separate z=0 control retains the infinity component.
[pcc:unverified]

The API owns its basis-verification step and attaches its point set only
after all six divisions finish. It never trusts an independently supplied
boolean flag. A failed basis/section check is rejected; a finite resource
cap is incomplete; only a completed empty support represents an empty
projected solution set.
