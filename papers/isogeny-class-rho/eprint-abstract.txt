An $\mathbb{F}_q$-rational isogeny of degree prime to $n$ restricts to an isomorphism of the prime-order subgroup $\langle P\rangle$ on which an elliptic-curve discrete-logarithm instance $Q = kP$ lives, so a generic algorithm should cost the same on every curve of the isogeny class. We test this in the only way an empirical statement about “which curve” can be tested: by carrying one instance, through explicit and verified isogenies, to many curves of its class and running the same Pollard rho on each, under a protocol frozen before the first measurement, with negative controls (the same curve in other coordinates or another field basis, an A/A replica), planted effects of known size, and the structural folds a crater curve admits as positive controls. Four classes are measured: a prime-field class of $347,004$ curves ($104$ measured, spread over all $12$ endomorphism-ring strata and reached through isogenies of degree $3$ to $61$), a $j = 0$ class ($45$ curves), a class of $327,080$ curves over $\mathbb{F}_{2^{37}}$ ($40$ curves) and the class of the Koblitz curve $y^2 + xy = x^3 + 1$ over $\mathbb{F}_{2^{41}}$ ($708,153$ curves, $11$ measured over all four strata). In $85,425$ solves, every one returning the planted logarithm, no curve, endomorphism-ring stratum, volcano level, isogeny degree, kernel-field degree, choice of generator ($100$ or $64$ per curve with the same secret) or source curve of the instance left the preregistered Bonferroni band, and no across-curve test detected heterogeneity; the planted negation fold ($0.63$ to $0.77$ of the unfolded mean, expected $0.707$) and the $4$-adding walk ($1.27$ to $1.36$) were detected every time. The only real effects are the crater curves' own endomorphisms: the order-$6$ automorphism fold at $j = 0$ cost $0.439$ $[0.401, 0.481]$ of the plain walk (expected $1/\sqrt{6} = 0.408$), and the Frobenius fold on the Koblitz crater $0.155$ $[0.141, 0.170]$ (expected $1/\sqrt{41}$), $0.113$ with negation (expected $1/\sqrt{82}$), still $1.7$ to $2.2$ times faster in wall time at $41$ extra squarings per step. Every isogeny leads away from the one curve that has them. On the way we record that, for odd $m$, every prime dividing the conductor of a curve over $\mathbb{F}_{2^m}$ is $\pm 1 \bmod 8$, so the Koblitz curve over $\mathbb{F}_{2^{41}}$ has no isogeny off its crater of degree below $409$; we find its $409$- and $1721$-isogenies (division polynomials of degree $83,640$ and $1,480,920$) in about one and five minutes each through $\ell$-torsion abscissae in $\mathbb{F}_{2^{41\cdot 204}}$ and $\mathbb{F}_{2^{41\cdot 215}}$, and we derive and verify an explicit V\'elu map on points for ordinary curves in characteristic two. This is a null result with passing positive controls, rho against rho; it is not an index-calculus result and makes no claim about any deployed curve. All code, records and raw per-solve rows are released.
