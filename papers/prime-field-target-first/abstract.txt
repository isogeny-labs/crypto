Title: A square-root barrier for explicit elimination in prime-field summation-polynomial index calculus
Keywords: elliptic curves, summation polynomials, index calculus, complexity, prime fields
Category: Cryptography and Security

Explicit elimination faces a square-root work bound in specified
representation models. A signed four-digit radix base gives full target
coverage and bounded specialized fibers. Fixed-target norms and batched
norms are analyzed alongside adaptive group comparisons and
degree-bounded coordinate tests.
