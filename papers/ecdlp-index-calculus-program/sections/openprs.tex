\section{Work in open pull requests}\label{sec:openprs}

At the paper's date, 111 open pull requests in the repository concern elliptic-curve index calculus or its baselines. Their results are not on \texttt{main}, have not been reviewed here, and are not used anywhere in the paper; the titles below are quoted as their authors filed them and are claims, not findings. Two bear on the text: \#2359 reports a plane-quartic IC/rho of $0.024$, but the plane-quartic $S$ values on \texttt{main} are unverified because their degree-3 reduction merges distinct classes (\S\ref{sec:corrections}); and \#2324 records that a Joux--Vitse sieve replication failed its acceptance rule and withheld its decisions. As these merge, they are folded into the sections above and the catalogue under the same rules as everything else. Source: \art{papers/ecdlp-index-calculus-program/data/open_prs.tsv}.
