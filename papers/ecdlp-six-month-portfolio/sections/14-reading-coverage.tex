\section{Full-text reading coverage and the outstanding requirement}\label{app:reading}

\subsection{What a reading receipt means}

The instruction to read everything is not satisfied by the repository
inventory. A separate ledger enumerates every tracked entry in the
nineteen pinned trees and records the source revision, Git object,
content hash, source-line intervals and an explicit acknowledgement of
what was read. A bounded text emission is not acknowledged automatically;
truncated or unacknowledged output cannot establish a full read. An exact
duplicate blob can inherit content coverage, but its surrounding local
context need not be the same.

The frozen ledger records 94 distinct files read in full, comprising
1,117,504 bytes. Fifty other cited sources retain the earlier status
``prior focused review only'': they were used selectively, without a
complete line-by-line reading receipt. The other 305,223 tracked entries
have no retained reading receipt. This last label does not assert that
no earlier human or model ever read them. It states what this review can
document. Machine parsing, indexing, hashing, compilation and test passes
are separate operations and never fill a reading interval.

The following bounded groups do have complete full-text receipts at
their pinned revisions: fifteen crypto root research notes (the fourteen
\file{RESEARCH_*.md} files plus \file{SPARSE_ALGEBRA_FOLLOWUP.md});
all twenty-one pinned ML Markdown files; nineteen ten-lead claim statements;
and ten lead descriptions. These groups overlap the totals above. They
do not include all ML code, feature branches, private evidence packets
or every underlying raw run. Two complete implementation reads cover
\file{koblitz_fast_arith.rs} and \file{semaev_s3_cost.rs}; the former
includes every test through its final line. Reading a test is not running it.

\subsection{Content outside the pinned-tree denominator}

Eight additional Drive texts were read in their entirety as returned by
the connector: two delivery guides, the later volcano report, the
original-C diagnostic, the Prime61 continuation/certificate report, a
field-effects audit and two communication drafts. Their external ledger
records provider identities, modification metadata and retained-text
hashes. PDF extraction does not independently inspect every visual or
certify that an embedded artifact is complete. Those external texts are
not silently counted as pinned Git files.

The earlier 2,935-vertex archive, later 7,937-vertex archive and separate C
archive have 29,409, 79,462 and 125 checked manifest entries respectively.
Hash-checking all those entries does not mean all archive content has been
read or mathematically replayed. Likewise, the 5,144 captured branch heads
have metadata coverage, not exhaustive tree or historical-blob reading.
The 51-GB-class historical Autolab mirror and private source packets
remain unavailable as raw evidence in this workspace. A search returning
no result is not proof that an artifact does not exist elsewhere.

\subsection{The remaining work is an open obligation}

The comprehensive synthesis and the exhaustive-reading requirement have
different completion states. The latter remains open in
\file{problems/ecdlp-portfolio-reading-coverage.md}, linked from the
repository problem index. Its exit criteria require closing the reading
ledger over the accessible source corpus, reconciling source/code/raw-run
chains, accounting for branch and historical content separately, and
obtaining or explicitly documenting inaccessible artifacts. A missing
receipt cannot be converted to a full read by relabelling a directory or
accepting a summary. No execution or spending cap is invented here.

The companion \file{evidence/reading-coverage.tar.xz} contains the
file-by-file CSV, acknowledged reading events, definitions and collection
script, with member and outer hashes. Its summary is also available as
\file{evidence/reading-coverage-summary.json}. This appendix makes the
unfinished scope inspectable; it does not waive it or claim that merging
the paper completes the underlying audit.
