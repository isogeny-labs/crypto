\section{Isogenies, graphs, fibres, conductors, and transfer}\label{sec:isogenies}

The isogeny work is not a single failed shortcut. It contains several
different objects: explicit walks and map certificates, conductor and
endomorphism-ring calculations, fibre-aware bases, gluing and
higher-genus constructions, weak-node searches, graph-wide experiments,
and cryptographic scheme prototypes. Their obligations differ.

\subsection{What transport conserves}

For a homomorphism $\varphi:E(\F_q)\to E'(\F_q)$, let
$\kappa=\ker\varphi\cap E(\F_q)$. Suppose
$\B'\subset\varphi(E(\F_q))$ and take the complete rational pullback
$\B=\varphi^{-1}(\B')\cap E(\F_q)$. The recorded transport argument
states that the number of ordered $m$-decompositions of $R$ over $\B$
is $|\kappa|^{m-1}$ times the number of decompositions of $\varphi(R)$
over $\B'$. Each image tuple has $|\kappa|^m$ lifts, and exactly
$|\kappa|^{m-1}$ have the required sum.\src{TRANSFER}

This is a useful scoped statement. It applies to the declared
homomorphism and complete pullback domain. It does not prove that every
alternative factor-base geometry has the same cost, nor that every
non-homomorphic or higher-dimensional reduction is impossible. It
directs attention to three things that can still change: rational
kernel symmetry, destination geometry, and arithmetic/solver cost.
Map evaluation and source recovery remain additional costs.

Isogeny loops act as endomorphisms; on a fixed prime cyclic subgroup an
applicable endomorphism acts by a scalar. Faster evaluation can help
known-scalar multiplication or a generic walk. Turning it into a
non-generic unknown-log advantage requires another mechanism. The
portfolio correctly keeps these claims distinct in its newer transfer
and curve-identity rules.\src{FIBRES}\src{C-RULES}

\subsection{Conductor gaps and fibre-aware IC}

The conductor-gap programme examines whether endomorphism-ring levels
change the measured difficulty of a decomposition method. Two fixed CM
pairs with large prime gaps give recorded floor/crater counted-$S$
ratios 0.975 with 99\% interval [0.603,1.451] and 1.127 with interval
[0.922,1.342], across 192 verified records and 96 stored exact replays.
These observations do not resolve the general conductor-gap question;
they fail to detect an effect in those exact comparisons. Both sides'
unpriced work remains relevant.\src{CONDUCTOR}

Fibre closure and two-torsion symmetrisation can reduce redundant algebra
on a matched abscissa base. A later correction shows why the domain
must be specified: at matched column count, an apparent symmetry saving
can disappear. Similarly, the fibre-aware scaling panels improve a
specific method but show its IC/rho envelope widening with size at
fixed cofactor. The reported ratios rise roughly from 8--9 to 35--37
at cofactor four, and from 13--20 to 60--63 at cofactor 72.
\src{FIBRE-SCALE}\src{PRIOR}

The right question is therefore a controlled comparison on the same
transported target, base convention, subgroup and resource envelope.
Comparing two curves with different subgroup sizes or comparing an
orbit-redundant base to an already-quotiented base does not isolate
intrinsic hardness.

\subsection{Replicated rho controls and a withheld deeper experiment}

The autoresearcher contains a much larger control programme than the two
IC comparisons above. Its degree-37/41 replication reports 160,000
verified toy recoveries across 392 jobs. All six family-adjusted 95\%
level/crater contrast intervals include zero. Only the three degree-37
contrasts meet the study's $\pm3\%$ equivalence band; the wider degree-41
intervals do not. ``No detected effect'' therefore does not become a
global equivalence verdict. These are repeated solver observations across
arms, not 160,000 independent curve classes.\src{VOL-REPL}

The degree-51 follow-up uses $r=17,192,986,687$ (about 34 bits), cofactor
130,972, and all sixteen size-seventeen action orbits in a fixed panel.
Its 25,600 verified recoveries give a floor/crater $\log_2$ walk-count
contrast of 0.00576 with 95\% interval [$-0.03217$,0.04337]. The five
A/A gates pass and the declared $\pm0.10$-bit equivalence criterion holds
under its fixed-orbit analysis. Orbit canonicalisation, map transport,
setup and final verification are outside that walk-count boundary.
This does not establish equivalent complete attack costs.\src{VOL-51}

The deeper degree-51 experiment must retain a different verdict. All
20,480 toy solves verify, and execution-integrity checks pass, but two
simultaneous A/A intervals extend outside the $\pm0.15$-bit control band.
Its inferential labels are consequently \texttt{WITHHELD\_AA\_ALERT}.
The candidate contrasts and their intervals remain diagnostics; neither
absence of an effect nor equivalence is admitted. Shared scalar labels
also do not constitute serialized isogeny transport. The audit notes
missing stored collision transcripts and a relocation-sensitive replay
path. Correct returned keys and an admitted comparative inference are
separate requirements.\src{VOL-DEEP}\src{VOL-DEEP-AUDIT}

\subsection{Large conductor directions: construction is still a stage}

The later conductor panel certifies 43 prime directions, spanning twenty
trace classes and seventeen geometries after twist deduplication. Its
102 order levels and 131 conditional placements are not independent
experiments. A K163 direction of degree 45,641 has an explicit kernel
abscissa orbit of degree 3,260, whereas the K283 degree-1,697 direction
has abstract orbit degree 239,984. Isogeny degree alone can therefore
mis-rank construction work.\src{CONDUCTOR-PANEL}

The retained K163 relative-tower probe enumerates 22,820 half-kernel
abscissae, using ninety inverse batches. Its recorded 56.65 seconds and
23,724 KiB peak RSS stop before the kernel polynomial or map evaluator.
A 0.36-second replay checks the retained line certificate, not a complete
map. A same-degree horizontal endomorphism control is a different
construction and supplies no vertical timing ratio. The panel has zero
complete NIST-scale map/dual/transport/recovery cost packages. Its small
field-degree-37, isogeny-degree-73 positive control does recover 1,024 transported
targets, establishing a toy capability rather than filling that gap.
\src{CONDUCTOR-PANEL}

\subsection{Corrections inside the fibre programme}

The full fibre report contains changes of interpretation that its early
sections do not capture. Identical formal Semaev degrees or Macaulay
dimensions do not imply identical ranks, Gr\"obner behaviour or solving
degree. Its early class-invariance arguments therefore do not establish
a general hardness theorem. The later report explicitly leaves
prime-field exponent equality as Conjecture~10 and retracts a universal
``isogenies only change constants'' statement: composite binary fields
can contain weaker isogenous representatives through GHS mechanisms.
\src{FIBRES}

Its saturation penalty also depends on the oracle. Enumeration on the
unquotiented base incurs the reported multiplicity; a quotient-coordinate
oracle can avoid much of it. The reported $m=3$ quotient ratios are
0.93--1.07 against 2.67--3.62 for enumeration on eight curves. Conversely,
the degree-31 $m=3$ quotient construction loses to a matched projected-$x$
comparison, and an unchanged-yield doubling prediction is falsified.
The class-wide batch study verifies 40,800 toy logs but concerns 64-target
batching, not a one-target gain. These refinements identify usable
representations and failed predictions without closing the whole lead.
\src{FIBRES}

A later main revision also lands the R6.10 literature audit, whose full
report was read during integration. Of forty-seven grouped reference rows,
it marks thirteen confirmed at its declared text/abstract boundary, four
confirmed through secondary sources, fourteen bibliography-only, eight
corrected, seven mismatched and one unverified. Those statuses are not
forty-seven independently checked full publications.\src{FIBRES-LIT}

The load-bearing priority correction is concrete: FHJRV, EUROCRYPT 2014
\S3.2, already gives the torsion-invariant base's decomposition loss,
smaller relation count and compensation through easier polynomial systems.
The programme's exact census and measured class factors remain useful;
the saturation law and qualitative oracle dependence should not be claimed
as new. The audit also changes the quarter-test comparison from avoiding
a square root to avoiding an inversion, and distinguishes constructing an
isogeny between given endpoints from discovering an advantageous weak
endpoint. Its GHS cost-attribution corrections separate a mixed local
model from published bounds. This review reads the audit, not all primary
publications it discusses.\src{FIBRES-LIT}

\subsection{The graph-building capability and its measured coverage}

The full-volcano work builds explicit finite graphs and routes, exports
explorers, verifies kernel maps, assigns conductor strata, and runs
bounded IC and generic solvers on selected vertices. This is a
substantial implemented capability. Graph generation, graph completeness
for a declared edge set, and graph-wide attack execution are three
different completion claims.\src{VOLCANO}

The earlier downloaded checkpoint covers three smaller classes with 24,823
declared vertices and 51 strata. That repaired checkpoint has 2,935
audited vertices: 989 in the mixed-conductor graph, 970 in the
three-power graph, and 976 in the two-power graph. All 51 strata have
samples. This is about 11.8\% of the declared vertex population,
not a completed graph-wide measurement.

\subsection{Two distinct advances in the original large-class work}

The original-C full-selected-point-order diagnostic covers 1,869 retained
models and 3,738 public one-target workloads per arm. Both direct EC
Pohlig--Hellman/BSGS and reduced Tate/Miller followed by finite-field
Pohlig--Hellman and checked factor-base relations recover every selected
target: 7,476 primary method records. Selected point orders range from
2,600,529,297,408 to 1,637,958,981,148,213,248, but every prime divisor is
2, 3 or 7. This is a working smooth-order transfer pipeline, not a
large-prime or large-embedding-degree IC result. Maximal point order and
whole-group cyclicity are not certified.\src{VOL-C-REPORT}

Setup records include 26,166 relation equations/queries and target-digit
work includes 161,336. Identity and dependent equations remain counted;
rank is separate. Both cold solver intervals include their declared
certification, projections, transfer, relation algebra, reconstruction and
EC verification, while shared fixture construction is separate. Unpriced
field/integer categories and unqualified clocks prevent a complete cost
ranking. The review checks all 125 entries of this archive's manifest;
the report's full same-implementation replay remains a historical claim,
not a new independent replay here.\src{VOL-C-FULL}\src{VOL-C-REPORT}

The separate Prime61 disk-backed continuation fixes
$p=2^{61}-1$, $a=p-3$, $b=111$ and prime subgroup order
$r=2,305,843,010,818,082,053$. It expands 1,024 sources,
discovers 4,184 curves and retains 15,360 maps. Its 12,288 streams make
1,228,800,000 capped target trials and find zero relations and zero solves.
This is traversal/storage progress with a bounded negative relation-stage
result, not a completed IC method. Its later exact form certificate
describes the selected component as $C_{34,081,013}\times C_2^4$, of
size 545,296,208, using explicit relations and a fixed-witness checker.
That cardinality certificate is not an enumerated vertex count or a
whole-class upper bound. Native class arithmetic and CM interpretation
remain outside its partial Lean coordinate proof. The underlying raw
certificate was not independently replayed in this review.
\src{PRIME61-REPORT}

\subsection{GHS mechanisms and the scope of a trapdoor demonstration}

The pinned main branch of \texttt{ghs-trapdoor} is a scaffold, but another
captured branch contains a Sage mechanism prototype. It describes a
composite-degree binary field $\F_{2^{20}}$ over $\F_{2^5}$, a weak curve,
an Artin--Schreier tower, a genus-four model, an explicit transfer, and a
small protected subgroup of order 7,717. The branch reports
homomorphism and subgroup-order checks, isogeny pullback, and end-to-end
toy ElGamal recovery.\src{GHS-MAIN}\src{GHS-BRANCH}

Those are mechanism claims on the branch, not a demonstrated scalable
escrow system. The toy destination solver is BSGS, whose square-root
cost is comparable to source rho at that tiny subgroup order. The
general claim that the public curve forces outsiders to use rho, or
that a secret chain is hard to replace with any useful public route,
requires additional analysis. Working-field ambiguity in the README's
Jacobian description also needs checking against the actual code and
certificates before a stronger transport claim is made. No fresh Sage
or Lean replay was performed here.

The distinction is especially important for ECC2K-130, whose extension
degree 131 is prime. A construction exploiting a degree-four extension
cannot simply be scaled by changing that parameter. The ten-lead
register contains finite order and map-class obstructions for particular
candidate Jacobians, but leaves other destinations and constructions
open.\src{TEN}

\subsection{What remains of the structural and hidden-route leads}

The structural-completeness draft organises seven families of known
isogeny/CM mechanisms. Its own correction now describes case-analysis
proof sketches that have not received independent review; an earlier
unconditional-proof sentence was removed. This paper therefore treats
it as an organised argument and source catalogue, not a proof that every
prime-field attack route has been exhausted.\src{STRUCTURAL}

A hypothetical hidden route should be assessed through separate
questions: mathematical existence, explicit construction, public
discoverability, subgroup action, destination advantage, evaluation and
recovery costs, and the availability of an equivalent public shortcut.
No inference about secret agency capability follows merely from a
large degree or a sparse weak class. Conversely, failing to make a
route practical in a toy study is not a proof of its universal
impossibility. These are the appropriate remaining obligations of the
programme's speculative endomorphism and isogeny leads.
