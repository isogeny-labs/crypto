Title: A Six-Month ECDLP Research Programme: Implementations, Experiments, Structural Leads, and Evidence
Category: Cryptanalysis; experimental mathematics; research infrastructure
Keywords: ECDLP, index calculus, Koblitz curves, binary fields, prime fields, Semaev polynomials, isogenies, Groebner bases, GPU arithmetic, reproducibility, Lean

A comprehensive retrospective of nineteen aburan28 repository snapshots from 19 April to 19 October 2026. The paper maps implemented capabilities, recorded experiments, measured incumbent improvements, structural and learning results, formalisation boundaries, corrections and open obligations. It distinguishes metadata volume from scientific output, component improvements from complete ECDLP advantages, and checked artifact bytes from mathematical or independent solver validation. It includes a pinned source register and a machine-readable catalogue. Research draft; not peer reviewed or submitted.
