For ordinary, non-anomalous elliptic curves over large prime fields, this
draft audits Semaev-summation-polynomial index calculus with a factor base of
size N.  It separates the exact relation-probability bound from distribution
heuristics, proves an entropy/length lower bound for faithful finite factor-base [pcc:unverified]
encodings, and derives a p^{m/(2m-1)} relation-collection barrier for an
explicit class of candidate/collision and quotient-materializing algorithms.
Intervals, small-height rational reconstruction, multiplicative cosets,
bounded-degree correspondences, split S3 trees, and explicit target batching
are audited within that class.  The draft does not establish a lower bound for
unrestricted arithmetic or rational circuits; a target-adaptive,
non-materializing direct-sum theorem remains the successor problem.
