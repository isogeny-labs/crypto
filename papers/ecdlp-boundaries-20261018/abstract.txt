Scoped Boundaries for Elliptic-Curve Index Calculus: Counterexamples, Conservation, and Oracle Models

Keywords: elliptic curves; index calculus; first fall degree; generic groups; conservation; summation polynomials; monodromy; isogenies; formal verification.
Category: cryptanalysis / mathematical foundations; research draft, not submitted.

A source-bound synthesis of six proposed negative results, separating exact polynomial counterexamples, bounded closure experiments, finite-fibre conservation, ordered-cover monodromy, and model-dependent oracle reductions. The draft records contradictions and unverified bridges, inventories the existing Lean conservation theorem, and specifies the missing concrete-pipeline generic simulation obligations. It introduces no scientific trials, claims no new theorem or novelty, and makes no deployed-curve security claim.
