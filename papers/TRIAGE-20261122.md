## How this was done

- Read each directory's `README.md` (status banner, what it says, what it
  does not say, the checklist owed before submission) and `abstract.txt`,
  and checked that its PDF opens (`pdfinfo`).
- Evidence standing: what each paper's own README says about review, replay
  and Lean checks; whether any of its numbers sit on the IC leaderboard or in
  `benchmarks/` under the validation gate (`docs/VALIDATION_GATE.md`); and
  the standing of every pcc claim id the papers cite, from
  `python3 tools/pcc/pcc.py standing <id>` on `main` at `ce396efc`.
- Prior assessment: [`research/paper_opportunities_20261029/REPORT.md`](../research/paper_opportunities_20261029/REPORT.md)
  (which index-calculus paper to submit; recommends the fall-degree paper).
- Publishability is a judgement of what a referee at the IACR ePrint archive
  or a specialist journal would make of the draft as it stands: **High** (a
  clear contribution, little left to do), **Medium** (a contribution, real
  work owed), **Low** (record, survey, correction or engineering note that a
  venue would not take as a paper).

Two facts apply to every row:

1. **No pcc claim behind these papers has passed the signed CI check.** Only
   the treatise cites claim ids (47 distinct), and all 47 have standing
   `unverified`. Across the whole ledger, `pcc standing-by-type` reports
   3,434 `unverified` and 151 `failed` claims, and nothing else.
2. **None is submittable without the owners.** Every source is anonymized
   (`\author{Anonymous}`). ePrint rejects anonymous papers, so the owners
   supply the author block, affiliation and emails (`papers/AUTHORS.example`,
   `make deanonymize`), and an author, not an agent, presses Submit. Action
   plan F1 (IP assignment) should be signed before anything goes out under
   the company's name.
