Pollard rho solves the elliptic-curve discrete logarithm in a group of
prime order r with A efficiently computable automorphisms in about
(πr/(2A))^(1/2) group operations, and no index calculus has beaten it on
a curve of cryptographic shape. This treatise collects what one research
programme proved and measured about index calculus on elliptic curves
between July and 6 November 2026. Its sibling ledger holds 1,402
decisions and 2,074 run directories to 30 September; this repository
holds 1,402 study directories, 745 ecbench sessions with 129,945 sealed
records, 145 admitted benchmark rows and 28 leaderboard rows, 19 of them
validated. Each number states its boundary (oracle stage, complete
solve, cold one-target cost or online cost) and its standing.
“Validated” means that a validation file names the result, and
“kernel-checked” that a Lean kernel accepted it. None of the 166
proof-carrying (pcc) claim records has a verdict, so a result without
one of these labels is its producer’s unverified assertion.

Theory. The Galois group of Semaev’s summation cover is (ℤ/2)^(m − 2)
for every curve, field and characteristic, with arithmetic monodromy
equal to geometric, and its fibres over 𝔽_(q) split by an exact law (a
draft proof upheld by an integrity audit; the characteristic-2
separability lemma awaits an expert check). Fibre statistics are
therefore constant on the factor-base locus, and the mean decomposition
yield at a fixed base size is fixed. For the binary Weil descent of S₃
the first fall degree is 2 for every n whenever every descended equation
has degree 2, as on every instance computed, Semaev’s chained systems
included, and the last fall degree is at most k + 2, or (t−1)k + t for
chains. Independently checked certificates give a last fall degree of at
least 5 on named instances at n = 33–45 and in the m = t = 3 chain, so
Semaev’s Assumption 1 fails as stated. An explicit-membership chain has
(d_(ff),d_(F)) = (4,5) at B = 3 for every p > 3 and
max (5,B+1) ≤ d_(F) ≤ 3B − 4 in general; PKM Kummer towers have first
fall degree 4 and a last fall degree that depends on the presentation,
not the ideal. The literal Petit–Quisquater Assumption 2 fails on all
119 census instances (unverified census), and a kernel-checked
left-kernel theorem removes the rank condition for one target. In the
generic group model, checked by Monte Carlo, a single-target index
calculus whose base logarithms are unknown costs at least 1.5× rho, and
1.875× with two base elements.

Algorithms. Root-finding and meet-in-the-middle oracles cost
400–5 ⋅ 10⁴× less per decomposition than F4 where they apply. FGHR’s D₄
symmetry at k = 4 cuts one decomposition 370–481×. Double-large-prime
genus-3 index calculus after the cubic GHS transfer moved from a fitted
q^(1.91) to q^(1.37) (local exponent 1.356 at q = 2²², above 4/3);
Diem’s plane-quartic route on the same family runs at exponent  ≈ 1.

Frontier. A priced campaign of 4,848 executions found no index calculus
below rho on any prime or prime-degree binary curve at any weight of a
pricing band applied to every arm. On the validated board the gap to a
matched rho is 3.4–11.5× on Koblitz curves of 37–47 bits (rule v7;
4.7–18× against rule v8’s same-binary rho), 5.5–13.5× on prime curves of
10–24 bits and 10.6–38× on random binary curves. The closest prime arms,
generic collision searches at rho’s exponent, are 1.3–1.7× at 36–42 bits
(w = 0). Index calculus reaches or crosses rho only in weak
extension-field families: the Joux–Vitse sieve route at 0.0065× a
matched negation rho at p = 1511 (weak class only; validated as
accounting, VAL-20261103-c31c76, no pcc claim); plane quartics at 0.005×
a negation rho near q = 2²¹ and genus 3 via GHS below the strongest rho
at q = 2¹⁶ under the registered unit price (neither has a validation
file); k = 4 in characteristic two at 5.2× rho at m = 15, with a
crossover near m ≈ 17.6 only by extrapolation. The plain Nagao arm of
the Joux–Vitse cover is above a matched negation rho at p = 1301 and
1451, so its extrapolated crossing near p ≈ 1330 does not hold. An
equal-advice audit against Bernstein–Lange rho retired every online win.
Nothing here concerns a deployed curve or key recovery.
