### Decisions made (and why)

- Compare per-traversal quantities in the traversal test, not `field_ops`. `field_ops` is a delta of the process-wide counter, and parallel tests leak into it (`502a5b298`).
- Reading of the results:
  - The gain grows with |D| and shrinks with log p, as the cost model predicts.
  - Multiplication counts overstate the wall-time gain. Per-call overhead of the general root finder (allocation, gcd, splitting) is not counted, so at |D|=256 Frobenius is slower in wall time despite 2–3× fewer multiplications.
  - `Auto`'s count-based model is therefore miscalibrated for wall time: 1.35× slower than the scan at 20-bit |D|=256.
  - For m=3 chains with |D| ≤ 64 the scan wins.
- F4 `field_ops` is 0 for m=2 at ≥20 bits because the systems have one generator (no S-pairs). The small nonzero count at 16 bits is not explained yet; it is identical across modes.
