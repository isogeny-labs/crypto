     - 31 replays, all of which fell back.
     - 382,248 word XORs against 133,813 plain (×2.86). This is a net loss.

     - `geometric` cuts row_ops by 10–55% but breaks the ~3% overshoot rule.

- The `lanczos` arm is reported, not changed. Its fix needs a design decision.
