
## E-222 / M-221 regimen: uncapped solver, mid-size check of the counter model (Claude)

### What was done
- User said "onwards yes" to looking into a solver without the field cap. The cap was not in the algorithm: it was range checks on `p` and `n` and an O(p) brute-force point count in `solve-prime-file`. Added an optional `group_order` to the input (supplied by the generator, which computes it by baby-step giant-step): counted and cross-checked below 4e7, above that the Hasse bound and divisibility are checked; `p` and `n` now up to 2^50 (trial-division primality). Tests: a field above the old cap (p = 67108859) recovers the planted log and the matched rho verifies; a missing or Hasse-violating order and a disagreeing order are rejected. 13 tests pass.
- Mid-size panel (`evidence/run_mid_panel.py`): 40 runs on 12 instances, subgroup orders of 26 to 31 bits, F = 128 and 512, 8 matched-rho runs per instance. All recovered the planted log; all rho runs verified; rho cheaper at every weight of the band in 40/40. Model against the runs: trials observed / predicted 1.001 (default, 24 runs) and 0.962 (H1, 16 runs), deterministic counters exact; elimination over-predicted by 16 to 18%. Distances at these sizes are 340 to 55,000 (quadrupling F cuts them by about 4; a 16-fold increase in n raises them by about 4).
- Cross-check: scaling the measured mid-size ratios (H1, F = 512) by sqrt(n_native / n) x (F / 2^20) x (bits ratio) gives log2 distance 97.8 (E-222) and 96.6 (M-221), against the model's projection range 96.7 to 97.8 and 95.7 to 96.7.
- Added claim C7, the 40 outputs with a hash manifest (`evidence/mid/`), recompute and replay extensions (replay: nonce `1a2ab8ded8a84359`, 127 runs identical, binary blake3 `1368eaf1...`; it also still reproduces the band rows recorded under the earlier binary). Gate tool still refuses the draft only for the two producer-run `independent` flags and the artifact-store robustness rows.

### Decisions made
- Kept the O(p) count and the cross-check for small fields so existing behaviour is unchanged; the supplied order is trusted only above 4e7 and only for the Hasse bound and divisibility.
- Stated the extrapolation gap as it now is: about 190 bits beyond the largest measured subgroup (31 bits).
