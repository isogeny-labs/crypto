## 2026-11-17 — Phase 1 (f4_fp degree-fall diagnostic) + Phase 2 (DB)

### What was done (commands, files, features)

- Phase 1 — rigorous measured first-/last-fall degree in `f4_fp.rs`:
  - `semi_regular_hilbert(degrees, n_vars)`: the Bardet semi-regular Hilbert
    function `[prod(1-t^{d_i})/(1-t)^{n_vars}]_+` (truncated at the first
    non-positive coefficient). Chosen over `complete_intersection_hilbert`
    (which assumes a square system, `/(1-t)^{#eqs}`) because the correct
    first-fall baseline uses the variable count and truncates for
    over-determined systems. Agrees with the CI form when `#eqs == n_vars`.
  - `degree_fall_diagnostic(input, basis, n_vars)`: post-hoc comparison of the
    realized standard-monomial count (`uncovered_in_degree` of the final basis)
    against the semi-regular expectation. A **degree fall** at degree `d` =
    realized `<` expected. Returns first-fall, last-fall, observed degree of
    regularity (first `d` the quotient vanishes), and the per-degree deviation.
  - Gated by new `F4Options::record_degree_falls` (default false → zero cost,
    never on the hot path, skipped on inconsistent/timed-out runs). New
    `F4Report` fields `first_fall_degree`, `last_fall_degree`,
    `degree_of_regularity_observed`, `degree_falls: Vec<DegreeFall>`.
  - Tests (hand-verified): `semi_regular_hilbert` series `[2,2]→[1,2,1]`,
    `[2,2,2]→[1,2]`, `[1]→[1]`; a regular sequence (`x²-1, y²-1` /F7) has no
    fall and d_reg 3; an over-determined system (`+ xy-x`, reduced basis
    `{x²-1, y-1}`) falls at degree 1 with d_reg 2; diagnostic off by default.
- Phase 2 — persistence:
  - `SolverStats` (methods.rs) gained `first_fall_degree`, `last_fall_degree`,
    `solving_degree_sum`, `degree_of_regularity_observed`, `degree_trace`
    (`Vec<ic_framework::stages::DegreeStep>`), `degree_story`, all
    `#[serde(default, skip_serializing_if)]`; populated in `from_report`
    (`degree_story` = `sat` vs `polynomial-system` by op_unit).
  - `SolverReport` carries `solving_degree_sum` + the degree-fall fields;
    filled from `SolverTotals`.
  - `schema.sql` `run_solver`: 6 new columns before `extra_json`

### Decisions made (and why)

- FFD/LFD measured rigorously by Hilbert-series deviation against the
  semi-regular model, not a pair-degree proxy from a Buchberger run (`GbStats`
  is `Copy` and its pair-degree drops are not the first-fall degree) — a proxy
  would be exactly the relabelling the repo forbids.
- Post-hoc diagnostic (reads the finished basis) over per-step instrumentation:
  correct, far simpler, and provably off the hot path.
- `f4_fp` chosen as the first instrumented engine because it already computes
  the complete-intersection Hilbert expectation and is the engine the genus-2+/
  cover Joux–Vitse descents (`jv_cover`/`jv_quintic`) run — Phase 3 wires the
  diagnostic through them to `SolverStats`.
