### Decisions made (and why)
- **Opt-in variant; the default is unchanged.** All existing specs and records stay byte-reproducible.
- **Why it is real work removed, not relabelling:** it deletes one hash-map insert per trial from the loop. The target stream and rows are unchanged, because a repeat that does not decompose contributes nothing whether or not it is probed.
- **Expected effect, not measured:**
  - On its own the change is a w = 0 tie (the charged work is identical except for at most one addition per eager-skipped repeat), with a native saving at w > 0. Under the frozen rule a lone run would therefore read "unresolved".
