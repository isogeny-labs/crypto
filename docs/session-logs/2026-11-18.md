### Decisions made (and why)

- **Three arms** (orbit / random / subspace) with the orbit arm fixing
  `ℓ_eff`: a Frobenius(squaring)-invariant `F_2`-subspace has dimension = a
  sum of 2-cyclotomic-coset sizes, so it cannot be an arbitrary `ℓ`. The
  orbit arm builds to the nearest reachable dim; random + window match it.
- **`S_3` is `a_2`-independent** (read from source): Koblitz `a=0` and `a=1`
  give the identical summation system, so `a` is not a factor for `d_ff`/`d_F`.
  Recorded as a finding in the prereg, not a hypothesis.
- **`d_F` (HKY) is natively well-defined over GF(2^n)** (Weil descent lands
  in the Boolean ring; square-free normal form is free), unlike the prime
  field which needed a PKM tower. Computability (`~C(nv,≤c)` columns) is the
  binding limit, not definability.
- Reuse the tested library `first_fall_degree` (rank proxy) + `solving_degree`
  and the `hky_par` closure rather than writing a new engine (owner rule: no
  Python; reuse not rewrite).

### Pilot result (done)

Ran 72 rows (`raw/pilot.jsonl`), gates G1 (`4 passed`) and G4/ordering
(`selftest OK`). `d_F ∈ {2,3,4}`, flat, matches the prior binary program.
**No Frobenius-orbit-specific effect** on `d_F` vs random in range (every gap
≤ 1, at the A/A floor; where a structured base is lower, the non-Frobenius
polynomial window is lower too). The firmest finding is **H-avail**: a
`τ`-invariant orbit base of dimension `ℓ ≈ n/m` is dimension-constrained
(availability tracks `ord_n(2)`); unavailable at `n=13,19` for mid `ℓ`,
available at composite `n` and at `n=23` (`ℓ=11`). The library
`first_fall_degree` proxy exceeded `d_F` in several cells (rank proxy, not a
first-fall). `RESULTS.md`, `POD_PLAN.md`, `raw/dF_by_arm.svg` written.
