stopped at the prelaunch quiet-load check: six competing high-CPU processes,

`c2f7f513674951488ac59a746afddd2807a0b309533450ce2a71de7ddc32ade4`;
the repository JSONL carries a sanitized load receipt and abort record.
`python3 -m py_compile` passed, and a fresh process check still found ten

### Decisions made (and why)

Do not time under current load. Record only PID, CPU and a short process name
on future checks, so unrelated command arguments do not enter published
artifacts. Give each retry a new append-only load-check ID; preserve the
prelaunch abort without counting it as a timed comparison.

### Open threads (unfinished, pick up next time)

### Errors (what went wrong, how it was handled)

The first runner used a shared quiet-receipt helper that captured full
unrelated command lines. Its generated raw JSONL was moved to a private
checkpoint; the published receipt preserves its digest and the admission
decision without exposing those lines. The runner now makes a sanitized
process snapshot directly.
