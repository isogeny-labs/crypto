### Decisions made (and why)
- New names (`mitm-coset`, `ic.k3_gaudry`) leave every existing method's counts and committed sessions unchanged.
- `ic.k3_gaudry` charges the real k=3 oracle's field multiplications, not a per-trial constant as `ic.k4_gaudry` does, because the k=3 solve is affordable.
- The cofactor-2 trace labels are counted, not charged (`mitm_coset_label_field_squarings_uncharged`), the same split as cryptanalysis.
- `oracle` was made required, because it changes the search. `lp_exponent` and `trial_cap` keep their defaults, following `ic.k4_gaudry`.
