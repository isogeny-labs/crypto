## Four-summand relation collection: tuned, frozen, fresh-curve holdout passed (`[pcc:unverified]`, user: "Devise novel approach to improve relation collection")

### What was done
- Idea: instead of matching scan points R +- F_k against pair sums (3 summands), match them against a table of all triple sums F_i +- phi^m F_j +- phi^n F_l (4 summands). Cost model (B^3 table build vs p/(12 B^3) candidates per relation) gives exponent p^(3/5) against p^(2/3); optimum B shrinks to about p^(1/5). Implemented as `summands=4` in `examples/prime_fast_j0_sym.rs` (`sym4_solve_ex`); every candidate relation is verified by exact point addition before it becomes a row, and the final scalar is checked with kG=Q.
- Tuning on separate curves (CSKIP=150, 12 rows per cell, rows in `sym4_tune/`): best B4 = 24 (28 bits), 28 (30 bits), 44 (32 bits). Frozen with hash before the holdout (`frozen_sym4_holdout.json`, commit c475944d45): prediction plain3/sym4 >= 6.0/7.0/8.0 and sym3/sym4 >= 1.8; abandon below 4.0 and 1.3.
