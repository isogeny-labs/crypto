### Decisions made (and why)

- Save rbx/rbp in the block rather than spill accumulators: no memory
  traffic in the inner rows; the alternative (8 words in a stack buffer)
  adds loads/stores on both carry chains.
- Asm for squares too: +4% end to end over asm products with mulx squares.
