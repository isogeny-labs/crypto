### Decisions made (and why)

- The Dickson closed form is kept only as the reference. It is correct but 0.36–1.72× of the gcd, because its ladder costs about n multiplications.
- The factored route was taken because the resolvent cubic split on 100% of S₄ quartics. That pointed to x(R±P₁±P₂) pairing.
- Irrational-u pairs are skipped exactly, under the guard x_R ∈ {X₁, X₂, 1/X₁, 1/X₂}. The proof is in the module doc, and the zero-root count was measured over about 6M pairs.
