## Cost and uncertainty limits

The 2.027 old/new wall ratio and its column-bootstrap interval reproduce from
the retained data. The interval is conditional on these runs and their column
costs. Two same-arm runs give one observed A/A difference, not a statistical
bound on run-to-run variation, and there is only one old-arm run. The frozen
protocol's threshold outcome remains a statement about this recorded comparison.

The recorded probe/attempt equality and the analyzer's three-operation-per-probe
proxy are reproducible. Complete charged-cost equality is **unmeasured**:
IC group additions and operation-kind counts were not emitted. The current
leaderboard therefore keeps the recorded proxy ratio separately and sets
`counted_S_old_over_new` to `null`. The historical zero at weight zero is an
empty lower bound, not measured zero total work. It does not establish the
cross-method order at that weight. No complete-cost improvement is inferred.
