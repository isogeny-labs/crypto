# APS coefficient-generation comparison

Status: exploratory observations; prior producer scripts and raw receipts were not retained. The numbers below are reported observations, not independently replayed measurements. [pcc:unverified]

## Setting and question

For a prime-order group generated by P, write Q = [w]P and generate labeled points R_i = [a_i]P + [b_i]Q. A signed point relation gives A P + B Q = O. If B is nonzero modulo the group order, w = -A/B; otherwise the relation is uninformative. The question is whether cheaper coefficient generation preserves useful relations under a four-block search.

The reported panel used E/F_65521: y² = x³ + 44x + 1, group order 65419, P = (13486, 56515), Q = (51044, 21692) = [12345]P; four blocks of 16 globally distinct x-coordinates; seeds 0–199. A signed pair-sum join enumerated the eight sign patterns up to global negation and checked reported solutions against the fixture scalar. This join is a direct point search, not the paper's polynomial-system solver. [pcc:unverified]

## Reported same-harness comparison

The producer reported fixed-base tables constructed once outside the timings, with each walk's seed and step computation included. First-useful times are conditional on success and do not charge retries; full scans continue after the first useful result. The fixed-base setup cost is excluded. Timings are single-host CPython observations subject to contention; no per-run records survived. [pcc:unverified]

These observations favor block-local walks on this fixture and direct search. They do not determine the cost of the paper's polynomial solver, performance at larger orders, or a complete cold single-target comparison. The 199/200 row leaves one failed attempt whose retry cost is absent. [pcc:unverified]

## Other reported screens

A single shared walk split across the four blocks produced 547200 trivial and zero useful signed relations in 200 runs. For R_i = R_0 + iD, any relation R_i + R_j - R_k - R_l with i+j=k+l has both coefficient sums zero. Independent walks per block avoid this particular cross-block construction when one point is chosen per block, but correlations across tuples remain and do not imply random-system behavior. [pcc:unverified]

In a separate implementation and seed range 410000–410199, filtering random pairs by x < floor(p/4) or x mod 4 = 0 required respectively 51208 and 51353 candidate points, compared with 12807 unfiltered; the reported useful-relation counts were 1613, 1631, and 1635, respectively. No solver-specific benefit was measured. [pcc:unverified]

With b=1, the separate screen reported 982 useful and 611 trivial relations, and useful results in 198/200 runs. Every balanced choice of signs has B=0. Independently selected signed two-bit coefficients gave 1581 useful and zero trivial relations with 199/200 successful runs; that implementation used generic fixed-base tables rather than a sparse-specific addition chain. [pcc:unverified]

## Reproduction gap and next test

The prior scripts were ephemeral, and no per-run outputs, machine configuration, hashes, or independent replay are available. The table is therefore a historical lead, not a reproducible benchmark or a validated performance claim. A fresh comparison needs a versioned implementation and frozen protocol, retained per-run outcomes and timings, charged setup and retry costs, resource details, independent point and scalar replay, and measurements of the actual APS polynomial construction and solver. Existing experiment admission and checkpoint requirements apply before running it.
