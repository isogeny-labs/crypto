# Fused companion validation contract

Exact finite computation, prime coefficient fields, public generator inputs.
This is a fresh component validation, not a restart or continuation of the
capped Schur producer. No elliptic source-field extension, scalar labels,
index calculus, GHS, or generic elliptic search.

Execute all 849 plan contexts once in candidate/list and once in AA/tuple
order: 1698 rows, 34248 products. First 369 per arm are ALL candidate contexts
from the sealed block panel, with every one of its 16164 products consumed
exactly once in its recorded full/inverse/principal stage. Archived outputs
were independently dense-verified; archived counts are retained as baseline
metadata, not a newly timed arm. Next 480 per arm are all Cartesian carry
fixtures specified in plan.json; seeded generator and vector entries drawn
row-major G then K then x with Random(row seed), or all r-1. Both directions.

Every product must equal its reference, agree exactly with reconstructed
field/native/byte counters and multiplication event records, reject four
changed output/count/trace/sign records and three equal wraps. Carry fixtures
also compare every packed intermediate to the coefficient-list reference,
check maxima against the proved bound and reject two uncertified radices.
One concrete primitive carry certificate distinguishes actual overflow from
proof-guard rejection. Total controls: 136992 mutations,102744 equal wraps,
3840 radix guards. AA payload hashes must match for every context.

Record raw output, counters, multiplication events, trace fingerprints and
carry coefficient maxima. Full traces are compared in memory; their hashes
are retained. All native volumes are descriptive counted units, not an
instruction count, elapsed-time or peak-memory estimate. Kernel loop/index,
assertion, trace callbacks and checker work excluded from arithmetic ledgers.
Independent accounting reconstructs every non-product native volume with
quotient/remainder arithmetic, checking each multiplication against operands.

Single local invocation with 3600-second timeout, no restart or retry.
Flush complete contexts, preserve partial output on error/cap. Full analyzer
and export only after producer exit0 and all1698rows. No excluded fixtures,
optional stopping, pilot numerical execution, hidden downsampling or postdata
threshold changes. All inputs/sources/analyzer/expanded seeds locked and
committed before first trial. Exact fixed cohort; statistical power and
performance timing not applicable. CPU/host measured descriptively.
