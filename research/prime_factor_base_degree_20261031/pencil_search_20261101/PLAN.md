## Construction hypothesis

Fix E:y²=x³+Ax+B and h0(x)=a x²+b x+c with a≠0. Let

    offset(u)=t0+sum_i w_i u_i,    u_i²-u_i=0.

Use every rational point with y=±(h0(x)+offset(u)) for at least one Boolean
label u. The two summand slots have independent labels, all of which remain
variables in the global selector. Keep all four ordinate-sign branches.
Do not require any individual shifted graph to split completely. Empty
fibres supply no points; repeated labels or signs are deduplicated in FB1.

The four secant residuals still imply actual point addition and curve
membership. Boolean equations force each label into the prescribed finite
code. Expected top ideal: the earlier genuine nonmonomial quadratic core,
plus squares of all2k selector variables. The predicted first vanishing
homogeneous degree is2k+3. This is a hypothesis until the actual canonical
top ideal, upper certificate and lower witness are checked.
