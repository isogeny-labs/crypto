# Frozen single degree-five node-orbit screen

`search.py`, SHA-256 `ec003d36d4257c16f20169820f43a59576ce0d54c7cf34592683c75b7eea94b1`, is frozen before counted runs. Case 0 uses the point `(1:zeta^127:zeta^143)`; case 1 uses `(1:zeta^218:zeta^72)`, with `zeta=GF(3^5,'zeta').gen()` in Sage. These are the two exponent patterns in Kudo--Harashita Section 4.1 (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.07270). The paper does not specify a minimal polynomial for `zeta`; thus the counted claim is about these **exact Sage field elements** unless a separate PGL3(F3) orbit-equivalence check establishes that they cover the paper's intended representatives.

Each node's Frobenius orbit has length five. Values and two derivatives at one point give 15 F3-linear constraints on 28 sextic coefficients, hence a 13-dimensional kernel (asserted by code). The screen enumerates all `(3^13-1)/2=797161` projective coefficient classes in each exact position. Nodes have no points over F3, F9, F27, or F81, so the normalization counts for degrees 1 through 4 equal plane-sextic counts. For degree 5, each of the five nodes contributes +1 or -1 relative to the singular point according to its tangent-cone split; this yields correction +5 or -5 for an ordinary orbit. Nonordinary fifth-count survivors are recorded but excluded from the ordinary-node conclusion.

Counted commands: `sage -python search.py 0 > FULL_0.json 2> FULL_0.stderr` and similarly for case 1. The output records all fourth-count survivors, their fifth plane counts and tangent data, and any of the eight trace-class matches. The 10,000-vector preflights were execution checks only. A class match still requires direct count replay, irreducibility, singularity and geometric-genus checks, complete L-polynomial, prior-work check, subgroup transfer, and full attack/rho benchmark.
