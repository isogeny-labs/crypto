# Field-count clarification

The frozen protocol's “eleven unavailable output fields” counts the eleven
underlying arithmetic requests. The three `kn_factorization` rows also
repeat their timeout in the derived largest-factor-bit-length field.
Counting these derived fields gives **fourteen timeout-valued fields** in
ten rows, associated with **nine distinct integers**. The target list,
formulae, search budgets and acceptance conditions are unchanged. Preserve
the original protocol with this clarification beside it.
