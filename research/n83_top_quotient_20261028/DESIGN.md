# Graded quotient certificates for the fixed n83 PDP systems

The preceding bounded Singular computations returned no finite top-form degree
of regularity. This stage asks whether explicit graded quotient maps can give
rigorous obstructions without a full Groebner basis. It keeps all48 saved n83,
ell27 systems, all three original curves, m2..9 and both target kinds. It does
not create a new factor base or change any original equation.

## Literature and interpretation

Caminata–Gorla, https://arxiv.org/html/2112.05579, distinguishes first fall,
last fall, solving degree and degree of regularity defined from the top-form
ideal. Kosters–Yeo, https://arxiv.org/html/1503.08001, establishes binary first
falls and explains why proximity to regularity needs justification. Both primary
texts were read again before this stage. The fixed-base source and exact fall
certificates were inspected. No claim of a new general theorem is made.

Let H be the ideal generated by the highest homogeneous component of each
**original saved input equation**, before any row reduction or added consequence.
We use dreg = min{d : (R/H)_d=0} when R/H is Artinian. A graded surjection from
R/H onto k[z1,...,zk]/(z1^2,...,zk^2) proves dreg >= k+1: the squarefree product
of all retained variables is nonzero in degree k. If every original variable
square lies in H, dreg <= nvars+1. These are bounds, not exact Hilbert series.
A graded surjection onto k[u] proves positive Krull dimension and no finite
vanishing degree in this convention. It says nothing about whether the
Castelnuovo–Mumford regularity is finite or about the affine ideal's dimension.

## Binary/Koblitz certificates

Parse the full expanded original polynomials over F2; cancel terms before
extracting each top. Original field equations contribute v_i^2 (never zero at
this step). Check every variable has its field equation. Test two preregistered
projections: retain the first27 base selectors, and retain all27m base selectors;
set other variables to zero, then quotient by retained squares. Every non-field
top monomial must either involve a killed variable or a repeated retained
variable. Keep all failed candidate projections and their surviving monomials.
Select the strongest verified lower bound from these two explicit certificates;
this is deterministic proof search, not a statistical comparison. All variable
squares give the upper bound. Fresh replay recomputes the entire certificate.

## Prime certificates

Check exact literal ring, every x-block definition, every S3-chain equation,
all selector field equations and equation counts against the frozen source.
The source S3 polynomial has degree-four top
x^2 y^2+x^2 z^2+y^2 z^2-2x^2yz-2xy^2z-2xyz^2 when all three coordinates are
variable, and x^2 y^2 when z is the constant target. The coefficient of the
first x-selector/intermediate squared times the first y-selector squared is1,
so the degree-four component is nonzero. Blocks are disjoint; constants a,b,T
cannot introduce degree-four terms beyond this formula.

For m2 retain all27 selectors of x0 and one selector of x1, and quotient by
all retained squares. x1^2 then vanishes, so all top equations vanish. This
proves lower bound29; the54 top field equations give upper bound55.
For m>=3 retain only the first intermediate u0. All selectors and all other
intermediates map to zero. Each degree-four top term uses at least two distinct
coordinate blocks, so it vanishes. The saved presentation has no field equation
on u0. Thus the map is onto Fp[u0]. The certificate does not add an absent field
equation or confuse positive-dimensional top forms with affine inconsistency.

## Controls and execution

Five native control tests: square-zero projection positive and negative cases;
linear top not silently dropped; missing field equation rejected; cancellation
before top extraction; prime m2/m3 formula fixtures and changed selector rejected.
These are software controls, not substituted experimental inputs.

Freeze and commit source/protocol before affected data. Run all48 inputs once,
then replay all48 certificates in a fresh process. One native process at a time,
8GiB virtual-memory cap,600 seconds each producer/replay,1500-second outer budget.
No retries or deleted files. Existing-host fallback follows the already recorded
EKS shared-bucket access failure; the EKS inventory must show no duplicate job.
Upload periodically and on exit; seal the raw manifest and verify S3 readback.
Counted exact statements only: no timing comparison, statistical inference or
hypothesis promotion. Incomplete certificates and failures remain recorded.

## Relevance to elite bases

Before spending another full enumeration, this certificate dataset distinguishes
base-selection effects from obstructions caused by the polynomial presentation.
A later comparison must specify whether it optimizes original top-form
regularity, solving degree, or LFD, and must retain original and transformed
systems separately. Adding derived low-degree equations may change the top ideal
without changing the solution set; such a gain is not evidence for a better
factor base. Matched candidate bases and held-out performance remain required.
