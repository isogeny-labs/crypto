## Retained changes

In `src/cryptanalysis/prime_root_trace.rs`, quadratic-modulus verifier powering now uses a two-coefficient quotient-ring representation rather than generic polynomial vectors and division. Three reductions of work:

1. Horner reduction for an arbitrarily long base, replacing quotient allocation and a long division.
2. Fused modular polynomial multiplication using `x²=-d*x-c`, eliminating the intermediate convolution and remainder.
3. Symmetric fused squaring, eliminating temporary square/reduction vectors and unneeded repeated cross products.

Both monic and nonmonic quadratic moduli are supported; the latter is normalized once. Other modulus degrees and the producer trace are unchanged. Public-data, variable-time code.
