# Root-finding optimization, round 36 (2026-11-19)

## Scope and changes

Changed `src/cryptanalysis/prime_root_bounded.rs`, in the independent
certificate replay (not the bounded root producer):

1. `Replay::remainder`: constant nonzero divisor returns zero remainder immediately;
   skip inversion when divisor is monic; eliminate the algebraically zero leading
   cancellation; skip zero high coefficients and zero divisor coefficients.
2. `Replay::monic_gcd`: reuse the improved remainder and omit unnecessary final
   inversion/scaling for already-monic GCDs, including zero coefficient work.
3. `Replay::square_diagonals`: retain the original dense traversal and select
   a zero-eliding diagonal traversal for sparse polynomial squares.

The replay implementation remains independent of the producer. No new claim
of verifying the producer's complete operation trajectory is made.

## Local profiling and limitations

CPU-pinned Intel Xeon Platinum 8573C, GCC and Clang `-O3` native C++
arithmetic mirrors, 13 alternating A/B medians, 5,760 exact comparisons
per compiler, and GCC UBSan passed. Local mirrors were used as regression
screens only, not substituted for actual compiled Rust results.

The `perf` executable was absent. A direct Linux `perf_event_open`
software task-clock attempt returned `EACCES` (errno 13); hardware
counters are likewise unavailable in this restricted container. The
locally reported timings are pinned wall-clock values, not `perf` cycle
counts or PMU-backed profiling. Keep this limitation explicit.

The CI workflow `Workflow files parse` still fails on an unrelated
missing historical artifact
`research/weak_curve_properties_lean_20261011/root_lib.stdout`.
Repository-wide validation is not complete. Keep the PR draft until
those checks and representative relation-generation throughput are evaluated.
