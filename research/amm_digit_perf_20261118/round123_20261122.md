# Round 123: cached leading inverse for zero-constant quadratics

2026-11-22. Draft [PR #4909](https://github.com/isogeny-labs/crypto/pull/4909).

## Retained production optimization

In `src/cryptanalysis/prime_radical_root.rs`, `RadicalRootCtx::quad_roots` previously called `Fp::inv(a)` for `a*x^2+b*x=0`, `a != 1`, `b != 0`. It now uses `self.inv_leading(a)`, which reuses precomputed inverses for `a=2,3,4` and otherwise falls back to the original inversion. The roots and public-input variable-time semantics are unchanged.

## Local perf comparison, two independent executions

The following are CPU-pinned **C++ arithmetic reproductions**, **not source-exact production Rust timings**. Linux `perf_event_open` **PERF_COUNT_SW_TASK_CLOCK** succeeded on Intel Xeon Platinum 8573C, GCC 14.2 / Clang 17, `-O3 -march=native`, 13 alternating A/B trials per execution, median nanoseconds/query.

| Case | Before ns | After ns | Speedup | Replication |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| p=1,000,000,007, a in {2,3,4}, c=0, GCC | 8.43 | 2.26 | **3.73x** | **3.91x** |
| p=1,000,000,007, a in {2,3,4}, c=0, Clang | 7.27 | 3.73 | **1.95x** | **1.92x** |
| p=4,294,967,291, a in {2,3,4}, c=0, GCC | 8.92 | 2.26 | **3.94x** | **3.07x** |
| p=4,294,967,291, a in {2,3,4}, c=0, Clang | 7.28 | 3.76 | **1.94x** | **2.11x** |

Random-leading controls showed mixed near-parity results, with 0.956–1.057x in the first execution and 0.990–1.026x in most repeated cases. General nonzero-constant quadratic controls ranged roughly 0.94–1.07x. **Do not apply the fast-path speedup to general root-finding.** Hardware PMU cycle/instruction/cache counters were unavailable (`ENOENT`); `perf` CLI absent.

## Validation

GCC and Clang each passed 49,152 equality comparisons per field, for both 32-bit prime fields, with independent polynomial evaluations of all returned roots; repeat executions also passed. GCC ASan/UBSan passed on the validation-only path. `round123_quad_ab.py` and `radical-root-round123.yml` implement native source-exact Rust correctness and paired timing checks, including two zero-constant workloads. **A completed native Rust run is not verified**, so these timings remain local C++ screening results.

## Rejected optimization candidates

1. Direct `sqrt(-c)` for monic `x^2+c`: rejected after GCC p=2^32-5 regressions around 0.89x on known roots and 0.90–0.98x on uniform coefficients.
2. Direct `sqrt(-c/a)` for nonmonic pure quadratics with small `a`: rejected after mixed compiler results, including a 0.87x uniform control.
3. General cached `inv(2a)` dispatch for `a=2,3,4`: rejected after regressions on random-leading controls.

The retained small-leading, zero-constant fast path remains **draft/unmerged** pending native Rust A/B, whole-repository CI and downstream throughput tests. No ECC2K binary-field relations/sec gain has been demonstrated.
