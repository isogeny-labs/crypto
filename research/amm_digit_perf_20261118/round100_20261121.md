# Round 100: remove identity operations from traced polynomial multiplication

Date: 2026-11-21 Pacific. PR: [#4880](https://github.com/isogeny-labs/crypto/pull/4880) (draft). Source baseline: `06d01cfbf7f4df79f9f247240124eea80e8e6bf4` (Round 99). Candidate code commit: `bddd56dff124e4366dfce5888f1fe9b4b5f2c601`. Tested workflow/harness commit: `ec848c0dd2ca25730e4f14276002219569cdcfb4`.

## Changes

In `src/cryptanalysis/prime_root_trace.rs`, `Engine::mul` now (1) skips zero right-hand coefficients (zero left-hand coefficients were already skipped), (2) skips traced field multiplication when either factor is 1, and (3) directly assigns a product to a zero coefficient accumulator instead of tracing an addition. The exact polynomial result and final `Product` certificate are unchanged; trace field-operation counts intentionally decrease.

## Validation and next steps

The Rust independent verifier accepts the shortened trace and verifies the exact `Product` result; the 26-test full isolated suite passed. Earlier workflow A/B comparisons that checksum *trace operation counts* can fail by construction because we intentionally remove field events; e.g. the legacy Round96 CI job failed its trace checksum comparison despite passing its targeted correctness tests. Maintain this distinction and do not silently rewrite old baselines.

**Keep PR #4880 draft and unmerged** pending (1) isolating and fixing or consciously accepting the reproduced 1e9+7 degree-eight regression; (2) broad repository CI; (3) representative ECC2K relation-generation throughput in relations/sec. All source-exact Rust benchmarks measure prime-field root solve and verification, not end-to-end index-calculus relation finding.

Raw local C++ `perf_event_open` CSVs, sanitizer results, and reproducer source are delivered in the conversation artifact `rootfind-round100-20261121.zip`.
