# Round 94 (2026-11-21): skip redundant producer operations

## Patch

Relative to frozen Round 93 at `05ee9475811cc432d83058e2064bc1065153ec96`, `src/cryptanalysis/prime_root_trace.rs` now:
1. Reuses the product without polynomial division in modular exponentiation when its degree is already below the modulus degree. The PowerBegin/PowerStep/PowerEnd independent verification remains intact; only a no-op Division event is omitted.
2. Skips inverse and coefficient multiplication for a Euclidean GCD result already monic.
3. Extracts a root from a monic linear factor as `-constant mod p`, rather than inversing its unit leading coefficient.

The changes preserve the certificate schema and independent verifier. New tests cover multiple prime fields, roots and trace replay. No index-calculus relation-generation throughput has been measured.

Intel Xeon Platinum 8573C, GCC 14.2 and Clang 17 with -O3 -march=native, 17 alternating before/after trials, median ns/op, 20,000 randomized equivalence comparisons per executable, UBSan passed. **These timings are C++ operation analogues, not native Rust production timings.**

### `perf` limitation

The local `perf` executable was not present and direct `perf_event_open` returned `Permission denied` for CPU cycles and task-clock. Local speedups therefore use elapsed time, not CPU PMU counters. Do not interpret the numbers as CPU cycles or IPC.
