# Round 109: bounded two-fold Mersenne reduction and sparse quartic modulus

Date: 2026-11-22. Base for incremental native A/B: frozen Round108 commit `ad28594bf268b163d740ee2c8d3d61740e20e17e`. Source implementation commit `c8d321ef73c489c54c88ba68984d7c693f4e6953`.

## Code

In `src/cryptanalysis/prime_root_trace.rs`:

1. For `p = 2^61 - 1`, replace the third `u128` end-around-carry fold in `quartic_mersenne_reduce` with a single subtraction after two folds. Each quartic coefficient is at most four products, hence `value <= 4*(p-1)^2`. After the first fold the high part is at most four, and after the second the residue is at most `p+4`. Therefore a subtraction when `>=p` is sufficient. This is not a general-purpose `u128` reducer.
2. In `quartic_reduce`, dispatch sparse moduli when `c[1]==0 || c[2]==0`. Skip multiplications by zero; subtract the high coefficient directly when modulus coefficient equals one. The ordinary dense inner loop is unchanged.
3. Add independently computed `u128` oracle tests for multiply/square over 12,000 dense/sparse/extreme quartic cases, plus boundary checks for folding.
