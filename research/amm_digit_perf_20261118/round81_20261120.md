# Round 81: independent verifier polynomial products and modular power (2026-11-20 PT)

## Scope

Baseline: Round80 commit `ef04a641d57addca62f4de1b75a89bcf08bc72c6`. Changed source: `src/cryptanalysis/prime_root_trace.rs`.
All changes are to the independently replayed/verifiable polynomial root certificate, not the root producer or certificate schema.

1. **Monic linear factor multiplication:** `vproduct` computes `f(x)(x+c)` by the one-product-per-coefficient identity `result[k]=c*f[k]+f[k-1]` instead of generic convolution, with a pure shift for `c=0`.
2. **Verifier stand-alone polynomial exponentiation:** `vpower` skips Euclidean reduction when `degree(base)<degree(modulus)`.
3. **Trace replay exponentiation initialization:** `PowerBegin` likewise reuses already-reduced base and the unit residue when the modulus has positive degree; never skips reduction for a constant modulus.
4. **Monic linear-root certificate checking:** compare `root==-constant mod p` without field multiplication when the polynomial's leading coefficient is one. Nonmonic factors retain the original independent check.

Proof obligation: canonical operands and prime `p<=2^61-1` ensure intermediate sums of two residues fit in u64; the specialized product agrees with full convolution coefficient by coefficient. Both operand orders and zero/one/boundary constants are explicitly tested over 3, 17, 65521, 1e9+7, and 2^61-1.
