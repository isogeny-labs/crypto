# Root-finding verifier optimizations: round 27 (2026-11-18)

Scope: exact production Rust `src/cryptanalysis/prime_root_trace.rs`, verifier-only arithmetic. This is **not** a measurement of complete polynomial root extraction or ECDLP relation generation.

## Changes

1. **Monic monomial multiplication:** when multiplying by `1` or `x^k`, copy and shift coefficients directly instead of reducing coefficient products. The shift allocates its complete destination once. An initial `vec![0; k]` + `extend_from_slice()` implementation regressed badly on actual Rust and was replaced after a benchmark.
2. **Monic monomial division:** divide by `x^k` using `(quotient, remainder) = (a[k..], a[..k])` with canonical trimming, avoiding modular long division and all field products.
3. **Very sparse squaring:** degree ≥5 with zero coefficients at indices 1 and 2 is scanned for at most two nonzero terms. Such squares require one or three field multiplications. Generic dense inputs return to symmetric squaring.

The optimizations operate on **public** verifier inputs, not constant-time secret-key arithmetic.

## Local C++ mirror profiling

Pinned Intel Xeon Platinum 8573C CPU 0, GCC 14.2 and Clang 17 `-O3`; deterministic input generation, 13 paired trials, medians of 128-fixture workloads. GCC and Clang each passed **16,128 exact equivalence checks** over seven primes, and GCC UBSan passed.

Representative results at p=1,000,000,007:
- Multiply by x⁴: GCC **2.54×**, Clang **2.44×**.
- Two-term polynomial square: GCC **9.55×**, Clang **5.94×**.
- Division by x²: GCC **11.24×**, Clang **11.23×**.
- Dense product control: GCC **0.97×**, Clang **0.91×** (a local regression).

The Linux `perf` executable is absent and direct `perf_event_open` returned `EACCES`, even for software task-clock. Thus **local measurements are CPU-pinned wall-clock**, not PMU cycles/instructions. The C++ numbers are screening reproductions, distinct from the actual production Rust measurements. Raw source, GCC/Clang CSV and UBSan/perf diagnostics are in the downloadable local artifact.
