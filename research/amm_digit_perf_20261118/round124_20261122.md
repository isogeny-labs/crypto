# Round 124: trace-engine arithmetic profiling (2026-11-22)

## Retained experimental optimization

`src/cryptanalysis/prime_root_trace.rs` now caches a 64-bit Barrett reciprocal once when the validated prime is at most `u32::MAX`. Canonical `FieldOp::Mul` and `FieldOp::Square` avoid integer division. The calculation is exactly modular reduction: for `x=a*b<2^64`, `mu=floor(2^64/p)`, and `q=floor(x*mu/2^64)`, `x-q*p` is in `[0,2p)`. One conditional subtraction produces the exact prior residue. The independent verifier remains unchanged.

## Investigated and rejected

1. **Quadratic cached inverse for nonzero constant terms**: `inv(2a)` replaced with `half(inv(a))` for a=2,3,4. Complete root time remained mixed and known-root controls regressed.
2. **Atkin square root, p=5 mod 8**: check against `-a` and eliminate the second square verification. Mathematically equivalent on canonical inputs, but Clang uniformly random tests regressed up to ~3%.
