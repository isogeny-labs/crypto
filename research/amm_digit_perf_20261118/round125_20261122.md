# Round 125 (2026-11-22 local): canonical degree scan and single leading normalization

## Starting point
PR #4909 was **already merged** when this round began (merge commit `0d4721abfeb0c402cb7de6227e0f2108101162dc`). Start from its current `main`, not from the stale Round114 branch. Optimize `RadicalRootCtx::roots` without changing its public interface.

## Candidate audit and changes
1. **Retained:** initial degree-trimming loop formerly called `is_multiple_of(p)` unconditionally. Check `x==0` and `x<p` first; only calculate `x%p` for noncanonical words. This removes division for canonical leading coefficients.
2. **Retained:** after the first scan has established that the leading coefficient is nonzero modulo `p`, a second scan rechecks the same predicate in degree 2–4. Replace it with one canonical-or-modulo reduction of that known-leading word.
3. **Control unchanged:** degree 0–1 normalization and linear-root dispatch were intentionally preserved after earlier negative native-Rust experiments on this code. Retain generic reduction of noncanonical interior coefficients. Do not claim an arithmetic/root-solver algorithm improvement.

Algebraic contract: the degree is the highest index with `f[index] % p != 0`, even if the representation has padding or noncanonical multiples of `p`. Both transforms preserve the canonical coefficient sequence, root ordering and errors.
