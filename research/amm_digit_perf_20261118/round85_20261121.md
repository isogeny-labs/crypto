# Root-finding optimization Round85 (2026-11-21)

**Experimental draft [PR #4876](https://github.com/isogeny-labs/crypto/pull/4876) based on Round84 [PR #4875](https://github.com/isogeny-labs/crypto/pull/4875). Keep unmerged.** Production source is `src/cryptanalysis/prime_root_trace.rs`.

## Changes and correctness

1. Fixed-degree descending producer polynomial division eliminates per-quotient-digit tail trimming. It skips zero high coefficients without producing field events and truncates/reduces the remainder once.
2. Monic divisor `X^k` uses quotient/remainder coefficient slicing rather than long division.
3. Shorter-degree dividends return zero quotient directly without its redundant allocation.

All paths preserve the independently verified `RootEvent::Division` certificate, existing sink/error and work-budget behavior. New source-exact Rust test compares the producer quotient and remainder against independent verifier `vdiv` for five primes, degrees 0..8, and four divisor shapes. The Rust test and seven pre-existing certificate/safety regressions passed.

## Local CPU-pinned C++ reproduction (not Rust)

AMD EPYC 9V74, GCC 14.2 and Clang 17 `-O3 -march=x86-64-v3 -fno-omit-frame-pointer`, 15 alternating paired trials, 10,000 operations per trial/workload, three primes and seven polynomial shapes. Each compiler passed **21,000 exact quotient/remainder equality comparisons** and GCC UBSan passed. At p=1e9+7:

| Division case | GCC before/after (ns) | GCC speedup | Clang speedup |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| degree 8 / monic X^3 | 60.642 / 31.458 | **1.928x** | **1.609x** |
| degree 2 / degree 5 | 24.396 / 17.061 | **1.430x** | **1.128x** |
| degree 8 / dense monic degree 3 | 94.825 / 95.699 | 0.991x | 1.102x |
| degree 8 / dense nonmonic degree 3 | 283.405 / 287.492 | 0.986x | 1.062x |

Independent repeat with GCC: X^3 2.004x; short dividend 1.427x; dense monic 1.006x; nonmonic 0.990x. These isolated kernel gains are not production Rust solver gains.

## perf diagnostic and next decisions

Local `perf` executable was unavailable. Direct `perf_event_open` for cycles and task-clock returned `EACCES` (kernel `perf_event_paranoid=2`). Thus local measurements use `steady_clock` paired medians and **no hardware PMU counter claims** are made.

Next: independently ablate the fixed-degree general division versus the two shape fast paths; repeat on isolated hardware with a usable `perf stat`; run full repository tests and measure representative **index-calculus relations per second**. Do not merge the PR until the p=1e9+7 degree-eight regression has been resolved and gains are demonstrated in end-to-end representative workloads.
