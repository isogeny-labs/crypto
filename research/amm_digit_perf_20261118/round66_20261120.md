# Round 66: unit-linear synthetic arithmetic (November 20, 2026)

## Production change

[PR #4706](https://github.com/isogeny-labs/crypto/pull/4706) now has four independently metered optimizations in `src/cryptanalysis/prime_root_bounded.rs`:

1. Producer division by `x+1` and `x-1`, generating quotient and remainder with one reverse synthetic pass.
2. Producer modular reduction by `x+1` and `x-1`, evaluating by Horner at the unit root.
3. Independent certificate replay remainder, using Horner with replay-specific metering.
4. Independent certificate replay cancellation, using Horner rather than in-place 17-coefficient cancellation.

For `x+c` with `c=±1`, the residue is `f(-c)`. Recurrence from highest coefficient: `h = a[i] - h` for `x+1` and `h = a[i] + h` for `x-1` (operations in Fp). Producer keeps a `COPY_WORK` charge for each nonzero leading cancellation, while replay checks every eliminated degree. These are public-input algorithms; no constant-time claim.

### Correctness

Local GCC and Clang C++ arithmetic reproductions passed **8,192 cases each**, comparing old vs new quotient, residue, and simulated budget counters, and checking the residue against an independent `u128` Horner oracle. GCC UBSan passed. The added production Rust regression tests check `(x+c)q+r=f` over multiple prime fields and compare both independent remainders to the wide-integer oracle. A separate complete root/event/certificate-stream test is included.

Source-exact native Rust A/B and complete root/event/certificate-stream equivalence are also covered by `round66_unit_linear_isolated.py`, integrated with the radical-root CI workflow. This report does not assert native Rust performance until CI results are available.

## Local compiler timing

CPU-pinned AMD EPYC 9V74; GCC 14.2 and Clang 17; `-O3 -march=native`; 13 alternating before/after trials, 110,000 operations per trial. Polynomial lengths 2–17; dense and sparse; primes 1,000,000,007, 1,000,000,009, and 2^61−1 (also p=101 for correctness).

Representative medians for p=1,000,000,007, dense inputs, divisor x+1:

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Producer division | 41.00 | 23.30 | **1.76×** | 49.68 | 22.16 | **2.24×** |
| Producer reduction | 42.95 | 22.24 | **1.93×** | 42.14 | 17.08 | **2.47×** |
| Replay remainder | 37.67 | 20.24 | **1.86×** | 43.80 | 17.04 | **2.57×** |

Across all 48 unique workloads per compiler, GCC mirror speedups ranged 1.59–1.97× (median 1.80×); Clang ranged 2.24–3.01× (median 2.64×). **These are C++ reproductions, not native production Rust or complete root extraction.** The native Rust metering/deadline overhead is intentionally not included in the mirror. Full raw timing CSVs and sanitizer diagnostics are retained in the local round-66 artifact.

## Local perf limitation

The executable `perf` is missing. Direct `perf_event_open` probes for task-clock, cycles and instructions returned `EACCES`. The sandbox has `perf_event_paranoid=2` and lacks `CAP_PERFMON`; hardware counters could not be collected. All local timing is `steady_clock` wall time on an affinity-pinned core, not PMU cycles or instructions.

## Scope and next measurement

The new paths apply only to **unit-linear divisors**, not dense general moduli. Source-exact Rust CI is necessary before treating speedups as production results. Representative complete root extraction plus certificate replay, then **index-calculus relations per second**, remain decisive measures. The branch/PR is not merged.
