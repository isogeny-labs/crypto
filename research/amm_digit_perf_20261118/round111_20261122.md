# Root finding performance, Round 111 (2026-11-22)

## Summary

Source: `src/cryptanalysis/prime_root_trace.rs`, draft [PR #4880](https://github.com/isogeny-labs/crypto/pull/4880).

The independent certificate verifier was doing some expensive calculations twice: once while checking all recorded power/GCD events, then again at the terminal completeness check or at the end of a split. Reusing a result is safe only when the **exact original operands** match and the recorded chain has been fully recomputed by independent verifier arithmetic.

**Retained candidate, restricted to p = 2^61-1**:
1. Preserve the independently verified `X^p mod f` from a full matched `PowerBegin`/`PowerStep`/`PowerEnd` replay and use it at `Finish` instead of recomputing `vpower`.
2. Preserve the initial verified `gcd(f, X^p - X)` matched to the verified Frobenius and use it at `Finish` rather than repeating `vgcd`.
3. Preserve the exact independently replayed `(X+delta)^((p-1)/2) mod h` result and use it at the corresponding `SplitEnd` rather than repeating `vpower`. If a binding does not match, the verifier falls back to its original arithmetic check.

This changes neither the producer's event trace nor the certificate schema or work counters. Tests alter `PowerEnd`, `GcdEnd`, split factors, and terminal values to ensure tampering is still rejected.

**Rejected**: descending fixed-degree `Engine::div` to avoid per-digit `pop` and `trim`. The local GCC/Clang analog was promising (e.g. ~1.3–1.5x for quadratic inputs) but two source-exact native Rust A/B jobs found significant dense and degree-eight regressions (0.95–0.99x). The production change was **reverted**, while its benchmark/diagnostic source remains archived.

The opposing 65,521 quartic shape swings, seen repeatedly even when the new caching behavior was disabled for that prime, cannot be attributed confidently to the algorithm and remain an acceptance blocker. Keep PR draft and do not merge. Repository-wide checks and ECC2K relations/sec have not been cleared.

## Local Linux software perf measurements

Host: AMD EPYC 9V74, CPU affinity pinned, GCC 14.2/Clang 17; `perf_event_open(PERF_TYPE_SOFTWARE, PERF_COUNT_SW_TASK_CLOCK)` succeeded, hardware cycles/instructions/cache-miss counters unavailable. The `perf` CLI was not installed. 13 alternating A/B trials × two executions per compiler, plus ASan/UBSan.

**Local C++ reproduction only, NOT production Rust:** `verify_reuse_probe.cpp` measures the affected *duplicated modular-power stage*: computing and independently checking a power, then either recalculating it (baseline) or reusing its verified result (candidate). Across quadratic/quartic and 3 prime fields it measured **1.90–2.14x** speedup. Representative quartic measurements:

| Compiler/field | Before task-clock ns | After task-clock ns | Speedup |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| GCC, 2^61-1 | 32,288 | 15,548 | **2.08x** |
| Clang, 2^61-1 | 38,196 | 18,071 | **2.11x** |
| GCC, 1e9+7 | 9,749 | 4,868 | **2.00x** |
| Clang, 1e9+7 | 12,104 | 5,766 | **2.10x** |

All reproduced local before/after results were equal; sanitizer checks passed. This isolated ~2x speedup cannot be substituted for the much smaller source-exact full Rust gains.

The prior `div_probe.cpp` contains the discarded fixed-degree producer candidate and raw local data. Full local source, measurements, and diagnostics were archived separately.
