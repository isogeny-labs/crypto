# Round 47: Quadratic splitting for p ≡ 1 (mod 4) (2026-11-19)

## Implemented

Expanded the existing monic squarefree quadratic split shortcut in `src/cryptanalysis/prime_root_bounded.rs`, with corresponding independent certificate replay:

1. For p ≡ 5 (mod 8), use the direct square-root exponent D^((p+3)/8), conditionally correcting by 2^((p-1)/4).
2. For p=1,000,000,009 (p ≡ 1 mod 8, v₂(p−1)=3), use three-stage Tonelli–Shanks with fixed quadratic nonresidue 11, then verify the square.
3. Use scalar Legendre-symbol tests at both recovered roots to reconstruct the precise polynomial Euler/GCD split factor, including shifted-zero cases.

For any unsupported or unverified case, the original polynomial exponentiation and GCD are retained. Both producer and replay use independent field arithmetic and independent modular-power traversals. No change to certificate structure or event sequence is intended. This is variable-time arithmetic, not constant-time.
