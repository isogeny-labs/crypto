# Round 103: quartic quotient-ring modular-power optimization

Status: **experimental, draft PR #4880; do not merge**. Baseline: frozen Round102 commit `0b88fc4e3e240d7a7aefe38fae9af3c6a57ec27a`. Candidate: `e654da6bd629a76c5c42440139164ead5c99840a`.

## Implementation retained

Production file: `src/cryptanalysis/prime_root_trace.rs`.

1. Degree-four quotient-ring product using stack-resident 7-coefficient convolution and direct reduction of x^6, x^5, x^4.
2. Symmetry-aware squaring: 4 diagonal and 6 unique off-diagonal products instead of a 16-term product.
3. For p <= 2,000,000,000, bounded 64-bit column accumulation, one modulus per output coefficient rather than per coefficient product. Each column has at most four terms, so `4*(p-1)^2 < 2^64`; the 61-bit prime falls back to the existing safe arithmetic.
4. Four-term Horner reduction for arbitrary-length bases and binary exponentiation without repeatedly allocating quotient/remainder polynomials.

Dispatch to the optimized quartic `vpower` is gated by `2^24 <= p <= 2^32-1`; small-field and 61-bit moduli stay on the established path. **The attempted quartic PowerStep replay dispatch was rejected and removed** after source-exact full-verifier regressions. The prover, certificate format and the original generic PowerStep replay remain unchanged.

All **29 non-ignored Rust tests passed** and **2,261 source-exact A/B rows had matching output checksums**. Native Rust microbenchmark improvements do not imply meaningful end-to-end root-finding speedup. Control regressions, particularly 61-bit verify-only controls that do not invoke the new dispatch, indicate whole-module layout/frequency noise or an unrelated regression requiring diagnosis.

### Ablations that informed the final implementation

- Initial quartic multiplication/squaring/replay candidate: isolated quartic power over p=1e9+7 improved approximately 1.37x, but full-solver/verifier results were mixed. [CI](https://github.com/isogeny-labs/crypto/actions/runs/44985644826).
- Bounded 64-bit lazy convolution **with** quartic PowerStep replay: quartic power improved 1.67-1.70x, but complete quartic solve+verify fell to 0.983x and verify-only quartic to 0.946x. **Rejected replay acceleration.** [CI](https://github.com/isogeny-labs/crypto/actions/runs/44985824190).
- Final bounded convolution **without** quartic PowerStep replay: isolated power 1.48-1.51x, complete quartic near parity. [CI](https://github.com/isogeny-labs/crypto/actions/runs/44985958442).

## Local profiling

CPU-pinned (logical CPU 0) GCC 14.2 and Clang 17 algorithmic C++ reproductions on Intel Xeon Platinum 8573C, 13 alternating A/B trials each. Lazy quartic power over p=1e9+7: GCC 4642.586 -> 2288.793 ns (**2.028x**); Clang 3370.037 -> 3119.597 ns (**1.080x**). These are **not production Rust timings**.

Both compilers passed 90,000 randomized multiply/square/Horner oracle comparisons and 3,000 modular-power comparisons. GCC UBSan passed. All benchmark result checksums matched.

The `perf` CLI is absent. Direct `perf_event_open(PERF_COUNT_SW_TASK_CLOCK)` returned `EACCES` (`errno=13`), so neither task-clock nor hardware PMU cycles/instructions were available. Local timings are wall-clock `std::chrono` observations only; do not present them as `perf stat` results.
