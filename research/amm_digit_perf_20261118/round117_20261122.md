# Round 117: specialized discriminant root finding over p=2^32-5

**Date:** 2026-11-22. **Branch:** `automation/radical-root-mersenne-sqrt-round114-20261122`. **PR:** https://github.com/isogeny-labs/crypto/pull/4909. **Production module:** `src/cryptanalysis/prime_radical_root.rs`.

## Retained optimizations
1. For canonical public inputs over `p=4294967291`, calculate the Jacobi symbol in `u32` arithmetic instead of `u64`, avoiding wider Euclidean remainders. The branch is restricted to that modulus after other modulus controls proved inconsistent.
2. Implement the addition chain for the sqrt exponent `(p+1)/4=2^30-1`: exponents `1,3,15,63,4095,2^24-1,2^30-1`. It takes 29 squarings and six field multiplications versus 29 squarings and 30 multiplications in binary powering.

Dedicated source-exact Rust test harness: `research/amm_digit_perf_20261118/round117_sqrt_ab.py`. Workflow: `.github/workflows/radical-root-round117.yml`. The harness reconstructs frozen pre-117 source, compiles both with Rust 1.100.0 at `-C opt-level=3`, pins to CPU 0, verifies 18,450 before/after outputs per run, checks checksums, and records 13 alternating A/B trials.

Intel Xeon Platinum 8573C, CPU-pinned; GCC 14.2 / Clang 17 C++ reproductions. Linux `perf_event_open` **software task-clock** successfully collected measurements over 13–15 alternating A/B trials. **Critical rejection:** Bypassing Jacobi entirely looked good in the local C++ microbenchmark but regressed source-exact Rust complete quadratic throughput approximately 20–22%; reverted. Local randomized arithmetic/equivalence and Clang ASan/UBSan passed.

## Acceptance decision

Leave PR draft and unmerged. Demonstrated **1.09–1.17× source-exact complete quadratic improvement** at p=4294967291 in two runs, but complete cubic gains remain unstable, larger repository validation is not established, and ECC2K binary-field index-calculus relations/sec is unmeasured. No claim of ECDLP speedup.
