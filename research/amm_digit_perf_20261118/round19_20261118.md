# Round 19: factor sparse cubics and even quartics (2026-11-18)

Production branch: `automation/amm-cuberoot-digits-20261118` in [PR #4706](https://github.com/isogeny-labs/crypto/pull/4706). Source: `src/cryptanalysis/prime_radical_root.rs`. Isolated Rust CI: [run #44349020701](https://github.com/isogeny-labs/crypto/actions/runs/44349020701).

## Changes (public-input prime-field roots)

1. **Depressed cubic `x^3 + P*x` (Q=0).** Factor `x*(x^2+P)`. One field square root and zero insertion replace Cardano discriminant, Fp/Fp2 cube extraction and rational reconstruction. `P=0` handles the single distinct zero root.
2. **Monic cubic with zero constant `x^3+c2*x^2+c1*x`.** Factor `x`, call `quad_monic(c2,c1)`, append zero and deduplicate. For `c2=0` with nonzero constant, bypass cubic depression and invoke `cubic_depressed(c1,c0)` directly.
3. **General cubic with zero constant in `roots()`.** Call `quad_roots(a,b,c)`, add zero, bypass leading-coefficient inversion, 3 normalization products and Cardano/AMM.
4. **Nonmonic even quartic `a*x^4+c*x^2+e`.** Set `z=x^2`, call `quad_roots(a,c,e)`, take `±sqrt(z)`, deduplicate. This skips Ferrari depression/normalization. The monic even case is intentionally guarded off: local GCC/Clang controls were neutral or regressed.

All operations are variable-time and intended for **public** polynomial coefficients, not secret-dependent cryptographic workloads.

## Limitations and follow-up

- Fast-path gains occur on sparse, algebraically factorable polynomials; **do not extrapolate** 4–5x speedups to dense roots, index-calculus relation throughput, or GPU execution.
- The isolated test module passed. The full library build is a separate gate and has previously failed due unrelated F4/APS diagnostics. Keep PR **draft** until full validation, holistic rootfinder benchmarks, and review pass.
- For future optimization, benchmark factorable polynomial prevalence in actual point-decomposition workloads and end-to-end relation throughput before prioritizing more sparse special cases.
