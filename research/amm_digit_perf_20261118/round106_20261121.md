# Round 106: one monic normalization per traced nonmonic modular power

Date: 2026-11-21 PST / 2026-11-22 UTC. Draft PR #4880.

Source file: src/cryptanalysis/prime_root_trace.rs. Frozen Round105 baseline commit 759c1202aa66ca5cbca1e56b058ac4f7b650399c; final candidate ae7dc6b3f23086f1c31ee81b9528377579d29894.

## Algorithm

During prime-field modular polynomial powering, the initial base reduction and every accumulator/square product reduction previously ran a traced long division by the same often-nonmonic polynomial. Each division traced a fresh Fermat inverse of the same leading coefficient. Normalize the nonmonic modulus once, using a single checked inverse and individually checked coefficient multiplications, then perform all subsequent divisions by its monic equivalent. Unit scaling preserves polynomial residues and roots. PowerBegin continues to bind the original modulus; the independent verifier is unchanged. Retain the original implementation in a separate branch for initially monic moduli.

Also screened fixed-degree descending long division, but did not retain it due to limited gains and regressions in local C++ reproductions.

## Same-source reproduction

The final CI job was rerun without changing the candidate source: [second passing attempt](https://github.com/isogeny-labs/crypto/actions/runs/44999062384) (job 137136796268). Again, 32 tests passed, one was ignored, and 624 A/B case-trials matched. The important repeated speedup ratios were **4.48x** for p61 nonmonic quadratic modular power, **3.26x** for p61 nonmonic quartic modular power, **2.14x** for complete p61 nonmonic quadratic solve+verify, and **1.48x** for complete p61 nonmonic quartic solve+verify. However, p61 monic degree-eight powering repeated its **0.81x** regression and complete monic quartic solve+verify repeated an **0.84x** regression. The negative controls are not noise-free successes: these are repeatable blockers and must not be hidden behind the strong nonmonic improvements.

The performance effect is specific to nonmonic moduli; fixing the monic dispatch/codegen regressions remains the immediate next step.

## Final code-layout screening (commit 1003a5a, 2026-11-22 UTC)
