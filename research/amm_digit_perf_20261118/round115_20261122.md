# Root-finding optimization Round 115 (2026-11-22)

**Status:** candidate implemented, correctness checked, draft PR #4909, not merged. File: `src/cryptanalysis/prime_radical_root.rs`.

## Retained change

For canonical 32-bit prime-field multiplication, compute `mu=floor(2^64/p)` once in `Fp::new`, then replace runtime `(a*b)%p` division with `q=high64((a*b)*mu)`, `r=a*b-q*p` and one conditional subtraction. For `a,b<p<=2^32-1`, `q` is either the exact floor quotient or one smaller, so `0<=r<2p`. Mersenne 61-bit and noncanonical paths retain their existing arithmetic. A separate in-source oracle checks 800,288 products across 8 primes.

## Other optimizations screened

1. Sparse `Fq2::mul` short-circuit for real scalar arguments: 1.7–2.3x isolated gain, **rejected** due dense-control regressions (3–6%) and imaginary-only regressions.
2. Branchless masked modular addition/subtraction: **rejected** due significant GCC regressions, even though Clang sometimes improved.
3. Constant-modulus specializations: **not adopted** due mixed GCC/Clang behavior and poor generality.
