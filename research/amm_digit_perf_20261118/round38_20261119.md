# Round 38 (2026-11-19): early unit GCD and monic normalization

## Production modifications

File: `src/cryptanalysis/prime_root_bounded.rs`, PR [#4706](https://github.com/isogeny-labs/crypto/pull/4706).

1. **Producer Euclid**: return the canonical constant polynomial 1 when the current nonzero divisor has degree zero, without spending a final long-division step and extra Fermat inversions.
2. **Producer normalization**: avoid computing an inverse or multiplying by 1 when the final GCD already has leading coefficient 1.
3. **Independent certificate replay**: recognize a nonzero constant divisor using its own separate Euclidean implementation, skipping the final redundant remainder and normalization.

These shortcuts are algebraically exact in a prime field: any nonzero constant is a unit in Fp[x]. They retain the per-iteration metering/deadline charge before the early return. They are variable-time, public-input algorithms, and do not claim constant-time behavior.

## Local profiling

Local sandbox: Intel Xeon Platinum 8573C, GCC and Clang C++ arithmetic reproductions, `-O3`, CPU pinning via `taskset -c 0`, 13 alternating A/B trials, separate exact checks for **768** polynomial pairs across 2 primes and 3 workload families (all four implementations compared). GCC UBSan passed all comparisons. GCC local coprime producer medians: 1.323x at p=1e9+7 and 1.243x at 2^61-1. Clang local coprime producer medians: 1.328x and 1.274x. Local results are a C++ mirror, **not native Rust**.

`perf` CLI was not installed, and direct `perf_event_open` returned `EACCES` for software task clock, hardware cycles, and instructions (`perf_event_paranoid=2`). No PMU or perf-stat counts were collected; local profiling used CPU-pinned wall-clock only. There was no local `rustc`; the native Rust results come from CI. Raw source, CSV medians, and perf diagnostics are retained in the automation's local downloadable artifact.

## Assessment and next target

Keep the GCD shortcuts: they are exact, improve coprime cases, and have effectively neutral shared-factor controls. Do not claim a material improvement for degree-8 root splitting. Focus subsequent rounds on the modular polynomial powering loop (`Producer::power`, `Replay::modular_power`) and the per-scalar gated arithmetic; measure complete root extraction and index-calculus relation-generation throughput with a frozen full-code baseline. The changes remain on draft PR #4706 until repository-wide validation is resolved.
