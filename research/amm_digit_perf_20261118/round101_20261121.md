## Implemented

In `src/cryptanalysis/prime_root_trace.rs`, the independent certificate verifier now reuses an *independently computed and checked* polynomial division when a following `GcdStep` or accepted `SplitEnd` matches the complete input/output identity. Older/nonmatching traces fall back to an independent `vdiv`; the solver and certificate format are unchanged. The reused remainder is moved instead of cloned. Cache use is gated to degree >=4 root polynomials, except degree >=8 over p<=65521, after native Rust regressions on cheap division. A new adversarial test confirms that a forged GCD quotient is rejected.

Other candidates screened but rejected: fixed-degree traced division, division unit-multiplication bypass, Karatsuba quadratic cross-term, and quadratic-power zero-coefficient guards. Local measurements were neutral or worse, or inconsistent across GCC and Clang.
