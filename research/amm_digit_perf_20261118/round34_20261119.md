# Round 34: guarded sparse product, reduction and independent certificate replay

Date: 2026-11-19 UTC. PR: https://github.com/isogeny-labs/crypto/pull/4706

## Production changes

File: `src/cryptanalysis/prime_root_bounded.rs`. Four public-input, variable-time optimizations:

1. **Producer `product`:** for structured factors whose constant coefficient is zero, skip zero multiplicands and the corresponding multiplication/addition. Preserve the dense loop otherwise.
2. **Producer `reduce_monic`:** for monic moduli with a zero constant or low-order coefficient, skip zero terms during polynomial reduction; retain the dense loop otherwise.
3. **Independent verifier `convolution`:** independently skip zero coefficient pairs on structured inputs while retaining output-diagonal traversal and replay checks.
4. **Independent verifier `cancel_monic`:** skip zero leading factors and zero low-order modulus terms in its descending traversal.

The cheap sparseness guard deliberately does not attempt a complete zero census. Polynomials with only interior zero coefficients may take the original dense loop. Operation counts and budget consumption may change where arithmetic is legitimately avoided; the externally reported roots and high-level events are checked against frozen code. This does not make any constant-time claim.

**Interpretation:** the new branches substantially accelerate sparse microcases, but the constructed-root full solver spends most time elsewhere, so its net acceleration is 0-2.5%. There is no demonstrated improvement to ECDLP relation generation.

## Local Linux perf and C++ screening

Attempted `perf_event_open` with software task-clock and hardware cycles: both `EACCES` (errno 13); `perf` CLI was not installed; `perf_event_paranoid=2`. Thus **no actual perf cycle/instruction counters were obtained**. No workaround or synthetic counter data was used.

CPU-affined GCC 14 and Clang 17 `-O3` C++ arithmetic reproductions used 13 alternating A/B timings. Dense controls were screened for regressions, and a constant/low-coefficient guarded path was retained instead of an unguarded always-check variant. All 11,520 C++ exact reference comparisons passed and GCC UBSan completed. The C++ results are separate from compiled Rust and should not be reported as production speedups. Local source, raw results and perf error diagnostics are archived outside the repository.
