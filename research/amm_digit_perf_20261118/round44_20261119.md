# Round 44 (2026-11-19): dense non-Mersenne root powering

## Retained optimization

Added a **width-three sliding-window modular polynomial exponentiation** path to the bounded root producer in `src/cryptanalysis/prime_root_bounded.rs`. Precompute `b,b³,b⁵,b⁷`, then scan exponent bits from high to low, using squarings and odd-power lookup rather than multiplying once for every set bit. This reduces full polynomial multiplication counts for the two root-extraction exponents `p` and `(p-1)/2` on non-Mersenne primes with dense 20–32-bit exponents. The guard applies only to degree >=4 moduli, exponents >=2²⁰ and <=u32::MAX with at least 12 set bits. All small-prime and 61-bit Mersenne paths retain their existing implementations.

The independent certificate verifier remains unchanged. A replay three-bit window increased replay field work and regressed standalone native-Rust replay performance around 4–5% on degree 6/8. A replay two-bit window screened near neutral with small regressions, so both candidates were **reverted**. This prevents a faster producer from hiding a slower verifier in the combined timing.
