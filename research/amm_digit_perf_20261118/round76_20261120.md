# Root finding round 76: identity pruning of producer field operations

Date: 2026-11-20 (Pacific). Draft PR [#4863](https://github.com/isogeny-labs/crypto/pull/4863), stacked on [#4862](https://github.com/isogeny-labs/crypto/pull/4862).

## Change

`src/cryptanalysis/prime_root_trace.rs`:
1. Assign the first nonzero polynomial-product contribution directly instead of recording `0 + z` as a traced addition. Subsequent additions remain traced.
2. For polynomial long division, bypass field inversion/multiplication when the leading divisor coefficient is one; always write the leading remainder coefficient directly to zero instead of multiplying and subtracting the cancelling term. Other nonzero divisor coefficients retain normal traced subtraction.
3. For GCD normalization, bypass the inverse and coefficient multiplication pass when the last coefficient is one.

The independent root-certificate verifier is unchanged. The new Rust regression test calls the complete solver and verifier for primes 17, 65521, 1000000007 and 2^61-1 across sparse and dense low-degree polynomials. It checks the presence of roots 1 and 2, completion and canonical verification. Source-exact native Rust A/B comparisons are configured in `.github/workflows/rootfind-trace-round76.yml`.

At p=2^61−1, GCC measured 1.037× for dense multiplication, 1.112× for sparse multiplication, 1.231× for monic division, and 1.608× for already-monic normalization. Clang respectively measured 1.047×, 1.080×, 1.201× and 1.500×. Small control regressions were recorded, including Clang 0.975× for nonmonic normalization.

For 10,000 degree-8 operations the field-op counts were:
- dense multiply: 1,620,000 → 1,450,000 (−10.5%);
- sparse multiply: 320,000 → 230,000 (−28.1%);
- monic divide: 550,000 → 400,000 (−27.3%);
- nonmonic divide: 990,000 → 890,000 (−10.1%);
- monic normalization: 90,000 → 0 (−100%).
Count totals include repeated inputs and, in the division cases, modular inversion work. These counts are *not* full solver field-work totals.

Baseline and candidate polynomial outputs matched on every tested input. GCC undefined-behavior sanitizer completed without diagnostics.
