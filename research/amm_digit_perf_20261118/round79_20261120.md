# Round 79: eliminate unused final modular squares

**PR:** [#4868](https://github.com/isogeny-labs/crypto/pull/4868), stacked on Round77 (#4864) rather than Round78 (#4866).

## Production change

- `Engine::pow`: shift the exponent before deciding whether another squaring is needed. The last square, modular reduction, and their field, product and division certificate events are now elided. The final `PowerStep` records the unchanged base.
- Independent `verify` replay: on new final steps, check the unchanged base without redoing a square/division. On historical v1 final steps, accept **only** the verified previous-base squared modulo the certified modulus. All nonterminal power-step checks remain unchanged.
- Regression test: full `solve → verify` over four primes, reconstruct old terminal `PowerStep` values and confirm compatibility, reject forged terminal bases.

Each positive-exponent modular power saves exactly one final polynomial square and its modular reduction, as no later exponent bit reads that base. Results should be unchanged; the certificate trace and charged work legitimately become shorter.
