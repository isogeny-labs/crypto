# Round 112 (2026-11-22): high-degree Mersenne quotient-ring power

## Change

The independent certificate verifier in `src/cryptanalysis/prime_root_trace.rs` now uses a bounded, explicit addition chain for polynomial modular powers in degree 5 through 8 over `p = 2^61 - 1`. Only exponent `p` (Frobenius) and exponent `(p-1)/2` (splitting) dispatch to this method. The producer, certificate schema, independently replayed PowerStep events and unrelated fields/exponents are unchanged.

Write `A_k = a^(2^k-1) mod f`. The identity
`A_(i+j) = (A_i)^(2^j) * A_j mod f` builds blocks of lengths
`2,4,8,16,32,48,56,60`. One extra square and product yields the 61-bit exponent.

| Exponent | Binary squares | Binary products | Chain squares | Chain products |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `2^60 - 1` | 59 | 60 | 59 | 8 |
| `2^61 - 1` | 60 | 61 | 60 | 9 |

This saves 52 quotient-ring polynomial multiplications per call, with no asymptotic claim. The helper reuses the verifier's existing `vsquare`, `vproduct`, and `vpower_reduce`. No production operation counter or root-finding work-budget semantics changed.

## Acceptance and limitations

**Keep PR #4880 draft and unmerged.** While the new degree-5..8 verifier optimization is source-exact, correctness- and checksum-validated, broad repository workflows still contain failures/ongoing checks, previously identified monic and small-field regressions are unresolved, and ECC2K index-calculus relations per second has not been measured. High-degree p61 full-solver runs may hit the existing event cap; do not equate isolated verifier power improvements with end-to-end throughput.
