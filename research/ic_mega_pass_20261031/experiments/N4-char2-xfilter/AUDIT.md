# AUDIT of N4-char2-xfilter (auditor, 2026-11-01)

Verdict: **partially_valid**. The instrument is correct and the raw data reproduce. The headline label "SUPPORTS H0" rests on a pre-registered decision rule that cannot fail, and several README sentences are wrong or overreach. No security claim is made anywhere (good).

## What I checked and what holds
- Code vs protocol: `gain()` computes pi_c(d)=Pr[h(P+Q)=c | h(P)^h(Q)^d=c] exactly (WHT over alphabet x cyclic convolution), matching KN-OPEN-91d4f6 and the RT-EXP-1 definition in KN-FIND-ffe1df (max over (c,d), M_eff, all L^2 pairs). Re-derived by my own brute force (different loop) on the real n=13 filter, j=1,2,3: max |diff| = 0.0. Their 90-case n=7 brute-force check is in raw/validate.json.
- Curves: PARI ellcard re-run for all 21 curves n<=19: #E = h*l, l prime, polynomial irreducible, all OK. Trace functional constant on <P> (value 1), exactly one degenerate mask per curve and it equals the trace mask (P1 confirmed from raw). enum2.c rebuilt with gcc -O3 -march=native gives a byte-identical x(kG) file to the shipped binary (n=17).
- Reproduction: Arm A n=13 random1 a=1 re-run: E_max 0.0439053, argmax 6604, null rep 0.0441228, all identical to raw. Arm B n=15 random1 a=1 j=3 climber: identical masks and E (0.053124). Saved best mask tuples for 7 further cells re-evaluate to the saved E.
- NULL-1sym keeps x(kG)=x(-kG) and the marginal; PRF null depends on x only. Both are real controls.
- Most README numbers match RESULTS.json (E* values, 53 cells, ratio median 0.999, range 0.946-1.107, Arm A ratio 0.932-1.000).

## Findings (severity order)

1. **The pre-registered SUPPORTS rule is vacuous.** Rule: REAL <= max(NULL-1sym, PRF) + E*. In all 29 `<P>` cells that have an E*, E* exceeds E_real itself (E*/E_real = 1.11 to 22.9). Since the nulls are >= 0, REAL - null <= E_real < E*, so the rule cannot fail. 24 of 53 cells have no E* at all (all j=2, several h=4 cells) and are scored "not exceeding" by construction (`exceeds_floor` is False when E* is None). Also, E* is the planted E at the smallest dose that was detected, and for n>=17 that is the lowest dose in the grid, so it is an upper bound on the floor, not the floor. The evidence that actually carries the conclusion is the REAL/NULL-1sym ratio (below), which the protocol did not use as the decision statistic.
2. **The decay explanation in README is wrong.** README says E falling with l at theta=1/4 "matches the Weil-type heuristic E ~ M^2 sqrt(h/l)". With M ~ l^{1/4} that expression is ~ sqrt(h), flat in l (and has an unspecified constant, so it is vacuous at E~0.007). The decay is the marginal (bucket-size) floor: re-evaluating saved best tuples, marginal-only E = M*max_c n_c/L - 1 is within 0.1-2% of E_real for 6 of 7 cells (n=15 j=3: 0.0512 vs 0.0531; n=17 j=4: 0.0469 vs 0.0476; n=19 j=4: 0.02184 vs 0.02196). The seventh (n=17, h=4, j=5) is 0.108 vs 0.136, and the null reproduces it (0.138).
3. **Koblitz cells have a mismatched null that biases REAL low (toward H0).** NULL-1sym destroys Frobenius orbits, so REAL's mask family has about 2^n/n independent members and a lower max. I ran 12 fresh NULL-1sym replicates at n=13 Arm A: Koblitz REAL 0.07474 vs null mean 0.07884, sd 0.00086, z = -4.8. Non-Koblitz h=4 (z=-1.6) and h=2 random2 (z=-1.2) are consistent with the null. README declares "Frobenius-preserving null not built" but not the direction of bias, and counts Koblitz cells (Arm A n=13; Arm B n=13,17,19) toward SUPPORTS. P2 (G(beta)=G(beta^2) exactly) is an identity from the Frobenius automorphism of Z/L, a validation of the code, not evidence about growth.
4. **j>=2 arm has zero demonstrated power, and the climber optimises the thing the null shares.** E_real ~ marginal floor, so the 24-evaluation climber mostly maximises bucket imbalance, which NULL-1sym reproduces by construction. K3b confirms zero climber power at every dose. Same-mask comparison (my re-run, 12 fresh permutations of the saved REAL tuples at n=13..19): z(REAL) between -1.40 and +1.28, null sd 0.3-2.5% of E. That is a genuine resolution statement (structure below ~2-5% of E is not seen on those tuples), but it applies only to the 24 tuples each climber visited. README does say (b) "excludes only typical filters", which is correct; the title line "SUPPORTS H0 ... no growth of G(M) seen" is stronger than that.
5. **Calibration does not test the decision statistic.** K1 compares planted PRF labels to PRF nulls (marginal-changing plant). K2 detection compares against the max over the UNPLANTED data's NULL-1sym family, not against NULL-1sym of the planted data, so detection at rho>=0.05 is largely the marginal change (E_mstar ~ rho). K3b oracle is the only matched-null test, and its margins over NULL-1sym are small at low dose: n=13 rho=0.02: 0.1803 vs 0.1691 (1.07x); n=17 rho=0.005: 0.1027 vs 0.0976 (1.05x); "detected" there is mostly exceeding the PRF K1 max. K2 was run at n=13,15 only, yet Arm A n=17 is reported as covered. Protocol said the K3 oracle "must detect"; it did not, and the design was changed (Amendment 2). Declared, but it was a failed calibration.
6. **README numerical slips.** (a) n=17 Arm A: README "0.01091 vs 0.01091"; raw is 0.010871 (REAL) vs 0.010886 (null). (b) "theta=1/4: 0.10-0.24 (n=13)": 0.239 is the j=4 (theta=1/3) cell; the theta=1/4 cells are 0.103-0.187. (c) theta=1/3 at n=17 quoted "0.074-0.077"; cells are 0.0715, 0.0744, 0.0770 and 0.1364 (h=4), the last omitted. (d) j is floor(theta*log2 l), so M drifts between theta_eff ~0.2 and 0.25 (n=21 M=16, n=23 M=32 vs l^{1/4}=45); the cross-n "trend" is at unequal theta_eff.
7. Minor/declared: "same proposal sequence" holds for the rng draws, not the masks (candidates are bit-flips of the current tuple, which diverges once acceptance differs). Peak concurrency was 4 processes while K3b overlapped the three lanes (limit 3), declared. PROTOCOL.md mtime (13:14) is after the arms started (13:03); amendments 1-3 are declared as such, but the original-text timing cannot be verified from the files. No cross-check against the RT-EXP-1 code/numbers (its P2 collapse 20.01 -> 1.003) was done; brute force + PARI validation suffice for correctness.

## Corrected outcome
No excess of G(M) over a marginal-matched NULL-1sym was observed on <P>: exhaustive j=1 (n=13,15 all curves, n=17 random1) REAL max E is within the null-replicate spread for non-Koblitz curves (n=13 random1 a=1 REAL 0.04391 vs 4 extra replicates 0.0435-0.0441), and 53 climbed j>=2 cells have REAL/NULL ratio 0.95-1.11 (0.99-1.01 for n>=17). Observed E is dominated by the marginal floor, which the null shares, so "E falls with l" is not evidence about Weil-type structure. Label: **no growth seen; powered only for j=1 and for the 24 visited j>=2 tuples (resolution ~2-5% of E at n>=15); inconclusive for rare structured subspaces and for theta>~0.25; Koblitz cells biased toward H0.** Class stays accounting. Not a bound, no security claim.
