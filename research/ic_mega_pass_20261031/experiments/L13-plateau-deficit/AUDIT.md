# AUDIT of L13-plateau-deficit (auditor, 2026-11-02)

Verdict: **partially valid.** The closure-side result is real and reproduces. The pure-model validation (P1) and several README sentences are misreported or overstated.
No security claim is made or implied by this audit.

## Confirmed (code does what PROTOCOL says; numbers match)
1. Formula algebra is correct. C(n+1,4) - R4(n,n+1) = n(n^3-38n^2+263n-274)/24 (sympy factor), roots 1.27, 7.37, 29.36; deficits 27:-1341, 29:-261, 30:+520, 31:+1488 (=31*48), 33:4070, 35:7665, 37:12469 all as stated.
2. Per-block counts are right: degree-3 part of (y1y2,y1y3,y2y3) in the squarefree algebra is 3(N-3)+1 = 3N-8 (+3 quadrics = 3N-5); degree 4 is 3C(N-3,2)+(N-3). The pairwise intersection in degree 4 is the monomials with exactly 2 y and 2 y' variables = 9 (I checked in common monomial coordinates; correct when the 6 forms are independent).
3. Closure predictions: all 10 stalled closure runs (n35 x3, n37, n31/k17 x2, n29/k16, syn n31 x2, syn n33) match predicted (low, dim, one=0) exactly, as recomputed from the formulas, not from analyze.py's own flag. n33/k16 x3 give one=1 as predicted (R4=36399 > 35960).
4. P4 numbers are real: macclose logs stored in raw/main.jsonl show round-3 new_low 1341 (n27, syn and C1), 261 (syn n29), 1123 (n25, vs surplus 1900, correctly recorded as a failure). The 261 for C1 n29 is from L1's README, not from L13 raw.
5. Prior-consistency numbers cited from L1 (2821, 37293) and L8 (3201, 45507, 8 UNSAT draws) match those READMEs. The README correctly says these were seen before the model was written. The model was fitted after seeing n=29/31, so genuinely out-of-sample cells are only (35,36), (31,34), (29,32), (33,32), and the unregistered (37,38).
6. BRIEF s0.6 does forbid running `conductor check`; README's blocker note is consistent. (I also did not run it, for the same reason; I am only writing AUDIT.md under experiments/.)

## Errors and overstatements (must be fixed)
E1. **README "Degree 3: n(3N-5) is exact on all 11" is false.** (n,N)=(4,10): rank3=83, pred3=88 in raw/model_p1.jsonl and RESULTS.json (shortfall 5). Exact on 10 of 11. PROTOCOL P1 required equality whenever n(3N-8)<=C(N,3), and 88<=120 here, so this is a second P1 falsification the README omits.
E2. **P1 count wrong.** README: "exact for N>=18 (4 of 4 pairs ...)". Only 3 pairs have N>=18 ((11,18),(12,20),(13,20)); the fourth exact pair is (6,12). Further, (6,12) and (11,18) are *saturated* (R4 >= C(N,4), rank = C(N,4)); they test only "full rank", not the R4 value. Only (12,20) and (13,20) test R4 as a sub-threshold number. And (5,12), where the model says full rank (R4 = C(N,4) = 495), gives 476: a miss at the border, listed as a plain failure in RESULTS but not discussed.
E3. **"Extra degree-4 syzygies ... not identified" is not the only reading, and the data lean the other way.** The shortfalls are draw-dependent: (3,10) seed 1 is exact (183) while seeds 2, 3 and the recorded seed give 175/167/167; (8,16) seeds 2, 3 are exact (1724), seed 1 gives 1719; (9,16) gives 1817/1818 vs 1780 at the recorded seed. For (3,10) the failing seeds are exactly those where the 9 random forms span rank 8, not 9 (seed 1: rank 9, exact). So at least the small-n failures coincide with non-generic random GF(2) draws, not a structural extra syzygy. P1 used one seed per pair, so "validated for N=18, 20" and the failures at N<=16 are single-draw statements. Fix: say failures are draw-dependent and the cause is unresolved (non-generic forms for 3n<=N; for 3n>N dependency among forms is forced and not analysed).
E4. **"17/17 closure runs exact" is inflated.** The 17 includes 4 "escape" checks whose analyzer criterion is only low > n(3N-5) (n25 C1, n27 C1, syn n27, syn n29), plus 3 n33/k16 runs where analyze.py checks low > plateau, not `one==1` (the data do show one=1). Honest tally: 10 exact stall matches on (low, dim, one=0); 3 predicted-UNSAT confirmed; 4 weak escape checks. Of the 10 exact matches, 3 re-use already-seen cells (syn n31 x2, syn n33). Also repeated draws/arms (C1, C2 are different systems, same output) show the output depends only on (n,N), which is what the model says but is less independent evidence than the count suggests.
E5. **"Validated empirically for 26 <= N <= 38"** overstates: exact (low, dim) stall equality is tested at N in {32, 34, 36, 38} only. N=26, 28, 30 are escape checks (the bydeg columns show deg-4 rank = C(N,4) there, consistent with R4 >= C(N,4) but not a test of R4).
E6. **Proof gaps in the "Model" section** (README itself admits genericity is assumed; I list exactly where):
   - "Two blocks cannot meet below degree 4" is a pairwise statement (true for independent forms). The degree-<=3 count n(3N-5) needs the sum of n ideals to be direct (no higher syzygies), not just pairwise trivial intersections. E1 shows it fails at (4,10).
   - R4 = n*D - 9*C(n,2) truncates inclusion-exclusion after pairs. "Triple products have degree 6" justifies dropping triple intersections only when the 9 forms of three blocks are independent in the monomial coordinates. With 3n >> N (n=31, N=32: 93 forms in 32 dims) the forms are necessarily dependent, so the reduction to monomial ideals does not apply, and inclusion-exclusion over subspaces is not a theorem even when it holds. The evidence is the exact match at N>=32, nothing more.
   - The passage from the top-degree algebra F2[x]/(x_i^2) to the Boolean closure (leading forms of W_4) is asserted, not derived; it is supported by the exact dim matches.
E7. **Minor.** (a) README says each run is "labelled by ... whether its draw was SAT or UNSAT"; the table and RESULTS have no nsol, so this is not recorded (protocol P2's "one UNSAT draw if s=0 is SAT" cannot be checked). The claim "solutions can only lower dim and never raise low" is stated without proof (W is computed from the equations alone, so SAT/UNSAT does not change the closure; the n25 low-cap is a separate effect). (b) n=37 was not pre-registered; the README table does not say so. (c) analyze.py hard-codes the P4 table (round3 numbers, ok flags) instead of reading the logs it stores; I verified the values against raw logs, but the pipeline does not. (d) "explains a measured stall" and "property of the rank-n shape, not of the field or generator" are stronger than a post-hoc-fitted, unproved, genericity-dependent count supports; the syn match shows generator independence only for forms drawn uniformly from F2^N, N=n+1.
