# Primary-source review2026-11-16

[Boyar and Peralta, A New Combinational Logic Minimization Technique with Applications to Cryptology, SEA2010](https://www.nist.gov/publications/new-combinational-logic-minimization-technique-applications-cryptology), DOI10.1007/978-3-642-13193-6_16. The primary NIST abstract describes separate optimization of nonlinear gates and linear circuit components. This is relevant prior art for a possible parity-network follow-up if affine chart substitution increases XOR cost.

Only the primary publication page/abstract was reviewed here. Its cryptographic circuit benchmarks are not rootfinding measurements. The proposed shared-chart selector formula follows elementary affine decomposition and Boolean selection; novelty is not certified. No claim is made to have implemented Boyar-Peralta optimization. Prior C110/C111code/MDD references remain applicable.
