# Primary-source novelty boundary

Read the official [PolyBoRi features](https://polybori.sourceforge.net/features.html) and [ZDD representation tutorial](https://polybori.sourceforge.net/zdd.html). They explicitly describe Boolean-polynomial ZDD storage,set-based arithmetic,shared substructures and variable-order dependence. Thus neither flat Boolean-ring arithmetic nor compressed polynomial representation is novel here. PolyBoRi also implements Groebner algorithms;C129 uses its own small arithmetic diagnostic and invokes no basis routine. This does not establish superiority to PolyBoRi or genuineF4/F5/F6. The new program question is whether projection onto the proved correction cones and an appropriate exact representation permits useful whole-cell certificates. The actual capped results do not yet establish that. No full-text literature certification or new complexity theorem is claimed.
