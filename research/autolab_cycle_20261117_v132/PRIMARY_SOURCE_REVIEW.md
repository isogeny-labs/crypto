Hensel lifting and finite-algebra decomposition are established techniques; see Neiger, Rahkooy and Schost, 2017, https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.10658 . Unit-direction substitution and rank-nullspace certificates are standard linear algebra. The proposed research contribution is the specific precision-filtration/tangent adaptation to this frozen guarded family, subject to counterexamples and wider primary-source review. No novelty or general complexity theorem is claimed. The earlier PolyBoRi primary documentation establishes that ZDD storage itself is prior art: https://polybori.sourceforge.net/zdd.html .
