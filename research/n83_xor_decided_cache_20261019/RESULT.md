# N83 decided-XOR-row cache: audited paired gate

The complete same-host `OFF, ON, ON, OFF` gate **failed its frozen 1.5x criterion**. The median completed decisions per measured SAT-search second were 70.947 without the cache and 100.393 with it, a ratio of **1.415**. This is a measured solver-access improvement on one public UUDD fixture, not a useful-rank yield or a total index-calculus runtime improvement. The [protocol](PROTOCOL.md) therefore does not admit a further cached U/D factor-base panel. `candidate_id`, `total_index_calculus_runtime_ms`, and `selected_best_total_runtime` remain null in the [panel audit](attempt-03/panel-01/audit.json) and [host audit](attempt-03/host-audit.json).

The worker used source commit `8cde893f774cace3e022438e168a0485b7268a57` with an explicit default-off cache. The completed host's binary SHA-256 was `23b1a36ea18b7a723678fbaea51342beda742ad96598f38ceee4001c1e0b3dc9`. It ran one CPU, hard 8 GiB memory, zero swap, no network, a pinned Amazon Linux container digest, and the exact same fixture and U/D archives in all four natural arms. Four preflights and four constructed controls passed before natural search; the latter independently replayed the same rank-one row for each cache setting and rejected changed-point and changed-row receipts. The [pre-result admission audit](attempt-03/controls-admission.json) was committed before the first natural outcome.

The cache reduced repivots substantially, but did not meet the predeclared decision-throughput threshold. The earlier [mixed U/D panel](../n83_d18_subgroup_orbit_census_20261018/mixed-v1/PROTOCOL.md) likewise left natural yield unresolved.

The U and D factor bases remain independently replayed and stored at the owner's `s3://crypto-autoresearcher/factor-bases/icv1/etc/` prefix. Each has 16,627 signed-Frobenius columns and 2,760,082 points; their signed-orbit sets are disjoint, giving 33,254 mixed columns. The [host audit](attempt-03/host-audit.json) binds the compressed archive SHA-256 values, fresh S3 HEAD metadata, construction times of 34.164 and 69.219 seconds as informational L0 costs, exact source bytes, binary, ordered panel, and cleanup. Its full provisioned interval was 4,101 seconds: 2,841 seconds summed across arms and 1,260 seconds for setup, replay, transfer, and idle time. The observed Spot-price point estimate for that host is $0.260983, with exact invoice, local CPU, S3, and network charges unmeasured.

Two earlier one-time Spot hosts were interrupted during their first natural arm. Their separate [first](attempt-01/audit.json) and [second](attempt-02/audit.json) audits retain eight passing admission arms each, partial progress, AWS `Server.SpotInstanceTermination` evidence, cleanup, and provisioned intervals of 1,539 and 1,684 seconds. These attempts are infrastructure-censored and contribute no arms to the completed host's ratio. Across all three hosts, the retained provisioned intervals total **7,324 seconds** and their observed-price compute-only point estimates total **$0.398922**; this is not an AWS invoice or a complete IC cost.
