\section*{Implemented constructions}
One- and two-large-point partials use exact affine keys and the existing
signed symbolic graph. Every candidate row is checked on the source curve
before insertion. Full constraints, unmatched attempts and dependent rows
are retained. Separate target columns support coupled recovery with dense
scalar elimination and original-curve checks.

Four-point recursive decomposition builds every pair support, joins exact
complements and retains complete increasing-index witnesses. Cold and shared
queries are checked against exhaustive four-loop enumeration. This is standard
meet-in-the-middle; construction is not free.

Four partition rules are scored on pilot targets only. The rule is then frozen,
pilot overlap refused, and fresh targets compared to a fixed-index rule.
Complete roots, admitted roots and zero-denominator relations remain visible.
The objective is pilot tuple volume per informative relation, not an unmeasured
Gr\"obner-basis cost prediction.
