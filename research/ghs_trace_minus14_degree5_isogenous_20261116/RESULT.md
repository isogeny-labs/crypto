# Degree-five cover exclusion for the trace -14 isogeny neighbor

16 November 2026. The frozen degree-five search completed on all twelve recorded trace -14 genus-five carriers for the exact source

`E_202/K: v²=u³+u²+(2a⁴+a³+a²+a+1)`, `K=F3[a]/(a⁵+2a+1)`.

This source has order `258=6·43` over `K` and is rationally 2-isogenous to the previously screened source `E_11`. Every one of the twelve cells covered the complete normalized degree-five denominator partition and had **zero exact cover identities**. Applying `a↦a³` extends the result to the five source equations in E_202's Frobenius orbit against the same twelve `F3` carriers: **60 exact equation pairs**. This is a finite, computer-assisted exclusion [pcc:unverified], not a statement about every genus-five carrier or every correspondence.

The carrier masks are `9735,17371,21624,44227,64725,90937,92153,100118,124586,144548,156795,159256`, with `Y_m:y²=f_m(x)` and `f_m=x¹¹+Σ digit₃(m,i)xⁱ`. Each cell enumerated the six denominator types `1`, `Q_i`, `(x-c)²`, `Q_iQ_j`, `Q_i(x-c)²`, and `H²`, using the carrier's actual rational branch factor for CRT interpolation. The logical coverage was **14,446,377 assignments per cell**, or **173,356,524** across twelve. All eleven rank-deficient systems per cell were handled by a complete `H` fallback, giving **7,761,732** direct fallback checks in total. The cumulative Sage CPU time was 759.166 seconds; this is cover-search cost.

Exactly one degree-five, coprime numerator survived interpolation in each cell, always with `B=Q_iQ_j`. Each passed polynomial divisibility but failed squarehood. A separately frozen [square witness](SQUARE_WITNESS.json) independently parsed all twelve `(A,B)` pairs and recomputed `P=B(A³+A²B+bB³)/f`. Six `P` polynomials have a nonsquare leading coefficient in `F243`; the other six have a forced degree-four square-root candidate whose lower coefficients leave a nonzero remainder. That replay does not call Sage's `P.is_square()`. The [audit](AUDIT.json) binds all cell receipts and witnesses to their exact source hashes, checks per-shape and fallback coverage, and reports zero hits. [DegreeFiveCounts.lean](DegreeFiveCounts.lean) checked the finite count arithmetic; it does not prove the geometric denominator classification or the Sage search.

Combined with the independently replayed [degree-four census](../ghs_trace_minus14_isogenous_degree4_20261116/RESULT.md) and the prior geometric exclusion through degree three, these sixty exact pairs have a `K`-cover degree floor of **six**. [Howe's published bound](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.04221) supplies a cover of degree at most 33 in this ordinary isogeny class, yielding the scoped interval **`[6,33]`**. Since `33<43`, the abstract Howe pullback is injective on the chosen order-43 subgroup by push-pull, but its coordinate formula and an evaluable map to `J(Y_m)(F3)` remain absent.

[Iijima–Momose–Chao 2015](https://eprint.iacr.org/2015/805) covers its specified direct odd-characteristic construction, and [Joux–Vitse 2012](https://www.iacr.org/archive/eurocrypt2012/72370010/72370010.pdf) already describes isogeny-assisted transfers. The exact E_202 and carrier parameters had no matching formula in the bounded local KB search; that is not evidence of worldwide priority. This result narrows a candidate class and supplies an explicit obstruction. It does not establish a new vulnerable curve, a new family, or a complete GHS-versus-rho cost.
