## Finding

The six total second partial derivatives of the centered third elementary
coefficient give a valid repair of the collapsed four-root Graeffe equations
on the original regular RR5 chart. They preserve every root in that chart,
and their six-by-three Jacobian has rank three there. The argument is
uniform on the stated chart; it does not require a random tangent normal or
the nonvanishing of a further generic discriminant.

The useful new application is this explicit repair of the collapsed
presentation without a distinct-intermediate-image cutoff. Differentiation
and deflation are established methods. Reduced support encodings also
already exist in this repository. This result does not establish a cheaper
global elimination problem, and it is not the first compact way to describe
the same regular relation set. [pcc:unverified]

The fixed native protocol concerns four leaves from the pure Dickson B509
component. It does not cover the mixed Y7 sectors of the full B677 base.

## Exact assumptions and chart

Work over a field of characteristic greater than three, and pass to an
algebraic closure for the local argument. Let the three original RR5
parameters be u=(u1,u2,u3). For a fixed target T, write -T=(r,s) and use the
normalized function

    L(X,Y)=(X-r)(alpha+beta X)+(Y-s)(X+t).

Its norm, divided by -(X-r), is the monic quartic M_u. The parameter names
alpha, beta and t may be renamed in the implementation, but all derivatives
below are with respect to these three original parameters.

At the solution under review require:

- four distinct original abscissae x_i, none equal to r;
- nonzero x_i+t and nonzero ordinates y_i;
- a separable selector D_N(X)-tau, with D_N'(x_i) nonzero;
- the original finite-target, normalized RR5 chart and its exact divisor-to-
  signed-group correspondence.

These are hypotheses about the original chart, not conditions that the
images remain distinct during the Dickson ladder. In particular, all four
terminal images are allowed to coincide with tau. For the fixed M31
protocol N=1024 and tau is required to differ from 2 and -2. Actual fixture
counts and those guards must be checked by the native control, not inferred
from this review.

The unchanged original chart matters. The fully collapsed coefficient
equations alone also admit repeated original roots; they do not themselves
enforce a squarefree M_u.

## Independent check of the rank argument

At an admitted solution, the distinct roots of M_u lift to formal local
functions. Put

    z_i(u)=D_N(x_i(u))-tau,
    F_j(u)=e_j(z_1(u),z_2(u),z_3(u),z_4(u)).

All z_i vanish at the solution. Let J be the four-by-three derivative of z
with respect to u. The Abel differential for the fixed sum gives

    sum_i dx_i/(2 y_i)=0.

Consequently the image of J is contained in the hyperplane

    W = {z : n dot z=0},       n_i=1/(2 y_i D_N'(x_i)).

Every n_i is nonzero. The derivative J is injective: if all dz_i vanish,
then all dx_i vanish, and then all dy_i vanish. The corresponding variation
of the normalized RR5 function has pole order at most four and vanishes at
the four summands and -T. These five distinct points force that variation
to be zero. Thus the three parameter variations are zero. Since W has
dimension three, the image of J equals W.

The cubic initial term of F_3 is

    C(v)=e_3(Jv).

Because F_3 has order at least three, all six second partials

    H_ab = partial_a partial_b F_3,       1 <= a <= b <= 3

vanish at the original solution. Their linear parts are the contractions
of the cubic tensor of C. Suppose a tangent vector delta is in the kernel
of all six linear parts. Since the characteristic exceeds three, this
implies that the quadratic directional derivative of C in direction delta
is the zero polynomial. Write w=J delta, an element of W. Then

    Q_w(z) = partial_w e_3(z)
           = sum_(i<j) (S-w_i-w_j) z_i z_j,       S=sum_i w_i

vanishes identically on W.

The ideal of the hyperplane W is generated by the linear form n dot z.
Hence Q_w=(n dot z)(a dot z) for some vector a. The polynomial Q_w is
multilinear: it has no z_i squared terms. Comparing those terms gives
n_i a_i=0 for every i. Since each n_i is nonzero, a=0 and Q_w is zero in
the ambient four-variable ring. Comparing its pair coefficients forces
all w_i to be equal to a common c, and then 2c=0. Thus w=0. Injectivity
of J gives delta=0.

This proves rank three for the six-by-three Jacobian of the H_ab at every
solution satisfying the stated chart hypotheses. In particular, it avoids
the exceptional tangent normals that can make the cheaper e_2-gradient
alternative singular. No general finite-characteristic termination theorem
for deflation is used. [pcc:unverified]

### Root preservation and the repeated-root boundary

Keep the four original centered coefficient equations. Appending the H_ab
cannot introduce additional solutions. Conversely, at every admitted
original root, F_3 has order at least three, so all H_ab vanish. The two
systems therefore have exactly the same roots on the original regular
chart. Full Jacobian rank makes the augmented local ring reduced there.
The chart is finite: selector roots and curve signs form a finite set, and
the normalized RR5 function for a given admitted divisor is unique.

The order argument does not extend through a repeated-original-root
coefficient chart. A small exact counterexample illustrates why: for

    q(X)=X^4+a3 X^3+a2 X^2+a1 X+a0

and the root map X to X^2, the third elementary coefficient of the four
image roots is a1^2-2 a2 a0. At q=X^4 all image roots are zero, but its
second derivative with respect to a1 is 2. Root-coordinate vanishing
order therefore does not imply the same order in symmetric coefficient
coordinates at a repeated root. The claim must retain the regular chart;
it must not say that all unsaturated repeated-root components survive.

### Conditional extension, not implementation evidence

The same linear-algebra argument works for m roots on a regular fixed Abel
fiber of dimension m-1, provided its tangent image is n-perpendicular with
every n_i nonzero. The coefficient formula for Q_w is still S-w_i-w_j.
The diagonal comparison again forces Q_w=0; comparing pairs forces
w_i=c, and then (m-2)c=0. Therefore characteristic greater than three and
p not dividing m-2 suffice for the cubic to have m-1 essential variables.
All m(m-1)/2 second partials in the m-1 chart parameters then have full
column rank at the admitted roots.

This is a conditional written extension: six entries for m=4, twenty-eight
for m=8. It does not supply an m=8 chart implementation, native check, or
elimination result. The condition p not dividing m-2 is sufficient; no
necessity claim is made. [pcc:unverified]

## Construction and finite-field accounting review

For a terminal monic quartic

    P(T)=T^4+g3 T^3+g2 T^2+g1 T+g0,

the centered third elementary coefficient is

    F_3=-(g1+2 tau g2+3 tau^2 g3+4 tau^3).

Its negative gives identical zero and rank conditions, but a checker must
fix the sign convention and check the complete formula. Differentiating
with respect to independent terminal graph variables is not total
differentiation with respect to the RR5 parameters.

A lifted differentiation circuit must propagate derivatives through both
the initial RR5 quartic and every Graeffe step. Graph variables that uniquely
record these values and derivatives introduce no new projected roots.
Initial conditions, mixed-derivative symmetry, and multiplication rules
must agree with the chosen convention: ordinary derivatives and Taylor
coefficients differ by factorials on repeated indices. The native control
requires third-order Taylor information because the Jacobian of a Hessian
uses third derivatives. A future equation constructor needs only the
second-order circuit to express the six additional constraints.

The fixed input dimension permits circuit differentiation with a constant
factor overhead relative to the ladder length. That observation is about
representation size. It does not bound expanded polynomial support,
Macaulay matrices, solving degree, finite-field root extraction, or memory.
Every added circuit operation and retained intermediate must be charged in
any later solver comparison.

Inspection of `PROTOCOL.md` found the appropriate fixed-panel controls:
sixteen predeclared signed quartets, exact norm reconstruction, a separate
root-jet construction, scalar Dickson replay, the rank check, an e_2
exceptional-normal control, and wrong-coordinate/wrong-formula controls.
An inadmissible fixed quartet must remain an explicit failed admission;
neither filtering nor replacement would satisfy the frozen panel. These
known-answer controls cannot be counted as relation discovery. Rational
roots, signed lifts, normalized rows and useful rank remain distinct
quantities in the later solver gate.

### Established deflation literature

[Hauenstein and Wampler, *Isosingular Sets and Deflation*, Section 3](https://academicweb.nd.edu/~cwample1/Preprints/hwIsosingular.pdf)
defines augmentation by appropriate Jacobian minors. Its ambient field is
the complex numbers.

[Hauenstein, Mourrain and Szanto, *On deflation and multiplicity structure*, Sections 2 and 3](https://aszanto.math.ncsu.edu/Deflation-preprint.pdf)
studies first-differential constructions without new variables. It also
explicitly describes a predecessor's preprocessing step that adds partial
derivatives known to vanish at the root. Its stated setup is a field
contained in the complex numbers. These sources establish the method's
prior art; their general theorems are not being imported as finite-field
theorems. The exact characteristic-safe cubic argument above supplies the
needed local statement for M31.
