# The member at infinity of the anchor pencil

For an anchor A=(u,v) the finite functions Y-v-tu form a pencil. Its projective member at infinity is u. Each rational root of u yields the signed place with ordinate v at that abscissa. When u splits, the sum with multiplicities equals A in the Jacobian. Since A is the current public input plus nP, this gives a row involving the source or target and the step place. The implementation subtracts the support from the known anchor expression. This uses ordinary reduced-divisor decomposition. The question is the marginal value of admitting it at every nonzero anchor, including degree drops.
