## Fixed proposed population and meaning

The proposed natural population has **two** cells: prime-field n=47, m=4, native `enumerate`, one curve seed, one generator and one uniform nonzero subgroup target per twist. Cell order is untwisted then quadratic twist. Each cell gets one sealed native attempt; construction failure, unsupported outcome, timeout, interrupted call, exhausted work budget, verification failure, and custody failure remain in the two-cell denominator. No replacement curve, generator, key, or seed is allowed. This fills a missing prime n47/m4 intersection; it does not replace the full n23–31 and n47–61 family/arity/key portfolio.

The claim is exact capture and custody of bounded finite-domain enumeration states. A verified witness is an m4 decomposition only under the recorded signed factor base and group law. A verified no-witness outcome is restricted to the complete recorded finite domain. A 4,096-transition budget stop or timeout is **censored**, not a negative. Scalar labels require independent native recovery and `[k]G=Q` replay. These two cells make no comparative runtime, first/last fall degree, orbit reduction, detector sensitivity, or solver ranking claim. A separately planted-positive detector control and any BSGS label-recovery work need separate identifiers/protocols and cannot replace a natural cell.

## Native capability boundary

At current reviewed main `d7e295c16b1f0eb0a096ba007b70a29ffd7f2686`, `DatasetPlan::validate` admits n=3..61, m=2..15, paired twists, and family `prime`; `solvers::capabilities` admits prime `enumerate` at m=2..15. The constructor chooses one prime below 2^n by a fixed rule, samples a nonsingular curve, certifies its exact Hasse-interval point count, and constructs the quadratic twist. Its boundary unit test covers prime n47 and both twists at factor-base request 2. These are source and unit-test capabilities, **not** a new n47/m4 run, source admission, or proof that a future seed and requested factor base will construct within the proposed cap. Prime F6 is unsupported; native root is m2 only; PKM requires p<2^31; wide-prime F4 m2/m3 requires its supervised original execution context. They are not arms in this plan.

The actual prime p, base and twist curve coefficients, orders, factorization, subgroup/cofactor, generator, target, factor-base cardinality, and binary SHA-256 remain **unbound**. Later admission must verify the public modulus against the native fixed-prime rule, nonsingularity, `#E + #E^twist = 2(p+1)`, both native point-count/twist certificates, subgroup factorization and `[r]G=O`, and independently replay `[k]G=Q` for any promoted scalar. Prime-field coefficient degree is 1 and has no nontrivial model-preserving field Frobenius action. Capture actual signed factor-base points and total finite-domain size; requested cardinality 3 can yield a different actual cardinality. Do not classify a negative from requested size or from a censored trace.

## Proposed finite budget and analysis

Each cell requests factor-base size 3 under a policy to be confirmed by native preflight, degree bound 3, and at most 4,096 counted group-add transitions. Proposed limits are 90 s native per case, 180 s outer command per cell, 2,048 MiB memory, 64 MiB trace, one native worker at a time, and an 8 GiB free XFS floor. The two outer limits sum to 360 s (six minutes); native limits sum to 180 s (three minutes), exclusive of validation and custody. These are hard planning ceilings, not predicted elapsed time or bytes. A separate two-hour campaign wall ceiling includes checkpoints. After the first attempt, a cap change requires a new protocol version and separate arm; the original two-cell limits and denominator remain unchanged.

No A/A, reference, or negative-control comparison decides the exact capture claim. A complete finite-domain no-witness cell is a restricted mathematical label, not a detector-sensitivity null. Analysis must report planned, attempted, completed, censored, failed, and custodied counts by twist; actual base size and finite-domain total; transition counts; verified PDP witnesses and independently recovered scalars; source/binary identity; archive bytes/hashes; and phase costs with eligibility. Whole-worker instruction-to-solve and controlled clock labels are ineligible without complete worker accounting and isolation. Native source paths, seed, scalar witness, post-solution traces, outcome/status cues and provenance remain out of public ML features. Group each curve and its twist, all arities, generators, keys and isomorphic/Frobenius-related artifacts in one split; both cells here occupy one split, so this block alone supplies no independent train/test partition.

Finish with official export checker, independent archive and mathematical replay sample, scoped cost eligibility and report. Every planned cell retains its real terminal or unattempted state.
