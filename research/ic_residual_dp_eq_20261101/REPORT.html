Pricing caveat (from the independent validator, added after validation). In the band as built, residual_table_bytes_uncharged is a byte count (table entries × (16 + 8·columns)), but its _uncharged name makes the band price it as operations at weight w. For the baseline collector it is 86–92% of priced cost at w = 1 and 52–69% at w = 0.1; the candidates store only distinguished points, so their counter is about 150–210× smaller. The large w > 0 candidate/baseline gains and the 48–53× baseline distances to rho in this report are therefore mostly an artifact of that pricing, not operation-count savings. The w = 0 results are unaffected. The tables below put the band as built beside the band with that one counter excluded (same data, same pairing).

What was implemented (src/cryptanalysis/ic_framework/residual.rs; baseline targets=residual unchanged): residual-fused-dp (one addition per step from a Jj − Pi table, distinguished points with b = ⌊log₂ r / 2⌋ − 9 = 8 on p36/p37) and residual-fused-eq (a negation-equivariant walk, so L and −L paths merge, on 2016 pair sums Ju + Jv − Pi with a 2-cycle escape and distinguished points). Priced per step: DP test (both), orientation read and one negation per flipped step (eq). Setup additions and the extra escape additions are counted group operations.

Classification and caveats

  • Engineering improvement of a generic collision search: the collector is a rho walk that needs B + 1 collisions, so its floor is about √(B + 1) times rho. At B = 1 the arms sit at 1.5–2.2× rho. It is not a structural index-calculus advance and does not beat rho.
  • Per-step pricing is deliberately conservative: a DP test, an orientation read and negations are each charged 1 GAE at w = 1, where rho's tuned walk charges about 1 operation per step in total. A cheaper model (DP as a bit test on the canonical x, as in rho) would lower the eq w = 1 figures; it was not applied here.
  • Only p36 and p37 (both ≈ 2^36) exercise the DP path; larger r is unmeasured. Local L0 run, counts only. No VALIDATION.json, so no leaderboard or benchmarks/README.md row.
  • Exploratory, before this study: a first equivariant attempt with 16 jumps fell into short cycles (walks trapped until the cap) and lost; the 64-jump pair-sum step set with a 2-cycle escape fixed it.
