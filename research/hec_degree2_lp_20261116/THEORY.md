# Exact collision budget and conditional population cost

For genus2, an irreducible monic quadratic norm factor Q with v=h modQ defines a degree2closed-place divisor E=(Q,v). A nonbranch Q has exactly two conjugate-sheet choices v,-v. Canonical identity MUST include the coefficients of v after the sign choice, not just the irreducible x-polynomial. Each principal row is a_i+epsilon_i L_label=0, where a_i is supported on chosen anchors and epsilon_i=±1.

For Nrows with Ldistinct nonzero labels, their quadratic-column submatrix has rankL. Choose one defining row per label; eliminate each later occurrence with its signed first row. This produces at mostN-Lpure-anchor rows and spans the entire intersection after eliminating quadratic columns. Branch v=0has order2and is known zero in odd-prime projection; Zsuch rows add at mostZconstraints (use twice the original anchor row for an original-group zero identity). Therefore anchor rank increment is bounded by N-L+Z, regardless of any endpoint-distribution heuristic. Repeated hits can still yield zero/dependent rows. This is standard rank-nullity, not new large-prime mathematics.

Appending these rows to existing rational-place relations does not change that budget. The independent check constructs [R 0; A E] with all quadratic columns and compares full/off-column ranks with R plus explicitly eliminated collision rows. No scalar logarithms are supplied or computed in this screen.
