Scope and references

All visual regions are schematic. Curves, groups, divisor classes, and subgroup orders are distinct objects. The cover example uses ECCp-131 over its original prime field, not the binary ECC2K-130 curve.

The hyperelliptic split illustrates valid rational constituent points and the appropriate infinity/base-divisor convention. A small factor base can still make usable relations scarce.

Cost exponents are representative fixed-small-genus asymptotic bounds under their standard hypotheses. They omit logarithmic factors and constants and do not predict execution time or prove lower bounds.
