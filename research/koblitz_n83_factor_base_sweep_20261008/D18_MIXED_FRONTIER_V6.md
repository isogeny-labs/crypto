# Ordered D18 mixed-base sweep suffix

`d18-mixed-frontier-v6.json` preserves every v5 ordinal and appends both orders of the exact primary D18 base with each of the 660 same-curve legacy base recipes, plus an explicitly redundant D18 self-pair. Each of the 1,321 pairs is crossed with residual summands 0, 1 and 2 and all 51,840 frozen solver tuples. The suffix adds 205,441,920 addressable cases; the combined finite ledger has 27,144,771,840. Use `python3 d18_mixed_frontier_v6.py --case ORDINAL` to inspect one tuple and `--check` to verify the pinned source and generated bytes.

The exact U/D18 pair appears in both orders. Its individual archives have passed import and custody checks, but no mixed-domain adapter, pair overlap measurement or complete double-large-prime pipeline exists for them. The other 659 legacy partners are retained with source and closure dispositions. A residual-zero case is listed but redundant as a mixed-pair query. Solver axes, including WDSat, FES variants, Gray enumeration, splitting, symmetry, threads and large-prime count, are all represented without treating an unavailable adapter as a run or a negative mathematical result. No tuple in this suffix has a measured total index-calculus runtime, so selection fields remain null.
