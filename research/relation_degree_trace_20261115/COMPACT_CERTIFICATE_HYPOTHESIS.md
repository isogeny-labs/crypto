Instead of repeatedly expanding every coordinate identity into the originals,
store an acyclic sequence of exact identities. Node j expresses its polynomial
using original generators and nodes numbered strictly before j. Later local
certificates can reference that polynomial with a unit coefficient. Checking
each identity once proves original-input membership by induction. Final basis
identities use those same certified generators; all original/auxiliary input
normal forms, monicity/reducedness and S-pairs still have to pass.

Reuse the existing polynomial coordinate, rewrite, Bezout and change-of-order
operations. Keep the old expanded constructor as an explicit control, without
altering its arithmetic or proof caps. The new route never accepts an
unchecked auxiliary polynomial or silently falls back to another solver.
