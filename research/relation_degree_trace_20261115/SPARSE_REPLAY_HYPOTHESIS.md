## Mechanisms and obligations

1. For monic f=a+f0 and g=b+g0 with relatively prime leading monomials a,b,
   S=b*f-a*g=f0*g-g0*f. Each nonzero summand has leading monomial strictly below
   a*b. Instead of expanding this representation, a wire marker may request the
   product criterion. The consumer must check relatively prime actual bound
   leading monomials, an empty explicit proof, and full pair coverage. The
   Singular checker binds the asserted leading monomials to the actual rows.
   All noncoprime pairs still require checked strict standard representations.
2. An opt-in constructor may stop incident resultant collection after at least
   two nonzero projections yield a certified GCD of degree2..4. Membership of
   this consequence alone is insufficient: coordinate construction, every
   original containment and the complete reduced-basis certificate must pass.
   On failure it may try the full existing constructor, charging both attempts.
3. Metadata-only recognized f3 pairs can replace unused cubic-difference and
   cubic-syzygy construction. Recognition remains exact; resulting identities
   and final acceptance checks remain mandatory.
4. A later fused worker can avoid repeated process launch and pretty-JSON
   transfer while retaining wire/script/raw CAS output and charging construction,
   proof production, serialization, checking and output consumption in full.

The legacy constructors and replay producers remain available unchanged as
controls. No input, proof, arithmetic-work, memory, file or timeout cap is raised
