## Sources, computation and hypotheses

Read SPARSE_REPLAY_HYPOTHESIS.md, the correctness qualification report and

Every outer workflow must return a complete validated answer. Prospectively,
if a replay/native attempt fails a cap, process check or algorithmic check, the
outer workflow makes exactly one fresh three-route regeneration recovery and
charges ALL failed elapsed work, recovery and output checking. It records the
inner failure as a failure, not as a successful proof. No cap is raised and no
sample is replaced. A failed/mismatched recovery is an outer failure. The plain
regeneration control is mandatory, so expensive recovery cannot manufacture a
lower-cost minimal baseline. Candidate follow-up requires its sparse proof fast
path, not recovery. Preserve all attempt outputs, including terminated files.

Regeneration setup is computed once as an observed reference but charged in
full to each regeneration-based control, matching previous paid baselines.
Replay constructors receive originals only, never the reference basis. A
recovery recomputes fresh references and must not reuse the first reference.
Each pipeline pays generation/preparation and complete output consumption;
cross-pipeline equality cost is charged uniformly. This explicitly compares
validation algorithms, without rewriting any old clock or result.

## Calls, gates and interpretation

Exactly one call per named workflow per case in the fixed order. All nine
controls, ablation and candidate must complete and match before ratios exist.
Keep all failures/missing outcomes, no retry or replacement. Report every paid
ratio, absolute cost, recovery cost/status and constructor/proof structural
counts. A zero proof cost, missing primary control or mismatched output rejects
the case. No speed ratio is computed from an incomplete inner attempt alone.

At most one case may be selected for a separately frozen matched campaign:
candidate succeeds on its proof fast path, has lower cost than every required control
and the full-projection ablation, and both primary native F4 and expanded paid
ratios are at least10. Rank by the smaller primary ratio; ties use fixed roster
order. The legacy compact replay and symmetrically optimized expanded/mature
controls remain visible, even when their ratios are less than10. This gate asks
whether the original two-control goal deserves confirmation; it does not claim
10x or15x against the fastest control. If the candidate loses to any such control,
select none and investigate the costs.

One call cannot confirm15x. Confirmation needs paired samples, A/A admission,
adjusted interval analysis and separately authorized prospective fresh scope.
Any later claim must name the denominator and validation policy. Do not promote
leaderboards from this exploratory screen.
