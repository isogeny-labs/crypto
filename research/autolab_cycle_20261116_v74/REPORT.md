# C74: torsion-cut decomposition and complete chain rationalization

New mechanism: an S3 root forces its non-two-torsion vertices to share one Frobenius type. Rational two-torsion vertices are the only cuts allowing a rational component to meet an anti-rational component. Removing assigned torsion cuts gives monochromatic components; the target anchors its component rational. Unanchored components retain both types. This is exact routing, not a standalone root or UNSAT certificate.

The stronger binary five-chain proof covers repeated source coordinates. Under C73 G1/G2/G3 and Tr(A)=0, the only remaining possible anti-rational pair must cancel. A proper rational subtotal cannot be torsion, and every source ordering then creates either a non-ground or infinite intermediate. Those are forbidden by the original finite-chain contract. The actual GF(2^83), A=0,B=1,d=18 guards were frozen and certified in C73, so full finite-chain rationalization applies. This is a theorem equivalence, not an enumerated hard root set.
