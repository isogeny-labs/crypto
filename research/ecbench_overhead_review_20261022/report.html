ecbench harness overhead: parse the registry once, allocate counter keys once

Two harness changes. docs/curves/registry.json (4.7 MB) is parsed once per process instead of on every registry lookup (Workload::on in every measured child and every planned target). GenericOutcome::count, called several times per BSGS and kangaroo step, allocates a counter's key only on first use. No solver, cost unit or counted result changes: 2376 records are identical to the old binary's outside timing, every session replays exactly under the other binary (ecbench verify --replay-all), and the §5a band is identical.

Why the solve window was affected

Nothing in the solve path reads the registry. Callgrind counts the same instructions inside the window for both binaries (1.110 M vs 1.112 M on a 20-bit rho.negation solve), and the old child executes 44 M more instructions before it (193 M vs 149 M: the second parse). The old binary drops its freshly parsed copy of the registry immediately before the solve; the 3.5–4 ms it then loses inside the window is wall time with no extra instructions and no syscalls. Disabling glibc fast bins did not remove it; the exact memory-system cause was not isolated. Wall times recorded by the old binary on small curves therefore carry this offset; counted results do not.

Host: Intel Xeon @ 2.10 GHz, 4 vCPU cloud container; sessions pinned by ecbench (--cpus auto) with --allow-busy, so below L2. Engineering change to harness overhead, not an index-calculus result.
