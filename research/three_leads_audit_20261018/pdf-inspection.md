# PDF inspection

The final `report.pdf` opens in PyMuPDF and contains six pages. Every page
was rendered into a labeled contact sheet and visually inspected. The three
plots/diagrams appear with captions; links, tables and mathematical text
remain visible. Text-block bounding-box checks found no content outside
the page. No scientific values were computed by the rendering tools.
