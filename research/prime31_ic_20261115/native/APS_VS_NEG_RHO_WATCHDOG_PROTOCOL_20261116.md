## Single change before execution

Add a fixed per-lane **512 consecutive steps without finding an entirely *new* distinguished point** guard. When exceeded, charge a fresh random public labelled starting point `aG+bQ`, clear the 1-step/2-step cycle trace, count `stagnation_restarts`, and resume without removing any previously recorded DP. A normal random independent walk with DP probability 1/16 or 1/32 has negligible probability of 512 misses. This is a conservative cycle escape, not an algebraic speedup or proof of uniform mixing.
