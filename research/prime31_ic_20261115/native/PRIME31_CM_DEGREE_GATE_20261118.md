## Exact mathematical result (integer certificate, not a performance benchmark)

The Frobenius trace and discriminant are

```text
t = p + 1 - n                    = 36,817
Delta = t^2 - 4p                = -7,234,443,099
      = 3^2 * (-803,827,011)
D_K   = -803,827,011            = -(3 * 13 * 20,610,949)
```

`20,610,949` is prime by exact trial division through its square root, as are 3 and 13. The product is squarefree, and `D_K ≡ 1 (mod 4)`, so it is the **fundamental discriminant** of `K = Q(sqrt(D_K))`. The order `Z[pi]` generated by Frobenius has conductor 3. The curve is ordinary because `p ∤ t`. Its (geometric) endomorphism ring is a suborder of `O_K` containing `Z[pi]`, so its conductor is **either 1 or 3**. The actual conductor is **not determined** by the discriminant computation.

For every endomorphism `alpha` outside the scalar subring `Z`, write it in the maximal order as
```text
alpha = a + b*omega,    omega = (1 + sqrt(D_K))/2,   a,b in Z, b != 0.
4 * deg(alpha) = (2a+b)^2 + 803827011*b^2.
```
This identity is the positive-definite imaginary quadratic norm form, valid for ordinary elliptic curves. Since `b != 0` and `2a+b` has the parity of `b`, its right-hand side is at least `803827011 + 1`. Consequently, every non-scalar endomorphism (even over the algebraic closure) obeys the **unconditional** lower bound

```text
deg(alpha) >= 200,956,753.
```

If `End(E) = Z[pi]`, then `b` must be divisible by 3 and the stronger **conditional** bound is `deg(alpha) >= 1,808,610,775`. Do not use this stronger figure until the endomorphism conductor has been independently determined. The baseline model also has `a != 0` and `b != 0` in short-Weierstrass notation, hence `j != 0, 1728`: its geometric automorphism group contains only `±1`.

**Meaning:** This removes cheap *low-degree nonscalar self-endomorphisms* and special-j automorphisms as a direct source of prime31 GLV or extra finite automorphism folds. **It does not prove the absence of efficient factored high-degree isogeny loops**, transfer maps, faster coordinate representations, higher-genus correspondences, or non-generic ECDLP algorithms. The cost of evaluating a factored map is the sum of component costs, not necessarily the expanded degree. This distinction is explicitly required by `docs/endomorphism-rules.md`; no security claim follows from this arithmetic screen.
