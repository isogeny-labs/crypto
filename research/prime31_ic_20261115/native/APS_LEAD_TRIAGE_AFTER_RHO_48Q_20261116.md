## Exact mathematical pass/fail for the novel algebraic track

The prospective target-conditioned system must encode signed elliptic eight-point equality ΣP_i=±Q for E(F_p), with x_i∈B and y_i²=x_i³+Ax_i+B. A polynomial consequence of S9 alone is insufficient: recover actual rational roots and y signs, account for multiplicities/infinity and **check on original E(F_p)**. For the multiset check space, B4 has C(11,8)=165, B8 C(15,8)=6,435 and B31 C(38,8)=48,903,492 possible unordered f. B4/B8 exhaustive enumeration is permitted for **independent verification only**, not as input to algebraic extraction. B31 enumeration is a disqualifying fallback.
