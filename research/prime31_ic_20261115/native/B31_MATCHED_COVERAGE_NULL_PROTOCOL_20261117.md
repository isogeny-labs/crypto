## Null mathematics: rigorous versus heuristic

An eight-leaf signed word over 31 labelled points induces an integer vector v∈Z^31 with l1 norm at most eight and l1 parity even; conversely every such vector can be padded to eight tokens with opposite-sign cancellation. The exact distinct formal signature count is

`N = Σ_{j even, 0<=j<=8} Σ_{support size} C(31,s) C(j-1,s-1) 2^s = 5,654,193,417`.

For *independent uniform abstract group scalars* `z_i∈F_n`, two distinct signature values `v·z` and `w·z` coincide with probability exactly 1/n: `(v-w)·z=0` is a nonzero linear form modulo prime n. Consequently the exact expected collision energy of the formal signature map is

`E[Σ_t c_t²] = N + N(N-1)/n`.

By Cauchy–Schwarz and Jensen, `E[|8A|] >= N*n/(n+N-1)`, an ensemble-average bound, not an assertion about Dickson31 or the conditioned distinct-x random bases.

**Only a heuristic** (values of correlated signatures incorrectly treated as independent uniform bins): `p_hit ≈ 1-exp(-N/n) ≈ 0.9281363`, predicting about 237.60/256 and 950.41/1024 hits. It is not a theorem, probability law or p-value. The 951/1024 original outcome alone does not establish algebraic structure.
