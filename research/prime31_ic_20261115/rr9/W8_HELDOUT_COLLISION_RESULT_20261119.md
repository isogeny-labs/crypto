## Exact geometry and mathematical mechanism
- (p=2^{31}-1=2147483647), (E:y^2=x^3+847514846x+2087444724), (#E(mathbb F_p)=n=2147446831) prime, cofactor 1, (G=(625037715,2100627251)).
- Canonical first 31 rational (x) base points as in #4728, with the same four overlapping eight-element windows, no deliberately weak curve. Reused original pinned `prime31-algebraic-ic` Rust crate at `1df714645aa683cf975bc76fcacb9842cb4780fd`.
- Historical eight-slot signed weights ([1,3,5,7,11,13,17,19]), proposed powers-of-two ([1,2,4,8,16,32,64,128]). Each of eight slots chooses one of 8 base points and a sign, i.e. 16 choices.
- For any fixed window (P_0,dots,P_7), let (S={sum_{j=0}^{3}epsilon_j 2^jP_{i_j}:i_jin{0,dots,7}, epsilon_jin{pm1}}). Then the full proposed W8 support is **exactly** (S+[16]S), since the right-hand weights are 16 times the left-hand weights. This identity is independent of the curve parameters.
- **Scale-16 fiber theorem.** The map ([16]) on a group of odd order is bijective, so equality and collision multiplicity among right-half words are **identical** to those among the corresponding left-half words. The two observed W1 half-table deficits of 40 do not represent independent anomalies: the right 40 are forced by the left 40. In particular the same 40 ordered first-word / later-word pairs collide in both halves.
- **Single short relation explains all 40 W1 non-formal collisions.** Independently authored Python affine group arithmetic enumerated all (16^4=65536) W1 four-slot words and found 65,496 distinct group sums with 40 collisions for each half. Across the 40 left pairs, the only difference in signed integer coefficient vectors was ((0,8,1,8,6,-2,0,-1)), and the right pair differences were exactly 16 times that vector. Thus on the W1 base points ((P_8,dots,P_{15})):
  [
    [8]P_9+P_{10}+[8]P_{11}+[6]P_{12}-[2]P_{13}-P_{15}=mathcal O.
  ]
  Original x coordinates for those six points are ((12,14,16,18,19,26)). The explicit colliding four-digit words 13394 (digits (2,5,4,3)) and 38343 (digits (7,12,5,9)) recover this relation exactly. Independently checked with affine modular arithmetic. The source-bound report includes explicit word certificates; the full all-40-per-half independent replay passed source-bound CI as a post-outcome verification amendment, not part of the preregistered yield analysis.
- The relation is a **small fixed-base finite-group dependence**, not a mechanism to recover arbitrary ECDLP logarithms: obtaining it relied on an enumerative out-of-band group oracle. The exact same second-half collision pattern follows from the group homomorphism; no logarithms or scalar trapdoor were inferred.
