**Hypothesis:** Use the degree-2 Semaev F3 equation to recover the second factor-base x from the public R and first factor x, with all 31 denominators inverted in one Montgomery batch. This may reduce the field-inversion and wall costs of a *two-summand primitive*. **Derivation (fixed):** For E:y²=x³+ax+b and public x(R)=r, factor x(P)=u, treat F3(u,v,r) as a quadratic in v:
`A=(r-u)^2`, `B=-2[(r+u)(ru+a)+2b]`, `C=(ru-a)^2-4b(r+u)` mod p.
Discriminant `B²-4AC =16 f(r) f(u) =(4 y_R y_P)^2`. If A != 0, roots `v=(-B±4y_R y_P)/(2A)`; otherwise solve the remaining linear `Bv+C=0` (and exclude the point-at-infinity root). They are exactly the x coordinates of R±P when finite. Reconstruct all ± factor-base signs and verify each solution by original-curve EC group addition.
