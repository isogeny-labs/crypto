# Composite degree breaks the cyclic-module inference

Lean constructs an actual faithful cyclic action of order21 on a six-dimensional F2 vector space, generated by one orbit, with a proper nonzero invariant subspace. Its dimension equals the multiplicative order of2 modulo21. Thus those algebraic conditions do not imply irreducibility for odd composite degree. This tests the algebraic step invoked in a composite-degree reading of Diem2003 Lemma22; it is not a constructed GHS function-field counterexample or a refutation of the norm-conorm kernel theorem. The primary is the archived March24,2003 draft of *The GHS Attack in odd Characteristic*,33PDFpages. Section7 explicitly starts with general odd n; Lemma22 invokes Lemma17, which was stated for odd prime n. The source applies the lemma to n5 and n7. We distinguish the draft from the journal citation,18(2003)1–32; no byte or statement equivalence with an independently retrieved published version is asserted. [pcc:unverified]

The counterexample uses the actual ring

```text
V = F2[X]/(g),  g=X^6+X^4+X+1
                  =(X+1)(X^2+X+1)(X^3+X+1).
```

The actual power basis proves dimension6 and cardinality64. The class x of X is an actual unit of exact order21: an explicit polynomial division certificate proves x^21=1, and lower-degree polynomial arguments exclude orders3 and7. Its actual cyclic subgroup of units has cardinality21. Multiplication defines a faithful actual Mathlib representation. The orbit of1 spans V, and1 generates the actual F2[C21] module obtained through `Representation.asModule`. Cyclicity and faithfulness are conclusions of the construction. V is not presented as a field. [pcc:unverified]

The actual kernel of evaluation at1 is a proper nonzero invariant F2 submodule. It contains x+1, proved nonzero by the quotient power basis, and does not contain1. Multiplication preserves the kernel. The actual invariant-submodule/group-algebra order isomorphism supplies a proper nonzero F2[C21] submodule. The closed theorem directly negates the claim that every invariant subspace is zero or whole for this actual action. No invariant-space conclusion, matrix-rank premise or abstract assumed kernel replaces the construction. [pcc:unverified]

A proved sufficient repair works for every odd n, including composite n: if the actual quotient polynomial f divides the n-th cyclotomic polynomial and has degree ord_n(2), then f is irreducible, by the official finite-field cyclotomic-factor theorem. Lean derives that every root-invariant subspace is zero or whole. It also proves that g fails the added cyclotomic-divisor hypothesis. Positive controls use actual irreducible quotients for n5 and n7, of dimensions4 and3 respectively, matching ord_5(2) and ord_7(2); both satisfy the repair and have no proper root-invariant subspace.
