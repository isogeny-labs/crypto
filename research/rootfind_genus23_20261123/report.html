The lever, and why this one

In hyperelliptic index calculus the relation search is a test of whether u splits completely. In the lab's pipeline (src/cryptanalysis/genus_ladder.rs) each candidate costs two things. First, one generic Cantor step D ← D + M_j: 219.7 multiplications and 5.0 inversions at g = 3, 88 multiplications and 4 inversions at g = 2. Second, the splitting test and root finding (rel_mul). On the lab's own g = 3 rows Cantor is 45 % of the arm's field multiplications when an inversion counts 10 and every multiplication counts 1. Under the §5a GAE band, where a Cantor step is one group operation and a native multiplication costs w, group operations are only 2.9 % of the arm total at w = 0.1 and 0.3 % at w = 1 (tables.md T3).

The lab has already covered the test side:

  • the parity prefilter;
  • Cardano–Lucas at g = 3 in the positive control (ic_positive_control_lucas_20261116);
  • Ferrari at g = 4;
  • 2LP, SGE and Wiedemann variants.

The survey's list is in PROTOCOL.md §0. One lever on the candidate supply had not been tried for divisors: the sibling. The step D + M computes xgcd(u_D, u_M), u_D·u_M and e1·u_D, and D − M = D + (u_M, −v_M) needs exactly the same three objects. So each walk step can also yield a second candidate, D − M, at a marginal cost. The lab has tried siblings only in the elliptic k-sum build (ic_ksum_sibling_20261118).

Siblings are emitted on 93.8 % of steps; a step is skipped when j_i = j_{i−1}, because then the sibling would be D_{i−1}.   • The instrument is calibrated. The port's forward-step counts are within 0.5 % of the lab's recorded cantor_mul/group_ops on the same curves. The negative control N (the sibling computed by an independent full Cantor) costs exactly 1.000 of a step, so the saving comes from the shared work and not from the counter.

    • In arm B, every sibling equals cantor(D, −M) bit for bit, over all 100 % of B steps.
    • For 200 sampled smooth siblings per curve, two further checks passed: the root places sum to D, and ca·G + cb·T = D (integer coefficients, T = k·G).

Duplicates (P3)

  • At g = 2, p = 524309 and at g = 3: no duplicate siblings.
  • At g = 2, p = 65537 (r ≈ 5–11 × 10^8): 9,329 duplicate siblings, so P3 failed by its letter at that cell.
  • The post-hoc diagnostic diag_dups.py traces this to the walk. On curve 17 over 60k steps, the forward points repeat 6,182 times, because 4096-step segments merge into earlier trails when r < 10^9. Siblings repeat 5,787 earlier siblings and only 3 forward points. Both arms share the cause.

Distance to rho (§5a band) [pcc:unverified, derived]:

  • What the sibling does to the band-priced total. At w = 0 it cuts the arm total to 0.73 (g = 2) and 0.78 (g = 3). At w = 0.1 it moves the total by 0.6 % (g = 3) and 1.5–1.7 % (g = 2); at w = 1 by 0.2 % or less. The relation phase and linear algebra dominate the band-priced cost, and the sibling does not touch them.
  • Genus 3, p = 65537. The arm is below matched rho only at w = 0 (0.071, projected 0.056). At w = 0.1 and w = 1 it is 2.2× and 11.9× rho, with or without siblings. The order with rho changes inside the band, so by §5a it is unresolved, and the sibling does not change that. The distance remaining at w = 1 is about 12×.
  • Genus 2, p = 524309. The current arm is above rho at every w (1.11 / 18.6 / 97.6). With siblings it would fall below rho at w = 0 only (0.81); at w = 0.1 and 1 it stays 18× and 98× rho. No crossing across the band. The distance remaining at w = 1 is about 98×.
  • Reconciliation with the lab's frozen genus-2 result. genus_ladder_scale_20261115 RESULTS.md X4 reads "failed: no crossing, distance 3–5×" in FPM. The sibling projection keeps that: in FPM the p = 524309 arm would go from 2.27–3.22× to 1.93–2.96× rho, still above rho at every reading.
  • Context, not a ranking. In FPM the g = 3 arm is below rho at every reading (0.18–0.35), and the lab's measured wall ratio on these rows is 0.20 (genus_ladder_wallclock_vs_rho_20261122, [pcc:unverified]). The §5a band is known to be unable to show such an IC win (problems/section5a-band-cannot-show-a-known-ic-win.md: a mulmod costs about 0.005–0.013 GAE on lab hosts). No per-host w_cal was measured here, so no "ranked at calibrated price" statement is made; the band verdict governs.

These are projections on the lab's rows, not an end-to-end solve with siblings. The first version of this section used a home-made pricing (tables.md T3c, superseded) and wrongly claimed "below rho across the band" at g = 3, a crossing at w ≤ 0.1 at g = 2, and "2.3× to go" at w = 1.

Verdict

  • Frozen decision rule: PROTOCOL §7 says "refuted if P2 fails". P2 failed at g = 3 in the first pass, so by the letter of the rule this study is recorded as refuted. P3 also failed at one cell.
  • What the evidence does support. The cost component the lever is about is measured exactly, in counts, and controlled:
    • siblings cost 0.45 (g = 2) and 0.57 (g = 3) of a Cantor step;
    • the walk's Cantor cost per candidate falls to 0.73 (g = 2) and 0.78 (g = 3).
  • Yield: a sibling split-rate deficit of about 0.8 % is plausible; its cost effect is negligible (0.783 to 0.787).
    • The replication interval only just contains 0 (−0.0011 [−0.0024, +0.0001], z = −1.76).
    • Both passes pooled (descriptive): −0.0014 [−0.0024, −0.0003], z = −2.5.
    • The post-hoc exact count puts siblings at the random-divisor rate and the forward points above it, so the gap may come from the forward walk rather than from the siblings.
  • What this is not:
    • not an end-to-end improvement;
    • not run in the Rust pipeline;
    • not a wall-time result;
    • not a change in exponent. It is a constant factor on the Cantor part of the cost, which is about 45 % of the field multiplications but only 0.3–6 % of the §5a GAE total at w ≥ 0.1.
  • Distance to rho is unchanged in substance: under the §5a band the sibling moves the arm total by 1.7 % or less at w ≥ 0.1; the arm stays about 12× (g = 3) and 98× (g = 2, p = 524309) rho at w = 1.

What was not done (and why)

  • No Rust port and no end-to-end solve. df -h / showed 751–752 MB free, below the workflow's 800 MB build line, so nothing was built. The next step is an IcExt::sibling flag in genus_ladder.rs with certified logs (open thread).
  • No wall time. Python counts only. The skipped and shared work is real, but its wall effect on the Rust Cantor is unmeasured.
  • Untested formula families. These counts hold only for the lab's generic Cantor (xgcd based). With explicit Harley/Lange formulas, the shared part (the resultant and inverse) is a different fraction, and rho would get cheaper too. Not measured.
  • Older genus-2 rows. The g = 2 lab rows come from the 2026-11-15 binary. The current g = 2 incumbent may differ, so re-anchor before any claim.
  • No research-kb, and no lead-atlas checkout in this container (as in the survey). The novelty claim is scoped to "not found by a grep of research/ and src/cryptanalysis/". The literature on simultaneous D ± M in hyperelliptic arithmetic was not checked.
  • No per-host w_cal for the §5a calibrated weight, so no "ranked at calibrated price" reading.
  • No AWS/EKS/S3, no bench record, no .resume checkpoint push. The task forbids them.
