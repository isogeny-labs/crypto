Scope and what it says

  • With the window / rank-d label-GAP lines of the rerun (PR #3188) this makes twelve structured x-base lines (six families × two θ) over 16–32 bits with valid controls, none showing κ growth with N: the pair-sum coincidence excess of these bases tracks a random base. Obstruction: max upper95(δ) = 0.0147 against 1/3.
  • Not covered, and why. The FAM-1 mitm-engine arms (subgroup, small_x, dickson of EXP-PFDR-011cd0) are not reproduced: that engine is not in this repository, the small_x arm is the x-window line already measured, and subgroup needs a proper subgroup of the curve group (these curves have prime order); the Dickson line here is a base-construction analogue, not that arm. The bbc53e-base is the planted shared-unknown base: it is the γ control (recovered slopes above), a plant for κ growth by construction, not a candidate family.
  • Sizes above 32 bits and other families are not covered. It prices no end-to-end index calculus and says nothing about rho. Run as a study in this repository (local, counts deterministic); the lead disposition is the sibling repository's call. Raw jobs are in the artifact store with manifests.
