# Curve-dependent inverse equation template

Compile actual target quartic and freshly discovered sections once, before reading any target coordinate vector. Store three curve-remainder polynomials and eight symmetric section polynomials, with field/schema/full quartic binding. Per-target specialization loads that template, checks field/quartic/monomials, and emits the same twelve direct inverse equations using only lambda0*h_i-lambda_i*h0, saturation and t=u0. No basis construction or tensor expansion in the specialization branch. No old equation/eliminant/divisor or expected scalar is a generation input.

Freeze source/lock/arithmetic/input/binary hashes, commit/checkpoint/gate before one compile and one specialization each for fixed D1/Bvectors. Retain stdout/stderr/statuses. Compare specialized equation files byte-for-byte against separately constructed direct equations after generation. Matching systems inherit their established finite solution evidence without rerunning solvers, but do not create a new solve/novelty/global-map claim. A wrong-prime template header must reject before output creation; preserve expected failure. Parser bounds100kterms/poly,totaldegree64,unique monomials,nonzero coefficients below631. Serialization must preserve every coefficient.

No timing here. Compilation cost must be charged in a cold pair comparison and cannot be moved offbooks. Reusing equations is an intermediate toward compiled inversion, not a10xmechanism alone. Equal msolve basis-size/pair-count statistics on two inputs do not certify a reusable symbolic elimination schedule: specialization may change pivots/critical pairs. Shared corpus EXP-GLVPRE-4c9f62makes that same distinction; no novelty claimed. Further schedule reuse needs independent exact algebra verification and fixed target-independent construction, not replay of an expected solution.

Earlier phase bounds reject section/group-only changes as sufficient with remainder unchanged. This gate changes repeated equation generation only; full cold/genus2/4and mathematical10xobjective remain open.
