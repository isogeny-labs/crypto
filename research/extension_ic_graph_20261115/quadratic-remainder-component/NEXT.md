## Count classes before choosing a collection budget

Working counting argument, without a formal proof artifact: there are
`p(p-1)/2` monic irreducible quadratics over Fp. Each has two distinct
conjugate roots in Fp². For one U, specifying V with degree below two and
`V²=H mod U` amounts to choosing an ordinate above each root. There are
at most four choices. Quotienting by simultaneous negation gives at most
two classes: a fixed point requires both ordinates zero, when there is
only one choice. Thus this particular canonical remainder universe has
at most `p(p-1)` classes, rather than the unconstrained Jacobian universe.
The descriptor's eight machine words are not eight independent field
parameters. This bound says nothing about the distribution of accepted
classes or how quickly collisions occur. [pcc:unverified]

## Required matched cohort

After component checks, freeze a separate native collection protocol with
source/image, public curve/target, small-base selection, B ordering, line
ordering, seeds, original deadline and finite resource/spending limits.
Use separate enumeration and extraction randomness. The current native
adapter caps sources and vertices at4096; any larger streaming adapter is
a new implementation and needs its own replay and resource controls.

Feed the identical line/s stream to the existing fully smooth and split-Fp
two-LP materializers and the new quadratic remainder extractor. Retain
every hit, refusal and attempted source; do not remove the earlier fully
smooth rows. Record actual original equations, canonical descriptors and
orientations, unique class counts, repeat counts, all emitted equations
including zero equations, every combination certificate and exact modular
rank on the same projected small columns. [pcc:unverified]

The Q-class block can be eliminated separately and its small-only rows
appended to the existing small-only rows: Q partials contain exactly one
quadratic-class vertex and no omitted Fp point. Preserve the original
source identifiers across these blocks and recheck each output from its
own original inputs. Adding the emitted row counts is not adding ranks.
The first discriminating quantity is the change in exact combined rank,
alongside every additional extraction, group check and graph operation.
[pcc:unverified]

## What each outcome decides

- Additional partials with no extra combined rank reject that cohort's
  useful-yield hypothesis; keep the data and identify repeat scarcity or
  dependence from the actual class/rank records.
- Additional independent rows establish that bounded algebraic component's
  useful yield. They do not establish lower collection or solve cost.
- A cost result requires admitted matched accounting of all added work.
  A pipeline result additionally requires target descent, the protected
  target/RHS, original elliptic replay and the protocol's reference.

Do not reuse a source-fixture search, the constructed control, or a
favorable class collision as a random-target yield estimate. Complete
native pricing, source custody and the applicable runner/checkpoint gates
remain open prerequisites for the corresponding measurement claims.
