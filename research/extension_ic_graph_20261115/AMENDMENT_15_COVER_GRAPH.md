## Cold public-input workers

Each arm starts a fresh OS process. Parent generates the public known-answer
challenge and writes a public-only fixture. Children receive curve/points/order;
the planted scalar is absent. Both native APIs receive zero in their legacy
answer field: those correctness flags are expected false. Actual returned
scalars undergo native verification and another public-curve multiplication;
only then does the parent compare with its held-out answer. IC workers rebuild
transfer, contexts, factor base, collection, descent and solve. No transferred
points, learned pool or earlier worker state are supplied. [pcc:unverified]

Primary cost is outer child-process wall, including spawn/exec, fixture load,
validation, setup/transfer, failures/fallback, complete solve, full replay,
report output and teardown. Retain inner worker wall too. Challenge generation
and fixture-file preparation are shared outside arms and reported separately;
outer cohort wall retains that work. Legacy operation totals omit graph and
wrapper costs and remain incomplete diagnostics. No ranking from those totals.
