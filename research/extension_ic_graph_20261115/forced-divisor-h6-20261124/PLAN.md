Fp and V over Fp², require U|(H-V²). Let W=(H-V²)/U, C=x+c0,
T=t0+t1*x+t2*x², A=UT-CV and S=UT²-2TCV-C²W. Check A²-C²H=US.

coefficient is-1. Use eight Fp coordinates ordered c0.re,c0.im,t0.re,t0.im,
t1.re,t1.im,t2.re,t2.im. These are seven quadratic equations, without an
independence/dimension or cheap-enumeration assumption.
