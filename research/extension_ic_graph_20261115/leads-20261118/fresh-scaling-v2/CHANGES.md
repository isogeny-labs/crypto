| **F1** exponent test cannot fail | C2/P1 was decided on a global OLS of ln C_total on ln l. A fixed descent cost flattens it: 0.119 (e=2) and 0.151 (e=3) against 0.488 for rho. It passes even when collection scales as l^(1/2). | The decision uses α* = the largest per-phase upper exponent limit (SUCCESS_CRITERIA T2 item 4), together with the top-pair local slope of the complete M2 total, against θ = α_rho − δ (δ = 0.05). There are four verdicts: FAIL, PASS, HELD (below the derived exponent 1/3, T2 item 5) and UNRESOLVED. The global fit is description only. | `protocol.draft.json` `decision_rule.exponent`; `tools/rule_check.rs` `v2_verdict` | **Resolved in design.** On v1's `estimate.csv`, e=3 now gives **FAIL** (α* = 0.500, top pair 0.422); e=2 gives HELD at the point estimate (α* = 0.333 on the derived exponent). In simulation e=3 FAILs 200/200 on both ladders, where v1 PASSes 200/200. The lesson L-eda09 two-phase case and the null (all phases at 1/2) FAIL 200/200. The fixed-cost case is HELD 200/200. **Caveat:** with G-cov failed, the e=3 case is only UNRESOLVED, because the M2 top pair (0.40–0.43) stays below θ. |

| **F8** p2017 may overflow u64 | l = 1.683e19 > 2^63; the dry run was tested only on p101 and p1009. | **Dropped p2017.** The frozen worker refuses it in every arm. `PublicCurve::validate` and each IC pipeline require 101 ≤ p ≤ 1511 and l < 2^63, at both 11c9a031 and fcda1e22. New gates: **G-dom** (l < 2^62 at admission) and **G-ovf** (planted pilot at every size, built with `overflow-checks=on`, which must match the measured build). | `family`, `gates.G-dom` (audit), `gates.G-ovf`, `dry-run/` | Resolved in design.
