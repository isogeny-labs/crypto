# CT71 — Normalizer obstruction for a single global torsion-point field

**Scientific standing: [pcc:unverified].** This is a conditional, exact field-theoretic obstruction for the *fixed-global-point-field* lifting strategy of CT69–70, not an obstruction to all characteristic-changing computations or a lower bound on arbitrary ECDLP algorithms.

## The theorem (explicit hypotheses)

Let \(E/\mathbb Q\) be an elliptic curve, let \(\ell\) be an odd prime, and assume its mod-\(\ell\) Galois representation is **surjective**:
\[
\rho_{E,\ell}(\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb Q}/\mathbb Q))
=\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb F_\ell).
\]
Choose \(0\ne T\in E[\ell]\), put \(L=\mathbb Q(E[\ell])\), and let \(K=\mathbb Q(T)\) be the field generated by *both coordinates* of that oriented point. Then:

1. \([K:\mathbb Q]=\ell^2-1\).
2. \(E[\ell](K)=\langle T\rangle\), containing precisely \(\ell\) points, **not** the full \(\ell^2\) torsion points.
3. \(\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathbb Q}(K)\simeq\mathbb F_\ell^\times\), of order \(\ell-1\), and its action on \(T\) yields exactly the nonzero scalar multiples \([a]T\).
4. In particular, a rational point-field automorphism does not send \(T\) outside its scalar line; to obtain a genuinely independent \(\ell\)-torsion vector one must use a larger field or a different point field.

**Proof.** Identify \(T\) with \(e_1=(1,0)\). The stabilizer in \(G=\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb F_\ell)\) is
\[
H=\left\{\begin{pmatrix}1&u\\0&v\end{pmatrix}:
u\in\mathbb F_\ell,\ v\in\mathbb F_\ell^\times\right\},
\qquad |H|=\ell(\ell-1).
\]
By Galois theory \(K=L^H\) and \([K:\mathbb Q]=|G|/|H|=\ell^2-1\). The \(H\)-fixed vectors are precisely \(\mathbb F_\ell e_1\): the unipotent \(\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)\) forces a fixed vector's second coordinate to vanish, and all of \(H\) fixes the first line pointwise. The normalizer \(N_G(H)\) must preserve the **unique** \(H\)-fixed line; conversely the entire upper-triangular Borel \(B\) normalizes \(H\), since \(H\) is the kernel of the top-left diagonal character \(B\to\mathbb F_\ell^\times\). Hence \(N_G(H)=B\), \(|B|=\ell(\ell-1)^2\), and \(\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathbb Q}(K)=N_G(H)/H\simeq\mathbb F_\ell^\times\). Its elements send \(T\) to the distinct \([a]T\). QED.

This proof relies on **full** \(\operatorname{GL}_2\) image. For a reducible, CM, or otherwise restricted Galois image, recompute the actual stabilizer and normalizer; no universal claim is made.

## Concrete CT69/CT70 order-three consequence

CT69 uses \(E=\text{11a1}:y^2+y=x^3-x^2-10x-20\) with full mod-3 Galois image (recorded separately in CT52; Cremona's mod-\(p\) table lists 5, not 3, as an exceptional prime for this model). For its explicit \(K=\mathbb Q[\beta]/H(\beta)\):

- \([K:\mathbb Q]=8\), but **\(E[3](K)=\{O,T,-T\}\)** and \(\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathbb Q}(K)=\{1,[-1]\}\) has order two.
- CT69's exact polynomial \(H\) satisfies \(H(-Y-1)=H(Y)\), and the nontrivial automorphism is \(\beta\mapsto-\beta-1\). Modulo 29 this interchanges roots \(5\) and \(23\), exactly as required by the negation \(P=(18,5)\leftrightarrow-P=(18,23)\).
- The **other six** nonzero geometric 3-torsion points are *not* \(K\)-rational. One degree-eight point field does not supply arbitrary full-basis lifts, even though CT70's factorization/Frobenius tests pass at both primes.

For the source subgroup \(\langle P\rangle\), all three points *do* have global representatives in \(K\). However, the theorem only **enumerates the candidates** \([a]T\). It does not select the correct \(a\) for arbitrary encoded \(Q=[a]P\) without doing additional work. Enumerating the \(\ell-1\) candidates and reducing them is a scalar lookup, not an uncharged efficient lift. This makes the unfilled algorithmic step explicit, without presuming a generic-group lower bound for other kinds of lifting.
