# CT93 — Exact place/embedding ambiguity of the mixed-characteristic 7-torsion bridge

**2026-11-14; scientific standing [pcc:unverified].** Exact fixed-fixture, conditional-on-CT80/CT82/CT91–92 arithmetic and classical algebraic geometry. This is **not** a non-enumerative transfer algorithm, not an attack, and not a generic ECDLP lower bound.

## The explicit mixed-characteristic transport family

Use CT80's elliptic curve E/Q given by y²+y=x³−x²−10x−20 and oriented global 7-torsion point T. K=Q(T) has degree 48. By CT82, E(K)[7] is cyclic, generated by T, and reduction at any of the six degree-one places v above 37 is an isomorphism onto E(F37)[7]=<P>, with P=(9,4). Label v by the unique u in F7× satisfying red_v(T)=[u]P.

At 2, CT91–92 independently establish two degree-24 primes w of K. For each w the residue field has **24 distinct F2-embeddings** into a fixed concrete F_(2^24), obtained by powers of Frobenius. The two sets of images of T are the two disjoint Frobenius orbits of length 24 on the 48 nonzero geometric points E[7]. In particular every nonzero binary 7-torsion point occurs as an image of T under exactly one such pair (w, residue-field embedding sigma).

For a source-place label u, a target prime w and an identification sigma of its residue field, the resulting **abstract torsion-group isomorphism onto a cyclic subgroup** is

    Phi_(u,w,sigma)([k]P) = [k*u^(-1)] sigma(red_w(T)).

Both source and target choices are held FIXED across P and Q. This formula is a mathematical specification on the finite group, **not an algorithm** for evaluating an arbitrary encoded Q without its source logarithm.

## Exact counts and the stabilizer theorem

- Six degree-one source primes above 37 (CT92: source splitting 1^6 3^14).
- Two binary primes above 2, each of residue degree 24 (CT92: binary splitting 24²).
- Twenty-four isomorphisms of each residue field to the fixed F_(2^24), yielding **6 × 2 × 24 = 288 labeled choices**.
- Exactly **48 different nonzero homomorphisms** E(F37)[7] → E(F_(2^24))[7], because a homomorphism is determined by the nonzero image of P, and every one of the 48 possible images is attained.
- **Every distinct homomorphism has exactly six labeled realizations**, because binary Frobenius satisfies pi⁴=[3] on 7-torsion, and 3 generates F7×. For each fixed u, the target residue embeddings cover each 24-cycle once; varying u merely permutes that cycle.
- The images are the **eight cyclic subgroups/lines** in the rank-two target E[7], six nonidentity points per line. The two binary prime factors produce four lines each.

The 288 labeled choices must *not* be called 288 distinct transfer maps. Conversely the 48 genuine abstract maps must *not* be mistaken for 48 independently computable ECDLP reductions.

### Negative controls

Fix a source place mapping T to P and one binary embedding mapping T to U. Change only Q's source label to u=2, while keeping the generator source place unchanged. The resulting map scales every nonzero Q image by 2^{-1}; all six nonzero public tests disagree with the fixed-place map. Independently apply pi⁴=[3] only to binary Q while leaving U fixed: again all six nonzero cases disagree. The identity remains fixed, as required.

## Different-characteristic obstruction: precisely the category

There is **no nonempty morphism of schemes** E_(F37) → E_(F2), whether taken over Spec Z or as ordinary absolute schemes. Indeed a scheme morphism would induce at each point a unital homomorphism of residue fields of characteristics 2 → 37, which cannot exist. Likewise there is no common field into which both finite base fields embed as fields. Thus the 48 torsion-group maps above are **not** algebraic morphisms of curves between different characteristics; they are set/group maps obtained via a number-field model, fixed choices of places and residue identifications.

This obstruction **does not** prohibit a mixed-characteristic integral model, specialization maps from a common algebraic torsion point, or an efficient specially encoded computational reduction. It only rules out incorrectly calling the resulting cross-fiber finite-group correspondence a scheme morphism. It also does not apply to correspondences/isogenies over one fixed characteristic, which remain under separate native research.

## What remains to achieve the goal

CT93 identifies and counts an intrinsic **choice ambiguity**, not a new speedup. A usable full algorithm must accept encoded Q over F37 without knowing k, recover its unique E(K)[7] representative at one fixed v (CT82 existence alone does not implement the inverse), transport to one fixed w with a fixed field identification, and recover k from the binary target. Its **cold** cost must include construction of the degree-48 point field/tower and 2-maximal bases, 37-adic/number-field selective recognition with certified precision/height, residue evaluation, target DLP and verification. Compare those measured costs against same-size optimized rho and admitted index-calculus before claiming any reduction advantage.
