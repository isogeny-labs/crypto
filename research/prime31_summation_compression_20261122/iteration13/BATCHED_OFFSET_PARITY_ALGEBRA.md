## Exact squarefree offset selectors

Keep the preceding hypotheses p>3, N=2^k>=4, 4N|p+1, regular c!=+-2, with the actual eligible base a filtered subset of the full Dickson fibre. Write m=N/4. For offset delta=gamma^d+gamma^(-d), 0<=d<=N/2, parity is (-1)^d. Define E_j by E_0=1, E_1=X and E_j=X E_(j-1)-E_(j-2), and D_j as usual with parameter 1. The exact squarefree parity selectors are

    P_+(X)=(X^2-4) E_(m-1)(X),
    P_-(X)=D_m(X).

Indeed D_(2m)-2=(X^2-4) E_(m-1)^2 and D_(2m)+2=D_m^2 by the Laurent functional equations. The roots of the first are the distinct traces of the even exponents of gamma, including both endpoints; those of the second are the distinct traces of the odd exponents. Distinct traces correspond precisely to inversion orbits, since t+t^-1=u+u^-1 iff u=t or u=t^-1. There are m+1 even and m odd orbits; all split over F_p since the torus is rational under inversion. The polynomials are monic and coprime, including at p dividing neither N nor 2. For N=64 their degrees are 17 and 16, not 32 each. N=2 is the direct endpoint case. No elliptic scalar residues have been inferred.

## Batch all offsets of one parity without factoring the offset selector first

For e in {+1,-1}, let A_e=F_p[X]/(P_e), with X the offset coordinate. For a fixed affine target r, use precisely the preceding quartic Q(X,S;r)=A(X,S;r)^2-C(X,S;r)^2 (X+2)(S+2), where

    A=(X-2)(S-2)r^2+(X+S-a)^2-4br,
    C=2r(X+S+a)+4b.

Q is monic in S, hence R_e=A_e[S]/(Q) is free of rank four over A_e even if Q has repeated factors, even at X=-2 and even when A_e has zero divisors. Its F_p dimension is 4 deg P_e. Starting h=S, do k-1 squarings h <- h^2-2 in R_e and subtract e*c. This computes L_e(S)=D_(N/2)(S)-e*c modulo Q. Let T_e be the 4x4 matrix of multiplication by h on the basis 1,S,S^2,S^3, and let J_e(X)=det T_e computed by a division-free determinant in A_e (not field Gaussian elimination). Define G_e=gcd(P_e, representative(J_e)) over F_p, monic.

For each root delta of P_e, specialize via evaluation A_e -> F_p. The matrix specializes to multiplication by L_e mod Q_delta; this is invertible iff gcd(Q_delta,L_e)=1, by Bezout or the quotient unit criterion. Consequently the roots of G_e are EXACTLY the offsets with at least one full-fibre norm hit. This works for multiple roots of Q, vanishing J_e on an entire parity block, and nonunits in A_e. There is no determinant-of-S3-low-rank fallacy: T_e is a regular-representation multiplication matrix, not the previously rejected rank-three pencil. All roots of P_e and L_e are rational under the pinned torus hypotheses. No Frobenius or root-factorization assumption about Q is needed.

Recover only the roots of G_e, then specialize Q and compute gcd with L_e by the constant-degree Dickson recurrence; recover s=A/C when C!=0, and both allowed signs when C=A=0. At delta=-2, s=0 only (using Q=A^2 is harmless for zero detection but use A for witness recovery); at delta=2 retain diagonal tangent; the identity target is separate. Recover the roots of Z^2-sZ+delta+S and require both in the actual M. Recover eligible signed group points and replay equality to the target. Repeated roots/signs and unordered pairs must be deduplicated at the declared witness contract. A nontrivial G_e need not give any actual-M witness. G_e=1 for both parities is an exact full-fibre miss, hence an actual-M miss. It is not a complete predicate for actual-M misses without the subsequent guards.

## Accounting and adversarial gate

Let Mpoly(n) be the field-operation cost of polynomial multiplication of length n; do not confuse it with base M. Forming P_e in O(Mpoly(N) log N) work and O(N) coefficient storage using the doubling identities (or the linear recurrence/product construction with its own charged cost) is source setup; include actual base/torus generation separately. Fixed monic modular reduction can be preprocessed at comparable cost. Each R_e operation uses a constant number of A_e operations. Construct h, T_e and determinant in O(Mpoly(N) log N) field operations and O(N) coefficients with linear-space modular multiplication, or explicitly charge the chosen backend's workspace. The two fast gcds cost O(Mpoly(N) log N); no enumeration of all offsets or factoring P_e is required for a full-fibre miss. An exact miss certificate can contain the two polynomial Bezout identities U_e P_e+V_e J_e=1, with O(N) field words; replay the recurrence/determinant and identities, do not trust supplied J_e. For hits charge split-root isolation of G_e (degree K_e<=deg P_e), constant-degree root isolation at each recovered offset, every sign, M/subgroup check and emitted witness/proof. A factorization timeout is indeterminate, not a miss.

For N=64 the two free ranks over F_p are 68 and 64 (total 132 coefficient slots for one residue element), versus 33 explicit offset scans. This does NOT reduce the asymptotic O(N log N) per-offset arithmetic in the preceding note, and cannot beat the O(B) group-complement two-leaf control when N=Theta(B). It does give a single exact univariate hit-selector per parity and allows output-sensitive offset root isolation, not output-sensitive total search. Extending this direct method to two independent offset coordinates uses A_e tensor A_f of dimension deg(P_e)*deg(P_f); the sum across four parity blocks is (N/2+1)^2=1089 at N64, before any midpoint/root algebra. Dense explicit batching across both offsets therefore still incurs Theta(N^2) coefficient work/traffic, with the precise caveat that no lower bound for implicit or sparse algorithms follows. A new four-leaf contraction must avoid materializing that tensor product or prove a genuine output-sensitive exact representation; low-degree equations alone do not imply it.

Validation plan: after freezing this note, check the two Dickson factor identities by formal integer polynomial arithmetic for m=1,2,4,8,16,32; verify the quotient norm/unit predicate on generic small split and repeated polynomial cases (not elliptic inputs), including zero divisors and an all-hit parity block. Future native validation must compare the batch selector to scalar-offset gcd and full signed group replay on every small-field target and actual M filter, with original Rust arithmetic and resource admission.
