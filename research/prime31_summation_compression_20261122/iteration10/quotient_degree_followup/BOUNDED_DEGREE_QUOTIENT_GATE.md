# Exact degree and sign gates for a Dickson-pair quotient

**Date:** 2026-11-23 UTC. **Lane:** #4977, Prime31 S3/pair-spectrum. **Standing:** written mathematical derivation with a symbolic identity check, no original-Rust execution or signed independent validation. [pcc:unverified]

## Scope and result

The preceding [leaf-norm review](../quotient_followup/DICKSON_PAIR_NORM_REVIEW.md) proves the exact identity R_M J_M = kappa C_M^2 but supplies an F7 counterexample to automatic uniform D2 inheritance by the pair spectrum. This follow-up asks how much a *different* low-degree polynomial or rational quotient could compress the actual pair-coordinate algebra.

For any split squarefree pair-support polynomial H_X of degree D over F_p, and any nonconstant rational coordinate phi(V)=P(V)/Q(V), defined on all X and of rational degree delta=max(deg P,deg Q)>=1, the subalgebra generated by phi(V) in A_X=F_p[V]/H_X(V) has dimension

    K = |phi(X)| >= ceil(D/delta).

In particular a fixed-degree coordinate cannot reduce the dimension to O(D^theta) for any fixed theta<1 as D grows. Achieving K=O(D^theta) requires delta=Omega(D^(1-theta)). This is an exact **dimension bound for the specified single-coordinate fibre quotient**, not a lower bound on target-query circuits, structured high-degree Dickson ladders, multi-coordinate indexes, or ECDLP. A degree-2 Dickson quotient can at best halve the number of distinct components. [pcc:unverified]

A second exact obstruction concerns the *two sign choices* of each off-diagonal S3 pair. For u!=v and f(u)f(v)!=0, if r_plus and r_minus are the distinct roots of S3(u,v,T), then

    D_2(r_plus)=D_2(r_minus)
      iff (u+v)(uv+a)+2b = 0    in F_p,

where D_2(t)=t^2-2. Therefore the D2 coordinate does **not generally** coalesce the two S3 output branches even before any global pair-spectrum indexing. The output label/sign split must be retained. [pcc:unverified]

## 1. Quotient dimension proof and rational exceptions

Let p>3, E: y^2=f(x)=x^3+a x+b nonsingular, and let X be the D distinct nonidentity abscissae of the actual signed two-leaf pair spectrum in an odd-order rational subgroup. Thus H_X(V)=prod_{x in X}(V-x) is monic, split and squarefree.

Let phi=P/Q with coprime polynomials P,Q over F_p, nonconstant, Q(x)!=0 for every x in X, and delta=max(deg P,deg Q). Put Y=phi(X) and c_y=|{x in X:phi(x)=y}|. For each y, the nonzero polynomial P(V)-y Q(V) has degree at most delta and vanishes at all c_y *distinct* x in the fibre. Hence 1<=c_y<=delta and

    D=sum_{y in Y} c_y <= delta |Y|.

By the Chinese remainder theorem, A_X is isomorphic to the product of D copies of F_p. Polynomial expressions in phi(V) are exactly the functions constant on the fibres of phi, because the distinct image values in Y are separated by Lagrange polynomials. Therefore F_p[phi(V)] is isomorphic to F_p[W]/prod_{y in Y}(W-y) and has dimension exactly K=|Y|. Pole values are deliberately excluded: if Q vanishes on X, the rational expression is not an element of A_X and must be treated using a separate chart. If P/Q is constant the claimed bound does not apply. [pcc:unverified]

For a Dickson coordinate phi_s(V)=D_(2^s)(V,1), its formal polynomial degree is delta=2^s in odd characteristic; its evaluation can use a binary recurrence of O(s) arithmetic depth. Hence degree growth is *not* itself a computational lower bound. It gives a necessary condition for dimension contraction only:

    K_s<=C D^theta  =>  2^s >= D^(1-theta)/C.

At fixed s, K_s=Omega(D). To target K_s=O(D^theta) for theta<1/2 requires 2^s=Omega(D^(1/2+epsilon)) for some epsilon>0 in the fixed-exponent formulation, but even such a large degree does not imply that any pair-spectrum fibres are populated near capacity. The actual Dickson leaf origin supplies no automatic fibre-size guarantee after elliptic pair summation. [pcc:unverified]

In the previously recorded regular F7 example X={1,2,3,4}, phi=D2, the three fibres have sizes 1,1,2. This gives D=4, K=3, satisfying 2<=K<=4 and disproving uniform rank-two freeness. It does not establish an asymptotic distribution for the inherited Prime31 X.

## 2. The exact S3 branch test

For u!=v, the classical S3 polynomial is A T^2+B T+C, with

    A=(u-v)^2,
    B=-2((u+v)(uv+a)+2b),
    C=(uv-a)^2-4b(u+v).

Direct polynomial expansion gives

    B^2-4AC = 16 f(u) f(v).

If u and v are rational nonzero subgroup abscissae then f(u),f(v) are nonzero squares; the discriminant is nonzero and the two roots r_plus,r_minus are rational and distinct. Their sum is

    r_plus+r_minus = -B/A
      = 2((u+v)(uv+a)+2b)/(u-v)^2.

Since D2(r_plus)-D2(r_minus)=(r_plus-r_minus)(r_plus+r_minus), the claimed iff follows. This is algebraically independent of leaf torus parameters; it does not identify r_plus versus r_minus with a chosen point sign until explicit signed group replay.

The inherited F7 control uses u=3,v=4,a=0,b=3, giving the two roots 1 and 4. Their D2 images are respectively 6 and 0 modulo 7: even the two alternatives for this *one* input pair do not collapse. This is a previously published handwritten fixture, cited here rather than rebranded as a new native test. [pcc:unverified]

## 3. Exact fibre-resultant decomposition and its charged dimensions

For each y in Y define H_y(V)=prod_{x in X, phi(x)=y}(V-x), of degree c_y. Then H_X=prod_y H_y. Define block factors

    B_(y,z)(T) =
      Res_U(H_y(U), Res_V(H_z(V), S3(U,V,T))).

Because all H_y are monic and split, these are exactly

    B_(y,z)(T) = prod_{u in X_y, v in X_z} S3(u,v,T),

and the full ordered-pair resultant factors as

    Delta_X(T) = prod_{y,z in Y} B_(y,z)(T).

The symmetry S3(u,v,T)=S3(v,u,T) gives B_(y,z)=B_(z,y). Exact formal degrees are

    deg_T B_(y,z)=2 c_y c_z                  (y!=z),
    deg_T B_(y,y)=2 c_y^2-c_y                (y=z).

The diagonal loss is one degree for every u=v, since S3(u,u,T)=-4 f(u)(T-x(2P_u)), with nonzero leading coefficient. The off-diagonal leading coefficients (u-v)^2 are nonzero. Summing block degrees recovers 2D^2-D, independently of the quotient partition. Equal *target roots* across blocks may cause multiplicity but do not alter these formal degrees. Thus this identity preserves exact zero support, but merely collecting all dense block coefficients is still quadratic in D. [pcc:unverified]

The fibre tables require D label entries and K<=D fibre headers. If the D roots X are already enumerated, evaluate an s-step Dickson ladder in O(Ds) basic field operations and group by output with O(D) expected hash operations or O(D log D) comparisons; input enumeration and arithmetic are not free. Explicit monic fibre polynomials have total coefficient count D+K and ordinary polynomial-building costs that must be charged. Constructing the *full* ordered pair block products directly visits D^2 root pairs; the established resultant/gcd pair scan remains the stronger quasi-linear-in-D online control.

A putative query algorithm may use the factored representation without materializing the dense Delta_X; the degree count does **not** rule that out. But to turn it into a faster exact target index one still needs a target-to-fibre selector that identifies every relevant block (including missed targets), or a genuinely faster global zero predicate, with the charged selector/index construction and false-positive exclusions. Knowing only a fibre label y for one guessed source tuple is not a relation finder. An aggregate norm-zero decision does not recover all signed leaf labels. [pcc:unverified]

## 4. Complexity gate and conditional implementation selection

For D=Theta(B^2) and near-linear-in-D source setup, the next proposal must supply an exact empty-inclusive target query of complexity O-tilde(D^theta+h), theta<1 for a component exponent gain, and theta<1/2 for the previously declared conventional m=4 pipeline budget. Here h is the actual requested signed output and proof traffic, not just distinct x-roots. A bounded-degree one-coordinate quotient cannot meet the *dimension* version of either target, but a separate data structure with higher-degree or nonpolynomial operations remains open.

Before selecting a Rust implementation, a candidate must exhibit:
1. A source-specific expression for phi_s and a charged proof that its actual X-fibre count K_s is substantially below D, not merely below D/2^s.
2. A target-dependent block selector or zero oracle with a justified operation bound on **misses**, not just on planted positives.
3. Exact rational-root and Frobenius guards, identity subtotals, the two S3 branch values, duplicate buckets, signed replay and independent-row accounting.
4. Comparable setup/storage/proof-output costs, an explicit growing eligible family, and the maintained O-tilde(D) original pair-spectrum scan as control.

The immediate bounded check on a frozen original-Rust fixture is to enumerate actual X (without scalar labels), record K_s and the histogram of c_y for selected s, and compare exact zero support against the unfactored resultant and signed group replay. This is a *proposed* test, not one executed here. Do not implement the quotient query until a nontrivial selector/extraction bound has survived mathematical review.
