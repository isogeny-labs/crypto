## Question and competing explanations

- H1: preserving Dickson composition lowers complete algebraic extraction cost against the existing expanded-Horner oracle on identical inputs.
- H2: dense quotient residues and gcd/extraction dominate, so the recurrence loses to the sparse-product or group-complement control despite its short evaluation circuit.
- H3: intermediate residues retain exploitable structure. This requires an explicit representation and extraction algorithm; low support alone does not establish a new exponent.
