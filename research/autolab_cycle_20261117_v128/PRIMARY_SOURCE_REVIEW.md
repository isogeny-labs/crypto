# Primary-source comparison

Re-read [Neiger,Rosenkilde,Schost2017](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.10658),abstract only. It computes representations of all power-series roots of a supplied polynomial at a fixed precision,including multiple roots. This is strong prior art for root extraction/lifting;it does not by itself search the unknown parameter family. [Lecerf2006 institutional abstract](https://researchportal.ip-paris.fr/en/publications/sharp-precision-in-hensel-lifting-for-bivariate-polynomial-factor/)describes lifting/recombination and linear-system solution bases. C128's top-coefficient module compression and standard Boolean ANF are not claimed as new general algebra. Their application here is a falsifiable program optimization. No full-text novelty certification or new complexity theorem is claimed;qualified matched complete benchmarks remain independently necessary.
