modulus `8388641`, coefficients `a₂=1334572` and `a₆=3534087`, and

The measured one-target component builds an ordered pair index on the
cleared base. A target is formed from the recorded SHA-256-derived
nonzero scalar and the first nonzero cleared point; scalar fixture
construction is outside query timing. The lookup checks `R-C` for each
third point until a pair is found, then verifies `A+B+C=R`. It found a
witness after six third-point attempts. Pair-index construction took
0.448 seconds and the target lookup, including group-law verification,
took 0.000179 seconds on an ordinary local host. Lifting took 0.392
seconds, direct membership checks 0.057 seconds, and cofactor clearing
0.024 seconds. These are stage observations, with no isolation gate.

**Exact-yield audit:** After the target lookup, the runner enumerates
the entire 232,891-point subgroup by repeated addition, assigns every
cleared base point a scalar coordinate, and counts all pair and triple
sums modulo 232,891. This 2.732-second census is an audit oracle, kept
outside the measured lookup. It gives 29,135 pair-sum residues and
232,881 three-sum residues, including identity. The independent audit
reconstructs the source instance and uses a separate direct 15,625,000
triple loop; it reproduces the full multiplicity histogram, 54 triples
for the frozen target, and the recorded group-law witness. Peak RSS in
the measured process was 404,193,280 bytes. The [checked Sage runtime](sage-runtime-info.json)
and [audit receipt](audit.json) retain the replay evidence.

**Proof map:**
[`H4PairIndex.pair_lookup_iff`](../pair_coverage_20261120/formal/PairIndexCoverage.lean)
proves that lookup of `R-C` is equivalent to a three-summand group
relation under explicit group laws.
[`H4CofactorProjection.additive_map_preserves_three_sum`](../subgroup_projection_20261120/formal/CofactorProjection.lean)
proves that additive cofactor projection transports relations. Both
Lean modules use pinned `v4.36.0`, build successfully, and report no
axioms. Applying them to this concrete curve and formalizing the exact
232,880-point cardinality remain partial: the missing bridge is a Lean
model of this binary elliptic group and kernel-checked certificates for
the encoded 253 lifts and the full subgroup image. The written group
argument is that `[36]` is additive, so a relation among original points
maps to a relation among the cleared points; the pair-lookup equivalence
then follows by adding `-C` to the target equation.

This is a decomposition-component result. The 250 cleared points do not
retain the original x-subspace constraint, and their logarithms, relation
matrix, and final recovery stage are not part of this packet. The
62,500-entry pair-index build is charged explicitly; the short lookup
alone is not a complete same-target comparison. The next experiment
should solve the cleared base logarithms with measured relation
