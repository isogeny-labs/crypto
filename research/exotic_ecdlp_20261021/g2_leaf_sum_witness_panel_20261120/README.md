# Leaf-sum F4 reduction persists on witness-backed S3 residuals

On two source-recorded factor-base relations, the invertible `s=u+v` coordinate change reduced msolve 0.11.0 rows from 71,925 to 59,309 (17.5%) and pairs from 35,896 to 29,623 (17.5%). Exact Boolean roots agree after inverse mapping, independent S3 field enumeration agrees with both solver bases, and each residual has factor-base point pairs that replay the complete three-point relation. Local exploratory wall time totaled 154.739 seconds for control and 119.390 seconds for leaf-sum, a paired total ratio of 1.296.
