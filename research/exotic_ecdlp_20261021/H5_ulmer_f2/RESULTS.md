## Why the corank is trivially Theta(r), and what it does not show

K is a finite-index sublattice of Z^r (index = size of the generated subgroup, <= |E_lambda|), so dim K = r always, and any O(1)-dimensional K_geo gives corank r - O(1). The informative content is the second observation: K itself is cheap to describe only if one can take discrete logs in E_lambda(F_{p^4}); its short vectors (median norm 2, weight-4 relations P_i ± P_j ± P_k ± P_l = 0) come from pigeonhole on a group of size ~p^4 ≈ r^2 and are not geometric (not in K_geo). Computing K needed the group's discrete logs, which is the DLP being attacked. Geometry supplied exactly two relations (each with multiplier 2).

No evidence that f=2 escapes the f=1 collapse.
