# Cold complete-recovery native panel

Frozen specs: `ecbench-n17.json` and `ecbench-n23.json`, generated by the
adjacent native `specs.rs`; hashes in `specs.sha256`. The n11/d4 smoke spec
is functional validation only, with two targets, one round and L0.

The final panel has8 independent public targets on each registered Koblitz
curve,5 measured rounds plus1 warmup, alternate order, one worker and L2.
Use standard polynomial subspaces of dimension5 at n17 and6 at n23. All
methods within each session receive exactly the same base/domain/targets;
these bases differ explicitly from the original component benchmark's
Frobenius-invariant bases. Sizes/base dimensions are fixed before the final
timings, not selected from a winning arm's results. No scaling claim.

Eleven arms: strong signed-Frobenius rho; F4 baseline/A-A/candidate;
F5 baseline/A-A/candidate; F6 with qualified native GF2 kernels; typed
generalized roots; batched Semaev; the full Semaev pair table. Baseline
F4/F5 keep the a78 recursive policy and the prior three GF2 switches;
candidates use identity-bound `matrix_runtime=quadratic-tail`. F6 names
`kernel_runtime=native-v1`. Generalized names its exact assignment budget.
Semaev names `batch_setup=1`. No environment-override custody exception.

Every measured execution is a fresh process that must recover the public
DLP and independently verify [d]G=Q. The method's cold solve wall clock
includes factor base, oracle setup, relation collection, linear algebra,
descent/recovery and internal verification. Also report complete child
process wall time, which includes common curve/workload construction and
startup. Compilation, warmup, calibration/orchestration and replay overhead
are not silently attributed to only one algorithm. Keep the whole job's
resource receipt. Never equate one PDP call with a complete DLP solve.

Each child has a300-second timeout and the fixed1,000,000 relation-trial
cap. Preserve every timeout, exhaustion and failure. The native harness's
sealed-session isolation, five-pair minimum, A/A and paired-bootstrap gates
are authoritative; do not remove an incomplete workload to get a win.
Use `verify --replay-all` and keep its audit certificate. Audit CPU time
does not enter timings. No overlapping builds or other work on the timed VM.

Publish each curve/session separately, not a pooled cross-size ranking.
Use native `compare` for F4/F5 baseline/candidate and matched-rho pairs,
and `table` for cold S and its generic floor/reference. Record every phase
and unpriced counter. New bounded-tail and generalized Gray assignments
are counted and explicitly unpriced, separate from calibrated Macaulay
word XORs. The generalized solver_ops unit and its named assignment counter
describe the same work and must not be charged twice in a sensitivity band.
A ratio of partially priced S values is not a total-cost comparison; native
wall outcomes remain conditional on admission and A/A.

Use detached dedicated c7i.2xlarge ephemeral runners, x86-64, automatic
termination, with frozen source/spec/executable hashes and host capsules.
Keep all outputs and status even when a session fails its admission or
correctness gates. No external-fastest, Arm64/GPU or rho-beating claim
follows just from completing this panel.
