# N12 model-held-out factor-base tuning protocol

Protocol `factor-base-heldout/20261025/v1`, frozen before inspecting any per-model training result or running the two new policies. The source cohort is the independent public known-answer replication (`factor-base-graph-replication/20261024/v1`); its full-cohort aggregates are prior evidence and are not selection scores.

For each of the seven bit sizes (16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 36, 40), sort the three distinct curve ICV1 identities bytewise. The first two models are **training** and the third is **evaluation**. This fixes 14 training models and seven evaluation models, 60 targets per model. The split is model-level; no target from an evaluation model enters tuning. The exact model identities are independently checked against the source archive by the validator.

The seven training policies are `(base denominator, large-prime acceptance, jumps)`: `(1, any, 8)`, `(1, any, 16)`, `(1, any, 32)`, `(2, one, 16)`, `(4, one, 16)`, `(2, both, 16)`, `(4, both, 16)`. `one` accepts a split relation if at least one root passes the target-blind base predicate; `both` requires both roots. When the denominator is one, all roots pass, so acceptance is `any`. The five `any`/`one` arms use the frozen replication raw records; the two `both` arms are new runs on the same training targets. This is a prespecified seven-policy search, not a full factorial claim.

Select the policy with the lowest *sum of candidate GAE divided by sum of matched rho GAE* over the 840 training targets in the representative price cell `S/M=0.5`, `I/M=10`, native logical-operation price `0.1` source-addition equivalents. A failed candidate has no finite selection score and cannot be selected. Break an exact tie lexicographically by `(denominator, acceptance, jumps)`. Freeze the selected policy and the training output SHA-256 before any evaluation execution. Preserve every training loss, failure and cap.

Evaluation targets use `factor-base-heldout-evaluation/20261025/v1` as the first component of the replication SHA-256 domain-separated derivation, with the original target-scalar, algorithm-seed and workload domains. Run the selected policy on all 420 targets on the seven evaluation models, 60 per model, with source-negation rho in the same process. Check every candidate and rho scalar by recomputing `kP` on the source curve and comparing the public planted scalar. Preserve failures and null candidate ratios. Evaluate all 36 prices: `S/M ∈ {0,0.5,1}`, `I/M ∈ {1,10,100,1000}`, logical price `∈ {0,0.1,1}`. Report per-model and per-size sums, wins and losses. Do not pool the evaluation score into training.

Native operation counts include construction, transfer, collection, elimination, and source verification; both candidate and rho are cold single-target costs. Shared-host wall time is diagnostic only. The reference is source negation rho, and the result is not a leaderboard claim. Run all targets even after a failure, recording the exit status and partial output; never replace an absent record with a zero cost.
