# N12 output dictionary

`PROTOCOL.md` fixes the model split, policies, target derivation and selection rule. `results/selection.json` records all seven training-policy scores and the exact input-file hashes. `results/training-price-grid.csv` contains 7 policies × 36 price cells. A failed policy has an empty candidate cost and ratio; its verified and failure counts remain explicit. The five inherited policy records and their artifact manifest live under `research/factor_base_replication_20261024/results/raw/`. The two new `train-fb{2,4}-both-j16-bits{16,...,40}.jsonl` files contain 120 training targets each.

The evaluation files `results/raw/evaluation-bits*.jsonl` contain 60 records each. Each record carries the public curve ICV1 identity, source workload, derived target and algorithm seeds, candidate counts and phase costs, a same-process source-negation-rho result, recovered scalars, source verification flags, and explicit exhaustion status. `results/evaluation-targets.jsonl` and `.csv` are standardized one-row-per-target views of the 420 raw records at the representative price. A failed candidate has a null `candidate_GAE` and `candidate_over_rho`; a failed rho has a null `rho_GAE` and ratio. The raw record is authoritative.

`results/evaluation-price-grid.csv` has 36 prices for each of 15 scopes: all 420 targets, seven 60-target sizes, and seven distinct 60-target models. The ratio is a ratio of candidate and rho **sums**, conditional on complete verified coverage within that scope. Candidate and rho GAE are each the sum of priced cold single-target operation counts. `S_per_M` prices a square in multiplication units; `I_per_M` prices an inversion; `logical_price` prices native logical operations, source field additions and bit operations in source-addition equivalents. The source-addition denominator is the instrumented `source_add` in each raw record. A null cost is empty in CSV and `null` in JSON.

`results/evaluation-summary.json` gives coverage, representative-cell ratios, and counts of winning, losing and tied price cells. `results/validator-report.json` records independent source-curve scalar replays and pricing recomputation. `results/selection.SHA256` binds the selected policy before evaluation. `SHA256SUMS` binds the committed packet. The SVG and PNG are layouts of native results, and the TeX/PDF report states the observation and its scope.
