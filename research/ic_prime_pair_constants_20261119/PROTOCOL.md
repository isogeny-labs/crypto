# Protocol: constants of the prime-field pair-table pipeline (2026-11-19)

Every figure this study produces is **[pcc:unverified]**. Written before the measured
sessions (SHA-256 of this file recorded in README.md). Predictions are not edited after
data; deviations go in README.md.

Scope (owner): generic prime curves, one target charged cold, no multi-target or batch
methods, no weak families. The residual collector (`res2/res4`) is excluded (a collision
search in disguise, `research/ic_planner_exhaustive_20261026`). Relabelling or accounting
changes are not improvements.

## Question

`research/ic_fb_size_model_20261119` fits GAE = a|F|^2 + b N/(4|F|) (a = 0.98, b = 1.2) for
`prime-abscissa` + `mitm-prime-blocked:m=2` + `targets=walk` + `incremental-gauss`, optimum
|F| ~ N^(1/3)/2, distance to rho 14.6x (p24, |F| = 128), 24.4x (p28, |F| = 256). At the
optimum cost ~ a^(1/3) b^(2/3). Does any existing variant of the same pipeline lower a or b
at the optimal |F|, measurably, across the section 5a band?

## Step 2: measurement

Curves: `research/ic_fb_size_model_20261119/specs` (p24 r = 16,782,299, p28 r = 268,407,199).
|F| = 128 (p24), 256 (p28). Same ecbench binary `target/release/ecbench`, RAYON_NUM_THREADS=1,
`--allow-busy`, isolation L2 or better recorded per record. 8 targets x 3 measured rounds
(+1 warm-up), **target_seed = 20261120** (not the 20261031 of the size-model study, because
a 1-target smoke test of every arm ran on that seed first: see "Smoke test"). Arms
(specs/p24.json, p28.json, generated by analysis/gen_specs.py):

`rho` (rho.negation, reference); `inc` (incumbent) and `inc_aa` (A/A, identical params);
`o_mitm_nf`, `o_mitm_unf`, `o_batch`, `o_rfmitm`, `o_rfsub`, `o_sub` (oracle axis);
`t_dg`, `t_stride`, `t_srenew`, `t_pfast`, `t_pb16`, `t_random` (targets axis, oracle
blocked); `dg_leader` (mitm:negation_folded=1 + stride-renew-deferred-guard, the arm of
`ic_dg_blocked`); `l_echelon`, `l_fullrank` (linalg); `k3_a`, `k3_b`, `k3_c`
(`ksum:m=3`, |F| = 24/32/48 at p24 and 48/64/96 at p28).

Analysis (analysis/analyse.py): for each arm, `ecbench compare --a inc --b ARM --weight w`
(w = 0, 0.1, 1; 4,000 resamples, seed 20261002) for the paired ratio and interval;
`ecbench band` for S and rho; distance to rho = mean S / mean S of rho.negation at each w;
per-phase GAE from the records: `a_eff = oracle_setup.gae / |F|^2` and
`b_eff = relations.gae / (N / (4|F|))`, which say which constant a variant moved.
`ecbench verify --dir D --replay 12` on each session.

### Decision rule

An arm is an **improvement** over `inc` on a curve only if the upper end of the 95% paired
ratio interval is below 1 at w = 0 AND the order holds across the band (ratio interval
below 1 at w = 0.1 and 1 too; AGENTS.md section 5a). An improvement needs both curves to
count as a pipeline constant improvement; one curve is reported as curve-specific. An arm
whose records equal `inc` record for record in charged work is a **tie by construction**
(ratio exactly 1), not an improvement. Distance to rho is reported for the best arm at
each w; it is not a pass/fail gate.

## Predictions (falsifiable; frozen)

Mechanism notes: table term a|F|^2 comes from `oracle_setup` (one add per folded pair);
trial term from `relations` (one add per trial under `walk`).

- **P1 (layout is free).** `o_batch`, `o_mitm_nf`, `o_rfmitm`: per-record charged work
  identical to `inc` on every record (ratio 1.0000, interval [1,1]) at w = 0; all three
  weights tie. They change neither a nor b.
- **P2 (unfolded table doubles a).** `o_mitm_unf` / `inc` at w = 0 is in [1.15, 1.60] on
  p24 and [1.05, 1.40] on p28, interval above 1 on both, `a_eff` between 1.7 and 2.3x
  `inc`'s, `b_eff` unchanged within 2%. (Derived from the table share of the fitted
  model, then widened after the smoke test; see below.)
- **P3 (no table loses badly).** `o_rfsub` and `o_sub`: ratio > 20 at w = 0 on both curves
  (b term multiplied by about |F|, a removed). They move b up, not down.
- **P4 (random targets).** `t_random` ratio > 20 at w = 0 on both curves (two scalar
  multiplications a trial).
- **P5 (no targets variant is an improvement).** None of `t_dg`, `t_stride`, `t_srenew`,
  `t_pfast`, `t_pb16`, `dg_leader` meets the decision rule on both curves. `t_pfast` is a
  tie by construction at w = 0 (identical charged work to `inc`). `t_dg` has its upper
  interval end above 1 at w = 0 on at least one curve (its earlier gain was measured
  against `walk` at planner sizes 130..741 on different curves, not at the optimum).
- **P6 (linalg).** `l_echelon`: ratio 1.0000 at w = 0, within 0.5% of 1 at w = 0.1 and 1
  (same pins and answer; row_ops are unpriced at w = 0). `l_fullrank` ratio >= 3 at w = 0
  on both curves (it waits for all columns, so b rises).
- **P7 (A/A).** `inc_aa` / `inc` = 1.0000 [1, 1] at every w on both curves (the counted
  cost is deterministic).
- **P8 (m = 3 moves the exponent, not the constant).** At least one of `k3_a/b/c` has the
  upper interval end below 1 at w = 0 on p28, and none of them is below 1 on p24 by more
  than the p28 gain; the best m = 3 arm's ratio at w = 0 on p28 is in [0.45, 0.85]
  (cost ~ N^(1/2) rather than N^(1/2) with a smaller leading constant at the pair size
  optimum N^(1/3)/2 being 1.7x too large for m = 3). On p24 the best m = 3 ratio is in
  [0.70, 1.20].
- **P9 (no m = 2 constant squeeze).** No arm with `m = 2` pair structure (everything
  except `k3_*`) satisfies the decision rule on both curves. The honest result of the
  squeeze is then: a and b of the m = 2 pipeline cannot be lowered by any existing variant,
  and the best arm's distance to rho stays at the size-model study's 14.6x / 24.4x
  (w = 0), within +-15%.
- **P10 (distance of the best arm).** If a `k3_*` arm satisfies the rule on p28, its
  distance to rho at w = 0 is in [10x, 20x] (rho S measured ~0.88 at 28 bits), below
  24.4x; it is not below 1x at any w.

### Falsifiers, stated plainly

P1 fails if any of the three differs from `inc` in S at w = 0. P9 fails if any m = 2 arm
meets the decision rule on both curves. P8/P10 fail if no m = 3 arm is below 1 on p28 or
the distance is outside the range.

## Smoke test (disclosed before data)

Before this protocol was written, `ecbench run` was executed once on each curve with 1
target and 1 round (+warm-up) for every arm, at target_seed = 20261031, only to check that
each arm string runs and verifies. Those 38 records were read (band table) and are not part
of the result; they informed the widened ranges in P2, P8, P10. The measured sessions use
target_seed = 20261120, so no measured target was seen. The smoke records are not kept.
