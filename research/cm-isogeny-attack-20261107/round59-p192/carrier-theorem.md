# Explicit global signed-carrier criterion

Derived specialization, 2026-11-08. This is a written argument with exact arithmetic certificates, not a Lean proof or a trusted scientific verdict. The translation/intersection method belongs to the established theory of zero estimates; no novelty of that method is claimed. [pcc:unverified]

Let E/F_p be an ordinary elliptic curve with p>3. Work over an algebraic closure. Assume End(E)=Z[pi], with pi^2-t*pi+p=0 and D=t^2-4p<0. Let G,Q be nonzero points in the same subgroup of prime order r!=p, and let Gamma=<(G,Q)> in E x E. Thus both coordinate projections of Gamma have order r. Put X=x(P),Y=x(R),Z=y(P)y(R), and give X,Y weight1 and Z weight3. Every signed polynomial reduces to F(X,Y)+Z*H(X,Y), using Z^2=f(X)f(Y). A reduced polynomial is required to be nonzero as a rational function on E x E. In this model, a nonzero pair of coefficient polynomials really is nonzero: f(X)f(Y) is not a square in the rational function field, so1,Z are linearly independent. [pcc:unverified]

Criterion. For an integer d>=1 put s=ceil((r-1)/(4d)). Assume s(s-1)>8d^2(r-1), 4d^2<r, and16d^2<|D|. If a nonzero reduced signed polynomial of weighted degree at most d vanishes on every point of Gamma other than the identity, then there exist integers a,u satisfying1<=a<=2d, 0<|u|<=2d and[a]Q=[u]G. In addition there is an integer1<=b<=2d with u^2=ab. Therefore absence of the broader bounded point relations excludes every polynomial of the stated global kind. [pcc:unverified]

1. Divisor budget. On E x E let F1={O} x E,F2=E x {O}, and H0=F1+F2. We have F1^2=F2^2=0,F1.F2=1,H0^2=2. X,Y have pole divisors2F1,2F2; Z has pole divisor3F1+3F2. Every allowed monomial has poles bounded by2d*H0. If f is the nonzero rational function represented by the signed polynomial, the effective divisor D0=div(f)+2d*H0 contains the r-1 nonzero Gamma points. Its numerical class is2d*H0, so D0^2=8d^2 and D0.H0=4d. [pcc:unverified]

2. A component with many points. Every irreducible curve C on an abelian surface has C^2>=0: translate C by a generic point so it has no common component with itself, use translation invariance of numerical classes, and count intersections. Intersections of distinct irreducible effective curves are also nonnegative. Consequently each component C_i of D0 has C_i^2<=D0^2=8d^2. Since H0 is ample and C_i.H0 is a positive integer, the number of distinct components is at most4d, even when D0 has multiplicities. Some component C contains at least s distinct nonzero Gamma points; write S for that subset. [pcc:unverified]

3. Translation stabilizer. The identity sum_(0!=gamma in Gamma)|S intersect(S+gamma)|=|S|(|S|-1) counts ordered pairs of distinct points. If C and C+gamma are distinct irreducible curves, the cardinality of their intersection is at most C^2. The strict incidence inequality therefore forces C=C+gamma for some nonzero gamma in Gamma. Prime order makes gamma a generator, so C is stable under every element of Gamma. It contains a point of Gamma, hence contains all of Gamma and in particularO. This argument uses only distinct geometric points; multiplicities in the intersection can only increase its upper bound. [pcc:unverified]

4. Genus and group structure. Translation by Gamma lifts to the normalization C_tilde. The action is free: a fixed point upstairs would map to a fixed point of a nonzero ambient translation, which cannot exist. Since r!=p, the quotient is a finite etale degree-r cover. Riemann-Hurwitz gives g(C_tilde)-1=r*(g(C_tilde/Gamma)-1). Adjunction on E x E, whose canonical bundle is trivial, gives g(C_tilde)<=p_a(C)=1+C^2/2<=1+4d^2. Since4d^2<r, the only possible multiple of r for g-1 in the interval[-1,4d^2] is0. Thus g=1. Choose a point of C_tilde over the ambient identity. Its map to E x E sends that origin toO and is a homomorphism by rigidity. Its image C is an elliptic subgroup. In particular its coordinate projections alpha,beta are isogenies: both have nonconstant image because Gamma projects to r distinct points. [pcc:unverified]

5. Projection and CM degrees. The two projection degrees a,b are bounded by D0.F1=D0.F2=2d, since C is a component of the effective divisor. Set theta=beta*dual(alpha). The dual identities give theta(G)=[a]Q and deg(theta)=ab. Write theta=u+v*pi. Then4ab=(2u+t*v)^2+|D|*v^2. The bound16d^2<|D| forces v=0. Hence u^2=ab and|u|<=2d; u is nonzero because both projections are nonconstant. This proves the criterion. As a<r, a is invertible modulo r and a detected compatible pair would recover the scalar u/a modulo r, followed by point verification. The point equation is necessary; arbitrary compatible triples are not asserted to realize an embedded elliptic subgroup. [pcc:unverified]

Finite charts. Suppose finitely many nonzero signed polynomial equations, in these same coordinates after clearing chart denominators, cover all the nonzero Gamma points. Their product is a nonzero rational function, since the ambient function field is an integral domain. Reduction by Z^2=f(X)f(Y) preserves the weighted-degree upper bound. Its weight is at most the sum of the individual weights. Thus the criterion also applies to a finite chart collection whose total numerator weight is at most d. Any chart-domain guards must really cover every point in the claim. This does not cover an equation that is only correct on a selected baby subset. [pcc:unverified]

Order-five certificate. Verify mu!=1 and mu^5=1 mod r. If1<=a<=B,0<|u|<=B and u=a*mu mod r, then r divides Phi5(u,a)=u^4+u^3*a+u^2*a^2+u*a^3+a^4. This integer is positive: for u!=a it is(u^5-a^5)/(u-a), and the fifth-power function is strictly increasing on the real line; for u=a it is5a^4. It is at most5B^4 in absolute value. With B=2^45 the native certificate checks5B^4<r, which rules out divisibility. No enumeration of that large box is needed. [pcc:unverified]

Construction boundary. This is a degree constraint on global polynomial equations and total finite-chart weight. A function of high degree may have a short arithmetic circuit: X^p-X and the Frobenius normalizer are concrete reminders. Universal rational-point equations also contain no target-specific mark. The next useful representation must retain the target-specific signed point action, use a compressed high-degree norm or another representation outside this bound, and account for the cost of building it from public G,Q.
