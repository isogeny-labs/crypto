# Labeled Mobius representatives and scalar recovery

Derived and natively qualified interface, 2026-11-08. Let E/F_p have prime rational group<G> of order r>5 and let Q=[k]G. Point encodings are ordinary subgroup points together with a separate projective infinity tag. Finite scalar0 is the group identity and is distinct from that tag. The norm primitive M_W(G) from Round60 computes[1/(1-z)]G when W=[z]G is in its domain. Every target-dependent constructor and action is charged. This is a written algebraic proof with producer checks, not a trusted novelty or performance verdict. [pcc:unverified]

Scalar-line action. Define S(z)=1-z and T(z)=1/(1-z). Matrices S=[[-1,1],[0,1]], T=[[0,1],[-1,1]] satisfy S^2=T^3=I and STS=T^-1 in PGL2(F_r). Their six transforms are z,T(z),T^2(z),1-z,1-T(z),1-T^2(z). A candidate obtains the first three points through at most two complete transverse norm calls and the remaining three through public point subtraction. If T(z)=z, the second call is reused. Public known cases0,1,infinity,-1,2,1/2 are handled directly. In particular T cycles0->1->infinity->0 and-1->1/2->2->-1. [pcc:unverified]

Representative and transporter. Normalize each invertible matrix by dividing all entries by its first nonzero entry. This gives a unique representative of its projective class. Among the six transformed point encodings choose the lexicographically smallest; break a tie by the normalized transporter matrix. Return both the point and that matrix H. A complete state carries M with current scalar f_M(k)=(a*k+b)/(c*k+d). After a public affine move L and canonicalization H, update M<-H*L*M. The candidate never looks up the scalar of its current point. Known-label scalar arithmetic is a separate reference backend only. [pcc:unverified]

Allowed moves. The frozen walk uses z->a*z+b with(a,b) among(1,1),(2,3),(3,5),(5,7), selected by the declared FNV1a point-encoding rule and restart index. These are affine transformations over F_r, so the rational degree remains1. Adding the original target scalar k to a generic Mobius state would instead produce(c*k^2+(a+d)*k+b)/(c*k+d), raising the degree. Such a move is excluded from this implementation. [pcc:unverified]

Collision equation. If two finite equal point encodings have transporters M1,M2, cross multiplication gives A*k^2+B*k+C=0, where A=a1*c2-a2*c1, B=a1*d2+b1*c2-a2*d1-b2*c1 and C=b1*d2-b2*d1. If the matrices are proportional this polynomial is identically zero and carries no scalar information. Conversely, invertible2x2 matrices representing the same rational function are projectively proportional, so a nonproportional pair yields a nonzero polynomial of degree at most2. Solve it in F_r and verify every candidate against the original public point Q=[k]G. Prime group order makes the accepted scalar unique. A projective pole gives the linear equation c*k+d=0 and is handled separately. [pcc:unverified]

Exceptional orbits. The orbits{0,1,infinity} and{-1,2,1/2} each have size3. A remaining short orbit exists exactly when z^2-z+1 has roots in F_r; its two roots form one orbit of size2, each fixed by T. Substituting f_M(k) into that polynomial yields coefficients a^2-a*c+c^2,2a*b-a*d-b*c+2c*d,b^2-b*d+d^2, hence a quadratic scalar equation. Other orbits have size6. One can see this by listing fixed points of the three involutions and the two elements of order3; those are exactly the two three-element orbits and the possible two-element orbit already listed. [pcc:unverified]

Why an orbit identity alone is insufficient. On a free orbit there is exactly one group element sending a given input to the chosen representative. Therefore H(Sz)*S=H(z) and H(Tz)*T=H(z) as projective matrices. Comparing two deliberately reordered descriptions of that same orbit gives identical full transporters and a zero collision polynomial. The native checker verifies this816 times across the five panels. The useful collisions arise only when the accumulated affine walk transformations give a nonproportional pair at the same encoded point. The sixfold orbit identification does not by itself supply a scalar equation. [pcc:unverified]

Frozen bounded walk. Start from Q+[offset]G for offsets0,1,3,7, with the corresponding affine transporter. At each stage canonicalize, check known-value or stabilizer equations, then check prior representatives. A tautological collision terminates that attempt and consumes the next frozen restart. Each attempt has at most r+2 stages and the total restart cap is4. The finite quotient guarantees a repeated representative within its cardinality if no earlier equation is used, but it does not guarantee that a repeated pair is nonproportional. All fruitless attempts are retained. No random-mixing or birthday-law conclusion follows from these bounded runs. [pcc:unverified]

Costs. A generic canonicalizer calls the norm primitive on the current point and its T-image. Those source targets change with the state. The candidate builds a fresh Q-graph for each actual norm call and does not reuse the reference's full action table. The direct norm is linear in r after explicitly generated inputs, so both constructors and evaluations remain material. The retained receipts count all1105 norm calls and195723 factor terms for exhaustive orbit qualification plus walks. The target-independent G graphs, public exceptional tests, point arithmetic, root solving and verification are additional work. No timing speedup is claimed. [pcc:unverified]

Primary-curve residue class. The native full-width verifier checks P-192's scalar order r=2mod3 and that-3 is a nonsquare in F_r. Thus its scalar line has no order-three short orbit. Its S3 partition has two orbits of size3 and(r-5)/6 of size6, giving(r+7)/6 orbits in total. The initial three controls all had order-three stabilizers, so amendment1 adds the F11/r17 twist control and amendment2 adds the first eligible a=-3 curve over F211 with prime order2mod3. That selection stops at b5,r197 and never examines recovery outcomes. The three genuine collisions in this second amended panel exercise recovery without the order-three shortcut. [pcc:unverified]

Full-width boundary. The P-192 program checks eight public affine compositions on the existing unknown target using independent affine/Jacobian point operations. Its twelve nonproportional linear, quadratic and pole collision controls are manufactured using the declared labels17,31,mu,lambda; they test root and point verification, not unknown-target discovery. No full P-192 norm or Mobius canonicalization is supplied by those controls. The primitive to be compressed is still the graph-dependent first-variation norm. [pcc:unverified]
