# Fixed-anchor budget

Across 41 alternating measured pairs per target the median original/full-baseline and group-additions-only times were 499500/62750 ns (48 calls) and 896708/521125 ns (200 calls). With the registered walk retained, deleting every theta operation, every check, and all precomputation yielded ratios of 7.96 and 1.72 respectively. The measured additions would still need to become at least 1.26 and 5.81 times faster to fit one tenth of the baseline times.

Exact baseline (base, subgroup) REDCs were (111654,610) and (204510,1190), versus (16456,0) and (138332,0) for the additions. Equal-price ratios are 6.82 and 1.49; reaching ten with unchanged additions requires a subgroup/base REDC price ratio of at least 86.73 and 990.60 respectively. These comparisons pertain to the fixed addition chains and current group implementation.
