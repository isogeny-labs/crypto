## The arity error

Every accepted theorem and completed measurement in
[`prime_factor_base_degree_20261031`](../prime_factor_base_degree_20261031/README.md)
is a **two-summand** result. The split-graph theorem, its Lean lemmas, the exact
toy audit and the F4 ladder establish presentation-specific facts at `m=2`.
They do not establish a competitive point-decomposition oracle at `m=3` or
higher.

Let the factor-base scale be

\[
  s = \frac{\log_2 p}{m}, \qquad |V|\asymp 2^s,
\]

and suppose one point-decomposition query costs `2^(e s)` up to polynomial
factors. Collecting `Theta(|V|)` relations then costs

\[
  2^{(e+1)s}=p^{(e+1)/m}
\]

before polynomial factors. Beating rho's `p^(1/2)` exponent therefore requires

\[
  e < \frac m2-1.                                             \tag{1}
\]

At `m=2`, (1) requires `e<0`, which is impossible for a nontrivial query.
The existing exact pair-enumeration/cost-bridge reference is already optimal
at that arity up to polynomial factors. A lower homogeneous or handled F4
degree at `m=2` remains a valid component result, but cannot change the ECDLP
exponent. The first structurally relevant fixed arity is `m=3`, where the
necessary gate is `e<0.5`.

## Solver-model correction

“Boolean SAT over a prime field” means a **hybrid CDCL(T)** architecture here:

- the Boolean layer owns the one-hot summand selectors, exactly-one clauses,
  disjoint-window constraints and learned Boolean clauses;
- an `F_p` theory propagator owns field addition, multiplication, inversion
  side conditions and the chained group-law constraints;
- the two implementation candidates are the existing CaDiCaL external-
  propagator loop, after binding its exact source revision, and cvc5 `QF_FF`;
- a pure CNF bit-blast is an encoding-cost control, not the theorem-bearing
  candidate. The quotient-ring identity `z^2-z=0` avoids arithmetic carries in
  `F_p[z]/(z^2-z)`, but a pure Boolean circuit still pays
  `Theta(log^2 p)` gates for a general field multiplication.

Calling the candidate merely “CaDiCaL CNF” or “ordinary CDCL” is incorrect.
Every field-theory call, explanation clause and independent replay belongs to
the complete counted interval.

At correction base `c5d13654`, current `main` exposes the native Rust callback
`src/cryptanalysis/sat.rs::solve_with_lazy_clauses`; it does not expose a
source-identified CaDiCaL `ExternalPropagator` implementation. That native
callback is a useful contract/control, but it must not be relabelled as the
reviewer's CaDiCaL loop. The exact external-loop revision remains a freeze

## Retained algebra and clauses

The arity correction does not discard the useful local results.

1. **Quadratic rational lift.** For a nonexceptional displacement `t`, the
   lift equation

   \[
      (2t\lambda-s_0)^2=f(x)
   \]

   is quadratic in `lambda` and has discriminant

   \[
      (-4ts_0)^2-4(4t^2)(s_0^2-f(x))=16t^2f(x).
   \]

   Thus, when `t` is nonzero, the lambda lift exists over `F_p` exactly when
   `x` has a rational curve lift. The `t=0` chart is kept as an explicit
   exceptional branch; it is not divided away.

2. **Three-summand rationality.** Put the three selected abscissae in disjoint
   windows. Frobenius fixes a rational lift and negates a quadratic-twist lift.
   For a rational non-2-torsion target, the nonrational subset would have to
   sum to rational 2-torsion. One nonrational summand cannot do so; two would
   be inverses and repeat an abscissa, excluded by disjoint windows; all three
   would make the target 2-torsion. Hence every accepted three-summand model is
   rational without an extra rationality circuit under these stated guards.

3. **Higher-arity rationality.** At arity at least four the preceding case
   split is no longer automatic. Retain the finite-field rationality circuits
   and charge their variables, theory work and explanations in full.

4. **Forbidden words.** Any binary field/index word outside its declared
   domain receives the single clause forbidding exactly that word. These
   clauses are semantic constraints and remain present in the candidate,
   null, planted and A/A arms.

These are algebraic/semantic ingredients, not an arity-three solver bound.

## Correct experiment and path to 40--80-bit fields

Fix `m=3`. Use three disjoint one-hot windows, chained `S3` constraints through
the quadratic lambda lift, and **no expanded `S4` polynomial**. The
preregistered exponent ladder is every integer `s=6,...,14`; a continuation
ladder may extend through `s=27`, corresponding approximately to 40--80-bit
prime fields (`3s=39,...,81`). At 80 bits rho is about `2^40` group operations,
so this program measures the PDP exponent; it is not expected to win a
wall-clock race against rho.

For each `s`, retain natural, planted and exact-UNSAT targets, a byte-identical
A/A arm, a curve-free support-matched null, and canonical exact enumeration.
Enumeration supplies the correctness oracle and the `e=2` reference for an
arity-three two-window scan. Fit `log2(complete counted work)` directly against
`s`; do not normalize the response by candidate-space size. The preregistered
advance condition is an upper corrected 95% interval below `0.5`. A finite
slope is only evidence about the frozen range and bundle, never a proof of an
asymptotic ECDLP speedup.

No scientific run is authorized by this correction. The generator, exact
external-propagator source, solver hashes, explanation checker, analysis code,
power calculation and runtime/memory preflight remain freeze blockers.
