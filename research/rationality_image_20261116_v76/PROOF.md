# Higher rationality-label image invariants

Factor all X roots from monic H; zero is rational torsion. In K[X]/H with H0 nonzero, X^-1=(H1+H2X+...+Hm X^(m-1))/H0. Put t=X+A+sqrt(B)/X and Lambda=sum_(i=0)^(n-1) t^(2^i). Compute its multiplication-operator characteristic polynomial C(Y). If H splits over K, the eigenvalues are the rationality labels lambda(x), including root multiplicity. Each label is zero or one, so C(Y)=Y^(m-k)(Y+1)^k where k is the number of anti-rational roots counted with multiplicity.

For degree at most five, the elementary coefficients at indices 1,2,4 are binomial(k,1), binomial(k,2), binomial(k,4) modulo two. By the binary digit identity these recover k exactly; all three zero iff k=0. Scalar parity detects only the first bit. Nilpotent Jordan parts from repeated roots do not change the characteristic polynomial. This is exact only under the stated split-field precondition; on a generic nonsplit H, nonzero coefficients are necessary rejections, while three zeros do not alone prove rationality or source support.

The actual main-target theorem from C74 permits these necessary constraints on valid original source norms, not arbitrary polynomial roots or other targets with torsion ghosts. Four constructed point fixtures from other targets include ghost controls that must remain classified outside that promotion. Construction cost includes polynomial inversion, Frobenius sums and characteristic coefficients. Stronger rejection without a cheaper whole-search mechanism is not a speedup. Construction currently uses explicit small-matrix determinants and is intended as a falsifiable probe, not a promoted implementation.
