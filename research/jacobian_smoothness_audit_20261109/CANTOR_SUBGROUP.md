# A cyclic smoothness counterexample in the complete Cantor model

For y²=x⁵−x over F3, Lean now proves the actual Cantor arithmetic on the
complete eight-element reduced-Mumford polynomial-pair model. The proper
cyclic subgroup generated by (u,v)=(X,0) has order two and is entirely
smooth. Its smooth fraction is 1, while the ambient fraction is 7/8.
Thus universal finite cyclic-subgroup smoothness equality is false.
[pcc:unverified]

This is a statement about the complete finite arithmetic model. The formal
identification with the geometric Picard/Jacobian group remains open.
The example has fixed order two over an odd field; it does not refute
MMT's binary large-prime heuristic, typical-subgroup behavior, Gaudry's
fixed-genus limit or a scaling-family assertion. [pcc:unverified]

## Actual arithmetic and completeness

The earlier proof already ranges over every actual polynomial pair with
u monic, degree u≤2, degree v<degree u and u dividing v²−f. Its complete
coefficient classification gives eight pairs. The new module proves every
v is zero and factors f as X(X−1)(X+1)(X²+1), with monic pairwise coprime
factors and explicit Bezout certificates. [pcc:unverified]

For each of the 64 input pairs, Lean proves the actual normalized gcd d,
the exact composition equation d²w=u₁u₂, monicity and w dividing f. The
full gcd with v₁+v₂ equals this d; three-input Bezout witnesses give zero
for the standard composition v numerator. If degree w≤2 the output u
is w. Otherwise the exact reduction equation is w·u=f. All output pairs
satisfy the original reduced-pair conditions. [pcc:unverified]

Generic monic-product degree and complementary-factor identities reduce
the branch selection to a closed Boolean certificate, proved with kernel
`decide`. Polynomial gcd/division is not trusted through native evaluation.
The output is proved unique. A group transported through an explicit
bijection with (Z/2Z)³ is then proved equivalent to this Cantor relation
for every pair and every proposed output. Group transport alone would not
establish the arithmetic. [pcc:unverified]

The subgroup carrier is exactly identity or (X,0). Lean proves closure,
equality with `AddSubgroup.zmultiples` of that generator, properness,
generator coordinates, exact order/cardinality two, and the fractions.
`universal_finite_cyclic_equality_false` directly disproves the assertion
that every cyclic subgroup has the ambient smoothness fraction in this
arithmetic model. [pcc:unverified]
