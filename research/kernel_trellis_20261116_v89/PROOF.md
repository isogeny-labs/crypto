# Exact finite-kernel path contract

Let phi:G->G' be a homomorphism of finite abelian groups and K its full rational kernel, explicitly enumerated with identity first and exact addition table. Source domain R is an explicitly supplied finite point list. Every image fiber in R has a chosen base b_i and the actual allowed subset A_i={k in K:b_i+k in R}; partial fibers are never completed by assumption. Image joins enumerate every ordered image tuple whose sum is phi(Q), including image infinity.

For that tuple put B_j=sum_{i<=j}b_i, and d=Q-B_m. Then d is in K and original point lifts are in bijection with label paths k_i in A_i whose cumulative state s_m=d. A finite original prefix j is equivalent to B_j+s_j != O. The backwards recurrence c_m(s)=[s=d], c_j(s)=sum_{k in A_{j+1},s+k not forbidden[j+1]}c_{j+1}(s+k) is exact by disjoint first transitions. DFS follows precisely transitions with nonzero suffix counts. This proves both coverage and exclusion, even for zero base prefixes, empty/partial fibers, torsion and noncyclic kernels. Intermediate points are restored from cached B_j+s_j; no image-prefix pole is grounds for rejection.

Setup per image tuple costs O(m|K|) original group additions and O(m|K|^2) small-state operations, storage O(m|K|) plus cached points. Enumeration costs O(mL) for L valid point-lift paths, plus full root serialization and verification. This is output sensitive in L, not necessarily in distinct x-roots. Deduplicate full source/intermediate x-vectors. Actual-width integration retains the certified C88 odd-order cyclic3 preconditions; generic curve tests exercise multiplication homomorphisms [2] and [3] on the rational group instead.

For the independently checked curve tests, every admitted x has all its rational y-lifts in R and R is negation closed. The finite roots of S3(x(P),x(Q),z) are exactly finite x(P+Q), x(P-Q) on a smooth elliptic curve. This follows from the addition formula (binary and odd), with repeated-x limits via doubling; a rational two-torsion double gives no finite branch, not arbitrary z. Thus induction along the finite S3 chain gives all intermediates rational and permits consistent source signs. Global negation makes the total Q instead of -Q without changing any x-vector. No odd group-order assumption is needed for this restricted all-rational-source-domain contract. It says nothing about source x-values having only nonrational lifts or unrestricted field-domain roots. Tiny literal polynomial enumeration, which does not call group operations, tests this claim including characteristic3, even binary extensions and repeated sources.

No mathematical novelty claim: backward state dynamic programming and kernel lifting are established techniques.
