# Open discriminating problems after the quartic twist24 panel

22 October 2026. These are implementation and evidence gaps, not proofs that an index-calculus improvement cannot exist. The [results report](QUARTIC_TWIST24_REPORT.md) and [frozen protocol](QUARTIC_TWIST_LINE_PROTOCOL.md) preserve the completed experiments.

## Polynomial relation-finder cost for the two invariant lines

**Observed:** The native four-factor meet-in-the-middle oracle independently replayed 69,045 residual queries and measured a larger hit yield for `x∈Fp ∪ t²Fp`. It uses an explicit signed-pair table whose cost grows with the physical base. The completed registered S5 pilot ran `gaudry_quartic::SymmetrisedS5` and `solve_s5_subspace` on a hit and miss at each of `p=37,73,97`; all six results matched the exact group oracle and each solve cost roughly `1.16–1.21×10¹² Fp M`. These were two queries per curve, not the full relation streams. A group oracle's hit rate is not a polynomial solver's work per relation.

**Blocker:** No polynomial `S5` formulation for the tagged line-0/line-2 union, full single-target S5 relation collection, or comparison to the same computation in msolve/Magma has been measured on this canonical panel. Without these, the effect of the 24 action on polynomial-system degree, solve count, duplicate roots and whole-method cost remains unknown.

**Exit criteria:** Derive `S5` for the exact `E/Fp⁴` model with factor variables restricted to line 0 or tagged line 0/2; prove the substitution corresponds to the group equation; cross-check every returned decomposition against the signed group oracle on a frozen full run and retain extraneous/censored roots. Run both systems on identical residuals, use the same one-target stopping rule and recorded source hashes, and compare the native solver with msolve on identical exported systems and host (Magma if available; otherwise name its absence). Record field operations, Gröbner reductions, memory, failures, and full cold `S` with the matched 24-fold rho. A positive result would establish a concrete polynomial relation-finding improvement on the tested panel only; larger-size scaling would still be open.

**Next bounded test:** On `p=37,73,97` and seeds `0,1,2`, replay the entire registered residual stream rather than the completed six-query sample, with an explicit per-instance time and memory budget. Expand to all seven curves only after the first three complete without a correctness mismatch. A solver timeout is retained as a censored result, not converted into a proof of infeasibility.

## Whole-method pricing and larger invariant bases

**Observed:** The mixed base raises same-target hit yield on all seven curves. Counted `Fp` multiplications favor it across all three seeds at `p=229,241`, but row operations increase everywhere. The two-dimensional invariant-space census tested every candidate on all eight curves and found `72–56,376` physical abscissae, including 72 at the one-dimensional empty-line control `p=13`. A bounded p=13 exact-oracle IC pilot recovered `[271]G` in seven queries with 4,041 decompositions and rank seven; an independent hash oracle replayed every query. A full p=37 exact-oracle pilot on all 624 2D points recovered `[271]G` after 53 residuals, 2,833,126 signed decompositions and rank 53. Its independent point-keyed oracle enumerated all 1,873,633 group logs, replayed 53 hit counts and selected rows, and verified the same rank and answer; A/A matched. The p=37 relation stream alone cost 4.00 billion `Fp M` and 41.3 million group operations. The tagged ordered, hash and direct-posting tables preserved complete relation sets on the same seven p=13 queries and three p=37 full-2D queries. On p=37, one addition per 12-action orbit reduced setup from 388,752 to 32,396 group additions and from 37.7 million to 7.80 million `Fp M`; the direct variant retained 47.7 MB against 91.0 MB allocator-requested payload. All six A/A diffs are empty and every posting passed an independent group-addition check. This is a measured **component setup and memory** improvement, not an isolated whole-method win. At p=337 the full signed pair-table construction would require 3,178,197,000 entries before hash collisions, so larger bases have a separate resource problem. The main 42-run suite records setup/rho group additions but not per-phase IC-stream group additions, hash probes, table allocations or an admitted 0/0.1/1 GAE band. All wall times are shared-host L0.

**Blocker:** These missing counters prevent a complete cold one-target `S`, a ranked IC-versus-rho result or a leaderboard row under `docs/VALIDATION_GATE.md`. The 2D census and p=13/p=37 exact-oracle solves cannot determine whether `x∈Fp+t²Fp` or a differently scaled invariant subspace gives a better total *polynomial* IC cost at larger `p`.

**Exit criteria:** Build an isolated single-arm producer that emits exclusive setup, pair-table, relation, matrix, target-check and rho counters including group operations, hash/table work and memory; charge the 0/0.1/1 GAE band on both IC and matched 24-fold rho. Run A/A and interleaved ABAB at least five rounds through `tools/isolated_bench.py` with one and many threads, independently replay every target, and compare a frozen baseline on the same curve and point. Promote a row only after a `VALIDATION.json` cites replay, validator and strongest rho reference. The two-dimensional exact liftable size is now known; measure actual table memory and set a cap before relation runs beyond p=13. A gain in one cost projection alone does not meet this exit criterion.

**Next relation-finder implementation:** The registered tagged-index experiment completed the previously proposed p=13 test and extended it to three p=37 residuals. Its next discriminator is an isolated, full one-target producer with the fastest measured table representation, including target-independent setup, full relation collection, modular rank, scalar verification, hash and allocator work, and the strongest same-target 24-fold rho reference. The full two-dimensional polynomial `S5` path remains a separate necessary comparison: an exact group oracle cannot establish the cost of obtaining relations through polynomial solving. Larger bases need a memory-capped construction or an implicit pair representation before their `B(B−1)` expansion is practical.

## Dense same-residual rank extraction and its remaining gap

**Observed:** The first residual alone has 561 p=13 or 53,551 p=37 signed four-factor decompositions. Treating the public generator factor log as known and retaining every rank-raising row solves the same target from **one** residual on both curves: anchored rank 6 or 52, recovered 271, all factor logs verified. The independent point-keyed signed-pair auditor checked the complete first-query counts, selected terms and rank after enumerating all subgroup logs; A/A non-timing diffs are empty. On p=37 the first 115 decompositions already contain 52 independent anchored rows. The earlier one-row-per-query p=37 oracle took 53 queries and 4,048,104,018 counted `Fp M`; the dense anchored path took one query and 119,740,945 counted `Fp M`, a **33.807×** reduction in this counted one-target exact-oracle path. The unanchored first-residual rank is 52 of 53; the fixed-residual row-difference argument proves its one-rank ceiling under the known generator log. This is a measured implementation/row-extraction improvement on the full 2D base, not a claim that the twist alone causes it.

**Blocker:** The exact signed group oracle precomputes quadratic-size pair tables and checks physical group sums directly. The dense result does not show that `S5` or a Gröbner solver can enumerate enough distinct decompositions of one residual cheaply. Its 119.74-million-`Fp M` arithmetic path is still about 343× the previously recorded same-target seed-zero twist24 rho `Fp M` count, before hash/allocation costs or an admitted GAE conversion. The p=37 dense producer also computes an unanchored rank diagnostic, so its shared-host wall time is not an isolated timing of only the proposed anchored algorithm.

**Scaling discriminator:** For `B` signed physical factors and 12-element abscissa orbits, `m=B/12` folded columns and a uniform residual has expected signed four-factor count asymptotic to `(2/3)B⁴/r`. The expectation reaches `m` at `B³≈r/8`, but the explicit pair table then has `Θ(B²)=Θ(r^(2/3))` entries, above constant-automorphism rho's `Θ(r^(1/2))` exponent. Its idealized setup-plus-query work-size projection is `Θ(B²·max(1,r/(8B³)))`. These are occupancy and implementation-model calculations, not rank guarantees or lower bounds for implicit or polynomial relation finders. A genuine scaling improvement therefore needs a cheaper way to find and enumerate rank-useful decompositions; orbit folding alone is a constant-factor change.

**Exit criteria:** Formulate a two-dimensional or tagged-component polynomial system that returns complete signed decompositions of the *first* residual, preserve all roots and extraneous candidates, and independently check every root against the group oracle. Charge polynomial setup, each solve, root filtering, rank selection, hash/allocator work and scalar verification on one target; compare with the strongest same-target 24-fold rho through the required isolated 0/0.1/1 GAE band and external msolve/Magma baselines where available. Test at larger subgroup sizes with a declared memory cap and frozen workload. The counted exact-oracle gain remains a component lead until this path is measured.

## Concrete field proof in Lean

**Observed:** [QuarticTwistSymmetry.lean](QuarticTwistSymmetry.lean) proves the abstract fourth-to-eighth iterate, composed eigenvalue and row covariance implications. The concrete norm and line formulas are handwritten and checked by native enumeration on the registered curves.

**Blocker:** There is no Lean model of `Fp[t]/(t⁴−d)` with the explicit twist isomorphism, norm identity, `ψ⁴=[-1]`, exact stable-line fibers and orbit counts. The conditional lemmas are not presented as a formal proof of those concrete facts.

**Exit criteria:** Define the finite field and curve group in a pinned Lean project; prove irreducibility, the twist map, the norm identity and the stable-line action under the stated `p≡1 mod12` and odd-prime subgroup assumptions; derive the zero-line and 12/24-orbit conclusions; pass the proof gate with no research axioms or `sorry`, and retain an axiom audit receipt. This would formalize the geometry; it would not by itself measure an IC cost.
