## Question and controls

On every one of the same 21 public cases, compare these exact zero-sum
domains, retaining both signs and requiring five different physical
abscissae:

1. Five ordinary L0 factors.
2. Four ordinary factors and one factor in phi(L0).
3. Four ordinary factors and one factor in [2](L0).

Keep the existing action fold and known generator-factor anchor. Image
logs use the already verified scalar action and introduce no new log
unknowns. The primary questions are whether the zero rows span any
nonzero factor-log rank and whether they reach all B-1 unanchored factor
columns. A zero relation has no target coefficient. No target recovery
is attempted or inferred from this diagnostic.

Report the pure and same-size mixed controls separately. Larger mixed
candidate domains, sign copies and projectively identical rows are not
twist gains. A phi/doubling difference in this zero fiber is conditional
on R=O and is not an estimate for a uniform random residual.

## Native exact enumeration without repeating BSGS

Use the already recovered and point-verified factor logs in the frozen
historical reference. Bind its bytes to the existing algebra-v1 workload,
verify every source/image point against its recorded factor-log action,
and keep that oracle input outside every polynomial candidate interface.
Do not rerun the historical collector or its BSGS oracle.

Build all signed ordinary pairs, grouped by sum modulo the exact prime
subgroup order. For the pure domain enumerate all signed ordinary
triples and join with their opposite pair sums. For a mixed domain
enumerate two ordinary factors and one allowed image as the triple,
again joining with ordinary pairs. Reject overlapping IDs and equal
abscissae. Canonicalize the five signed physical factors.

Every pure physical tuple must occur in exactly ten triple/pair
partitions; every mixed tuple in exactly six. Check these multiplicities
before deduplication. Verify every retained relation by actual point
addition, then independently form and reduce its anchored factor-log
row. Count all-zero rows and canonical projective rows explicitly.
Exclude the zero target column from the factor-log RREF while retaining
the known-anchor right-hand side. A full factor-log solution must verify
all source and image factor points; it must never be labelled a newly
recovered target scalar. Preserve the exact signed sets and their hashes.
