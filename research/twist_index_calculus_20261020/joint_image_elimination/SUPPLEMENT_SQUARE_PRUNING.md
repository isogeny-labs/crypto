# Sharpen the pure-zero obstruction before using a pruning rule

Frozen before running this stronger target. Keep the completed
discriminant certificate and its source/receipts unchanged as historical
evidence. Use the same four exact H4 coefficients and the same finite
weighted linear search, now targeting

    S = 64*s4 - (4*s2-s1^2)^2,
    S^3 = sum rational Ai*Ci.

If a representation exists, clear denominators, independently multiply
out the full integer identity, reject a +1 mutation, and state every
characteristic excluded by the actual denominator. If no representation
is found, claim no nonmembership or impossibility. Preserve native Rust,
one thread, busy reservation, 8 GiB and 60 s after separate compilation.

Why this target matters: C3=0 fixes the quartic's linear coefficient.
If also S=0 in characteristic other than two, the monic quartic is the
square of a monic quadratic. Over a field S^3=0 implies S=0. This would
exclude three distinct roots even when a fourth target abscissa repeats
one of them. These structural implications require their own faithful
proof, not just a polynomial identity.

Only after this is verified, audit the proposed prefilter against the
existing frozen physical oracle: when a peeled S4 residual is finite and
its abscissa lies in Fp, it should have no decomposition into three
distinct ordinary factors. **Infinity must be retained**: three-factor
CM triangles may sum to O. Count every tested/rejected system, require
zero rejected physical relations, and use the same phi, doubling and pure
controls on the full 9,658-residual tape. This is a post-run oracle audit,
not a repeated 189-case polynomial collector.

Do not deploy the prefilter from the weaker discriminant certificate.
Do not call a saved negative-system count a speedup. A viable nonzero
pruning result would require a frozen complete collector comparison with
all the original costing, A/A, matched-reference and price gates.
