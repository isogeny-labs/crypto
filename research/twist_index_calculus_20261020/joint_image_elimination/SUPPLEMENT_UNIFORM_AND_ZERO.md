## Counting constraint

Let the ordinary base have L distinct abscissae and a disjoint image have
L distinct abscissae, with one stored representative up to sign for each.
The mixed 3+1 pattern admits exactly 16*binomial(L,3)*L signed physical
tuples. Each has one sum in the order-n subgroup. Consequently the sum of
the signed-tuple counts over all n residuals is that same number for both
phi and doubling, and their uniform-residual means are equal. This
statement concerns signed physical tuples, not projective row counts,
rank, conditional trajectories, success probability or solving cost.

Pure L0 instead has 16*binomial(L,4) signed physical tuples. Its partial
three-plus-source encoding has four presentations of each physical tuple.
The fully symmetric control removes this presentation multiplicity. These
different candidate-domain sizes must not be attributed to a twist.

Prove the finite counting statement for an arbitrary list of candidates
and bounded residual-label function in Lean. Check the full residual
histograms for p=37, all three original seeds, using the separately audited
group/BSGS scalar oracle. Compare original L2, phi, doubling and pure L0.
Retain exact integer mass, support, second moment, histogram and zero fiber.
The mixed-arm masses must equal their combinatorial domain sizes. Such an
oracle census is not a new relation-finding algorithm.

## Zero residual

The zero residual is fixed by the entire group action. Its concentration
can differ despite equal uniform means. It might support cheaper factor-log
precomputation if its relations add independent rows. Endomorphism
identities can also produce many physical tuples with zero folded row.

Audit every zero relation for all 21 original public cases in the same
four arms. Use a complete signed pair oracle, deduplicate physical tuples,
verify each actual group sum, and compute independent RREF. Record zero
rows, projective rows, rank and the number of factor-log unknowns. Every
target column must be zero. Do not claim target recovery from these rows.

An independent-rank gain at zero would motivate an actual invariant
polynomial collector plus charged target descent. A zero-row concentration
alone does not. A null or bounded result does not establish a general
obstruction. Preserve one thread, 8 GiB and 1,200 s per case; no additional
budget cap, target substitution or leaderboard promotion is introduced.
