in SUPPLEMENT_IMPLICIT_AND_FULL_SYMMETRY.md and inverse normal forms in
SUPPLEMENT_INVERSE_REDUCTION.md.

For each mixed implicit representation, use four relation components, its
explicit inverse equation, and V*Delta3-1. This has six variables
(e1,e2,e3,U,W,V). No high-degree membership polynomial is inserted. Retain
and charge rejection of field roots whose ordinary or source abscissae

discriminant. Check the integer discriminant identity against the squared
product of the six root differences, with a plus-one negative control.
Decode all four roots, check membership in L0, and enumerate all sixteen
group sign choices. This retains all four-factor permutation symmetry
while excluding repeated abscissae.
