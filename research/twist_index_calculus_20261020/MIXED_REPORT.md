## Question and executed scope

The earlier [quadratic-twist report](REPORT.md) counted third summation-polynomial
roots at four small fields. It did not test mixed points of `E(F_{p²})`,
matrix rank, a recovered DLP, or complete cost. This follow-up tests those
missing quantities on six ordinary prime-order curves, three factor-base
sizes, and three independent one-target workloads per size. The full relation
census queries **every nonzero point of each base group**: 4,104 target points
per base size and arm, or 49,248 target-arm queries across the panel. The
predeclared three-target experiment has 216 index-calculus solves and 54
same-target rho solves. A later exhaustive cost sweep repeats cold setup
for all 49,248 target-arm cases and 12,312 matched rho targets. Every
recovered scalar in both runs verifies. A final matched-policy control
adds 12,312 rational-only versus mixed target pairs.

The 6 curve orders were recounted directly. Independently, every one of the
5,438,142 extension-field abscissas in the panel was tested by the norm
character. The resulting `#E(F_{p²})` equals `#E(F_p)#T(F_p)` in every case.
All six `#E(F_p)` are prime, so each nonzero rational point is a generator;
all six twist orders are odd. Every selected mixed factor is checked against
direct `F_{p²}` curve arithmetic, its Frobenius trace and anti-trace.

## The exact group obstruction

Write `π` for `p`-Frobenius on `E(F_{p²})`. Since this group has odd order
in the tested panel, division by 2 is unique and

```text
F = A + S,       A = (F + πF)/2 in E(F_p),     πS = -S.
```

The anti-invariant group is the image of the rational quadratic twist under
`φ(x,y)=(x,u y)` with `u²=d`. For a rational target `R`, an **exact**
extension-field relation satisfies

```text
R = Σ F_i    if and only if    R = Σ A_i  and  Σ S_i = O.
```

The forward direction follows by applying the two Frobenius projections;
the reverse direction follows by adding the components. On an odd-order
subgroup this remains valid after projection even if the ambient curve has
2-torsion. Therefore the rational-component relation oracle on the
projected base `{A_i}` admits **at least every** exact mixed relation on the
same signed factors. Twist cancellation is an added constraint. This is a
group-law dominance statement, not a theorem about the runtime of every
possible polynomial representation or solver.

The formal additive numerator identity `2F=(F+πF)+(F−πF)` is sealed as
[`IDC1h1e5ca2ba1c38a562`](twist_projection_identity.certificate.json)
and regenerated by the [native issuer](../../examples/identity_certificate_twist_projection.rs).
The certificate checks its integer coefficient identity over a large prime;
the eigenspace assertion also uses `π²=1`, and the group projection proof
above supplies the elliptic-curve context. The certificate alone does not
prove the group-law dominance theorem.

The experiment makes that distinction concrete. It deliberately chooses
three twist colors with `T₃=T₁+T₂`, which creates exact width-three
anti-component cancellations. The complete signed-color enumeration finds
exactly two zero-sum color vectors in each field, spanning one dimension.
Thus the `mixed-unique` width-three relation matrix has two structural
color constraints; its rank cannot exceed `B-2`. The measured exact rank
equals `B-2` for every size and field. The `mixed-rect` base repeats
`B/3` rational points across three twist colors: it has `B` raw lift
columns but only `B/3` unique traces. Its raw matrix rank is `B-2`,
while the target-relevant quotient matrix reaches rank `B/3`.

## Full-rank failure and target-row span recovery

The [original frozen protocol](MIXED_PROTOCOL.md) required full rank for
every trace column. The first full run showed that `mixed-unique` cannot
meet that rule on any of the 54 workloads. A replayable
[full-rank control](mixed_full_rank_control.ndjson), run from the current
source with `--full-rank-control`, preserves all 54 failures at ranks
`10/12`, `22/24`, or `34/36`. The initial observed failure file is
also retained as [historical raw output](mixed_full_rank_attempt.ndjson);
its exact intermediate source revision was not frozen, so the replayable
control is the evidence for the rank conclusion.

The amended run uses a different valid DLP stopping rule: collect until the
known color-compatible row space has rank `B-2`. When a target row reduces
to zero against that basis, the same reduction gives the target's scalar
without recovering all `B` individual logs. This is explicitly a
**target-row span** solve. It recovered and independently checked all 54
`mixed-unique` targets. The other three arms used full column rank and
recovered all 162 targets.

## Complete one-target cost in the standalone exact harness

Each record contains a cold factor-base scan, pair-table construction,
target-independent relation trials and matrix work, target descent, and a
fresh scalar-multiplication check. The online interval begins only after
the relation basis is ready. Its five exclusive phase times sum exactly to
`online_ns`: `target_query`, `target_PDP`,
`target_relation_check`, `target_descent`, and
`target_recovery_check`. The records also carry rational and twist
group-law counts, field and modular inversions, pair-table entries and
probes, and modular row operations. A matched negation-quotient rho walk
solves each same target from an empty table; its target-dependent jump
construction and final scalar check are included.

The exact harness deliberately includes direct `F_{p²}` checks of each
mixed factor and up to 256 setup witnesses per one-target run. These are
extra audit work inside the reported wall interval; an optimized attack
could validate fewer witnesses. The operation vectors price the component
group laws but do not calibrate every field, hash, and row primitive.

The original frozen run included three separately verified targets per
`(p,B)`. A subsequent exhaustive cost sweep rebuilt the cold base and
solved **every nonzero public target** in each group for every arm: 49,248
IC solves and 12,312 matched rho solves. Its compressed
[raw records](all_target_cost.ndjson.gz), independent
[audit and summarizer](summarize_all_target_cost.py), and
[summary](all_target_cost_summary.json) retain every target, phase,
resource vector, and distribution endpoint. The table gives the median

## Matched rational-only mimic control

The first cost table deliberately preserved the repository's ordinary
full-rank native producer. That leaves a stopping-policy confound for
`mixed-unique`, whose target-row span rule stops at rank `B−2`. A second,
post hoc control removes it. `native-mimic` uses the same `B` rational
components and the public symbolic color rule `(+T₁)+(+T₂)+(−T₃)=O`,
with `T₃=T₁+T₂`, but performs its pair table and queries entirely in
`E(F_p)`. It filters a candidate triple by that fixed color pattern and
stops at the same rank `B−2`. The timed mimic arm never constructs or
adds a twist point.

The [control raw file](native_mimic_control.ndjson.gz) covers every nonzero
public target. The [independent paired audit](audit_native_mimic.py) and
[summary](native_mimic_audit.json) check **12,312 matched target pairs**;
the [run report](reports/run-20261021T0545Z-native-mimic/report.md) has the
graph and PDF.
For every pair, the recovered scalar, exact relation hits, unique rows,
setup trials, rank, online queries, descent outcome, and all rational
elliptic-curve addition counts are identical. The exact census asserts
equality of the complete projected relation sets in all 18 cells, not just
of their sizes. The mimic uses zero twist group additions; mixed-unique
adds 288, 1,152, or 2,592 twist group additions at `B=12,24,36` in the
median run, respectively. Thus the twist arithmetic supplies no extra
relation or rank in this construction; the same relevant linear system
is obtained from rational arithmetic and a three-color constraint.

## Answer to the research question

For a rational ECDLP and an unrestricted exact group-law relation oracle,
mixed quadratic-twist factors cannot increase the set of usable
target-relevant relations beyond their traced rational components. Our
favorable additive-color construction produces more *raw* lift witnesses
than its small native trace base in some rows, but no new full-width
projected rows, no extra trace rank, and no measured complete-cost advantage
over the matched smaller native base in this panel. The same-policy
`native-mimic` control reproduces the mixed-unique relation set and each
of 12,312 one-target descent paths using identical rational additions
and zero twist additions.

This does **not** rule out a twist-based improvement through a different
mechanism. An explicit rational isogeny can move a DLP to an isogenous twist
model (as the [degree-three control](REPORT.md) checks), and a
twist-induced endomorphism or a different algebraic representation can
reduce a solver's orbit, factor-base, or equation cost. Those mechanisms
require their own same-target, full-pipeline measurements; they are not
established by adding mixed factors to this ordinary prime-field target.
