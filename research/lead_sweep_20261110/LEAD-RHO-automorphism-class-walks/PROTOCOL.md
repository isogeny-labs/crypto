## 2. Hypothesis
Recorded obstruction, re-derived: on a j=0 curve over F_p (|Aut|=6, generated by -1 and the cube-root-of-unity map), an r-adding walk on orbit representatives, with fruitless cycles handled by abandoning the walk, needs between 0.41 and 0.60 times the steps of the plain walk (ideal 1/sqrt(6)=0.408; the gain is capped at sqrt(m) and eroded by fruitless cycles), and it is not below 0.38 (no super-sqrt(m) gain). Negation alone (m=2) gives 0.65-0.85 on the same curve and on a generic curve. So the lead gives a bounded constant factor <= sqrt(6) in steps, with no change in exponent.

## 3. Preregistered prediction and falsification criterion
- Statistic: R6 = mean steps(aut6) / mean steps(plain), averaged over three toy sizes (steps = point additions until a collision that yields k, counting abandoned walks); also R2 (negation only, same j=0 curve), R2_generic (negation vs plain on a random j!=0 curve) and AA.
- Consistent: 0.41 <= R6 <= 0.60 and 0.65 <= R2 <= 0.85 and 0.65 <= R2_generic <= 0.85 and AA in [0.9,1.1].
- Falsified: R6 < 0.38 (beats the sqrt(m) bound) or R6 > 0.70 (no better than negation, the lead's gain does not survive cycle handling), provided AA is in [0.9,1.1]. Anything else, or AA out of band: inconclusive.
- Null / negative control: generic curve with only negation (m=2), predicted ~0.71 not ~0.41; plain walk on the same curve is the baseline (m=1).
- Positive control: the m=2 and m=6 arms must show a gain over plain at all (R<1); the instrument detects the known sqrt(m) effect.
- A/A arm: plain_a vs plain_b (different seeds, same curve and instance), ratio of means must be 1 within 10%.
- Steps only: per-step canonicalisation cost (kappa) is not priced, so no net-cost or section 5a claim.

## 4. Method
Python 3 stdlib (run.py). For each size lo in {2^13, 2^16, 2^19}: pick prime p in [lo,2lo), p=1 mod 3; j=0 curve y^2=x^3+b with point count by quadratic-residue table; subgroup of prime order q (q=1 mod 3, q>=N/8) with generator P; secret k and Q=kP. Identify the endomorphism scalar mu with [mu]P=(zeta x, y). A second random generic curve gets the same treatment (m=2 only). Walk: 32-entry r-adding table R_i=u_iP+v_iQ, partition by canonical x mod 32; state is the orbit representative (lexicographic minimum over the 6 (or 2) images), with (a,b) updated by the image's scalar. Store all visited states; a repeated state with different (a,b) and b!=b' gives k=(a'-a)/(b-b') (checked against true k); repeat of the same state within a walk = fruitless cycle, abandon and restart from a random point; cap 300*sqrt(q) steps. 60 trials per size per arm, arm order shuffled per trial, derived deterministic seeds (master 20261110). Cost: well under 2 minutes; 500 s internal guard; < 200 MB (dict of ~10^3-10^4 states).

## 5. Classification: runnable_local

## 6. What a run cannot establish

Per-step canonicalisation cost, the 5a GAE band, matched cross-method rho references and replay certificates are not produced. It says nothing about curve families with larger automorphism groups (the lead's revisit condition), nor Koblitz/Frobenius orbits (binary fields). Results are labelled [pcc:unverified].
