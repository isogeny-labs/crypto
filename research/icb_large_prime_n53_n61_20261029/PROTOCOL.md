# Protocol: one- and two-large-prime collectors on the Koblitz compact-orbit m = 3 pipeline, n = 53

Lead: `LEAD-ICB-large-prime-variants` (crypto-autoresearcher, `knowledge/leads/ecdlp/ic-binary/`).
Written and committed before the ladder sessions finished; the specs in `specs/` are generated by
`make_specs.py` and were frozen before any ecbench session of this study ran.

## What exists, what this adds

`mitm-frobenius(-counted):m=3,large_primes={1,2},lp_domain_factor=c` (PR crypto#1640 series,
`ic_framework/large_prime.rs`) is already on `main` and was measured at n = 23, 31, 41 in
`research/ic_techniques_20261012/double_large_prime` with `ic bench`. n = 23 and 31 are saturated;
at n = 41 the double large prime beat the equal-envelope plain table by 2.1x and the single did not.
The lead asks for the same question at the Table C sizes. Nothing here changes what a registered
ecbench method counts: the arms differ only in oracle parameters and column counts.

## Question

At n = 53 (log2 r = 44.3), on the same workloads, does the large-prime collector lower counted S of the
single-target `ic.pipeline` (compact-orbit-scan base, three-summand Frobenius pair table, walked
targets) against (a) the plain table over the same envelope F u D and (b) the best plain table found in
the bracket, and how far is the best arm from the matched strong signed-Frobenius rho at 0, 0.1 and 1 GAE
per unpriced native operation (AGENTS.md section 5a)?

## Workloads and sessions

Curve `icv1-f2m53-tm56619371-dac20a85` (K_0, `kind: koblitz, a: 0, n: 53`), planted logarithms, public
targets. Four sessions `n53-s1..s4` with target seeds 202610291..4, eight workloads each, two measured
rounds, no warm-up (counted S is deterministic: a repeat reproduces it exactly, and the A/A control and
`ecbench verify --replay-all` test that). 32 workloads x 2 rounds per arm.

## Pilot that chose the grid (not part of the evidence)

`ic bench` on the same curve, two repeats per cell, seed 20261029 (kept out of the evidence directory):
plain optimum near 440-660 columns (S 6.4-7.0); 2LP optimum near 4 (envelopes 320-440); 1LP no better than
plain at equal envelope. The ladder brackets these.

## Analysis (fixed in advance)

* Primary: paired geometric mean over (session, workload, round) of S(arm) / S(equal-envelope plain
  control), for the 2LP and 1LP arms, with a 95% bootstrap interval over workloads; and S(arm) /
  S(plain-k440) and / S(best plain) for the optimum.
* Distance to rho: S / S(rho-strong) at weights 0, 0.1, 1 via `ecbench band` (native counters of both
  arms priced identically, rho included). An ordering is reported only where it holds across the band.
* Verdict classes: IMPROVEMENT (interval below 1 at every weight against both controls, controls
  pass), LOSES AT PRICED WEIGHT (below 1 at weight 0 only), NULL (interval spans 1), WORSE.
* Controls: A/A pair-ratio equals 1 exactly; lp0 reproduces plain counters; every run verified
  (planted logarithm recovered and checked by the independent verifier); memory beside time
  (peak RSS of one cell per arm).
* Out of scope: any key-recovery or deployed-scale claim; the 32-target aimed collection of Table C
  (a different pipeline, not combined with large primes here); n = 61 unless stated in RESULTS.

## Accounting

S is group operations in GAE / sqrt(r), the harness's own counters. The large-prime collector's
native work (hash probes, eliminator row operations, domain scan) is listed in the record's
`unpriced` set and priced by the band; the pair-table build over F u D is counted as `oracle_setup`.
