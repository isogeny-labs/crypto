Single-large-prime residual sweep (2026-10-28)

Lead ICP-residual-partial-relations. Every residual R - P_i of a target is stored by abscissa; a collision of two residuals is a base-only relation. Three sessions per size in one binary; all arms alternated within each session.

Slope of log2 S against log2 B per size (model: +0.5): 20-bit +0.32, 24-bit +0.36, 28-bit +0.36, 32-bit +0.36.
Slope of log2 S against curve bits at fixed base (model: 0 for the sweep): sweep-b2 -0.035, sweep-b4 -0.043, sweep-b8 -0.018, sweep-b16 -0.014, sweep-b32 -0.017, sweep-b64 -0.020, sweep-b128 -0.025, pair-table (base ~ r^(1/3)) +0.058.

Findings

  • The sweep beats the pair-table recipe in the counted unit, by a lot. At 32 bits S is 2.82 at B = 2, 5.38 at B = 16 and 11.55 at B = 128, against 33.01 for the incumbent (B = 1024) and 1.02 for rho.negation: the distance to rho falls from 32.4x to 2.8x. Every one of the 1008 sweep runs verified.
  • Its cost is a square root, and it is cheapest where there is least index calculus. At fixed B the S does not grow with the curve (slope of log2 S against bits: -0.035, -0.043, -0.018, -0.014, -0.017, -0.020, -0.025), i.e. the work is c*sqrt(r) additions; S grows with the base as B^0.35 (model 0.5), so the smallest base wins. At B = 2 the base is four points and the search is a birthday collision of aG + bQ values.
  • The memory is the price the unit does not see. The residual table holds 131737 entries at 32 bits for B = 2 (7.0 MB) and 675776 for B = 128 (36 MB); it grows like sqrt(r B). rho.negation keeps O(1) memory. S counts group additions only.
  • What this is. A real, verified reduction of the cost of an index-calculus-pipeline arm and of its distance to rho; also the cost profile of a baby-step/giant-step collision search with a small signed base. The owner's rule that relabelling is not an improvement applies: this PR therefore does not put the sweep on tables A-C of the leaderboard and leaves that decision to the owner.
  • Unpriced native work. Each residual costs a hash insert or probe that S does not charge. Priced at 1 GAE per probe the band keeps every ordering (sweep below the pair table, rho below the sweep).
