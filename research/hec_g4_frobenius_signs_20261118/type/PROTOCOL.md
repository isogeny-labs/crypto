# Geometric type from even theta constants

Evaluate all 256 duplication quadrics on each of the four surviving quotient roots, retaining their coordinates. Require the 120 odd zeros and agreement of the number of even zeros between representatives. On the previously qualified source representative (4) require ten vanishing even theta constants. Search all 2048 Boolean polynomials of degree at most two for a sign change mapping the first vector to each other vector on its actual support. Record an explicit witness when found. Monomials are ordered by ascending four-bit subset mask.

For a smooth geometric genus-four Jacobian in odd characteristic, a hyperelliptic curve has ten vanishing even theta constants by the branch-partition formula. For a nonhyperelliptic curve, an effective even theta characteristic is a degree-three pencil whose square is canonical. The sections s²,st,t² give the canonical quadric cone. Conversely a cone ruling gives such a pencil, and a smooth canonical quadric has two distinct complementary geometric rulings. Thus zero even vanishings means a smooth canonical quadric, one means a cone. This diagnoses geometric type and does not decide rational splitting or a twist.

Freeze and push before one invocation, bounded at 600 CPU seconds. Source: [Hanselman, Pieper and Schiavone](https://math.mit.edu/~sschiavo/articles/reconstruction-20240205.pdf).
