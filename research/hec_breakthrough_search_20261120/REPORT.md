# Genus >= 2 index-calculus breakthrough search (workflow, 2026-11-20)

Desk research, not a measurement. Every number is `[pcc:unverified]`; numbers quoted from the lab cite their study, and predictions are the agents' own, not measured.
Raw output of every agent: `workflow_result.json` (run `wf_570c1b51-60d`).

## How it ran

1. **Review (Sonnet).** Reviewed the lab's prior genus >= 2 prime-field work and wrote a baseline per genus, the closed leads and the open leads.
2. **Explore (six Fable agents in parallel).** Each built on the review in one direction:
   - genus-3 transfer;
   - non-hyperelliptic Diem;
   - large-prime theory;
   - genus 2;
   - linear algebra;
   - cross-genus transfers.
   Together they made 17 proposals and listed 35 dead ends.
3. **Referee (Fable).** Tried to refute every proposal, then ranked the survivors.

All agents were read-only on the repositories, ran small exact checks only, and ran no timed benchmarks.
Limitations: research-kb is not installed here, and the lead atlas path does not exist. Literature exponents are recalled from repository documents, not from the knowledge base.

## Surviving claims (referee's wording)

- **#1 Destination-first: measure Diem IC on GENERIC prime-order, prime-field plane quartics (native non-hyperelliptic genus 3), all phases, sparse LA, L10-corrected rho, w-band**: A correctly-priced, sparse-LA, fully-charged Diem line-IC measurement on generic prime-order prime-field plane quartics across a prime ladder (p ~ 2^11..2^18, within the g=3 #J guard ~2.08e6) is the lab's single most reachable clean genus-3 exponent demonstration (q^1 vs rho q^{3/2}) AND the prerequisite that decides whether any hyperelliptic->quartic transfer is worth building.
- **#2 Port the lab's existing validated block-Lanczos LA into the genus_ladder binary (replacing coordinate Wiedemann) for genus 3-5 hyperelliptic**: Block Lanczos moves the validated genus-4 x* IC/ideal-negation-rho from 0.876->~0.63-0.68 (p=16411) and 0.640->~0.45-0.49 (p=32771) at w=1, and is the cheapest shot at resolving genus-3 IC below rho at w=1; composes with #1 (quartic lane already has the solver) and with #4.
- **#3 Determine the affine-type (2,2,2) codomain type for prime-order genus-3 hyperelliptic Jacobians (quartic vs hyperelliptic): the single gate to a UNIVERSAL 4/3->1 exponent drop**: If the affine (2,2,2) codomain is a plane quartic (decidable on ONE concrete curve by S_8-genericity of the 30 affine Lagrangians), prime-order genus-3 hyperelliptic DLP transfers 4/3->1 on ~100% of curves at poly(log q) transfer cost; the codomain type is the one unresolved binary, and it cannot be settled by combinatorics (even-theta-null vanishing depends on moduli).
- **#4 Non-hyperelliptic genus-4 canonical (hyperplane) IC at exponent 4/3 with a CORRECTED instrument - a strictly lower exponent than the lab's best validated (hyperelliptic genus-4, p^1.54)**: A correctly-instrumented (dedup lines once; charge LA after large-prime elimination) non-hyperelliptic genus-4 canonical IC can demonstrate a measurably lower exponent (4/3) than the lab's best validated hyperelliptic genus-4 row on a generic, abundant target class; the d=4 control is the instrument gate.
- **#5 Odd-l (l=3) transfer feasibility census for prime-order genus-3 hyperelliptic (extension-degree k distribution), as a cheap gate before any (3,3,3)-isogeny work**: Whether a positive-density (say >=5%) sub-family of prime-order genus-3 curves carries a rational l=3 Lagrangian with kernel field degree k<=4 is decidable by a pure-Python charpoly-mod-l census (no CAS, no isogeny) and cleanly gates the entire odd-l route.
- **#6 Orbit-closed triple-large-prime elimination on special-automorphism/RM Jacobians (the only in-lane exponent lever, 2-2/(g-1))**: IF orbit sums supply large-prime dependencies that are rank-independent of factor-base folding AND the special family contains prime-order instances, t=3 could reach 2-2/(g-1); both conjuncts are unlikely for prime-order targets, so this is parked, not pursued.
- **#7 Genus-2 split/elliptic-subcover obstruction theorem + O((log p)^c) weak-curve detector**: There is no exponent-moving route for prime-order genus-2 Jacobians over prime fields (genus 2 is the crossover where best IC exponent q^1 = rho q^1); only constants are available, and the lab's measured gap is 3-5x with no crossing. Adopt the detector as cheap cryptographic hygiene and stop seeking a genus-2 exponent.
- **#8 In-lane constants cluster: weight-aware relation purge, min-weight cycle basis, degree-2 large primes at g3/4, L01 end-to-end g2, automorphism-orbit g2**: These are legitimate but modest constants; only weight-aware purge and min-weight cycle basis are worth a one-shot Python check because they COMPOSE with #2 (block Lanczos) on the LA-dominated genus-3/4 rows - but none changes an exponent and none is a priority over #1-#4.

## Cross-cutting insights

- The destination is the bottleneck, and three independent explorers converged on it: nonhyp-diem P1, cross-genus P1 and g3-transfer P2 are the SAME measurement - Diem IC on generic prime-order prime-field plane quartics with corrected rho and sparse LA. It is simultaneously (a) a standalone density-1 genus-3 exponent win (no transfer; generic genus-3 curves ARE quartics) and (b) the logical prerequisite for every hyperelliptic->quartic transfer (g3-transfer P1/P3, cross-genus P2). Nothing downstream is worth building until it is shown that the quartic destination actually beats q^{4/3} at reachable p under honest pricing. Run it first.
- Unifying exponent theorem across explorers 1,2,3,6: for PRIME-ORDER hyperelliptic Jacobians over prime fields, the GTTD exponent 2-2/g is a hard floor for degree-1-place index calculus (large-prime-theory's counting: t=2 optimal, t=1 and unstructured t>=3 strictly worse). Every exponent gain therefore requires EITHER leaving the hyperelliptic locus onto a native non-hyperelliptic (lower-gonality / plane) model - which is free and density-1 for the generic curve and gives 2-2/(g-1) - OR special-curve algebraic structure (automorphism/RM/isogeny kernel) that is almost never prime order. This single principle refutes the whole large-prime-restructuring family and redirects effort to native non-hyperelliptic targets.
- Simplicity is a double-edged sword that the explorers use inconsistently. Prime order => simple Jacobian: this KILLS all split/decomposable and cover routes for g2 and g3 (genus2 P1, cross-genus dead-ends - correct and airtight), but it also GUARANTEES any isogeny codomain is again a simple g-dim Jacobian (hyperelliptic XOR, for g=3, a plane quartic). The transfer program lives entirely on whether that codomain is the quartic case - which my exact J[2] computation shows reduces to the type of the affine-Lagrangian quotient, an open moduli question NOT settled by combinatorics or A_3 genericity (cross-genus P2 overclaims; g3-transfer P1 is the honest version).
- Block Lanczos (linear-algebra P1) composes with the destination measurement (#1): the quartic lane ALREADY has validated block Lanczos while the hyperelliptic genus_ladder binary does not. So the LA solver flows BOTH ways - port block Lanczos into genus_ladder for the hyperelliptic rows, and the quartic lane's existing solver makes the #1 destination measurement's sparse-LA requirement free. The LA-exponent risk that broke L08 (dense/pre-elimination LA) is the common failure mode to guard against in #1 and #4.
- Pricing discipline is where most lab genus>=2 rows die and where these proposals must be held: the L10 rho corrections (/1.73 negation over-pricing and the 0.447 <rho> class-walk) and the §5a w in {0,0.1,1} band flip signs repeatedly (genus-3 unresolved at w=1; C-route vs IC flips between FPM-low and FPM-mid). Only the validated genus-4 x* row survives the full band. Any new claim (#1, #4) must apply the corrections and report all three w values, or it is the relabelling the success criterion forbids.
- Instrument-design is a recurring silent failure: L08 counted duplicate lines as relations and charged LA before large-prime elimination; L12 had a defective stop rule; L06's gate was miscalibrated. The lesson (freeze a gate only after proving it reachable at the study's own size, and prove the positive/d=4 control recovers its known exponent) is the gating condition for #4 and must be built into #1.
