# One-time offsets of the paired streams

All four arms verified all 44 public targets. A rational affine branch place was available on 24 targets (six curves); the other 20 kept the ordinary streams. The audit checked doubling of the branch divisor to identity and the exact translated anchor and signed pencil relations. The ordinary nonbranch offset was available on all 44.

Branch translation improved median time on 14/24 changed targets. The branch policy reached 2.050–3.470× original time in genus two, 2.752–4.720× in genus three and 4.020–11.412× in genus four over the full panel. Only genus four p=31 cases 0 and 1 exceeded tenfold with either offset policy. The branch result is conditional on availability of a rational affine branch point. Other rational two-torsion classes from higher-degree factors were outside this experiment. Timing differences on unchanged streams receive no translation credit.

Adding an ordinary nonbranch offset to both streams reduced the anchor count on 9/24 genus-two inputs yet slowed all 24 complete median calls. A valid change of representative alone is insufficient to establish a broadly faster relation schedule. No fixed policy reached the full-panel gate.
