## First algebraic extraction slice: bounded linear consequences

This is checked linear extraction from the reported, independently unverified native basis, separate from independent group traversal. It is available only with a complete retained native basis object (native basis may be partial algebraically; original candidate verification remains mandatory). Deterministic repeated passes choose the lowest original variable index and then lowest basis ID having a nonzero univariate linear remainder after substituting already bound variables. Canonicalize remainders with bounded sort/merge; zero imposes no assignment, nonzero constant rejects the branch. Solve a*x+b=0 through bounded field inversion, validate any finite leaf domain, and record variable/basis ID, exact substituted coefficients, assignment and cumulative work. At most V assignments and V+1 full passes; no branching and no repeated variable assignment.

When all V coordinates are assigned, evaluate every original polynomial independently. Only all-zero residues yield a reported_basis_linear_candidate whose original-equation validity is independently checked.
