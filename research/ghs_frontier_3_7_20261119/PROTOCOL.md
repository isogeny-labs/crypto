Status: implementation and deterministic validation are complete where stated;
no admitted measurement has launched and the frontier is not regraded
`[pcc:unverified]`.

## Question and boundary

The study asks whether the R17/R36/R64 optimized GHS strong-rho candidates,
their admissible composition, the matched restricted-family index calculus,
or a currently reachable solver configuration changes the established
performance frontier.

ECBench domains use `S = total charged operations / sqrt(r)` and the applicable
automorphism floor `sqrt(pi/(2A))`. The GHS restricted-family panel uses frozen
base-field multiplication equivalents and matched optimized rho on exactly the
same curve, subgroup, target, seed policy, and resource envelope.

## Hypotheses

- H3: production parallel distinguished-point rho lowers one-target latency at
  P=2 or P=4 without unreported total work, overshoot, memory, DP traffic, or
  queue work. Latency alone is not a counted-cost frontier move.
- H4: at least one source-bound composition of baseline/R17/R36/R64 lowers an
  admitted complete cost. Standalone operation deltas are not added.
- H5: the historically IC-led restricted family remains below the optimized
  matched rho across the complete grid and all required pricing weights.
  Missing cells or an interval crossing one leave this limited or unresolved.
- H6: every live implementation is executed on an applicable canonical cell or
  carries an explicit unsupported, blocked, timeout, OOM, soundness, or
  unlike-unit disposition. Registry presence is not performance evidence.
- H7: no frontier output changes unless an independent gate can rederive its
  headline from sealed raw evidence and reject corruptions.

## Cost chain

```text
C(baseline) = setup + walk/relation + table/filter + solve + recovery + verify
            = C' (restructure phases only; total unchanged)
            -> C'' (replace exactly one named mechanism with measured delta)
            = C(candidate)
```

Each replace line is measured on identical inputs. Composition restarts this
chain from the last independently accepted binary; it never sums isolated
deltas. Unpriced operation counters are listed for both arms and read at 0,
0.1, and 1 GAE per native operation, with byte counters uncharged. A calibrated
field-operation price is reported only with an admitted same-host calibration.

## Admission and execution

Before any paid or timed run:

1. exact source, protocol, inputs, seeds, requests, finite dollar ceiling,
   deadline, retry allowance, and acceptance criteria are committed and pushed;
2. `check-experiment-git` and the checkpoint gate pass;
3. current daily/monthly budget headroom and all-tenant pool/quota state are
   known and sufficient;
4. the digest-pinned image contains the exact source and binary;
5. L2, warmup, at least five measured rounds where wall is reported, reference,
   matching A/A, whole-core placement, and zero automatic measurement retries
   remain intact; and
6. failures and ambiguous submissions retain their original IDs.

The selected runtime currently fails the identity/checkpoint prerequisites.
The offline standalone-GHS contract is deliberately non-admitting because a
Pod-authored isolation claim cannot grade itself. No local timing substitutes
for EKS evidence.

## Analysis and stopping rules

- Every recovered scalar is independently checked.
- A/A, exact replay, phase sums, `useful + overshoot = total`, identities, and
  pricing bands are fail-closed.
- Development and holdout identities remain disjoint; no holdout retuning.
- Timeouts, OOMs, wrong answers, interrupted jobs, missing counters, and
  non-applicability remain rows.
- Unlike native solver units are not cross-ranked. A common comparison needs
  complete E2E cost or matched verified L2 wall at component scope
  `[pcc:unverified]`.
- A Spot interruption invalidates the timing interval. Resume is accepted only
  through the qualified CRIU/EBS contract followed by a fresh epoch.
- The grid is not reduced to fit budget or capacity. A prospective amendment
  leaves the original request unresolved.
