Prior evidence is `../ghs_n7_q32_ic_20261115/REPORT.md` (complete q32 ratio 2.683), `../ghs_n7_factorization_20261115/REPORT.md`, [Hess's genus and L-polynomial theorem](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/315278965D8D277A85812D9498236A88/S146115700000108Xa.pdf/generalising-the-ghs-attack-on-the-elliptic-curve-discrete-logarithm-problem.pdf), and [Menezes--Teske's weak-field analysis](https://cacr.uwaterloo.ca/techreports/2004/corr2004-25.pdf). Use `q=64`, `K=F2[z]/(f)` with f the first irreducible trinomial `X^42+X^j+1`, `1<=j<=21`, or, if none exists, the first irreducible pentanomial `X^42+X^i+X^j+X^k+1` in lexicographic `1<=i<j<k<42` order. Choose the least polynomial-basis mask `a∈F64` with absolute F2 trace one. Let `σ(x)=x^64`, `V1=ker(σ³+σ+1)` and `V2=ker(σ³+σ²+1)`. Each is F2-dimension 18. For each nonzero v in each arm, set `b=(1+v)^2`. Exclude any v in the proper absolute subfields F_(2^14) or F_(2^21); all remaining b have full absolute degree 42. The two excluded-selector subspaces have predicted dimensions 6 and 9 and intersection dimension 3, yielding `2*(2^18-2^6-2^9+2^3)=523152` full-degree coefficients. Verify those dimensions/counts rather than assuming them.

Enumerate all full-degree coefficient masks by arm and sort by `(arm,b_mask)`. Screen exactly the first 128 rows. For each record `#E(K)` of `E:y²+xy=x³+a x²+b`, trace, factorization, largest prime r, cofactor h, arm, and b mask. Compute `#E0(F64)` for `E0:y²+xy=x³+a x²+1`. Select the **first** row with `h=2`, `r>=2^40` and `r∤#E0`; if none, retain no selected curve and stop. This fixed rule seeks a large same-source rho baseline and does not establish weakness. Preserve all rows and exact script SHA-256 in immutable `screen.json`.

After selection, check exact-equation prior work and freeze point/map, full Euler factor and complete GHS/rho protocols before each measurement. A vulnerability claim needs a nonzero selected-subgroup map, all projected factor-base relations, full-rank solve, target descent, and measured complete GHS cost below matched rho on the same P and Q. Preserve timeouts, censored output and failed ranks. No q32 extrapolation is a q64 result.
