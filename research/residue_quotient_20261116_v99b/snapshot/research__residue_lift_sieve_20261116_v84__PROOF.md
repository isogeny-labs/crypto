# Lift-coupled residue splitting and infinity constraints
For K=F2[T]/M with degM83 and source coordinates polynomials of degree<18, the monic source quintic has coefficient bounds deg e_i<=17i. Only its constant coefficient can wrap modulo M: e1..e4 have degree<=68<83, e5<=85. Thus every supported H has a unique lifted polynomial F(T,X) with F0=H0+q(T)M, degq<=2 (eight choices). The same q must pass every test. Reducing canonical field coefficients modulo unrelated polynomials without these lifts would be unsound.

For any irreducible pi(T), every polynomial root reduces to an element of F2[T]/pi. Therefore the full lifted monic quintic must split into five linear factors there, repetitions included. The allowed coefficient table is precisely the image of all five-root multisets over that residue field. Distinct multisets produce distinct monic polynomials by unique factorization. Tables are necessary local constraints, never a sufficient source-support certificate for the original field. Keep each global lift until it fails a constraint; do not independently choose incompatible lifts per residue.

At infinity, every nonzero polynomial root has an integer degree0..17 and leading coefficient1. The upper Newton polygon of coefficient degrees consequently has integer negative slopes in that range. Every edge's leading residual polynomial is (Z+1)^edge_length overF2. Failure is a sound rejection. X factors represent zero roots and are stripped only for this test, with no loss. This necessary test does not claim global factorization or all-roots completeness.

A cheap whole-family consequence: H4 has fixed leading bit at degree17 equal1 in the actual affine chart. Let k be the number of degree17 roots; k must be1,3or5. Bits(u,v,w,t)=(H3[34],H2[51],H1[68],q[2]) are respectively binom(k,2),binom(k,3),binom(k,4),binom(k,5). Thus allowed H flags are000,110,001, with q[2]=w. If k1or3 then maximum total root degree<=81or83, so q[1]=0. Each flag branch is an affine linear system on the73Boolean parameters; its rank and exact size are measured without enumeration. Input directions are checked independent. This can refute the hypothesis of a new guard if the old relaxation already implies these conditions.

Residue observations of all five nonleading coefficients are GF2-linear in the73parameters plus3lift bits. Stacking them gives a linear code/coset; allowed per-residue tables form a table-XOR constraint problem. Projection rank and syndrome dimension are exact, but table cardinalities cannot be multiplied into an independence/hardness/speed prediction. Generic birthday algorithms seeking some matches discard solutions unless their filtering partitions are exhaustively covered. They cannot certify all roots by themselves. Any promoted exact search must retain all solutions or issue a verifiable exhaustion certificate, and verify surviving lifted polynomials with full factor/support/original-source reconstruction.

This combines classical local factorization, Newton polygons and linear-code constraints. It is a new tested research route here, not primary-literature novelty clearance or a qualified speedup claim.
