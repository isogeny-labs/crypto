# Primary-source review2026-11-16

[Forney, Codes on Graphs: Fundamentals, arXiv1306.6264v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.6264v2), author-deposited abstract, discusses linear/group-code realizations, graph fragments and minimality on cycle-free graphs. This is relevant prior art for representing the finite-domain observation code as a graph. Our cut dimension follows directly from the elementary subspace intersection identity; no claim is made that C111invented a trellis bound.

[Forney and Gluesing-Luerssen, Codes on Graphs: Observability, Controllability and Local Reducibility, arXiv1203.3115v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1203.3115v2), author abstract, studies local reducibility, observability and controllability of code realizations. It explicitly connects independent parity checks with controllability. This supports treating rank/redundancy preprocessing as established methodology rather than novelty.

The reviewed material in this cycle is the two linked primary abstracts, not an exhaustive reading or literature novelty certification. C110's Compact-Table/Compact-MDD references remain relevant for nonlinear finite-domain state compression; the67bit AFFINE bound does not exclude those stronger mechanisms. No published benchmark is imported as a measurement on this family.
