## Population and claim

The fixed natural population is 26 cells: m=3,…,15 crossed with untwisted and twisted Koblitz curves over F_(2^31), one curve seed, one generator, and one target key per cell. Each planned cell remains in the denominator after a construction error, unsupported status, timeout, interruption, exhausted budget, or custody failure. This is an **additional** n31 block; it does not replace the n23 Koblitz/binary/prime 78-cell arity portfolio or any n47–61 size block.

The exact-computation claim is coverage and verifiable artifact custody for this selected population. A completed bounded enumeration may certify a witness or a no-witness result only for its recorded finite factor base and m-specific search domain. `budget_exhausted` and timeout are censored observations, never negatives. A scalar is a label only after independent native recovery and `[k]G=Q` replay. No timing, cross-method ranking, first/last fall-degree, orbit-reduction speedup, or detector-sensitivity claim is made by these cells. A distinct planted-positive control requires a separate protocol, identifiers, and split group; it must not replace a natural cell or enter this 26-cell denominator. BSGS is a possible label-recovery phase, not a second arity arm.

## Fixed axes and resource bounds proposed for freeze

Use all 26 cell IDs in `plan.draft.json` in m-major order, untwisted then twisted, with one native attempt per ID and no replacement. Proposed common axes: requested factor-base size 3 under candidate `legacy-small-x-v1` selection, subject to native preflight confirmation of the actual policy; degree bound 3; work limit 4,096 counted group-add transitions; one curve seed, generator, key; native per-case limit 90 s; outer command limit 180 s; memory 2,048 MiB; trace 64 MiB; at least 8 GiB free disk; at most one native process; campaign wall ceiling 8 h. The 26 outer limits sum to 4,680 s (78 min), exclusive of custody/checkpoint overhead. The seed remains deliberately unset until freeze. After the first attempt, any cap change creates a new protocol version and separate arm; the original 26 cells, their limits, and their denominator remain unchanged.

Requested factor-base size is **not** the actual signed base cardinality; native construction may retain both signs and yield four points. Capture its exact points, signs, ordering, orbits, and cardinality for each cell. If cardinality is four and no witness appears, full enumeration needs 84, 340 and 1,364 transitions at m=3,4,5; m≥6 can be censored by 4,096. If cardinality differs, compute the finite-domain total from the actual native definition and record it before classifying a negative. The native count is authoritative. The n23 resource-conditioned 4,096-transition prefixes took roughly 21–30 s in prior ordinary-binary cells; 90 s is planning margin for n31, not a prediction or performance claim. Timeout remains a counted outcome. Clock and whole-worker instruction labels are ineligible unless isolation and complete boundary accounting are separately specified.

## Controls, analysis and stop rules

This is absolute fixed-population accounting with no comparator or statistical test. A/A and matched baseline are inapplicable to its exact coverage claim. A natural cell is not a negative control for detector sensitivity; verified complete no-witness results are finite-domain labels only. The separately labeled planted-positive plan may test capture sensitivity but cannot establish prevalence in natural data. Analysis counts planned/attempted/completed and statuses by m and twist, actual factor-base sizes, transitions, verified PDP witnesses, independently verified scalar labels, bytes, native artifacts, and custody outcomes. Every timeout, partial result, failed check, or interrupted cell remains visible. Stop after each cell at its frozen cap, and stop campaign admission at the eight-hour ceiling or when disk/memory/custody gates fail. Never rerun an already sealed attempt; reconcile local marker, process, Git and S3 state first.

The ML split key must group a curve and its quadratic twist, all generator/key samples, all arities, and all Frobenius/sign-related or transported artifacts into the **same split**. Build a canonical group identifier from family, n, irreducible polynomial, canonical isomorphism/twist-pair identity, and curve seed before hashing to the split. Keep all 26 cells together: this block alone supplies no independent train/test split. Strict structural features must exclude target scalar, target-dependent post-solution traces, source filenames/paths/status cues, and labels; label files stay restricted. A later multi-seed/multi-curve dataset can split by the canonical pair group, with deterministic salt frozen before data and duplicate/isomorphism audit across splits.
