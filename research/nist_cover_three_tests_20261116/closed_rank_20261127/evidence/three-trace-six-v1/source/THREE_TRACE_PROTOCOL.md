# Three whole residual traces: six-image necessary condition

Frozen before implementation or execution. Existing mixed/cross-alias,
graph and degree-six tests are complete; do not repeat them. Closed rank
on the original 225 columns is six. [pcc:unverified]

A line section with two signed old image slots A_i,B_i has residual
degree-two image trace T_i=kappa-A_i-B_i. Consequently closure of three
whole residual traces, T_1+T_2+T_3=O, requires the six old image slots to
sum to 3kappa. This target was not covered by the completed six-symbol
tests at O,kappa,2kappa or2rho. The necessary condition includes repeated
slots and residual closed points with rational aggregate trace. It does
not cover cycles involving only selected individual residual points,
more than three traces, or anchors outside the signed original domain.
[pcc:unverified]

Bind kappa freshly from the actual reference split line H. Reuse the
unchanged checked shifted-triple engine. Compute rho=[(r+1)/2](3kappa)
and check [2]rho=3kappa with native GMP and named OpenSSL. The coefficient
reference is three times H's signed word. Enumerate every unordered triple
on the 451-symbol alphabet, compare every shifted sum/hash between both
engines, and compare the complete grouped and indexed-merge row/witness
sets. No formal zero row is required or inserted. Enforce existing caps;
a cap violation rejects completeness rather than truncating.

Verify three actual split line sections, their complete quartic factor
products, all actual map images and their residual trace identity in both
group implementations. These are control witnesses, not an asserted
three-trace closure in the actual domain. Reuse the public nonzero G/2G
fixture and exhaustive small six-word comparison. Additionally check a
nonempty trace-closure fixture with kappa=2G and T_1=T_2=G,T_3=-2G:
anchors (G,O),(G,O),(2G,2G) yield a nonzero coefficient row (-4,2).
Check all three traces are nonzero, their sum is O in both engines, and
a changed anchor rejects. Fixture rows never enter the actual matrix.

Keep the original six rows and 108x429 tangent matrix fixed. Verify all
row sums and ranks in both group engines, retain every candidate row
with a minimal witness, and add only independent original-column rows
to capped exact bases. Compare old-column rank with full minus auxiliary
rank. A positive image candidate is not goal completion: realize its
actual residual supports/cover or KM data, then obtain separate elliptic
and elimination replay before claiming added verified rank.

C++17 build <=300 seconds; each worker <=600 seconds; dense helpers
<=256 rows and <=1024 columns; retained rows <=4096. All mathematical
