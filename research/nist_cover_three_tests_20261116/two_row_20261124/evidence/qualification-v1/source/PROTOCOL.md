## Complete necessary cases

Each new part has one of these forms, up to overall sign:

* A-type: one new column X with coefficient one, plus q in A on old columns;
  its residual trace is X+q.
* B-type: one new column X with coefficient two, from two identical signed
  images; its trace is 2X and has no old residual term.
* C-type: two distinct new columns with coefficients ±1 and no old term.

Opposite images in one column would give zero new part, already excluded by
the T_i outside A+A premise. Identity residual images are included in A-type
by q=O. Identity images produce no coefficient column. These forms cover two
individual residual images, including equality after projection.

Two nonzero new parts cancel only if proportional. Different support sizes
cannot cancel. For two C-types the ratio is ±1 and T_i ± T_j=O is necessary.
For two B-types the same trace equality is necessary. For two A-types,
T_i ± T_j lies in A+A. For ordered A,B types, 2T_i ± T_j lies in 2A.
Since O belongs to A+A, the first lookup includes the B/B and C/C cases.
Thus these tests are necessary for every two-row cancellation, regardless of
whether the resulting old row would be nonzero or independent of the baseline.
The written case argument is not a formal or independently signed proof.
