# Next implementation: Kummer coordinates and divisor-class lift

Design notes,2026-11-16. These are proposed implementation steps, not executed map results. Keep the goal active until the actual source divisor-class map, public transfer and restricted-base recovery are tested. Kernel1/2 conditional nonsquare behavior does not satisfy the same-field target requirement. Finish the resumed kernel3 run first; preserve its outcome before selecting another representative.

Primary source reviewed: Tian arXiv:2007.03172, Propositions3.2/3.3 and section4.2.3. With theta-square functions indexed by(a,b), a,b in F2^3, Proposition3.2 at b=0 gives projective Schrödinger coordinates from the signed sum over the second index. At the origin the existing64-value vector therefore determines a projective theta-null vector X0. Proposition3.3 at a=0 recovers X_i(D)*X_i(0), up to a common factor, from the eight even Weil functions indexed(0,b). If all X_i(0) are nonzero, divide by them to obtain Kummer coordinates using eight functions. Require the origin consistency identity and reject a zero-coordinate chart; do not assign an arbitrary square root.

On the target, use the existing bitangent/conic raw Weil functions. For an even label S not directly La-Lb, express S=a+b with a,b such differences and a even. The product raw_a(E)*raw_b(E+a) has the required divisor. At the canonical even origin E0 its value is nonzero, so normalize it directly to the corresponding pinned theta-square value. The choices must be fixed by label/parity before function data, with diagonal/heldout checks. This could supply the eight even target functions without constructing an entire64-function normal set.

For Tian's good functions on C^3, the space H0(2*infinity1+2*K) has degree10 and dimension8. In the current infinity chart use basis{1,x,x^2,y,y^2,xy,x*(y-m2*x)^2,y*(y-m2*x)^2}, where infinity2=(1:m2:0). The cubic terms have double vanishing at infinity2. Form the120 symmetric triple products. Impose diagonal vanishing on a fixed bounded balanced schedule of point pairs, not a schedule with one point held fixed (which has too little rank). Require rank112 and eight kernel functions, plus heldout diagonal checks and rank8 on generic triple evaluations. The theoretical containment/dimension argument must accompany the sampling check.

Evaluate these functions and the target Kummer coordinates on generic triples to fit the linear coordinate change, with heldout triples. This gives the actual Kummer-image equations. Then solve the finite lift system and verify every recovered divisor on the curve and against all coordinates. An installed algebra package is insufficient qualification: the tested local Giac modular mode fails the actual-prime known-answer control. Another supported mode or a native large-prime solver must pass independent known-answer checks before its output is used.

For target class arithmetic, extend the existing evaluation-space backend to the auxiliary field and closed degree3 divisors. Convert a finite cubic ideal between Riemann/base coordinates by computing the multiplication matrix of the new x-coordinate in the cubic algebra, its characteristic polynomial, and y interpolation. Encode E-E0 through the two classes D0+E-3R0 and D0+E0-3R0, then subtract. Verify Frobenius invariance of the resulting class for an Fp transfer; extension-field coordinates for a representative alone do not decide the class's field.

Final acceptance remains an executable phi with kernel/Frobenius/addition/dual checks, elliptic transfer composition[6], public-point transport, destination restricted-base relations and scalar checking on the source. A Kummer point, differential matrix, candidate curve isomorphism or restored resource budget alone does not meet it.
