Frozen before implementation/call, 2026-11-24. Extends the user-authorized
local fixed P-192 pipeline in PR #5123. No new point search or paid compute.
All earlier source, results, protocols and claim subjects remain immutable.

H-T: the five nonidentity recorded quartic cycles are the actual forward
images of the independently computed pullbacks; their independently computed
transpose traces, minus the source-degree times the three reference traces,
equal the recorded source duals and triple pullbacks. Their independently
computed elliptic norms equal the recorded norms and [6]P. Identity must
check explicitly. The panel remains O,G,-G,2G,Q,-2G.

Use the preceding separate OpenSSL BIGNUM/base-polynomial/divisor arithmetic
as a frozen build-only header, with main removed. Import no original producer
arithmetic. Add quotient-algebra, extension-polynomial/Cantor arithmetic,
factorization, Frobenius traces and elliptic arithmetic in new native C++.

Forward binding: evaluate the exact global cubic U and quadratic V over the
source quotient algebra. Verify the recorded support and coordinates in the
rank-nine tensor algebra, including the separating coordinate. Require its
nine power vectors to have full prime-field rank, so incidence alone cannot
substitute for the complete pushforward cycle.

Transpose: independently factor each target support, replay factor product
including multiplicities, verify irreducibility, substitute target coordinates
in both cleared graph equations, compute their saturated elimination gcd,
recover source y, verify graph and source incidence, Cantor-reduce and sum
all Frobenius conjugates. Require generic raw degree nine. Independently
replay the three retained rational reference points with raw degree eight;
record these boundary degrees and subtract source-degree times their sum.
No new degree drop or silently unsupported chart counts as success. This
replays the retained affine/saturated correspondence convention; a proof
covering arbitrary projective charts and general subgroup inputs is separate.

Norm: independently factor the reduced source support, evaluate the cover
map in each residue field, and add all Frobenius-conjugate elliptic images.
Include repeated factors. Compare the descended result with the recorded
norm and a fresh sixfold elliptic multiple. Check the exceptional -2G panel
binding from the preceding separate checker. Reject changed cycle y, a valid
wrong source dual, changed norm, dropped reference summand, and changed graph
equation. Controls must reach their relevant comparison, not pass by omission.

Input bytes: original local run's transfer/result.json, cover/quartic input,
relations/result.json pool, retained global-correspondence-v2/result.json and
formal-series-v1/result.json reference indices. Snapshot/hash before calls.
Deterministic factor splitting retains shifts 0..1023; exhaustion fails.
Strict build <=300 seconds and native replay <=600 seconds, each <=1GiB on
the same allowed CPU. Retain fresh attempt directories, errors/exits,

No cap expansion after observing results. Native subphase clocks attribute
setup, references, each fixture and controls, with process overhead separate.
