For F=X³+rX²+sX+t,G=hX+k, target vanishing fixes t=z³+rz²+sz+(hz+k)yT. At fixed h,k all binary norm coefficients are affine F2-linear in original r,s; no trace-zero Artin–Schreier normalization is required. The exact coefficient-span fiber is computed by general binary elimination and checked against every small r,s assignment.

At x!=z, point vanishing is equivalent to (x+z)r+s=x²+xz+z²+((hz+k)yT+(hx+k)y)/(x+z). Project the constant s-only image once and recover every original r,s solution by affine back substitution. Both point lifts are retained for nonzero G(x). For G(x)=0, solve the single lift-independent equation even if the curve has no rational point at x; this retains F(x)=G(x)=0 ghost branches. At x=0, include the unique square-root lift of B. Source x=z is excluded only because the target is explicitly outside the source domain. Every projected fiber is lifted back to original coordinates before comparison with direct norm-root evaluation. This prototype validates exact conditional fibers, not a native speed extension or a complete original-chain solver.
