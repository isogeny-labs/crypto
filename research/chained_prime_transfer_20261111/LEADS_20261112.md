# Chained-transfer leads, 2026-11-12: amortized IC, certificates, genus 3 at larger p

Owner request (2026-11-12): chase the open leads. The transfer is a one-time
cost, because with a trapdoor it happens once, so it never enters a
per-instance total. Three parallel experiments ran, and the integration
session reproduced each from the final source (`repro/REPRO.md`). Every
number is `[pcc:unverified]`.

## 1. Amortized index calculus (`amortized/AMORTIZED.md`)

The trapdoor argument applies to IC's own per-curve work too. Factor-base logs
on `J(C)` are computed once, and each target then costs only a descent. The
protocol was frozen before data. It compared against plain rho and against
Bernstein–Lange (BL) precomputed rho matched by memory and by budget. That was
the control that removed an earlier "IC ahead of rho online" reading here
(`research/20261016-n83-precomputed-rho-control`).

- **Against plain rho:** the online cost per target is 2.0–3.1% of plain rho
  at all 11 primes from 211 to 205,661. At p = 1619 it is about 2,100× below
  the earlier IC that put the target into every relation (15.7 against 33,196
  group ops) [pcc:unverified].
- **Against matched BL rho (the admissible comparison):** IC is 1.3–11% above
  it online in 10 of 11 rows. At p = 25,693 the two tie, and the paired
  interval contains 0. The frozen rule fails in 11 of 11 rows [pcc:unverified].
- **Why (integration analysis):** the factor base is at least as large as the
  target group in every row, with `fb/r` from 0.98 to 125 [pcc:unverified].
  Genus-2 IC over `F_p` needs about `p/2` degree-1 places. The transferred
  elliptic subgroup has order `r ≤ #E ≈ p`. So the precomputation stores a
  log for as many places as the group has elements. With that much memory any
  method answers in a few operations; tabulating all `r` multiples of `G` would
  answer at once. The low online cost is a time–memory trade, which BL rho
  with the same memory reproduces. Amortizing pays only when the factor base
  is much smaller than the group. That holds for an attack on the whole
  Jacobian (about `p^g` elements), not for an elliptic subgroup of size about
  `p` pushed into it.
- The LA here is a weight-2 union-find, 26–148× cheaper than the dense count
  in `results_v2.json`. Pricing it either way leaves the verdict unchanged.

## 2. Certificates, oracle-free LA, real instance (`V3.md`)

These close replay findings F4, F5 and F14 [pcc:unverified]:

- **F4:** IC's elimination no longer checks against a BSGS answer.
- **F5:** the instance pair is `(φ^*(P), φ^*([x]P))` itself.
- **F14:** every solve emits a `chained.solve.certificate/v1`, and
  `verify_certs` passed 16 of 16 [pcc:unverified].

IC/rho is 8.0–40.8× at 0 GAE and 42–754× at 1 GAE. That is within 1.6 of
v2, on the same curves and planted logs.

There is an open instrument question. On this path the LA mulmod counter
equals `(fb + 2) × rows` in every case. That looks like a matrix-size count,
not elimination work.

## 3. Genus 3 at larger p (`tower_large/TOWER_LARGE.md`)

A word-size Jacobian (`hyper64.rs`, `p < 2^31`) matched the `p < 256`
implementation on every comparison at 8 primes before any larger-p number was
taken [pcc:unverified]. Results from p = 251 to 64,007:

- IC3/IC2 is 2.92–3.10 for `p ≥ 503`.
- Trials per relation are 2.00 and 6.04 against `2!` and `3!`.
- IC group ops grow with slope 0.99 in p, and rho walk steps with slope 0.56.
- Genus 2 is 47× rho at p = 64,007 at 0 GAE.
- The only sub-rho cell is genus 2 at p = 251 at 0 GAE (0.67). That ordering
  flips inside the band.
- Genus-3 LA scales with slope 2.1 (2.2·10⁹ mulmods at p = 64,007), and rho on
  E is dominated by fixed set-up (slope 0.21).

## Next steps

1. **Endomorphism-degree census.** A non-integer endomorphism of degree `d`
   needs `x² + |D|y² = 4d` with `y ≠ 0`, so `d ≥ |D|/4`. Count how often lab
   and ecdb curves have one of degree ≤ 11. That decides the reach of the
   small-`D_E` protocol (`research/small_de_endo_fb_20261112/PROTOCOL.md`).
2. **Volcano ascent to the maximal order.** `End(E)` varies within an isogeny
   class and `d_K` does not. Carry the target to the smallest-`|D|` curve in
   its class, counted as a one-time transfer, before endomorphism-adapted IC.
3. **The small-`D_E` experiment itself**, on curves step 1 shows exist.
4. **Cover mechanism test.** Push `J(C)` relations through `c_*` to `E` and
   measure the induced factor base. The bielliptic cover should reproduce
   2-term IC on `E` over about half its points. That makes "the Prym does not
   help" a measurement, and it sets the bar a cover must clear.
5. **Instrument fixes.** Use structured Gaussian elimination plus Wiedemann
   with a counted LA, double-large-prime variation (needed for the
   `Õ(p^{2-2/g})` exponent at `g = 3`), and a rho reference of walk steps plus
   amortized set-up with the negation map.
6. **Validation gate.** Replay certificates, a validator report and
   `VALIDATION.json` for v3 and tower_large, plus a replay by an agent other
   than the producer.
7. **Proof note.** `J_C ~ E × P` and `c_*c^* = [deg c]`: every map out of
   `J_C` either kills the target subgroup or factors through `E`, and a
   smooth-order Prym simplifies only the complement. A Lean statement of the
   `c_*c^*` step would be a candidate for crypto-lean.
