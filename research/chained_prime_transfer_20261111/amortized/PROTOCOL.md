# Protocol: amortized (precompute-once) genus-2 index calculus vs matched precomputed rho

Frozen 2026-11-12, before any measurement of the arms below. Later changes are
dated amendments appended at the end; nothing above them is edited.
All numbers in this file are predictions or definitions, `[pcc:unverified]`.

## Question

The transfer `E1 -> J(C)` (cover construction, isogeny search, `phi^*`) is a [pcc:unverified]
one-time cost and is never added to any total here. The same logic applies to
index calculus (IC) on `J(C)`: the logarithms of the factor-base elements depend
only on the curve and the generator `G`, so they need computing once. Once they
exist, how much does one more target cost, and does that beat rho?

The earlier IC (`measure::genus2_index_calculus`) puts the target into every
relation, so all its work is per target (8.6x to 39.2x of rho at 0 GAE
[pcc:unverified]). This protocol measures the amortized regime and puts the
control that the N83 study required in the comparison: rho given matched
Bernstein-Lange precomputation
(`research/20261016-n83-precomputed-rho-control/README.md`). Amortized IC online
against plain rho alone is not an admissible comparison and is reported only as
a secondary column.

## Group and unit

All arms run in the order-`r` subgroup of `J(C)(F_p)` generated by `G = phi^*(G_E)`, [pcc:unverified]
the real pulled-back generator of the verified transfer (not the
`jac_instance` surrogate). Targets are `T_t = phi^*([x_t] G_E)` with planted
`x_t` uniform in `[1, r)` from a seeded generator. One counted unit is one Cantor
addition or doubling (1 GAE). Linear-algebra work is counted separately in
modular operations over `Z/r` and priced at 0, 0.1 and 1 GAE each (AGENTS.md
section 5a). Hash-table lookups, union-find finds and hashing are not priced in
any arm (stated limitation).

## Arms

1. **plain rho**: `measure::rho_on_jac` per target (`rho_reference` conventions;
   its own `[d]G = T` verification is inside its count).
2. **IC amortized** (three variants, all with the full degree-1 factor base,
   about `p/2` places, as in `chained_transfer_bench`):
   - `ic_comb_q90` (headline): one-time phase draws `R = [alpha]G` from a table
     of `[2^i]G` (the table's doublings are counted), keeps degree-2 reduced
     classes whose two roots are both in the base (weight-2 relations, as in
     `measure.rs`), and stops when at least 90% of the base has a determined
     logarithm. The solve is my own structured elimination exploiting weight-2
     rows (weighted union-find over `Z/r`, each cycle fixes a component), not
     the BSGS-oracle path. 2-torsion places (`y = 0`) have logarithm 0.
     Base elements outside the order-`r` subgroup are handled by projecting with
     the cofactor: the unknowns are logs of `[cof] g_i` base `[cof] G`; since
     `r` is prime and `r^2` does not divide `#J`, `cof` is a unit mod `r` and
     relations among subgroup elements are unchanged. Per target: draw `beta`,
     form `R = T + [beta]G` from the same power table, repeat until both places
     of `R` have determined logs, read `x = sum eps_i L_i - beta`.
   - `ic_walk_q90`: as above, but after the first draw each next candidate is
     the previous one plus a stored jump `[a_j]G` (one addition per trial).
   - `ic_comb_q99`: as the headline with the stop rule at 99% determined.
3. **BL precomputed rho** (r-adding walk, 16 jumps `[a_j]G` with random full
   coefficients; every table point is a known multiple of `G`; distinguished
   probability `2^-k`; walks continue past a distinguished point, restart
   after a table collision or `20*2^k + 64` steps without one; walk starts are
   `[a]G` from the same power table). Online: start at `T + [beta]G`, walk, and
   on a table hit `x = a_table - b`; hits are compared as exact divisors.
   - `bl_b` memory-matched: the table has exactly `fb_size` entries (IC stores
     one log per base element).
   - `bl_a0`, `bl_a01`, `bl_a1` precompute-matched: walk budget in group ops =
     headline IC one-time group ops + price * IC LA ops, price 0, 0.1, 1. The
     table holds all distinguished points found in that budget (memory is not
     limited, which is the generous reading for rho and the N83 convention).
   - `k` is chosen per curve and arm from `0..=bits/2` on 24 tuning targets
     disjoint from the evaluation targets (mean online ops); tuning is not
     charged and is reported.
4. **Certificate**: every recovered log in every arm is checked by a fresh
   double-and-add `[k]G == T` written in `amortized.rs` and compared to the
   planted `x_t`. IC and BL are charged this certificate's group ops in the
   headline online cost (plain rho already includes its own check); the
   uncharged variant is also reported. A failed certificate marks the target
   failed and is a finding, not retried silently.

## Budgets and sizes

Primes 211, 419, 821, 1619, 3203, 6421, 12841 and larger (next prime above twice [pcc:unverified]
the previous) while the total wall time of the row stays under 300 s; the first
row exceeding it is the last. Curve selection is `chained_transfer_bench`'s
(same loop, seeds, `r >= 50`, `r` not dividing `#E2`, isogeny hop and chain
verified, `jac_instance` exists); the four small curves are checked against
`results_v2.json`. 128 targets per curve (the minimum is 64), 24 tuning targets.
Maximum IC trials `200 * p`; a target gets at most `20000 * r` BL steps.

## Reported per arm and prime

One-time cost (group ops, LA ops split into multiplications and total modular
operations, priced total at each price), per-target online cost (mean, median,
min-max), the paired ratio to each BL arm with a 95% percentile bootstrap
interval of the mean (2000 resamples, seeded), and the break-even
`N*` = smallest integer `N` with `OT + N * online < N * online_other`
(`OT_other` included for non-rho comparisons), `inf` if the slope never allows.
The transfer (cover construction and isogeny search wall ms, `phi^*` wall
microseconds per target) is a separate column and never enters a total.

## Decision rule (frozen)

Per prime `p` and price `pi in {0, 0.1, 1}`: let `IC*` be the IC variant with [pcc:unverified]
the lowest online mean (best case for IC). `IC*` **beats** an arm if its
online mean is lower with the bootstrap interval of the paired difference
excluding 0 AND `N*` is finite (so its total falls below the arm's total for
large enough `N`). It **loses** if the online mean is higher or `N*` is `inf`
with the opposite sign; otherwise **mixed**.

- Secondary (plain rho): IC* beats plain rho at `p` when it beats it at all three [pcc:unverified]
  prices. This is an improvement over the old per-target IC baseline and over
  plain rho in the amortized regime; it is not the headline.
- **Headline**: "IC amortized beats matched precomputed rho" is stated at `p`
  only if IC* beats `bl_b` and `bl_a` at all three prices. The overall verdict
  states it only if it holds at every prime >= 1619. Any other outcome is
  reported as "does not beat precomputed rho" with the ratio of IC* to the
  nearest BL arm, online and at N = 64, 1000, 10^6, as the distance remaining.
- A result where any recovered log fails its certificate, or any BL/IC
  comparison is missing, is reported as invalid, not as a loss or win.
- Relabelling the cost accounting after seeing data is not allowed; an amendment
  may add arms or columns but cannot change the rule above.

## Frozen prediction (probabilities, before data)

1. IC* online mean (with certificate) is below plain rho at every prime >= 419: [pcc:unverified]
   0.97.
2. `N*` against plain rho at price 0 is finite and at most 1000 for every prime
   up to 12841, and grows with `p` over the last four sizes: 0.80.
3. The structured solve keeps total LA operations below 10% of IC one-time group
   ops at every prime, so the 0 to 1 GAE band moves `N*` against plain rho by
   less than a factor 2: 0.75.
4. `bl_b` online mean (with certificate) is at most IC* at every prime >= 1619:
   0.60.
5. `bl_b` one-time group ops are below the IC headline one-time group ops at
   every prime: 0.85.
6. `bl_a` (budget matched to IC) has online mean at most IC* at every prime
   >= 1619 and every price: 0.90 (a table covering the group several times over
   leaves a few walk steps).
7. Headline "IC amortized beats matched precomputed rho" holds at every prime
   >= 1619: 0.07. Probability the verdict is "does not beat": 0.93.
8. IC* online (with certificate) is at least 100 times below the old
   target-in-every-relation IC per-target cost at p = 1619: 0.90.

## Amendment 1 (2026-11-12, after run 1 data; run 1 kept as `run1_*`)

Run 1 (frozen protocol, unchanged) showed that the best IC variant (`ic_walk_q90`)
has a much lower one-time cost than the headline `ic_comb_q90` to which the
`bl_a*` budgets were matched (for example p = 6421: 9,234 group ops against
55,184). Budget-matching BL to the headline variant while testing a cheaper
variant against it is not matched. This amendment adds arms and changes no rule:

- `bl_s0`, `bl_s01`, `bl_s1`: BL tables whose budget is IC*'s own one-time cost [pcc:unverified]
  (group ops + price * LA ops) at price 0, 0.1, 1. The `bl_a*` arms and
  `bl_b_memory` stay.
- The decision rule's "beats `bl_a`" now requires beating `bl_a*` and `bl_s*`
  and `bl_b_memory` (a strictly harder condition for IC*).
- Run 2 is the reported run; run 1 files are kept for transparency. Run 1 had
  already shown the verdict "does not beat", so this amendment was made with
  knowledge of the run-1 outcome; it can only make BL cheaper, not IC better.
