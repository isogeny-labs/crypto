## Coverage boundary

The executed original EC algebraic mechanism is #24's bounded Semaev-resultant specialization. #6, #8, #14, #16, #21, #27, #29, and #31 have narrower controls; none of those controls executes the proposal as written. The other rows have an exact premise screen or a named missing construction. A screen of the literal proposal does not exclude a redesigned algorithm. Every future experiment must retain exact target identity, signed point witnesses, independent rank, and a charged same-target baseline when making solver claims.
