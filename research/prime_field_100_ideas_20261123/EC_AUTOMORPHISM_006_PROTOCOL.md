# Frozen finite EC automorphism orbit quotient, proposal 6

Frozen 2026-11-25 (America/Los_Angeles), draft PR #5165, before producer execution. The submitted #6 proposes quotienting a factor base under a large nonabelian `PGL₂(F_p)` action. Generic `PGL₂` is not a point-group automorphism of an elliptic curve, as the prior [#6 screen](EC_LEADS_001_034_PROTOCOL.md) showed. This experiment tests the **valid finite subgroup** generated by `φ(x,y)=(ζx,y)` on a public `j=0` CM curve, with `ζ³=1` and `ζ≠1`. It makes no claim for full `PGL₂`.

## Fixed curve, base, and target

Use `E/F_1009:y²=x³+3`, `#E=948=12·79`; take the first deterministic nonzero point `G` of order 79 found by scanning liftable `x≥1`, choosing GP's square root, and multiplying by 12. Find the unique nontrivial scalar `λ∈F_79` with `φ(G)=[λ]G` by bounded scan `1..78`; guard `λ³=1`, `λ≠1`. Traverse `[k]G` for `k=1..78` and select the first five **point** orbits of size three under `φ`; the first four provide 12 distinct factor-base points, ordered `rep,φ(rep),φ²(rep)`, and the fifth orbit's representative is the one fixed target `R`, excluded even up to sign from the base. Validate every point and subgroup order. This is one fixed public subgroup and one target; no outcome-based target changes.

## Same-target comparison and exact checks

Baseline: enumerate all `12³×8=13,824` ordered triples and sign patterns, compute each signed three-point sum (two group additions), compare with `R`, and record the complete witness mask keyed by `(i,j,k,mask)` and 13-column exact rows `[base coefficients|-1 target]`. Quotient: partition the 1,728 ordered triples into simultaneous `φ` orbits of size three, choose lexicographically least triple, and evaluate all eight sign patterns only for each of 576 representatives. For each representative signed sum `Q`, compare against the three preimages `R,φ⁻¹(R),φ⁻²(R)`. A match for preimage `e` induces the exact original candidate and row after applying `φ^e` to all three base points. Compare complete 13,824-bit witness masks; stop on mismatch or duplicate. Count actual point additions, target comparisons, orbit-classification operations, and CPU/wall milliseconds separately. A lower group-add count can coexist with equal target-comparison count and nontrivial setup; this is a component comparison, not a complete ECDLP cost.

Project each exact 13-column relation row to five columns (four base-orbit representatives and target) using the certified scalar `λ^e` for each orbit position. Verify every projected row as a literal signed point identity on the curve, and report both original and projected modular ranks over `F_79`. The projected rank lives in a different column space; equality of the two numeric ranks is not required. Retain all full rows and target identity through quotient reconstruction. Set-up of `G`, `ζ`, `λ`, base, and target is charged as common setup; orbit-classification setup is charged only to the quotient. All original rows remain represented, including dependent rows.

Its deterministic subgroup generator was `G=(378,580)`, and the fixed target was `R=(385,631)=[5]G`; the 12 base points form four order-three point orbits with representative scalars 1, 2, 3, 4. The independent qualifier checked all 12 emitted base coordinates against `[k]G`, the target against `[5]G`, and the scalar orbit action `k↦55k (mod 79)`. The V2 source additionally checked every projected row as an exact point identity using the four orbit representatives and the target. That quotient-only validator makes 612 more `elladd` calls and 612 small-scalar `ellmul` calls for the 153 rows; these are included in its elapsed time but **not** in the signed-sum addition counter above. The independent scalar qualifier checked the same projected identities modulo 79 and reproduced 153 hits, 576 triple-orbit representatives, the full witness mask, and ranks 12 and 4. This preserves the complete original witness set and its full-row rank while reducing *candidate signed-sum additions* by exactly a factor of three. It does **not** yield a measured collection-time reduction on this small instance: quotient reconstruction and extra point-certificate work keep the observed collection times nearly equal, and orbit classification adds 5 ms. Because the timed arms perform different validation work, their wall times are a diagnostic, not a clean performance comparison. GP integer-millisecond timings are also too coarse for a stable speed estimate.

**Setup-accounting correction.** The V2 `common_setup_cpu_ms=0` / `common_setup_wall_ms=0` timer starts *after* the deterministic search for `ζ`, `G`, and `λ`, so it times only the shared orbit/base/target construction. The earlier frozen prose said all common setup was charged; the source and raw result show that the first three shared searches were not timed. They are identical input-generation work for both arms and do not affect the measured 5 ms *incremental* orbit-classification cost, but the reported total is **not** a complete cold setup charge. A cold setup comparison needs a separately frozen instrumented run. The quotient also assumes a known group automorphism and its subgroup scalar action; finding those on another curve must be charged. There is no full `PGL₂` reduction or complete index-calculus comparison here.
