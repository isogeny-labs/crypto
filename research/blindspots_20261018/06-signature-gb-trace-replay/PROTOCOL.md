# Item 06 protocol: a signature-based Boolean Groebner engine and Joux-Vitse trace replay

Frozen before any measured run. Source design: section "06" of
`research/blindspots_20261018/EXPERIMENTS.md` and its "Rules every item
follows". Written by the experiment-director lane, 2026-10-18.

Class: **stage diagnostic** (reductions to zero, matrix entries touched and peak
memory, for one oracle under four engines). It computes no `S`, no rho ratio and
no GAE. It is not a cross-method comparison (every arm is a Groebner engine on
the same polynomial system), so the section 5a three-point pricing band
(0, 0.1, 1 GAE per native operation) does not apply to its own rows. An arm that
wins is reported as a multiplicative factor on the oracle term of item 02's
model, and item 02's band prices it there. The gap to matched rho is therefore
not changed by this item and is reported as the unchanged distance remaining.

## Question

Does a signature-based Boolean engine (GVW/F5 criteria over the Boolean ring,
squares and Frobenius syzygies included) eliminate the reductions to zero that
dominate the degree-4 steps of the descended chained Semaev `S_3` systems, and
does a recorded trace of one target (Joux-Vitse F4Remake) amortise symbolic
preprocessing and the zero reductions across the other targets of the same
shape?

Cell notation is `(n, l, m)`: field degree `n`, factor-base subspace dimension
`l`, `m` summands. Unknowns `N = m*l + (m-2)*n`: `m*l` leaf bits then `(m-2)*n`
intermediate bits. Equations are the `n` bit coordinates of each of the `m-1`
links (the item 05 builder, same bit layout, same sparse irreducible).

## What already exists, and what is reused (read before designing)

* **The incumbent is the F4 of item 05, not `inherited_f4`.** The design's
  arm (a) is "incumbent inherited F4". `src/cryptanalysis/inherited_f4.rs` is a
  fixed-degree Macaulay reducer around a splitting tree: it selects no pairs,
  so it has no reduction to zero in the sense of the design, and its unit is
  64-bit word XORs on packed rows. The committed Boolean-ring F4 that selects
  pairs, builds symbolic-preprocessing matrices and counts entries touched is
  the one of `research/blindspots_20261018/05-elimination-orders/PROTOCOL.md`
  (its `grevlex` arm), which in turn re-implements the item 07 builder. Arm (a)
  here is that engine, re-implemented verbatim (the same `add`/`step`/`run`) in
  this instrument, and **validated by reproducing the committed item 05
  `grevlex` rows bit for bit** (control 2). Its reading on the same shapes
  (`research/blindspots_20261018/05-elimination-orders/raw/card-02-main-c17-4-3.
  stdout.log`: 6,347 of 8,063 selected pairs reduce to zero on target 0 of
  `(17,4,3)`; 241-348 million touches; `D_mat = 4`) is the anchor: the design's
  premise (zero reductions dominate the degree-4 step) is already measured
  there, and is re-measured here on this item's own draws.
* **Trace replay was measured once, for a different engine, and was a loss.**
  `research/pdp_shape_replay_20261015/README.md`: recording which Macaulay rows
  of the *inherited* (splitting-tree) F4 are dependent on the first root of a
  shape and dropping them from later roots cost 0.95-0.98x (default divided by
  replay) on nine registered cells, with recording charged. That result is for
  the splitting-tree oracle at `n` prime, stage word XORs, and is **not reused
  as an arm reading**: arms (c) and (d) here replay pair selection and reducer
  choice of a pair-selecting F4 on the four design cells, a different object. It
  is cited as the prior and as the reason replay is charged here exactly as
  there (recording and every fallback in full).
* **msolve (arm e) is not run.** msolve is not installed on this host; building
  it needs GMP, FLINT and MPFR development headers (none installed) and the
  disk free is 3.8 GB. Its only readings on these systems are the committed
  `research/ic_next10_20261014/06-equivariant-f4-m4` F4 step degrees (`D_F4 = 4`
  on the m = 3 and m = 4 draws inspected) and wall times, in msolve's own unit;
  they cannot enter a column-touch ratio. Arm (e) is reported **BLOCKED**
  (Deviation 1); what would unblock it is an msolve build with its libraries and
  a counter for matrix entries, neither of which the rule needs.
* **Nothing else of the design is already measured.** No committed result holds a
  signature-based Boolean engine or a pair-level trace replay on these cells.
  The draws of the four cells are not in the repository (item 05 holds three
  of the four cells but with eight targets each and a different seed) and are
  generated by the instrument (`gen`), committed in `draws.tsv` and bound into
  every card by sha256.

## The instrument

`examples/blindspots_06_signature_gb.rs`, native Rust, standard library only,
compiled by each card with `rustc --edition 2021 -C opt-level=3
-C target-cpu=native` (Deviation 2). One binary, five modes: `gen`, `run`,
`selftest`, `xcheck`, `summarize`.

Common to every arm: the ring `B = F_2[x]/(x_i^2 + x_i)`, squarefree monomials
as masks, one monomial order (degree, then grevlex with variable 0 largest: the
committed DegRevLex convention), the same equations in the same (construction)
order, the same refutation test (a new element whose leading monomial is `1`).

### Arm (a) `f4`: the incumbent

Item 05's `grevlex` engine, unchanged in algorithm: pairs of leading monomials
plus field-equation pairs `(g, x)` for `x` in `LM(g)`; Gebauer-Moeller product
and chain criteria on ordinary pairs; **normal strategy** (all pairs of least
degree of the lcm per matrix, a field pair of `g` has degree `deg LM(g) + 1`);
symbolic preprocessing adds, for every monomial of the matrix divisible by a
leading monomial, one reducer `t*g` (the divisor with the fewest terms, then the
earliest); each S-row is accumulated densely and reduced left to right by every
pivot it meets; a row that keeps a non-pivot column becomes a new pivot; new
pivots are back-substituted against each other; new elements are added smallest
leading monomial first.

### Arm (b) `sig`: the signature-based engine (GVW/F5 criteria over `B`)

The same pair/batch/matrix skeleton as (a) (so the comparison isolates the
signature machinery, not the linear algebra), with these changes and nothing
else.

* **Labels.** Each basis element carries a signature `(i, s)`: the index `i` of
  the input generator it descends from (generators in construction order) and a
  squarefree monomial `s`. The module order is position over term: a higher
  index is larger; the same index compares monomials in the order above.
  Initial elements are `(i, 1)`.
* **Signature of a pair.** For elements `a, b` with `t_a = lcm \ LM(a)` and
  `t_b = lcm \ LM(b)`, the labels of the two multiples are `t_a*sig(a)` and
  `t_b*sig(b)`, taken in the polynomial ring (exponents add, so a variable in
  both the label and the multiplier gives a square). The larger label is the
  pair's signature; equal labels are a **singular** pair and are dropped. The
  S-row carries the larger label. A field pair `(a, x)` has the label
  `x*sig(a)` (the Koszul pair of `a` with `x^2 + x`, whose generator has the
  lowest index).
* **Criteria (each is a syzygy whose leading term divides the label, so the pair
  is redundant; all are sound for any pair order, no rewritten criterion is
  used).** A candidate with label `(i, s)` is discarded when
  * **K0 (square)**: `s` has a square. The Koszul syzygy of the generator with a
    field equation has leading term `x^2 e_i`. This also removes every field
    pair `(a, x)` with `x` in `sig(a)`.
  * **K1 (Frobenius)**: `LM(f_i)` divides `s`. In `B`, `f_i^2 = f_i`, so
    `(f_i + 1) e_i` is a syzygy of leading term `LM(f_i) e_i`.
  * **K2 (lower-index Koszul, the F5 criterion)**: some basis element `y` of
    index `< i` has `LM(y)` dividing `s`. `f_i * v_y - y * e_i` is a syzygy of
    leading term `LM(y) e_i`.
  * **K3 (principal syzygies of the pair's elements)**: for `x` one of the two
    elements of the pair with index `i` and `y` any basis element of index `i`,
    the syzygy `f_y v_x - f_x v_y` has leading term the larger of `LM(y)*sig(x)`
    and `LM(x)*sig(y)`. If that term is squarefree and divides `s`, discard.
    With `y` the pair's other element this discards every coprime pair (the
    product criterion).
  * **K4 (discovered syzygies)**: an S-row of label `(i, s0)` that reduces to
    zero (or is empty when built) is a syzygy of leading term `(i, s0)`; any
    later candidate whose label `s` is divisible by `s0` at index `i` is
    discarded.
* **Regular reduction.** Rows (S-rows and reducers `t*g`, the latter labelled
  `t*sig(g)` in the polynomial ring, so a reducer whose multiplier meets its
  label in a variable has a square label) are eliminated in increasing label
  order, and a row is reduced only by a pivot whose label is strictly smaller.
  A row whose leading column is held by a pivot of equal label is
  super-top-reducible and is dropped. The reducer chosen for a monomial is the
  divisor of least label (fewest terms on a tie), not the one with the fewest
  terms.
* **No Gebauer-Moeller thinning.** Buchberger's chain criterion is not combined
  with the signature criteria (their combination needs a signature-aware
  chain rule); K3 reproduces the product criterion, K2/K4 the F5 and GVW
  syzygy criteria. All unprocessed pairs of the selected degree are candidates.
* **Batches.** The same normal strategy as (a): all unprocessed pairs of least
  lcm degree per matrix. This fixes only the order in which pairs are
  processed; the criteria above are order independent, so completeness does not
  depend on it.
* **Termination and output.** Same as (a). A finished basis is a Groebner basis
  of `I + <x_i^2 + x_i>` (certified on toys by Control 1); on the refutation
  cells the end state is `1 in I`.

### Arms (c) `f4r` and (d) `sigr`: Joux-Vitse trace replay (F4Remake)

A **trace** is recorded by running (a) (respectively (b)) on the **held-out
target** of the cell (index 16 of the cell's 17 draws; the measured targets are
0-15, so the trace target is never measured). It holds
* the set of **useful pair keys**: the pairs whose S-row became a new basis
  element (for (a) the key is the unordered pair of leading monomials and the
  variable; for (b) the unordered pair of `(index, signature, leading
  monomial)` triples and the variable), and
* the **reducer map**: for every monomial of every matrix, the key of the basis
  element chosen as its reducer.

**Replay** on a target runs the same engine with two changes: a selected pair
is dropped, with no row built, unless its key is in the useful set; and
symbolic preprocessing looks a monomial up in the reducer map first (one
divisibility test if the recorded element exists and divides it) and searches
the basis only on a miss. Every divisibility test of symbolic preprocessing is
counted (`sym_tests`).

A replay is **sound** because it only ever adds rows of the ideal: a refutation
it reaches is a certificate. If the replay's pair list runs out without `1`, it
is a **fallback**: the full engine is run from scratch on the target, and the
target's cost is the replay's plus the full run's. The trace is **charged**: the
held-out run's touches are added to the cell total (Rule). Two descriptive
replays bracket the effect: `f4rs`/`sigrs` replay on the trace's own target (the
ceiling: everything predicted is right), and `f4rx` is the fallback control
(Control 4).

### Counted quantities

Per (cell, target, arm): status (`refuted`, `gb`, or a cap: `timelimit`,
`memcap`, `colscap`), `D_mat`, `D_sel`, steps, candidate pairs, pairs selected,
pairs discarded by each criterion (K0-K4, singular), S-rows built,
**reductions to zero** (S-rows empty when built plus S-rows that reduce to zero
in elimination), super-top-reducible drops, new elements, final basis size,
rows and columns of the largest matrix (and their product, and the stored
entries), the sum of columns, `touches` (matrix entries written while building
rows, S-rows and reducers, plus entries toggled by every row operation of the
elimination, plus entries toggled by back-substitution: the repo's
"monomial-column touch" at the granularity of one entry, identical definition
in every arm), `sym_tests`, wall time, and **peak memory per solve** (the
resident high-water mark, reset before each solve through `/proc/self/clear_refs`,
and an analytic estimate of the matrix storage as a fallback if the reset is
refused). Wall time is descriptive.

## Controls

1. **Instrument soundness (selftest, exact, positive control).** On toy cells
   with 6 to 20 unknowns (the item 05 toys plus random Boolean systems of 8-12
   unknowns, satisfiable and unsatisfiable), `f4`, `sig` and `sig0` are run to
   the end. An unsatisfiable system must end `1 in I`. A satisfiable system must
   end with a finished basis whose **reduced Groebner basis (minimal basis,
   fully tail-reduced) is identical for the three engines** and whose number of
   standard monomials equals the brute-force solution count; every basis
   element must vanish on every solution. The replays are run on every toy
   with their own trace and with a trace of another toy: each ends `1 in I`
   (refuted) with a certificate or falls back, and never reports a finished
   basis for an unsatisfiable system. This is the design's "basis equality
   across arms as positive control" on the cells where the basis is not
   `{1}`: on the unsatisfiable measured cells every basis is `{1}`, so
   equality there means every arm refutes every target. Any failure exits 2.
2. **Code-identity control (reuses a committed result).** `b06 xcheck` runs
   `f4` on item 05's committed draws for `c17-4-3` targets 0-7 and compares
   `touches`, `build`, `elim`, `back`, `zero_red` and `D_mat` with item 05's
   committed `grevlex` lines. All eight must agree exactly, or the arm is not
   the incumbent that item 05 measured, and the run exits 2.
3. **Sensitivity / ablation control (the right null for the signature
   criteria).** `sig0` is the same engine, labels and linear algebra without
   K1-K4. A win of `sig` over `f4` that `sig0` shares is not due to the
   criteria; the rule is applied to `sig` and the table prints `sig0` beside
   it.
4. **Fallback control for replay.** `f4rx` replays on `c17-4-3` targets with
   the trace of the held-out target of `c13-3-4`: the keys do not match, every
   pair is dropped, the replay exhausts and the full run follows. Its cost must
   be the replay's small cost plus `f4`'s, i.e. a ratio at or just below 1; if it
   is above 1, the accounting is giving replay a free lunch and the run exits 2.
5. **Peak-memory sanity.** The resident high-water mark of each solve is
   reported next to an analytic storage estimate; a ratio outside 0.2-20 is
   printed as a warning (descriptive, not a gate).

## Metric, pairing and the decision rule (frozen)

All comparisons are **paired by target** against `f4` on the same system, over
the targets 0-15 on which both finished (`refuted`).

* touches ratio `R_X = touches(f4) / cost_X`, with `cost_sig = touches(sig)`
  and `cost_sigr = touches(sigr) + touches(sig on the held-out target) / 16`
  (the trace is charged, amortised over the 16 measured targets; a fallback
  adds the full run). The cell statistic is the geometric mean of `R_X` over the
  targets.
* zero-reduction ratio, largest-matrix ratio, `sym_tests` ratio and peak-memory
  ratio, same pairing, reported beside it.
* **identical bases.** A target counts for a cell only if both `f4` and `X`
  ended `refuted` (basis `{1}`); control 1 certifies equality of the finished
  bases where they are not `{1}`.

A cell **qualifies** for `X` when the geometric mean `R_X >= 1.5` **and**
`R_X >= 1.5` on at least 12 of the 16 targets (Deviation 5), and every target
of the cell was refuted by `f4` and `X`.

* **ADVANCES (constant)**: `X` in {`sig`, `sigr`} qualifies in at least **3 of
  the 4 cells** (the design's "at least 3/4 cells" with "identical bases").
  The level is **constant**: a multiplicative factor on the oracle's
  touches. The replay ratio, `touches(f4)/cost_f4r` and
  `touches(sig)/cost_sigr`, is reported separately and is not itself a verdict
  line.
* **TRADES**: `X` qualifies on touches in at least 3 cells but its largest
  matrix is more than 1.5x larger, or its peak memory is more than 1.5x larger,
  than `f4`'s in those cells.
* **REGRESSES**: not ADVANCES or TRADES, and the geometric mean `R_sig` is
  below `1/1.5` in at least 3 cells (`f4` remains the best engine).
* **MATCHES**: all four cells countable and none of the above.
* **INCONCLUSIVE**: fewer than 3 countable cells (a cell is countable when `f4`
  and the compared arm refuted at least 12 of 16 targets), or a control failed.
* **BLOCKED**: a cell the instrument cannot run at the host's limits is named
  with the cap that stopped it and counts as not countable. Arm (e) is BLOCKED
  as above.

The same rule is applied by `b06 summarize` mechanically over the recorded JSON
lines; the summary reads the numbers and does not interpret them.

## Budget and execution

One thread per job (the instrument is single-threaded). Every run is wrapped in
`prlimit --as=4294967296` (4 GiB); the instrument stops itself with `memcap` at
88% of the limit and `colscap` at 400,000 columns. At most 2 CPU-hours of
measured runs, 45 minutes per job (a per-job `--time-limit` is always set),
every card under 9 minutes (each command has a hard timeout; the instrument's
`--total-limit` records `skipped` instead of starting a job that cannot finish).
Build products live in `target/blindspots-06/` (no per-item `CARGO_TARGET_DIR`;
`rustc`, not cargo, see Deviation 2) and card 1 removes nothing it needs later;
the last card removes the binary. Raw outputs and receipts go to
`research/blindspots_20261018/06-signature-gb-trace-replay/raw/`.

The card plan (cards, arms, per-job caps and the summed time limits) is appended
below once the instrument's speed on the development draws is known; it is
committed before any card is run.

## Deviations from the frozen design

Each tightens the design or makes it concrete; where one narrows a listed arm
or cell it says so.

1. **Arm (e) msolve is not run** (blocked: not installed, library headers
   absent, 3.8 GB disk free). It is outside the rule, whose comparison is (b)
   or (d) against (a). The committed msolve step degrees are cited above.
2. **Build tool.** The design's build command is `cargo build --release
   --example`; the instrument is std-only and is compiled by `rustc` to a path
   under `target/blindspots-06/`, as items 03, 05 and 07 do (the library has
   not compiled at some points of this branch, and a std-only file needs
   none of it).
3. **Arm (a) is item 05's F4, not `inherited_f4`** (reason above); the
   identity of the engine with item 05's committed rows is a control, not an
   assumption.
4. **Hand-written signature criteria, no rewritten criterion, no
   Gebauer-Moeller thinning in (b).** The design allows "GVW or F5 with
   rewritten-criterion". The rewritten criterion depends on the order in which
   elements are added and is delicate in a batched (F4-style) setting; the
   chosen syzygy criteria (K0-K4) are order independent and each has a stated
   proof sketch above; the cost is that (b) may process pairs a full F5 would
   rewrite. If (b) loses, the verdict says it is this criteria set that lost.
   Completeness is checked rather than assumed (Control 1; every measured
   target must end `refuted`).
5. **Rule tightened.** The design asks for `>= 1.5x` column touches on `>= 3/4`
   cells with identical bases. Here a cell also needs `>= 1.5x` on at least 12
   of 16 targets (not only on the geometric mean), the trace is charged, and
   fallbacks are charged in full. Each only makes ADVANCES harder.
6. **Unsatisfiable targets only**, chosen by an exact count; a satisfiable
   system's full basis is a different, larger computation (covered by the
   selftest).
7. **Peak memory per solve** is the resident high-water mark of the process after
   resetting it before the solve; the instrument runs the jobs of a card in one
   process, so the reset (not process boundaries) is what makes it per solve.
8. **Equation order is the construction order** (link 1 bits, then link 2 bits,
   ...); F5-type engines are sensitive to the order of the generators, and no
   other order is measured. Reported as a limit of the result.
9. **The held-out target** is the 17th draw of each cell, generated like the
   others; it is not a different system class. A replay on a target whose
   leading-monomial structure differs from the trace's fails by construction;
   how often that happens is itself a result.

### A. The engine as built (supersedes "The instrument" where it differs)

`examples/blindspots_06_signature_gb.rs` (sha256 `d9d7ffc3...a6d1`, bound by every
card). Arm (a) `f4` is bit-identical to item 05's `grevlex` (control 2, passed in
the smoke test on all eight committed `c17-4-3` rows; `smoke-test.txt`).

Arm (b) `sig`:

* **Row = the larger multiple.** The F5/GVW formulation: a pair `(a, b)` contributes
  the single row `t_x * X` of the larger-signature side (the polynomial ring order
  on `t * sig`, exponents add; position over term, higher index larger), not the
  S-polynomial. The smaller multiple is cancelled in elimination by the reducer of
  least label for the lcm. Two pairs with the same `(X, t_x)` in one batch give one
  row. (This replaces "the S-row carries the larger label" above.)
* **Criteria.** K0 (square), K1 (Frobenius), K2 (lower-index Koszul), K3 (principal
  syzygy of the pair's elements with an element of the same index; with the other
  element of the pair it is the product criterion) and K4 (discovered syzygies) are
  as defined above. **K5, the rewritable criterion, is added**: a pair with label
  `(i, s)` is dropped when an element of index `i` that is *newer* than the larger
  side `X` has a signature dividing `s` (F5's rewritten criterion with the
  "newest representative" rule). This supersedes Deviation 4's "no rewritten
  criterion" (the design asks for one).
* **Soundness of K5 is not proved here.** The proof of the rewritten criterion
  assumes pairs are processed in increasing signature order; a batch by lcm degree
  (the normal strategy, kept so that the comparison isolates the signature
  machinery) does not give that order. Completeness is therefore *checked*: the
  selftest runs `f4`, `sig`, `sig` without each criterion, each criterion alone and
  the construction-order variant on 132 systems (60 unsatisfiable, 72 satisfiable;
  the toy cells of item 05 and random Boolean systems of 6-11 unknowns, under- and
  over-determined) and requires identical reduced Groebner bases, brute-force
  standard-monomial counts and vanishing on every solution, and every measured
  target must end `refuted`. A wrong prune that lost completeness would show as a
  `gb` status on an unsatisfiable system.
* **Generators** are taken in ascending degree (stable): the signature index of an
  equation is its rank by degree. This is a pre-registered convention (F5 engines
  are order sensitive); `sigc` keeps the construction order and is a descriptive
  arm. `f4` keeps the construction order (as item 05).
* **Elimination.** Rows are processed in increasing label; a column may hold
  several pivots (a raw reducer and a reduced S-row can share a leading column);
  a row is reduced by the sparsest pivot of strictly smaller label; raw reducers
  are installed at their label position and are never reduced; a row whose leading
  column holds only a pivot of equal label is super-top-reducible and is dropped.
  Back-substitution among the new pivots obeys the same label rule.
* **Symbolic preprocessing** takes, for each monomial, the divisor of least label
  (fewest terms on a tie), found by looking up the submasks of the monomial among
  the leading monomials. `sym_tests` therefore counts lookups for `sig` and basis
  scans for `f4`: it is comparable inside an engine (replay against none), not
  between engines.
* **Ablations** (descriptive, same engine): `sig4` = K0-K4 (no rewritable
  criterion), `sig0` = K0 and singular pairs only (labels exact, no pruning),
  `sigc` = construction order. They supersede the "`sig0` = K1-K4 off" sentence.

Arms (c) and (d), replay. Two filters on the same trace, both sound (a replay only
adds rows of the ideal; a replay that runs out of pairs without `1` falls back to
the full run, both charged):

* the **exact** F4Remake filter (`f4r`, `sigr`): process only the pairs the trace
  found useful, and take the reducers the trace chose, per matrix step, by basis
  index; this is the arm of the design and the arm of the rule's replay ratio;
* the **negative** filter (`f4n`, `sign`): drop only the pairs the trace found to
  reduce to zero and keep every other pair, the reading of the survey lead
  (`research/pdp_shape_replay_20261015`: "drop the dependent rows"). Descriptive.

Pair keys are the unordered pair of leading monomials plus the variable (`f4`) or
of `(index, signature, leading monomial)` triples (`sig`). Matrix steps that
contribute no new element are not numbered, so a replay that skips them stays
aligned with its trace. `f4rs`/`sigrs` replay the target's own trace (the
ceiling); `f4rx:c13-3-4` replays another cell's trace (control 4).

### B. Stopping a signature job, and what a stopped job says (tightens the rule)

Development readings on the held-out targets and on separate development draws
(below) showed that `sig` does not finish in any practical multiple of the work of
`f4` on these systems (it reached selection degree 5 on `c13-3-4`, `c15-5-3`, and
exceeded 20,000 basis elements without reaching `1` on a `c17-4-3` target). A
signature job is therefore stopped when its touches exceed **2 times those of `f4`
on the same target** (`--touch-ratio 2`; status `touchcap`), or at its time limit
(`--sig-time-limit`), the 400,000-column cap or the memory cap. The stopped job's
touches include the unfinished matrix. Because the work done so far is a lower
bound on the work needed, **R = touches(f4) / touches(X) is bounded above by
touches(f4) / touches(X at the stop)**. The rule is applied with these bounds:

* a cell **qualifies** only if all 16 targets finished (`refuted`) for `f4` and
  `X` (unchanged), so a stopped target can never help ADVANCES;
* a cell is **regress-certain** for `sig` when all 16 targets are either exact or
  bounded and the geometric mean of the exact ratios and the bounds is `<= 1/1.5`
  (the mean of upper bounds bounds the true mean from above);
* **REGRESSES** is `sig` regress-certain in at least 3 cells (not ADVANCES or
  TRADES); **MATCHES** needs all four cells exact; otherwise INCONCLUSIVE or
  BLOCKED as before. `summarize` prints the bound with a `*` and never an exact
  ratio for a stopped job.
* the trace of arm (d) is recorded with a looser stop (6 times, `--trace-ratio 6`).
  If the recording run does not refute there is no trace, the target is recorded
  `notrace`, and arm (d) is **BLOCKED** for the cell with that reason.

### C. Development exposure (disclosed)

Before this protocol was extended, the instrument was run on: item 05's committed
`c17-4-3` draws (target 0 only, to find why `sig` did not terminate and to compare
three generator orders), two development draw files (seeds 31337 and 777; cells
`c17-4-3`, `c13-3-4`, `c15-3-4`, `c15-5-3`, 2-3 targets each) and the held-out
target 16 of each frozen cell (to size the cards: `f4` 0.6-21 s, `sig` stopped at

Item 05's committed draws are shared with this item only through control 2.

### E. Deviations added

10. **Row = larger multiple, K5 and the order of generators** (A above) are choices of the
    engine; `sig` may lose because of them. Reported as limits of the result.
11. **Two replay filters.** The design's replay is the exact filter; the negative filter is
    added because the exact one cannot transfer between targets whose leading-monomial
    structure differs (shown on development draws: it fell back), and the survey lead was
    the negative one. Only the exact one is arm (c)/(d) of the rule's replay ratio.
12. **A signature job is stopped at 2 times the touches of `f4`** (B above).
13. **Peak memory** is the resident high-water mark after a reset before each solve
    (`/proc/self/clear_refs`); a stopped job's peak is not comparable with a finished one.
14. **Per-card build** instead of one build in the first card (cards cannot share a file).
