# Item 07 protocol: blocked Boolean closure (F4-equivalent) for the deciding degree cells

Frozen before any measured run. Source design: section "07" of
`research/blindspots_20261018/EXPERIMENTS.md` and its "Rules every item
follows". Written by the experiment-director lane, 2026-10-18. This item may
use a 7 GiB address-space cap per job (`prlimit --as=7516192768`); no other
item may.

Class: **stage diagnostic** (a solving degree). It computes no `S`, no rho
ratio and no GAE, so the section 5a three-point pricing band does not apply
to this item's own rows; it feeds item 02's `D` rows, which are priced there.

## Question

What is the solving degree `D` of the chained Semaev `S_3` descent system at
`(m, l) = (4, 5)`, `(4, 6)` and `(3, 6)`, measured by a memory-blocked
elimination, and where does the ladder `(n, l, m)` = (11,4,4), (13,4,4),
(15,5,4), (17,5,4), (19,6,4), (19,6,3) stop being computable on this host?

Cell notation is `(n, l, m)`: field degree `n`, factor-base subspace
dimension `l`, `m` summands; unknowns `N = m*l + (m-2)*n`, equations `3n`
at `m = 4` (`n` per `S_3` link). `D` is measured at degree-`D` column count
`C(N, <= D)`.

## Arms

* **(a) Blocked closure, the candidate (new, native Rust, std only).**
  `examples/blindspots_07_blocked_closure.rs`. The object computed is the
  MutantXL fixed-degree closure `V_D` of the system in the Boolean ring: the
  smallest subspace of degree-`<= D` polynomials containing the equations and
  closed under multiplication of any element of degree `< D` by a variable.
  `1 in V_D` is the refutation; `D` is the least such degree. The Macaulay
  matrix is never built whole: columns are indexed by the combinatorial rank
  of the squarefree monomial (so no hash map and no column list), the basis
  is kept in reduced echelon form with **triangular rows** (only the words
  from the pivot word on are stored), and product rows are reduced in **row
  blocks of K rows** streamed against the basis, with each block's new pivots
  folded into the basis in a single pass over it (the blocked panel pass).
  Counted: 64-bit word XORs, bytes of basis streamed and read, rows x columns
  per degree, basis bytes, peak RSS. This is the design's "blocked F4
  elimination ... row blocks" without a disk tier (Deviation 3).
* **(b) G2 equivariant pass: not applicable at the ladder cells.** The cells
  are random-subspace draws of the chained system; they carry no Frobenius
  symmetry to quotient. The committed G2 pass (item 06 section 3, bit-identical
  to the full `F_2` RREF at n = 7, 11, 13, 17) acts on a different system and
  is cited. Recorded as "not run, condition not met".
* **(c) msolve: reference only, committed results reused** (above). No new
  msolve run.
* **Incumbent for the candidate:** the committed dense in-core closure
  (`closure_refutation_scan`, `ReducedBasis::from_system_closed_full`) at
  (13,4,4), citing its peak RSS, word ops and wall. The candidate is run on
  the **same** committed draw (closure-study draw 1) and on item 06's draw u0
  (the draw msolve crashed on).

## Controls

* **Instrument soundness (selftest).** Combinatorial rank / unrank are
  inverse on every column of a small cell; the DegRevLex order (degree, then
  numerically smaller mask first) agrees with the committed `cmp_mono` on all
  pairs; the symbolic `S_3` chain system built here has exactly the committed
  number of solutions on the committed small draws (exhaustive enumeration of
  the polynomials at N <= 20, and the field-level exact counter on the cells
  up to (11,4,4) against the `solutions` fields in the committed JSONL).
* **Positive control (the design's).** At (11,4,4) the blocked closure
  reproduces the committed degree and basis: `D = 4` and `1 in V_4` on draw u0
  (draw 3 of seed 20261014: v_basis 493,1083,1298,447; x_R = 33), the
  committed msolve reading; `D = 4` on u1..u3 if budget allows. In addition,
  on the committed small m = 4 draws (cells (4,1) to (10,3), four draws each)
  the per-draw closure degree equals the committed `closure_degree` (the
  incumbent engine) and, where the cell was run, the msolve reading, and the
  degree-3 `closure_rank` equals the committed one where the fresh draw
  carries it (for example 1799 at (13,4,4) closure-study draw 1). Any
  mismatch fails the control and stops the item (no ladder cell is run).
* **Negative control (the right null).** Satisfiable draws of the same cells
  ((11,4,4) draw 0 of seed 20261014, 154 solutions; (13,4,4) closure-study
  draw 0, 48 solutions) are closed at the same degree: `1 in V_D` must be
  false on every one. A refutation of a satisfiable system fails the item.
* **Sensitivity control (can the instrument read more than 4?).** Random
  systems with the same unknown count and the same number of terms per
  degree in each equation as the m = 4 (6,2) cell (N = 20, 18 equations)
  are closed at degrees 3..6. The committed random-cubic control reads
  semi-regular `D_reg = 7` at that shape and msolve reached degree 6 on it;
  the blocked closure must report `D > 4` (refute only above degree 4) on all
  of four seeds. An instrument that reads 4 there is blind and the item is
  INCONCLUSIVE.

## Cells, ladder and stop rule

Ladder, in order (columns are `C(N, <= 4)`, the degree-4 width; "tri" is the
triangular reduced-basis upper bound `cols^2/16` bytes, "dense" the full
square `cols^2/8`):

| cell (n,l,m) | N | S = n - m*l | columns | tri GiB | dense GiB | status before running |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|---|
| (11,4,4) | 38 | -5 | 82,993 | 0.40 | 0.80 | control; msolve committed |
| (13,4,4) | 42 | -3 | 124,314 | 0.90 | 1.80 | incumbent committed (7.1 GB, 244 s) |
| (15,5,4) | 50 | -5 | 251,176 | 3.67 | 7.34 | new, if it fits time and 7 GiB |
| (17,5,4) | 54 | -3 | 342,541 | 6.83 | 13.66 | tri bound alone is at the cap |
| (19,6,4) | 62 | -5 | 597,619 | 20.79 | 41.58 | far beyond the cap |
| (19,6,3) | 37 | +1 | 74,519 | 0.32 | 0.65 | cheap; measured (m = 3 branch) |

* **Draws.** Unsatisfiable only (a satisfiable system has no refutation
  degree). (11,4,4): the four committed draws. (13,4,4): item 06's draw u0
  and the closure-study draws 1, 3, 4, 7 (the incumbent's). New cells: the
  first unsatisfiable draws of the instrument's own deterministic generator
  (splitmix64, seed recorded; subspace basis of `l` random independent
  vectors, random `x_R`), each verified unsatisfiable by the field-level exact
  count (an additive-polynomial root solve per link, validated against the
  committed counts) **again inside the card** before the closure runs. The
  committed draws came from `rand::StdRng`, which a std-only build cannot
  replay; new cells therefore use new draws (Deviation 1).
* **Stop rule.** The m = 4 ladder is run in order and **stops at the first
  cell beyond the cap**. A cell is beyond the cap when any of: (i) the
  instrument's own basis-bytes counter would exceed `--mem-cap-mb` (6,600
  MiB, a margin under the 7 GiB address space for block buffers and tables)
  at a block boundary; (ii) the `plan` projection's tri bound plus block
  buffers exceeds 7 GiB before the run starts; (iii) the cell cannot finish in
  its cards' time (the instrument stops itself at `--time-limit` and prints
  the partial rank, products and the projected remainder; the cell is then
  UNMEASURED/BLOCKED(time), never evidence for `D`). A beyond-cap cell is
  recorded BLOCKED with the exact projected resident bytes, the projected
  disk bytes (resident bytes beyond the RAM budget, against about 4.9 GB free
  host disk), the projected word ops and wall, all labelled projections.
  (19,6,3) is a different branch (N = 37) and is measured regardless of where
  the m = 4 ladder stops.
* **Replicates.** One draw per new cell is the minimum; more draws are added
  in card order while the 2 CPU-hour budget lasts. A cell with fewer than four
  draws is reported with its draw count, never as a four-draw cell.

## Unit and metrics

Solving degree `D` (first degree at which `1 in V_D`; "> 4" if degree 4
saturates without it; "unmeasured" if the run was stopped). Per degree:
products reduced (rows), columns, final rank, 64-bit word XORs (the unit; the
same as the incumbent's `closure_word_ops`, counting forward reduction,
in-block reduction and the folding pass), bytes of basis streamed by the
block passes, bytes of basis rows read by reductions, peak basis bytes, peak
RSS (`VmHWM`), wall seconds (descriptive only; the host is the shared 4-vCPU
container at isolation level L0).

## Decision rule (from the design, made operational)

Record `D` per cell. Then:

* **D stays 4 at l = 5 and l = 6:** item 02's `D = 4` rows become measured to
  that `l`. Here: `(4,5)` is **already** closure-measured at 4 (N = 42, 44,
  eight draws, reused) and is extended to N = 50 only if (15,5,4) completes;
  `(4,6)` needs N >= 62 (smaller n makes unsatisfiable draws essentially
  impossible: `S = n - 24` is hugely negative) and is expected BLOCKED; `(3,6)`
  is measured at N = 37 and reused at N = 34, 36, 40, 42.
* **D >= 5 at any measured cell:** the growth model returns for that `(m, l)`.
* **BLOCKED cells** name the projected memory and disk needed.

Per-cell outcome vocabulary: `D = 4`, `D = 3`, `D > 4`, `BLOCKED(memory)`,
`BLOCKED(time)`, `UNMEASURED`. Item verdict vocabulary: MATCHES (the blocked
engine reproduces the committed degrees on the controls and on the ladder
cells it reaches), TRADES, ADVANCES, REGRESSES, INCONCLUSIVE, BLOCKED,
each stating its level (primitive here: a memory/time boundary of the
instrument; exponent only if `D` is seen to grow). Nothing here is an
improvement over the index-calculus baseline by itself; the gap to matched
rho is carried by item 02 and is not computed here.

## Budget

One thread per job (no thread is ever spawned), `prlimit --as=7516192768`
on every run, at most 2 CPU-hours of measured runs in total, every card
under 9 minutes (the instrument carries `--time-limit` below the card
timeout so a slow cell ends with a recorded partial rather than a kill).
Disk: the instrument writes only its text outputs (no scratch file, no
spill); build products go to `target/blindspots-07/`, which each card
removes at the end. Host: shared 4-vCPU, 15 GB, at most two lanes at once.

## Deviations from the frozen design

Each tightens or makes the design concrete; where one narrows a listed arm it
is said so plainly.

1. **New draws for new cells.** The repository's draws came from
   `rand::StdRng`. The library does not compile at this base (25 errors in
   `ecbench` and `gaudry_quartic`; see 2), so the instrument is std-only and
   cannot replay `StdRng`. Committed draws are reused wherever they exist
   ((11,4,4), (13,4,4), the small control cells); only the cells with no
   committed draw use the instrument's own generator, and say so.
2. **Build tool.** `cargo build --release --lib` fails at the branch base, as
   item 02's protocol already recorded, so the shared warm `target/` holds no
   `crypto_lib` rlib. The instrument depends on `std` only (it re-implements
   `find_irreducible_sparse`, the `F_{2^n}` structure constants and the
   symbolic `S_3` chain system, checked against the committed solution counts
   and committed closure ranks, see Controls) and each card compiles it with
   `rustc --edition 2021 -C opt-level=3 -C target-cpu=native`
   to a path that does not yet exist. The broken library belongs to other
   lanes and is not touched.
3. **No disk tier.** The design says "streamed in column panels from disk
   (row blocks memory mapped)". This build keeps the reduced basis in RAM
   with exact-size triangular rows and streams *row blocks of products*
   against it. A scratch-file tier was not built because the host has about
   4.9 GB free disk, which is below the shortfall at the first cell that
   exceeds the RAM cap ((17,5,4): tri bound 6.83 GiB against a 7 GiB address
   space already shared with block buffers), so a spill tier cannot change
   which cell is first beyond the cap; the instrument instead records the
   exact projected memory and disk for every beyond-cap cell, as the rule
   requires. The memory saving against the incumbent is measured, not
   assumed.
4. **Arm (c) narrowed to cited results; arm (b) not applicable** (see Arms).
5. **Closure rather than literal F4.** The measured object is the
   fixed-degree closure; the justification is the committed per-draw equality
   with msolve's step degree on the inspected pairs and the (11,4,4) control. A cell
   where the two could differ is not claimed to be an F4 reading beyond the
   control.
6. **Satisfiable-draw control at (13,4,4)** runs the closure to saturation;
   if that exceeds the budget it is replaced by the (11,4,4) satisfiable
   draw only, and that replacement is recorded.
7. **Conductor.** `conductor` is not installed on this host (CLAUDE.md asks
   for a task check); the hooks warn and let edits through.

## Outputs

`raw/` holds the receipts, the per-card logs, `selftest.txt`, one JSON line
per measured degree, and the `plan` records of every beyond-cap cell.
`README.md` (verdict by this rule), the SVG and PNG graph and `REPORT.pdf`
are produced by the assess stage, not here; speed rows go to
`benchmarks/20261018-blindspots-blocked-f4/` at integration.

### 1. Development disclosure

The instrument was written and tuned before the cards were frozen. The runs
below were made for correctness and for sizing the cards (every card must end
inside nine minutes). **None is a result**; each is re-run from scratch by a
card, and only card outputs are reported.

* `selftest`, many times, and the committed-control table builder once.
* (10,3,4) once (3.6 s) and (4,1,4)-scale tiny cells while debugging.
* (11,4,4) draw u0: about twenty runs, varying block rows (256 to 32,768),
  tile width (32 to 256 words) and work order (three), all reading `D = 4`
  with `1 in V_4`; the satisfiable (11,4,4) draw 0 once (saturated at rank
  82,839 of 82,993, no `1`).
* (13,4,4) item-06 draw u0: six runs (three work orders, two builds), all
  `D = 4`.
* (19,6,3) draw 0 once (`D = 4`, 23 s); (19,7,3) committed draw 0 once
  (saturated at degree 4 with rank 94,230, equal to the committed incumbent
  rank).
* (13,5,4) item-06 draw 14 once (`D = 4`, refuted at rank 173,122 of 179,447,
  249 s, peak RSS 2.35 GB).
* (15,5,4) draw 8 (unsatisfiable by the exact count): three runs, one
  abandoned at about 90 s when the row-storage code was changed, two complete

rule's second branch ("D >= 5 and the growth model returns") is now a live

a transition cell is added (item 4 below). The rule is unchanged.

### 2. Engine parameters, fixed

Block rows `K = 4096` products per reduction block; tile 256 words in the
panel pass; work order 2 (highest-degree fall first, last in first out). The
order changes only how much work is done before `1` appears, never `V_D`
(saturated ranks agree across orders and block sizes); it was chosen as the
least work on the two development draws that are also card controls. The
instrument records `chunk_rows`, `tile_words` and `order` in every output
line.

### 3. Corrections to the text above

* **Control statistics.** The "28 draws" sentence is replaced by what the
  selftest measured on the development build: 104 of 104 committed solution
  counts reproduced; 88 of 88 per-draw closure degrees equal to the committed
  incumbent closure (N <= 30, degrees 3 to 5); 36 of 36 per-draw degrees
  equal to the committed msolve F4 step degree; 104 of 104 degree-3 closure
  ranks equal to the committed `closure_rank` (N <= 44). The card re-runs the
  selftest and the numbers it prints are the ones reported.
* **Added control: saturated ranks.** The committed incumbent closure of
  `(m, n, l) = (3, 19, 7)` (N = 40) saturates at degree 4 without `1`, at rank
  94,230 (draws 0 and 3), 92,838 (draw 5) and 94,926 (draw 7) of 102,091
  columns. The blocked engine must reproduce those ranks exactly (`--expect-rank`)
  on draws 0, 5 and 7. A rank is the dimension of the closed space, so equality
  is the strongest available test that this engine computes the incumbent's
  space, and it is the positive control for the "no refutation" direction (an
  engine that stopped early or missed products would give a smaller rank).
* **Sensitivity control** is run to degree 5 (not 6): reading "no `1` through
  degree 5" on all four seeds proves `D > 4`, which is all the control needs;
  degree 6 at this shape costs minutes per seed.
* **Deviation 3 corrected.** The plan record for (17,5,4) gives a resident
  upper bound of 7.70e9 bytes against the instrument's 6.92e9-byte cap, a
  shortfall of 0.78 GB, which is far below the 4.9 GB of free disk. So a
  spill tier would *not* have left memory as the limit at (17,5,4); what limits
  (17,5,4) in these cards is the nine-minute card (projected 9 to 11 minutes

  cell uses far less than the bound (2.9 GB at (15,5,4) against a 3.9 GB

* **"28 + 20 draws" in the Controls paragraph** is superseded by the item

**Stop rule, operational.** The m = 4 ladder is attempted in order (15,5,4),
(17,5,4); the first cell whose run ends `timelimit` or `memcap` is BLOCKED,
and cells after it are not attempted (a card for a later cell, if it exists,
is not relayed). (19,6,4) is never attempted: its `plan` record (resident
upper bound 22.97 GB, 16.05 GB beyond the cap) is the BLOCKED record. (4,6)
cannot be reached below N = 62 because smaller n makes unsatisfiable draws
essentially impossible (`S = n - 24`).

**Added outcome.** A cell whose degree-4 closure saturates without `1` is
reported `D > 4 (saturated at rank r of C)`. Its degree-5 closure is BLOCKED
and `plan --degree 5` records the projected memory and disk (for (15,5,4),
2,369,936 columns, 3.5e11 bytes of reduced basis).

**Replicates.** A cell with `k` measured draws is reported with `k`; the
(15,5,4) reading is the set of its four draws, not a median of one.

### 5. What a card cannot decide

A card never interprets. The assess stage reads `raw/` and applies the
Decision rule above. In particular the (13,5,4) transition cell and the
saturated-rank controls are additions to the design's list, recorded here so
they are not mistaken for it.
