## Overall conclusion (strictly scoped to the sizes tested)

What the twist cannot do. At the sizes tested (toy to 40-bit prime fields, binary m <= 19, m=2,3 decompositions, x-only and Semaev/summation arms): for a target R in E(F_q), any decomposition through twist-class points has twist part B in E(F_q)[2], so B=O for odd-order R and A=R (E0: 0 violations in 44,200 classes; E1/G3: 0 mismatches against the structural predictor over 91,200 and 508,800 targets). Every tested use of the twist cost at least as much as its matched baseline in counted operations and no row went below 1: a joint E+E' table saved nothing (E2, 1.000-1.062); the F_{q^2} route cost 53-2812x more per E-relation (E3); the Koblitz shared base doubled the counted solves for identical relations (E4, 2.0246x); the Kummer x-only walk cost 4.6-6.2x A0 at I/M=3 and 1.29-5.9x the sound negation-map rho (E5, G1; unresolved only under rule 5a pricing at the largest I/M and w=1, never a win); the decoupled projection cost at least 1.0069x direct decomposition with an exact tie on subfield targets (G2, an identity). No twist-based arm is an improvement over the index-calculus baseline or rho here, and E5 and G2 are formally inconclusive.

What the twist can do. It supplies exact verified structure: the separation lemma, a mixed-solution family for n_T>0 at rate about c_ref n_T/q, the Frobenius eigen-decomposition, a genus-2 gluing family with #Jac=#E#E', and a second independent DLP on E' of order n'. These are structure, not speedups; the genus-2 cost comparison is a model only.

Open questions. (1) The mixed-solution rate at 2^20 to 2^24 and for m >= 4 (G3 20-bit intervals span [0.29, 1.47]; 24 bit unresolved). (2) Extension-field factor bases, larger m, and real Semaev/summation solvers (E2, E3, E4 are toy m=2 or 3 designs). (3) A measured genus-2 or Jacobian arm: Cantor arithmetic, a real rho or index-calculus run on Jac(C), and numerical verification of H4. (4) GAE 0/0.1/1 pricing of every arm including rho (E1-E4 and E6 use work units or models; E5 and G1 used an I/M band and a native-work set). (5) Whether a cheaper x-only step (ideal Lucas chain about 22 M-equivalents vs 31 for the ladder; projective or batched inversion) moves the Kummer walk below negation-map rho at large I/M; the K-mix/N1 step-ratio interval [0.944, 1.118] contains 1. (6) A decoupling metric that is not an identity (G2). (7) Replications with different seeds and code, since every replication here is a same-seed determinism check.
