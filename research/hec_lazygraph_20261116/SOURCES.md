- Gaudry, Theriault and Thome, author-hosted February2005 draft: https://cacr.uwaterloo.ca/techreports/2004/corr2004-29.pdf . Sections2/3 explicitly distinguish signed/multiplicity cycles yielding Full versus FP relations. This is established graph elimination; the source draft has three authors, while the final published2007GTTDpaper includes Diem.
- Kaltofen and Saunders,1991: https://kaltofen.math.ncsu.edu/bibliography/91/KaSa91.pdf . Read introduction and start of section2. The black-box representation supports structured operators, not just explicit sparsity. Page2 explicitly states that an algebraic circuit for A gives a transpose black box at the same asymptotic complexity, citing Kaminski etal1988 and Kaltofen/Pan1991. Therefore the transpose construction here is prior art, not a claimed discovery. Initial Duke-hosted URL returned403; author-hosted NCSU copy read successfully.
- Wiedemann,1986: https://www.csd.uwo.ca/~mmorenom/CS424/Ressources/WIEDEMANN-IEEE-1986.pdf . Read introduction and nonsingular square case: methods use operator application and do not require explicit matrix entries. Square/singular transformations and probabilistic solver guarantees remain separate from the circuit correctness gate.
- Avanzi and Theriault,2010: https://www.aimsciences.org/article/doi/10.3934/amc.2010.4.189 . Publisher abstract only, explicitly not full-text review: harvesting oversamples relations and selects a smaller matrix, with factor-base retuning. No harvesting novelty claimed, and its reported gains are not this implementation's results.
