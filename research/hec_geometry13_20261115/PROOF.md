If a polarized threefold decomposed geometrically, it would split into
positive-dimensional factors of dimensions1and2(possibly further splitting).
The sections of2M are the tensor product of section spaces of dimensions
2and4. In the resulting coordinates the image lies in the Segre variety
P1xP3 inP7 and satisfies its nonzero2x2minor quadrics. A change of basis
preserves existence of nonzero quadrics. Rank36excludes this. Thus BOTH
quotient ppavs are geometrically indecomposable.

Oort–Ueno, Principally polarized abelian varieties of dimension two or three
are Jacobian varieties, Theorem4, https://ir.cwi.nl/pub/27912/27912.pdf,
works over an algebraically closed field in arbitrary characteristic.
The scanned statement, good-curve definitions and positive-characteristic
proof were read visually (printedpages1–4). It supplies a good curve; its
reducible case has product polarization, just excluded. Therefore each
quotient is the polarized Jacobian of a smooth connected genus3curve over
the algebraic closure.

If that curve were hyperelliptic, it has a Weierstrass point over the
algebraic closure and an odd-degree model in characteristic127!=2.
Müller, Explicit Kummer varieties of hyperelliptic Jacobian threefolds,
Section3/Table2/equation(3),
https://www.math.uni-hamburg.de/home/js.mueller/28-11-2012-kummerg3.pdf,

symmetric theta divisor or changing its section basis cannot remove the
existence of that quadric. Contradiction. Thus BOTH curves are geometrically
nonhyperelliptic, hence have smooth canonical plane-quartic models.
