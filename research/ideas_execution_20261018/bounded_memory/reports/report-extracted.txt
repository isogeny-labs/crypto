Scope, accounting and evidence

The pivot cache has an explicit 1,048,576-byte row-payload limit with LRU eviction. Index/LRU metadata
and temporary rows add overhead; the process-wide address-space limit bounds total address space. Journal
and trace caps are 1 GiB and 64 MiB per phase. Row-size guards allow 16 MiB of terms; temporary XOR
vector reservations may use 32 MiB before the guard. The reducer is serial and disk-backed. There is no GPU,
distributed executor, or incumbent-oracle integration.
Per-process CPU, wall time and peak RSS are recorded separately for every launch in resources.json les. The
host was shared (isolation tier 0). Initial n=15 rst/minimum-weight runs recorded 26,932/27,048 KiB peak
RSS. Build, export, generation, failed attempts, verication, copied state and resumed reductions are separate
phases. Journal counters omit index reconstruction/input reads and rolled-back sux work; process resource
records retain their executed costs. No matched external engine or full pipeline/rho comparison was run.
The policy hooks allow only choosing between rows with the same leading monomial. First-pivot and minimumweight controls log lead, row index, incoming and pivot weight, replacement action, and feature/decision timer
counts. No learned model, training/calibration/heldout split, charged training experiment, learned column policy
or heldout transfer result exists in this deliverable.
Checkpoints ush journal data before atomically renaming metadata, binding input SHA256, policy, cache size,
next input oset, journal boundary and counters. The tests establish the stated process restart and input-identity
checks; power-loss/fsync durability and arbitrary journal-corruption handling remain open.
Item 7 remains partial: canonical frontier ell=6/7 and guess-count ell=8/9 closure trials, full F4/MutantXL
scheduling, certied large-case replay and distributed/GPU kernels remain outstanding. The selected n=17/ell=6
cell is a diagnostic, not a substitution for a canonical frozen frontier requirement. Item 9 has deterministic legal
hooks only. No solving-degree, deployed-security or fastest-engine claim is made. Canonical leaderboard and
solving-degree plots stay unchanged.
The study directory is research/ideas_execution_20261018/bounded_memory/.
