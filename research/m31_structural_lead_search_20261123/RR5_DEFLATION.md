## Written lemma

Let theta=(alpha,beta,t) and R=(u,v)=-Q. The normalized function is
F(X,Y)=(X-u)(alpha+beta X)+(Y-v)(X+t).
Use the inherited monic quartic M=-Norm(F)/(X-u). Work only at a solution with four simple distinct roots x_i distinct from u, with x_i+t nonzero and nonzero lifted ordinates. Let H be a selector with simple roots at all x_i, e.g. the Dickson selector or the squarefree exact support product. In the completed local ring lift the four roots and define z_i=H(x_i(theta)). Define g_j=e_j(z_1,z_2,z_3,z_4), j=1..4. These symmetric expressions are polynomial functions of the monic quartic coefficients and selector. The equations g_j=0 are equivalent over an algebraic closure to all z_i=0 (the characteristic polynomial is Z^4). The original four terminal equations therefore remain mandatory.

Claim: append all six ordinary second partial derivatives of g_3 with respect to theta, i.e. the symmetric Hessian entries, to the original system. Every original solution in this chart is preserved, and the augmented Jacobian has rank three in theta at each regular solution, in characteristic >3.

Proof: At a solution all z_i vanish, so g_3 has order at least three. Its Hessian vanishes there, and thus appending it cannot remove a solution. The leading cubic is e_3(l_1,l_2,l_3,l_4), where l_i is the first-order variation of z_i.

Any three l_i are independent. Along the nonsingular local curve branch, implicit differentiation of F gives the first-order root variation proportional to the evaluation of delta F; the factor is nonzero since the norm root is simple, the ordinate is nonzero, and H'(x_i) is nonzero. After dividing each row by the nonzero factor x_i-u, its direction is (1,x_i,-(alpha+beta x_i)/(x_i+t)). If alpha-beta*t=0 then F factors as (X+t)[beta(X-u)+(Y-v)]. Its zeros can have at most four distinct abscissae (one vertical and at most three on the other line), contradicting the five distinct abscissae including u. Consequently alpha-beta*t is nonzero. Multiplying each row by x_i+t converts these three rational basis functions to an invertible transform of (1,x_i,x_i²). Three distinct x_i give an invertible Vandermonde matrix.

Choose linear coordinates X=l_1,Y=l_2,Z=l_3. Then l_4=aX+bY+cZ with abc nonzero by the same independence. The leading cubic is
C=XYZ+(aX+bY+cZ)(XY+XZ+YZ).
If a tangent direction d annihilates the linear parts of all second derivatives of g_3, then every second derivative of the homogeneous quadratic D_d C is zero, so D_d C=0 (characteristic >3). The coefficients of X²,Y²,Z² in D_d C are a(dY+dZ), b(dX+dZ), c(dX+dY). They force d=0 because abc and 2 are invertible. Hence the six linear forms span the full three-dimensional cotangent space. Invertible linear coordinate changes preserve this rank. The augmented local ideal equals the maximal ideal by Nakayama's lemma, so the solution is reduced. The original equations prevent extra points. QED, written argument only, no identity certificate, Lean proof or independent native replay in this turn.

Unlike requiring distinct intermediate images, this argument does not impose extra conditions on collisions in the selector image. It does not cover repeated original roots, target-overlap, infinity, zero denominator, inseparable selectors or other exceptional charts. Keep those explicit, rather than interpreting omitted points as empty outputs. No assertion about radicality of an unguarded global system is made.

## Circuit and test hypothesis

The potential advantage is removing artificial local multiplicities without partitioning the regular root set. It is NOT a new bound on solving degree. The original collapsed presentation is a coefficient pushforward (Graeffe for Dickson); compute the total, not partial-with-internal-variables-fixed, derivatives through the existing circuit. A three-variable truncated degree-two jet has ten scalar coefficients (1+3+6). Addition is componentwise and multiplication uses truncated convolution; a naive product uses 28 scalar products before simplification (1 constant, 6 linear, 12 constant/quadratic, 9 linear/linear). This is an implementation inventory, not a runtime prediction. The extra jet constraints may cost substantially more than the saved elimination. Compare against the existing compact remainder and quotient-product representations which already avoid the particular collapse; defeating the singular encoding alone is insufficient.
