# E2: is the S0*d cut a scaling gain or noise? (2026-10-30)

**Verdict: constant factor.** Re-measured with fresh targets and rank seeds, the cut of the best recipe over the
baseline is 2.0x to 3.5x at six sizes. Slope of log2(cut) vs n is -0.021 per bit of n, 95% CI [-0.078, +0.036]
(vs log2 r: +0.047 [-0.006, +0.099]). The README's 3.75x at n=61 does not reproduce (2.4x to 3.1x here).

Instrument: the same binary as the main note (sha256 ce8f8d23...), counted GAE metrics with the same formulas
(`analyse.py` reproduces the README S0, d at n=41/53/61). Admitted new sizes: n=51, 57, 59 (n=43, 47 are refused
by the producer; their r is 93053 and 7.1e6). Note r for these is much smaller than 2^n (r = 1.7e10, 2.75e11, 1.0e10),
so I also fit against log2 r. Skipped: batching projection (needs a producer change; not cheap, not measured).

Design. Selection on seed 1 (32 targets): K ladder 2..192, norm16, sym recipe = argmin S0*d, table cap 3e7 entries.
Evaluation on seeds 2,3,4 (fresh 64 target scalars each, rank_seed = seed), so selection bias is excluded.
Baseline, primary (A, fixed rule, chosen after seeing that rule B below is degenerate): plain norm16 with
B = W^2 n ~ 0.6 sqrt(r), W = 10K; this reproduces the original baselines' table sizes within ~15%.

## Why it is a constant factor, and a warning on the baseline

- Pair symmetry halves the build and leaves d unchanged: exactly 2.00x by count (best-plain / best-sym S0*d is 2.00
  at n=41, 51, 57, 59; 2.7 at n=53, 61 where the best sym K sits at the cap, so some of that is table growth).
  The rest is retuning K, which is a property of how badly the baseline is tuned, not of n.
- Sensitivity (B): if the baseline is the argmin of S0+d on seed 1, the cut is 104x at n=41 (K4 is picked, a tiny
  table with huge d), 2.8-7.4x at others, and the slope vs n is -0.23 [-0.45, -0.006]. The slope is negative
  (cut shrinking), driven by that n=41 outlier. The cut is only meaningful against a stated baseline; it is
  sensitive to that definition far more than to n.
- Caveats: 6 sizes, 3 seeds, 32/64 targets; recipe at n=53, 61 is at the K ladder cap (3e7 entries), so a larger
  table might cut slightly more; only norm16 was gridded for new sizes; sizes with small r (51, 57, 59) are
  not on the same x axis as n. Not compared with published IC.
