Residual collector: one addition per step, and distinguished points

What was implemented. Two target modes in src/cryptanalysis/ic_framework/residual.rs, both leaving the baseline path (targets=residual) unchanged: residual-fused takes one group addition per walk step from a precomputed table of Jj − Pi (16·|F| setup additions, counted); residual-fused-dp adds distinguished points: one DP test per step (counted native op, the analogue of rho's per-step canonicalisation), and only residuals in a 2−b fraction (b = ⌊log₂ r / 2⌋ − 9; 0 for log₂ r < 20, 8 for the 36–37-bit curves) are tested against F, probed and stored. Counts are in ecbench's counted unit; every answer verified by the harness; ecbench verify --replay 12 audits OK (2048/2048 records per study).

Classification and caveats

  • Class: engineering improvement of a generic collision search (lower priced native work: lookups and stored bytes), not a structural index-calculus advance; the curve-free-null conclusion of ic_prime_residual_lp_20261026 carries over. It lowers the pipeline's counted cost against the frozen baseline on the same instances.
  • The candidate does not merge L with −L after a collision, so it needs about 1.5× more steps than the fused no-DP walk (exploratory, 16–24 targets). A negation-equivariant step was tried and lost to short cycles (walks trapped until the cap); not part of either study.
  • Run locally at isolation L0 (isolab-eks route unavailable here; counts are deterministic and carry no wall-clock claim). L2 specs are committed for a later isolab-eks replay.
  • Only 4 curves, two DP-active, both ≈2^36; behaviour at larger r is not measured. No validation-gate artifacts (replay certificate, validator report, §5a-banded matched-rho VALIDATION.json) exist yet, so no leaderboard or benchmarks/README.md row is added.
