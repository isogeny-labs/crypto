# Certified nonlinear redundancy removal, not just a linear basis reduction

For each relation r let B_r be its complete finite set of rejected coefficient
tuples. A selected subset is equivalent to all relations iff its union of B_r
equals the full union. Both unions are exact truth bitsets, so equality is an
exhaustive certificate. This may retain fewer equations than a linear basis:
the implication is over the finite Boolean cube and can use products of other
equations, not just linear combinations. No generic-rank assumption is used.

Candidates include degree2 and degree3 kernel bases, with the same feature
order (the degree2 feature list is a prefix of degree3). Deterministic greedy
selection maximizes new rejected states divided by term-count-plus-one;
integer cross-products avoid floating ties. Backward deletion removes a
relation only after rechecking complete union equality. It is a bounded
heuristic for compression, with no claim of a minimum cover. Every retained
equation is necessary and complete-union equality proves local equivalence.

Selected equations are linearly independent: a relation linearly generated by
previous selections rejects no tuple that satisfies them, so it has zero new
rejections and cannot be selected. Subsets left by backward deletion stay
independent. Thus C104's span-preserving basis weight reduction remains valid.
Together with its separately successful full singular-family certificate,
the same cubic-plus-guard circuit is exact on all fourteen local predicates.

Eight tiny cubes independently compare all scalar acceptance values with the
bitset certificate. The actual profile verifies complete rejection unions and
32 shared76parameter assignments against the old numeric predicates. There is
no SAT search in this worker. Source selection and every byte/hash/resource
limit are frozen before execution; every failure is retained. The compiler
may still be slower or larger overall, and no gate count is a speed ratio.

Truth storage<=512MiB, one core,4GiB per worker,90-second outer deadline and
78-second internal demotion remain. The selected relations and graph are new
exclusive artifacts. No old relation basis, source, manifest or raw result is
overwritten. Full original root/chart/ghost/U completeness remains separate.
