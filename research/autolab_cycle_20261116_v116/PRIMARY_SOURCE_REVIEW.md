# Novelty boundary

C115reviewed [Neiger,Rosenkilde,Schost2017](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.10658) and the [Roth-Ruckenstein2000institution record](https://cris.technion.ac.il/en/publications/efficient-decoding-of-reed-solomon-codes-beyond-half-the-minimum-/). Known power-series/root-prefix/factor-lifting ideas remain relevant; no complexity claim for a given polynomial transfers to solving our unknown coefficient family. Weighted degree bounds and affine chart/logic synthesis are standard tools, not certified new algorithms.

C116's measured result is a complete leading-case necessary cover on a pinned raw finite-field norm family, preserving the same quotient lifts and all leading cases. It does not establish general-algorithm novelty or a qualified runtime gain. Prime-field interval/carry semantics remain a separate proof and implementation stream.
