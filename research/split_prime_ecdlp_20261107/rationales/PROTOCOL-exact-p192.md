# Protocol: exact class groups of the five P-192 Frobenius fields (frozen before the run)

Date: 2026-11-08. Study: `README.md` §5 (pending paragraph). Implementation:
`crypto::isogeny::class_group_subexp` and the `classgroup` binary (library
commit 4d3ebf3885 and descendants). Route: this host (4 cores, 15 GB), isolation
L0, a stated deviation from AGENTS.md §9a under the precedent of
`followups/README.md` (counted, deterministic mathematical outputs; no timing
claim is made from these runs).

## Question
What are h(O_K), its factorisation, the 2-rank and Sylow structure of its small
primes, and the index in Cl(O_K) of the subgroup generated by the prime ideals
above the split primes l <= 50, 163, 691 and 2000 (and above Mazur's primes), for
the five P-192 curves of the census (p192-139, -2198, -5228, -5405, -6013)?

## Controls (done, before this protocol)
Same binary against PARI/GP 2.15.4 `quadclassunit`: D = -(10^d + 3) for
d = 30, 40, 45, 50 digits, all fundamental: the class numbers agree exactly
(125355959329602; 11536936302246622592; 3530192901419423199168;
1642444268997017700963200). Unit tests agree with form enumeration.

## Predictions (stated before data)
1. h is within 5 % of the analytic estimate h* = (w / 2 pi) sqrt|D_K| L(1, chi)
   (Euler product to 10^7); expected log2 h: 94.0, 93.6, 93.8, 94.0, 93.6 (the
   `facts` stage).
2. Cl(O_K) is not cyclic for any of the five (Delta has at least four distinct
   odd prime factors for p192-139: 5, 11, 31 and a 184-bit prime), so the 2-rank
   is 3 for that curve; no single prime form generates the group.
3. The subgroup generated by the split primes <= 50 is all of Cl(O_K) for at
   least four of five curves (heuristic: the first dozen split primes generate
   the odd part, the 2-part needs about r + a few).

## Acceptance
- Accepted lattice index N: ratio N / h* in [0.95, 1.05]. Under GRH (Bach:
  prime ideals of norm <= 6 ln^2 |D| generate Cl, the factor-base bound used)
  N is a multiple of h, and the analytic estimate then forces N = h. Every
  factor-base form must satisfy g^N = 1 (`order_checked`).
- Not accepted: no value is reported as h; the attempt and its log are kept.

## Falsification and stop conditions
- Prediction 1 fails (ratio outside [0.95, 1.05] after the maximum number of
  relation rounds): record as failed, change nothing in the acceptance interval.
- Wall limit per curve: 4 h (detached run, 4 threads); on overrun record the
  partial log and stop; a timeout is not evidence about the group.
- Parameters are the binary defaults (bound = 6 ln^2 |D_K|, half-length 2^14,
  support 10, slack 26, excess max(64, 3n/10), seed 1, DetGcd, 4 subsets).

## Artifacts
`data/cl/rust_<label>.json` (result), `data/cl/rust_<label>.log` (stderr), launch
script `tools/run_classgroup.sh`. Hashes in `data/SHA256SUMS`.

## Outcome (2026-11-10, appended after the runs; the sections above are unchanged)

All five curves accepted at the first or second relation round (ratios to the
analytic estimate 0.99997, 0.99983, 1.00002, 0.99994, 0.99989). Results in
`data/cl/rust_<label>.json`, structure in `structure_<label>.json`, PARI checks
in `pari_check_<label>.json`; summary table in `README.md` §5.

- Prediction 1 (h within 5 % of h*): held on all five, within 0.03 %.
- Prediction 2 (no cyclic group; 2-rank 3 for p192-139): held; 2-ranks 3, 2, 3,
  3, 2 equal the genus-theory values on all five.
- Prediction 3 (split primes <= 50 generate for at least four of five): held;
  four of five, p192-139 has index 2 at 50 and 1 at 163.
- Wall per curve 668–2502 s of computation; three container restarts and one
  harness cap interrupted the runs, each resumed from the checkpoints
  (`p192_launch_time.txt`, session logs 2026-11-09/10).

## Extension to the P-224, P-239 and P-256 fields (frozen 2026-11-10 before any run)

Request (user, 2026-11-10): the same computation for the remaining 14 census
curves: P-224 (p224-777, -1485, -5736 skipped; -7026 published, conductor 3),
P-239 (p239-36, -1562, -2491, -2682, -2863 skipped; -2185, -2320, -3048
published; conductor 5 for -2320 and -2682), P-256 (p256-876 skipped,
-1842 published). D_K has 221–258 bits.

Same method, acceptance and stop rules as above, with these additions.

- Parameters may be tuned per field *before* that field's claim runs, on the
  sieving-rate readout of a short probe (relations per 1000 products,
  products per second) and the dense-column count; the chosen parameters are
  recorded in `data/cl/rust_<label>.json` (`params`) and are not changed
  after a run is accepted. Tuning changes cost, not the answer: the lattice
  index is an exact integer whatever the parameters.
- Wall limit per curve: 24 h of computation for P-224/P-239, 48 h for
  P-256, across resumptions (`.ckpt`/`.det`); overrun is recorded and is not
  evidence about the group.
- Predictions (from the `facts` stage, log2 h*): P-224 110.5, 110.3, 110.4,
  108.1; P-239 117.1, 118.1, 116.8, 115.2, 117.6, 115.7, 117.7, 117.5; P-256
  126.4, 126.9. (1) h within 5 % of h* (expected within 0.1 %). (2) The 2-rank
  equals the genus-theory value for every curve; no cyclic group where that
  rank is ≥ 2. (3) The split odd primes ≤ 163 generate every class group; ≤ 50
  generate at least 10 of 14. (4) No structural quantity (2-rank, Sylow
  invariants, generating bounds) separates published from skipped curves
  within a field.
- The P-192 outcome is not used to adjust these predictions beyond what was
  written on 2026-11-08 (prediction 3's "at least four of five" becomes "at
  least 10 of 14" by the same 80 % rate).
