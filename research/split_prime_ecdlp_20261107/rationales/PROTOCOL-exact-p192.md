# Protocol: exact class groups of the five P-192 Frobenius fields (frozen before the run)

Date: 2026-11-08. Study: `README.md` §5 (pending paragraph). Implementation:
`crypto::isogeny::class_group_subexp` and the `classgroup` binary (library
commit 4d3ebf3885 and descendants). Route: this host (4 cores, 15 GB), isolation
L0, a stated deviation from AGENTS.md §9a under the precedent of
`followups/README.md` (counted, deterministic mathematical outputs; no timing
claim is made from these runs).

## Question
What are h(O_K), its factorisation, the 2-rank and Sylow structure of its small
primes, and the index in Cl(O_K) of the subgroup generated by the prime ideals
above the split primes l <= 50, 163, 691 and 2000 (and above Mazur's primes), for
the five P-192 curves of the census (p192-139, -2198, -5228, -5405, -6013)?

## Controls (done, before this protocol)
Same binary against PARI/GP 2.15.4 `quadclassunit`: D = -(10^d + 3) for
d = 30, 40, 45, 50 digits, all fundamental: the class numbers agree exactly
(125355959329602; 11536936302246622592; 3530192901419423199168;
1642444268997017700963200). Unit tests agree with form enumeration.

## Predictions (stated before data)
1. h is within 5 % of the analytic estimate h* = (w / 2 pi) sqrt|D_K| L(1, chi)
   (Euler product to 10^7); expected log2 h: 94.0, 93.6, 93.8, 94.0, 93.6 (the
   `facts` stage).
2. Cl(O_K) is not cyclic for any of the five (Delta has at least four distinct
   odd prime factors for p192-139: 5, 11, 31 and a 184-bit prime), so the 2-rank
   is 3 for that curve; no single prime form generates the group.
3. The subgroup generated by the split primes <= 50 is all of Cl(O_K) for at
   least four of five curves (heuristic: the first dozen split primes generate
   the odd part, the 2-part needs about r + a few).

## Acceptance
- Accepted lattice index N: ratio N / h* in [0.95, 1.05]. Under GRH (Bach:
  prime ideals of norm <= 6 ln^2 |D| generate Cl, the factor-base bound used)
  N is a multiple of h, and the analytic estimate then forces N = h. Every
  factor-base form must satisfy g^N = 1 (`order_checked`).
- Not accepted: no value is reported as h; the attempt and its log are kept.

## Falsification and stop conditions
- Prediction 1 fails (ratio outside [0.95, 1.05] after the maximum number of
  relation rounds): record as failed, change nothing in the acceptance interval.
- Wall limit per curve: 4 h (detached run, 4 threads); on overrun record the
  partial log and stop; a timeout is not evidence about the group.
- Parameters are the binary defaults (bound = 6 ln^2 |D_K|, half-length 2^14,
  support 10, slack 26, excess max(64, 3n/10), seed 1, DetGcd, 4 subsets).
