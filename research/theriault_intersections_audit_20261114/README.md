# Thériault 2003: the intersection lower bound fails in its stated model

Lean directly refutes the lower bound in Theorem1 of Nicolas Thériault's original *Index Calculus Attack for Hyperelliptic Curves of Small Genus*, ASIACRYPT2003/LNCS2894, printed75–92. The source claims `2s²/(3n) ≤ E(n,s) ≤ s²/n` for `3 ≤ s < n/2`. In its actual independent uniform paired-point model, n=8 and s=3 have exact expectation11/16, strictly below the claimed3/4. The counterexample extends to every even n≥8 with s=3, retaining all printed guards. This refutes that qualified finite lower constant; it does not refute the paper's asymptotic single-large-prime runtime exponent. No novelty claim. Signed standing remains unverified. The actual points are `Fin M × Bool`, with cardinality n=2M. The actual negation flips Bool. Lean proves involutivity, absence of fixed points and equality of orbit labels iff points are equal or negatives. This retains the source's assumption on printed81 that self-negating points lie in the factor base, leaving large primes in free pairs. The unchanged genuine `WalkBoundaryAudit.uniformPMF` on all finite point words has pointmass equal to the product of independent uniform pointmasses, proving sampling with replacement. The actual intersection count is s minus the number of distinct orbits observed; every subsequent occurrence of an orbit contributes one intersection. No expected-value or probability-recurrence conclusion is a premise. [pcc:unverified]

The source's Q(n,s,k) is defined from probabilities of actual count k under that PMF. Lean proves nonnegativity and normalization over k<s for s>0. The exact source expression `E = ∑ k<s, k*Q` equals the actual paired-word expectation. An explicit equivalence splits orbit labels and Bool labels and proves the expectation's equality to actual label occupancy. Actual finite absent-label indicator products derive `E = s−M+M*(1−1/M)^s` and the source recurrence `E(s+1)=(1−1/M)*E(s)+s/M`. [pcc:unverified]

For s=3 the exact expression is `3/M−1/M²`, or `6/n−4/n²`. The printed lower bound is `3/M=6/n`, which is strictly larger for every M≥4. The concrete n=8 fixture has exactly512 actual point triples. The source correctly derives its closed expectation, then its last inequality chain on printed86 drops the finite s−1 correction too aggressively. The formal counterfamily and direct universal negation use the source's qualified model, rather than a hypothesis deletion. [pcc:unverified]

Lean also proves a repair. The actual derived recurrence gives `s(s−1)/(3M) ≤ E` for s≤M+1 and `E ≤ s(s−1)/(2M)`. Consequently `4s²/(9n) ≤ E ≤ s²/n` holds throughout the original `3 ≤ s < n/2` window. This retains the quadratic order used in the runtime analysis.
