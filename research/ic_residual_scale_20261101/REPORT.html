Frozen rule C1 (p40 and p42, 8 cells, below 1 at w = 0, 0.1 and 1): not met, 6 of 8 cells hold. The failures are both at w = 0: p40 res1dp [0.683, 1.182] (12 targets, wide) and p42 res4dp [0.148, 1.295]. The latter comes from one censored baseline run: res4 on p42, round 1, was exhausted at 108 trials (S = 87.9 recorded). The baseline stores every residual in a table capped at 222 entries (TABLE_CAP); at 242 it needs about 8·106 steps, so after the cap later steps can only collide with the first 4.2·106 residuals, collisions become rarer, and this run hit the trial limit. The candidates store only distinguished points and stay far below the cap. All w = 0.1 and w = 1 cells hold (0.22–0.43 and 0.065–0.17).

Classification and caveats

  • Engineering of a generic collision search, as before; rho stays ahead at every weight (1.3–3.0× for the best arms), no structural index-calculus content, no claim to beat rho.
  • The baseline's table cap is a limit of the existing collector, not of the candidates, and it affects only the baseline arms at large r; the censored pair is reported, not dropped.
  • Curves p40 and p42 were generated for this study (random curve with a prime group order, found by a Hasse-interval BSGS); gen_curve.py checks that N is prime, lies in the Hasse interval and that N·P = O for the generator P, which fixes the group order. Distinct seeds from earlier studies, so sessions are not pooled.
  • No VALIDATION.json; no leaderboard or benchmarks row. Isolation L0; L2 specs committed for an isolab-eks replay.
