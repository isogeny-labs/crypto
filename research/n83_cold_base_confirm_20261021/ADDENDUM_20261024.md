# Addendum 2026-10-24 to RESULT.md (K1103/K1107 cold base confirm)

Frozen evidence in `RESULT.md` is unchanged. This addendum adds a scope clarification and a pointer to the matched-precomputation control.

## 1. The online rho/IC ratios exclude setup

The ratios 25.58 (K1103) and 37.21 (K1107) reported in `RESULT.md` are paired geometric-mean ratios **rho/IC**: the same-point rho online time divided by the IC one-target online time (rho online geometric mean 2,334.964 ms). Read as rho/IC, IC is 25.58x (K1103) and 37.21x (K1107) faster than un-precomputed rho on the online clock. Check: 2,334.964 / 91.264 = 25.58. They do **not** include the per-process setup or rank stages. The results table in `RESULT.md` reports, per base:

| Base | Mean setup | Mean rank stage | Mean cold process | Geometric mean one-target online |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| K1103/root11 dynamic | 46.818 s | 67.999 s | 114.950 s | 91.264 ms |
| K1107/root11 dynamic | 47.166 s | 66.486 s | 113.755 s | 62.750 ms |

Setup plus rank is about 114 s per process, roughly three orders of magnitude above the online clock. Reading 25.58 or 37.21 as a cost ratio for the cold protocol is wrong. The cold selector in `RESULT.md` compares the two IC bases with each other (paired difference 1.195 s), and does not compare IC with rho.

## 2. Read together with the preprocessing-rho control

`research/20261016-n83-precomputed-rho-control/` is the matched control for the setup question. Its `README.md` verdict and table report, for the K3300 Tier 1 base:

- Time-matched arm (a′), precomputed rho with table walks equal to the IC setup wall (680 s, one thread): median IC/precomputed-rho ratio **3.74** (95% CI 2.65 to 11.05), with IC online median 32.78 ms and precomputed rho median 8.11 ms.
- RSS-matched arm (b), precomputed rho table equal to the IC max RSS (10.79 GB): median IC/precomputed-rho ratio **40.61** (95% CI 24.27 to 71.72), with precomputed rho median 1.17 ms.

For the K600 hosted base the same README reports 81.52 (time-matched, a′) and 395.53 (RSS-matched, b). Its verdict is that in every time-matched and memory-matched comparison the 95% interval lies entirely above 1, so precomputed rho is faster online.

## 3. Scope limits

- The control uses the **K3300 and K600** bases on the 16 hosted eval points of run `gha-38597929540-1`. It does **not** measure the K1103 or K1107 bases of this study. No matched precomputed-rho ratio for these two bases exists in the repository.
- The control's figures are online medians of the control's own runs, not the 25.58 or 37.21 geometric means above. The two sets of numbers must not be substituted for each other.
- The control's operation-count band (AGENTS.md §5a) admits no ranking, so the control supports its verdict only under its time-matched and memory-matched definitions.

Reading the two together: the 25.58 and 37.21 figures are online-only ratios against an un-precomputed rho. The control points the other way. Its ratios are IC/rho, so values above 1 mean IC is slower: once rho is given matched precomputation, precomputed rho is faster online than IC for K3300 and K600. The control therefore reverses the 25.58x and 37.21x reading, but only for its own bases; it does not measure K1103 or K1107. The online-only comparison above is not the matched one. The matched comparison for K1103 and K1107 is outstanding.
