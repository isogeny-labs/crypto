# Conditional dispatch contract and cost hypothesis

Each branch is the already validated exact fixed-offset original-S3 solver: squarefree field-root GCD and complete finite linear-factor splitting, or full finite-difference scan on the exact canonical interval. Both enumerate all finite common intermediates and verify both original equations. The dispatcher chooses solely from public field bit length and interval cardinality. Thus a completed dispatch output equals the same complete conditional root set; an exhausted branch remains unresolved. No independence, rationality or multiplicity branch is dropped.

This experiment changes interval cardinality, not the mathematical target contract. Large intervals contain every earlier permitted shift plus new shifts, all of which must be searched. The full degree-12 polynomial factorization cost is independent of interval size; finite differences require O(12 M) additions and O(12) state. This predicts a crossover, not a uniform factor speed advantage or an 8x/50x gain. The thresholds are exposed training heuristics frozen from C71, not tuned on C72 outcomes. All five fields, all declared sizes and losses remain.

Cold children include interpreter startup, parsing, polynomial construction, shifts, all auxiliary roots, literal verification and serialization. Parent supervision charges final stdout and full lifetime. Deadlines demote exact counts and completeness. Memory records wait4 lifetime peak plus child peak and post-exec /proc samples, which distinguish inherited/pre-exec footprint from observed runtime. These are development measurements without qualified external host isolation, and genuine F4/F5/F6 are not comparators here.
