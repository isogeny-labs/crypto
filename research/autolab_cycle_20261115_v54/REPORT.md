# C54: general binary conditional norm solver

Hypothesis: eliminating the constant S-image before each root condition preserves all affine solutions and removes most repeated matrix work even for unnormalized ordinary and Koblitz targets. Generalize the native kernel to arbitrary binary A,B,h,k, include zero, and retain G(x)=0 ghost roots without requiring a rational curve lift.

The proof and preceding independent small-field oracle are retained. For each fixed conditional chart H is affine in the original r,s bits. Every supported monic quintic has a source-domain root; the union of every complete root-conditioned affine fiber covers exactly the candidates before the exact (L_V mod H)^8 support filter. The quotient by the constant S-image is an exact linear projection followed by back-substitution. Literal H evaluation, independent portable row checks and complete factor extraction verify outputs including zero and multiplicities. Unsupported dimension/cap conditions fail explicitly.
