# C94 degree-block preprocessing and certificates

Hypothesis: eliminating degree-at-least-two monomial columns in original Boolean equations reveals pure linear source relations. Exact affine substitution may collapse the conditional S3 problem before a genuine Gröbner engine is needed. Prediction: generic rank-53 symmetric diagnostics expose 18 relations x+y=s, then the remaining equations become linear. This could refute their apparent hardness under the weak C93 baseline. It is not a new Gröbner algorithm.

Represent Boolean polynomials as sets of squarefree monomial masks over F2. Row XOR is an invertible operation on the equation system. Eliminating only nonlinear columns gives linear combinations with no nonlinear terms. A witness bit vector independently reconstructs each such relation from the CURRENT original-equivalent equations. Solving the linear relations gives an injective affine map from k free bits onto their entire solution space. The verifier checks every relation vanishes under the map, k equals n minus independently computed rank, and an identity coordinate for each free bit proves injectivity. Cardinality 2^k then proves surjectivity onto the linear solution set. Substitute this map into EVERY equation, not just the extracted rows. Consequently the residual and original root sets are bijective. Composition restores all original variables and orders. A contradictory linear combination proves UNSAT. Repetition terminates after at most n successful dimension reductions; a stable nonlinear residual is passed to the unchanged genuine F4/F5 engine. A zero residual requires output of all 2^k roots subject to an explicit output cap; an excessive cap or incomplete native run yields unresolved with exact count null.

The producer uses high monomial pivots and high variable pivots. A separate checker uses low variable pivots, independent polynomial composition, checks source combination witnesses and exact final residual/map equality. It does not trust the producer rank. Tiny literal S3 all-pair enumeration and random independent MQ systems test completeness, including no constraints, repetitions, zero/torsion and B!=1. A certificate mutation must be rejected. Actual profile caps exhaustive residuals at 12 bits, preserving unresolved wider systems. This proof concerns sets of field-valued polynomial roots, not multiplicity schemes or rational point decompositions.

Complexity: sparse row XOR on at most R rows and M monomials, worst O(R^2 M); affine substitution can expand exponentially in polynomial degree and carries ordinary finite input budgets. S3 here is quadratic in source bits, so expansion is bounded by quadratic monomials in remaining variables. Original-domain enumeration is never hidden in preprocessing.
