A calibrated battery of cheap weak-class tests on the NIST prime curves’ b

2026-10-22

Status

One native run; no ECDLP cost measured. Protocol: PROTOCOL.md, frozen in commit b06037d99 before the real curves were scored. Run: run/, produced by target/release/b_weak_class_battery --real at that commit (clean tree, rustc 1.97.0, 4 threads, 933 s wall; run/run.json). The pre-flight estimate in the protocol was 35 minutes; the run took 15.6.

This is not an index-calculus measurement. It has no rho ratio, no phase is priced and no scoreboard row is owed (AGENTS.md §7a). It tests hypothesis 1 of research/p224_hypothesis_ranking_20261008/ (a weak class recognisable from (p, a, b), found by grinding seeds) with a wider family of statistics than the single KS test of b mod q.

Claim classes (controlled-experiment §1): every sentence about a curve below is comparative/statistical. The unit-tested algebra (division polynomial, Jacobi, NAF identity) is exact computation. The label DERIVED marks one consistency argument; OPEN marks what is not established.

Result

Observation (MEASURED, run/summary.json).

  • No curve × statistic is flagged. The pre-declared rule is Bonferroni over m = 5 × 17 = 85 tests, α = 0.05, flag iff p ≤ 5.88 × 10^-4 (at most 3 of 8000 nulls at or above the curve). The smallest real-curve p is 0.034 (P-256, smooth_c), 57 times the threshold.
  • Positive controls passed in 74 of 85 cells. In every passing cell at least 99 of 100 planted instances were detected at the corrected threshold. 11 cells are inconclusive (grey in the figure): d3 on all five curves, and depth_b and depth_disc on P-192, P-256 and P-384. Their real-curve values are reported but are not evidence either way.
  • Calibration gate passed. In all 85 cells, 0 to 62 of 1000 held-out null draws score p ≤ 0.05 (gate: at most 70; counts below 30 are ties on coarse discrete statistics and are conservative, not a failure). The mean number of seeds tried per accepted null draw is 9.6 to 12.1.
  • Two of the 74 conclusive cells have p ≤ 0.05 (P-192 hw_c, 0.044; P-256 smooth_c, 0.034). Under a continuous null about 3.7 would be expected; the discrete statistics make the count conservative.

Why the 11 cells are inconclusive

These are limits of the detector, not of the curves.

  • d3 (rational 3-isogeny kernels): its support under the conditioning is {0, 1, 2, 4}, and the largest value has about 6% null mass, so no value can reach the corrected threshold (the best attainable p on any curve is 0.055 to 0.077 in the dry run, 0.063 for P-224’s value here). On P-192 and P-384, p ≡ 2 mod 3 and d3 is 0 for every null draw. Predicted in the protocol (§4).
  • depth_b, depth_disc: the power-residue depth is capped by the small-prime part G of p − 1. P-192 and P-384 have v₂(p − 1) = 1 and no other small factor, so the support is {0, 1}; depth_disc cannot be planted there (p ≡ 2 mod 3). P-256 was not predicted in the protocol: v₂(p − 1) = 1 there as well, so G = 2550 = 2·3·5²·17 (log2 G = 11.32) and the planted maximum (11.32) is reached by 5 of 8000 nulls (p = 6/8001), just above the threshold. Disclosed here; the rule (inconclusive) was applied as written.

Observations outside the pre-registered rule

Exploratory, not part of the Bonferroni family, not findings.

  1. smooth_j is exactly 0 on P-192, P-224 and P-256. The integer j has no prime factor below 2^16 on three of the five curves (P-384: 19.5 bits; P-521: 6.3 bits). The pre-registered direction for smoothness is the upper tail, so these cells are p = 1. The null frequency of smooth_j = 0 is 0.046 to 0.056 per curve (run/nulls_*.csv.gz), so three or more of five has probability about 10 × 0.05³ ≈ 1.2 × 10^-3 by hand arithmetic. It was noticed after the run, among 17 statistics and both tails, so it carries a look-elsewhere factor of at least 3 (three smoothness cells) and in fact more. No mechanism is known, and it is not confirmed. A confirmation needs a new frozen protocol and an independent set of curves generated by the same procedure (for example the other X9.62 and SEC 2 seeded prime curves).
  2. d3 = 4 on P-224 (all four 3-division x-coordinates rational, none with a rational point). DERIVED: with no rational 3-torsion point, this says Frobenius acts as the scalar −1 on E[3], i.e. 3 divides the conductor of Z[π]. The earlier measured P-224 conductor f = 3 (research/p224_hypothesis_ranking_20261008/run/cm_discriminant.json) predicts exactly this, and P-256 (conductor 1) gives d3 = 1. So d3 = 4 is a re-expression of a known fact, not new structure.
  3. Torsion carries no information on these curves. The curves have prime order, so there is no rational 2-torsion or 3-torsion point and no rational 2-isogeny. The unit test confirms this for all five curves (the conditioning accepts them). A battery that reports “number of rational 2-/3-torsion points” would report 0 for every real curve and every conditioned null.

Interpretation (labelled, not a finding)

Within these seventeen statistics, on these five curves, nothing is more extreme than the corrected threshold where the detector demonstrably works. That narrows hypothesis 1 only to classes that stay inside the null on all 74 conclusive cells. Hypothesis 1’s grind scenario was already closed beyond about 30 bits of selection by the seed provenance work (research/nist_seed_provenance_20261016/); this battery adds no weight to that and takes none away.

Method in brief

PROTOCOL.md is the specification. In short:

  • Null (negative control). 8000 draws per curve: random 160-bit seed, c = x962_c_from_seed, b a square root of −27/c with a seed-derived sign, conditioned on odd order and no rational 3-torsion (cheap necessary conditions of the prime order every NIST curve has). The same code scores the null, the held-out draws, the plants and the real curves.
  • Statistics. jac_* (Jacobi pattern of b, c, j, 4a³ + 27b² over 32 odd primes), depth_* (joint power-residue depth), hw_*, run_*, naf_b (bit structure and signed-digit weight), smooth_* (smoothness below 2^16), d3 (3-isogeny pattern from the division polynomial). All are computed from (p, a, b) and c without counting points.
  • Positive controls. 100 constructed instances per cell (Chinese remaindering, products of small primes, r^g, bit patterns), through the same conditioning and statistic code.
  • Multiple testing. Bonferroni over 85 tests, declared in the protocol.

Power limits

A null here does not show that no weak class exists.

  • The instrument sees a class only if its members move one of the 17 statistics beyond the top 4 of 8000 null draws. The planted classes show what that takes: a quadratic-residue pattern on all 32 primes, a 40-bit run, a 24-digit NAF, a fully 2^16-smooth integer, a depth far above the null maximum. A subtler bias, for example “slightly more residues than expected on the first 12 primes”, is not claimed to be visible. No power curve in selection bits was measured.
  • A class defined by the group order, the trace, the CM field, an isogeny degree of 5 or more, or any other function of b outside these families is invisible. The 3-isogeny statistic has no power at the corrected level.
  • The depth statistics have no power on P-192, P-256 and P-384, whose p − 1 has no useful small-prime part.
  • The null is conditioned only on odd order and no rational 3-torsion, not on prime order itself, and uses a seed-derived sign that the real curves may not follow. A statistic correlated with 5-torsion or higher would be mis-calibrated, and none of the 17 is.
  • The statistics within a curve are dependent. Bonferroni is conservative.
  • Five curves, one real instance each: there is no replication on a second set of real curves.
