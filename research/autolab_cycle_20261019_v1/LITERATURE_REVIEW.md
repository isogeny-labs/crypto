# Primary-source novelty check, 2026-10-19

- Choo,Soos,Chai,Meel, **BOSPHORUS: Bridging ANF and CNF Solvers**, DATE2019, https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.04580 ; full HTML https://arxiv.org/html/1812.04580v1 inspected, especially sectionsII–III. It combines bounded SAT, algebraic propagation, XL and ElimLin to obtain facts and strengthen a problem. Therefore hybrid ANF/CNF reasoning, Gaussian elimination, and bounded conflict learning are established approaches; our use of them is not novel. Its application benchmarks do not imply an8x result on our original all-root S3 contract. The current cube cover adds a curve-specific necessary predicate; it is not evidence of a new general solving paradigm.
- Ozdemir,Kremer,Tinelli,Barrett, **Satisfiability Modulo Finite Fields**, CAV2023, author abstract https://theory.stanford.edu/~barrett/pubs/OKT%2B23-abstract.html ; DOI10.1007/978-3-031-37703-7_8. Author record confirms a prime-field implementation in cvc5. Full author PDF was unavailable through the web tool in this session. Inspect its decision procedure and all-root output before classifying it as Groebner-free or using any comparison; it cannot silently replace genuine F4/F5/F6 in the gate. It is a concrete additional prime-field engineering lead, not a measured baseline here.
- Brickenstein,Dreyer, **PolyBoRi: A framework for Groebner-basis computations with Boolean polynomials**, J.Symbolic Computation44(9),2009,1326–1345; project primary citation https://polybori.sourceforge.net/cite.html ; DOI10.1016/j.jsc.2008.02.017. Decision diagrams and Boolean-polynomial solving have substantial prior art. A BDD encoding alone is no novelty claim and can itself be used inside Groebner implementations.

Scope: these primary records and the Bosphorus sections were reviewed, not a complete literature survey or novelty clearance. Boolean cube covers and greedy set cover are classical. The separate pair-cell note supplies a falsifiable domain-specific theorem/next experiment, not a claimed new universal algorithm or measured gain.
