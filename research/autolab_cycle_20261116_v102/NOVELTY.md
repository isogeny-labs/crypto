These sources refute any novelty claim for general Gaussian/XOR-aware SAT. Our measured question is only its effect on this frozen finite-field circuit; the experiment found fill and did not solve the necessary family. CryptoMiniSat was not installed or executed. Its documentation is not execution evidence, and the native Z3 run has no independently checked UNSAT proof. Future novelty must be evaluated for a complete new algebraic reduction and matching output contract, not the familiar substrate.
