# Overlapping binary chain: original-top regularity

This is a new certificate method on the unchanged saved systems, not a new
factor base or a measured solving-degree improvement. The proposed theorem below
has a written proof and native hypothesis checks; full algebraic formalization
and independent scientific review remain open. Development controls precede a
separate frozen production protocol. Earlier certificates remain immutable.

## Definition and hypotheses

Use the first degree with zero homogeneous quotient as `dreg`, on the ideal
generated by the **original individual highest forms**, including Boolean
equations' squares. Do not perform affine row combinations before taking tops.
Let K=GF(2^n), m>=3, L=m-2, and ell>=1. Require n>=2ell and n>=ell+2.
Every selector block uses the same ell independent F2 weights w_a in K;
every intermediate block uses a full F2 basis. The precise cubic coordinate
forms are field products X0 X1 U0 and U(j-1) X(j+1) Uj. The terminal quadratic
coordinate forms are (Ulast Xlast)^2+t Ulast Xlast with coefficient Frobenius
only: Boolean selector variables are not squared in these bilinear forms.
All n coordinates of every group must have the stated individual top degree.
The native parser rejects any mismatch, zero top coordinate, extra equation,
wrong field, unsupported selector domain, hash or witness suffix.

Under these hypotheses the proposed value is

    dreg = n(m-2) + ell + 1.

The exact formula is not asserted for prime fields, nonuniform/affine/nonlinear
selector domains, rank-deficient weights, different equations or homogenization,
affine solving degree, first fall degree, last fall degree, or asymptotic attack
cost. A point enumeration and its x-selector presentation remain different data.

## Proof

Extend scalars to K; graded dimensions do not change. In characteristic two,
invertible linear substitutions preserve the ideal of squares. Apply the full
Moore coordinate change to each U block, writing its independent variables Ujk.
Write Xik=sum_a w_a^(2^k) xia; these are n linear forms of rank ell, not n
independent variables. Coefficient Frobenius transforms the cubics into
U0k X0k X1k and U(j-1)k X(j+1)k Ujk, for every k modulo n. The terminal group
becomes Dk=P(k+1)+t^(2^k)Pk, with Pk=Ulast,k Xlast,k.

For t=0 these generate all products Pk. For t!=0, modulo the terminal ideal Q,
Pk=c_k h, h=P0 and c_k=t^(2^k-1)!=0. The saved quadratic certificates give
explicit combinations in the original coordinates for each relation. Thus adding
h gives the full product ideal. Also h Ulast,k=h Xlast,k=0, since each is a
nonzero scalar multiple of Pk times one of its square-zero factors. Invertibility
of the full U and ell-row selector transforms shows that **all original local
variables** in the two terminal blocks annihilate h.

For S subset of the cyclic embedding indices let r(S) be the K-rank of the
rows (w_a^(2^k)) for k in S. Every consecutive cyclic ell-row window is independent:
it is a Frobenius transform of the initial invertible Moore matrix. Wrapping
uses Frobenius^n=id. Consequently each window contains at most r(S) members of S.
Summing over all n windows proves n r(S)>=ell |S|. This argument does not assume
that arbitrary ell rows are independent. Native certificates explicitly invert
every cyclic window, check both products, and check cyclic Frobenius recurrence.

For the augmented ideal H+(h), decompose the square-zero algebra by intermediate
monomial supports (S0,...,S(L-1)). Its selector quotient has generators
X0k X1k for k in S0; X(j+1)k for k in S(j-1) intersect Sj; and Xlast,k for
k in S(L-1). These are on disjoint selector blocks except the initial pair.
The initial pair quotient has highest nonzero degree 2ell-r(S0): r independent
row relations give r disjoint pair products after simultaneous coordinate
changes, proving the upper bound; setting their span in X1 to zero annihilates
every pair relation and gives a square-zero quotient in 2ell-r(S0) variables,
proving the matching lower bound. A linear block has degree ell-r(S).
Therefore this support component has highest degree

    sum_j |Sj| + m ell - r(S0) - r(S(L-1))
        - sum_{j=1}^{L-1} r(S(j-1) intersect Sj).

Put d_j=n-|Sj| and D=sum d_j. The sum of the endpoint/intersection cardinalities
is at least (L+1)n-2D (the two endpoints count S0 twice when L=1).
Using the rank density bound, the displayed degree is at most

    L n + ell - (1 - 2ell/n) D <= L n + ell.

All supports full attain L n+ell. Hence the augmented quotient has exactly that
highest nonzero degree.

It remains essential to account for h, rather than silently discarding it.
The kernel of R/H -> R/(H,h) is generated by h in degree two. Its annihilator
contains both terminal blocks and all original cubic generators. After killing
those blocks, the surviving algebra is a chain of q=L-1 intermediate blocks,
q+2 selector blocks, no terminal product relation, and a free final selector.
For q=0 its degree is 2ell. For q>=1, the same support decomposition has penalty
r(S0)+sum adjacent intersection ranks, without the final endpoint. Cardinality
sum is at least q n-2D. The highest degree is therefore at most q n+2ell for
n>=2ell. The degree-two shifted kernel has degree at most

    (L-1)n + 2ell + 2 <= L n + ell,

where the final inequality is precisely n>=ell+2. The graded exact sequence
then proves R/H vanishes above L n+ell as well. A quotient retaining X0 and
every intermediate variable kills every original top form; its product of all
L n+ell retained variables is nonzero. This proves the matching lower bound.

## Native checks and independent small controls

`src/bin/chain_exact.rs` is a separate instrument. The old `chain_upper.rs` and
its evidence are preserved. The new instrument reuses its strict full-source
parser, coefficient checks and quadratic relation checks, then checks all n
cubic embeddings, all cyclic windows and the theorem inequalities. The finite
case certificate binds every supporting file by SHA256.

Development controls form explicit mixed-degree squarefree Macaulay matrices
over F2 on fields of degrees 3 and 5. They compute complete Hilbert functions
for the original terminal quadratic, the full-product augmentation, and the
truncated chain used for the defect bound. Exhaustive small support enumeration
independently checks the augmented socle formula and rank density. Cases outside
the theorem hypotheses must be refused even if a numerical degree coincides;
all control results and failures are retained. These abstract structural ideals
are not relabelled as full smaller-curve Semaev inputs. Production source checks
separately establish the required shapes for the exact retained n83 inputs.

The intended production panel is all84 previously pinned original/baseline/
spike40 Koblitz/binary m3..9 planted/natural systems. No new targets or factor
base points are sampled; prime and m2 evidence remains as previously recorded.
No proof or independence claim is inferred merely from passing finite controls.
