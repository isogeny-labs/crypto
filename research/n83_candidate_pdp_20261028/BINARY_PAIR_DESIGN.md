# Binary m2 original-top pair certificates

Scope: twelve retained n83, ell27, m2 source identities: original/baseline/
spike40 times Koblitz/binary times planted/natural. No new target or point draws.
Baseline and spike40 use the existing paired targets; original planted targets
are distinct. Repeated natural inputs are retained identities, not independent
samples. All earlier sources, protocols, failed attempts and results remain.

## Exact object and proof

The object is the ideal generated by the highest homogeneous parts of each
saved, Boolean-reduced S3 descent equation and each selector field equation.
Its degree of regularity here means the first degree with zero quotient graded
piece. This is not the final affine Groebner basis degree or solving degree.

Write K=F2[z]/(z^83+z^45+z^2+z+1), ell=27, X=sum(w_i*a_i),
Y=sum(w_i*b_i), and t=target_x. The full saved S3 polynomial is
X^2 Y^2 + t^2 X^2 + t^2 Y^2 + t X Y + curve_b.
Boolean descent replaces selector squares by selectors. Consequently its
quadratic cross coefficient is (w_i*w_j)^2+t*w_i*w_j. The checker reconstructs
EVERY source byte including all equations, field equations, ring declaration
and planted witness suffix. It also requires every nonzero descent equation
to have quadratic cross-bilinear highest part. If this fails, no lower proof.

Let Q_r (r=0..82) be these coordinate quadratic forms, and C_r the coordinate
forms of X*Y. The native checker finds and replays an F2 XOR witness for every
C_r in the span of the Q_r. A failed inclusion is saved explicitly and yields
no exact certificate from this method. It does not imply a different degree.

For k=0..26 put A_k=sum(w_i^(2^k)*a_i), B_k=sum(w_i^(2^k)*b_i).
The stored Moore matrix M and its stored inverse are multiplied both ways
and checked against identity over K. No unverified Moore-rank assertion is
used. Explicit K-linear combinations of the ORIGINAL Q_r are then checked
coefficient by coefficient against A_k*B_k (27*729 checks per case).
Coefficient Frobenius is used to form these combinations; this does NOT
raise the polynomial variables to 2^k powers.

After this invertible linear change, the original ideal contains
J=(A_k^2,B_k^2,A_k*B_k : k=0..26). In characteristic two an invertible linear
change preserves the ideal of variable squares (the coefficientwise squared
matrix is invertible). The quotient by J has Hilbert series (1+2T)^27:
a standard monomial chooses none, A_k, or B_k in each pair. Its degree28 and
higher pieces vanish. Since J is contained in the transformed original ideal,
this is an upper bound on the original quotient's graded dimensions.

Setting every b_i to zero gives exactly F2[a_i]/(a_i^2), because all other
original highest forms are cross-bilinear. This quotient has Hilbert series
(1+T)^27 and supplies a nonzero degree27 witness a_0*...*a_26. It gives the
matching lower degree bound. Extension from F2 to K preserves each graded
vector-space dimension: extending a basis gives a basis after tensoring.
Thus whenever all checks pass, the original-top degree is exactly28.
The full Hilbert function is NOT asserted: coefficientwise bounds are
(1+T)^27 <= H(T) <= (1+2T)^27, not equality to either endpoint.

## Field and development controls

Rabin irreducibility certificate:83 is prime by trial division through9;
z^(2^83)=z mod f; gcd(f,z^2+z)=1 since f(0)=f(1)=1. All83 Frobenius
squarings are checked by a separate full carryless product/long division.
Another128 deterministic multiplication pairs are compared. The coefficient
descent is checked against direct S3 evaluation on512 Boolean assignments.

For ell1..4, explicit squarefree Macaulay multiplication matrices compute
all Hilbert coefficients, without the pair upper-bound shortcut. Four
positive cases must have exact degree ell+1; four negative target1 cases
must reject product-span inclusion. Negative rejection is not a degree claim.
Corrupted XOR witnesses, singular and perturbed inverses are rejected.
Pair-slot Hilbert counts are exhaustively checked for ell1..6. Source suffix
validation includes non-Boolean rejection. Five native unit tests cover these
controls. Producer and fresh-process replay recompute all certificates.

BinaryPairSlots.lean proves only the elementary finite-slot upper-degree
argument. Field arithmetic, source descent, ideal inclusion, coordinate change,
scalar extension and lower quotient are written/native evidence, not Lean
proofs. See BINARY_PAIR_PROOF_MAP.json. Independent scientific review remains
separate from replay by the producer implementation.

## Execution and preservation

Single pinned native Rust binary, one thread,2GiB virtual memory,120s producer
and120s replay; no automatic retries. Existing-host fallback follows retained
EKS shared-bucket AccessDenied; no IAM/capacity change or timing claim.
Protocol and source are committed and uploaded before production. Every
input, partial/error result, full certificate, executable and log is retained;
all raw bytes receive a SHA256 manifest and S3 archive. A downloaded archive
is fully hash-checked and replayed. Data commit precedes the derived report.
No base-quality, speed, universal FFD/LFD, full attack or larger-m claim.

## Primary sources checked before implementation

- [Notes on summation polynomials](https://arxiv.org/html/1503.08001):
  characteristic-two summation equations, Weil descent and Frobenius/trace
  relations. These motivate inspecting the actual coordinate presentation;
  the full-source equality is independently checked for every input.
- [FLINT irreducibility algorithm documentation](https://fredrikj.net/fdoc/html/algorithms/polynomials/irreducibility.html):
  Rabin's criterion, with every condition specialized and checked above.
- [Greenhill, finite-field representations](https://web.maths.unsw.edu.au/~csg/papers/fpaper.pdf):
  primary discussion of the Rabin criterion and modular polynomial operations.
- [Stacks Project, algebra base change](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/05G3):
  quotient presentations under scalar extension. Graded dimension preservation
  follows directly from a vector-space basis; no characteristic-zero rank
  theorem is transferred here.

The disjoint-pair bound is the explicit elementary argument above. No novelty
claim is made. Applicable internal sources: existing pdp.rs S3 generation,
top.rs highest-form checks, original and paired frozen input manifests.
