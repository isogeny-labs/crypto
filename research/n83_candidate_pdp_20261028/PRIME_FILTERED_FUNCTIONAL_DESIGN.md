## Candidate coefficient functional

Use the last normalized selector block z with ell=2r+1 and terminal
intermediate U. Let H be the product of r paired differences. Project all
other selector blocks and intermediates to zero, and reduce z_i^2 to zero.
For a homogeneous polynomial of degree r+3, define lambda as the coefficient
of U^2*z_0*...*z_(ell-1) after multiplication by H and this projection.

Every nonterminal top generator projects to zero: even the penultimate
quartic, whose third argument can be U, has both its first and second
arguments projected to zero. Selector squares are zero in the quotient.
The terminal top form is U^2*X^2, and H*X^2=0. Thus lambda kills the degree
r+3 part of the ideal generated by all top forms. This statement quantifies
over all multipliers; it is not just a check of the generator values.

Let A_i=NF(H*X^i) in the idempotent quotient z_i^2=w_i*z_i, for i=0,1,2.
The coefficient of U^2 in the saved affine consequence g is

    C = A2 - 2*t*A1 + t^2*A0.

Complement-mask pairing with H extracts its degree r+1 part, and predicts

    lambda(g_(r+3)) = (-2)^r * (sum_i w_i - 2*t).

There is no factor 2 from a second selector block: U is an unrestricted
single variable here. If the delta is zero, this functional is inconclusive.
A nonzero delta is not yet recorded as an LFD bound by the development code.

## Proposed filtered bridge (written proof, review pending)

Let f_i have degrees d_i, and suppose every homogeneous syzygy on their top
forms of weighted degree at most D is generated homogeneously by Koszul
relations. Let W_d be the span of a_i*f_i with deg(a_i)+d_i<=d.

For h in W_d with degree e<d, consider the largest degree s of a chosen
bounded representation. If s>e, the degree-s coefficients form a top-form
syzygy. Write it as sum of homogeneous Koszul relations, with pair
coefficients of degree s-d_i-d_j. Replace those top coefficients using the
same Koszul relations on the actual affine f_i. Their sum is identically
zero, and each replacement cancels the degree-s coefficient while adding
only lower-degree terms. The maximum representation degree decreases.
Induction gives h a representation bounded by its own degree e. Consequently
W_d intersect R_(<=e)=W_e for e<=d<=D.

If h is in W_d and deg(a*h)<=d, use its degree-deg(h) representation and
multiply by a to obtain a representation in W_d. Therefore W_d is closed
under every degree-bounded multiplication and equals V_d, since it contains
the input generators of degree at most d. Its degree-d leading forms lie
in the ideal of the original top forms.

Subject to the full syzygy hypothesis above with D=16, a nonzero lambda on
g_16 therefore proves g not in V_16. The saved degree-17 derivation places
g in V_17, giving a strict first filtered fall at 17 and LFD>=17. No global
LFD upper bound follows. This is a proposed conditional extension requiring
independent mathematical review, source binding, and a new production
protocol; it is not a result of the unit tests below.
