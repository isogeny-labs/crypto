saved polynomial, after cancellation within that polynomial. No affine row
reduction or derived equations are added. dreg is the first degree at which
the graded quotient vanishes. A surjection onto the square-zero polynomial
ring in k variables certifies dreg>=k+1. Every failed projection retains its
surviving monomials and contributes no lower bound. Positive dimension means
no finite graded vanishing degree here, not infinite CM regularity.

For binary/Koblitz cases test six predetermined coordinate projections:
first27 selectors, first26, all27m base selectors, low26 in every base block,
and ascending/descending greedy independent sets of squarefree top monomials.
The greedy rule admits a variable only if no complete squarefree top monomial
would survive; it does not claim an optimal independent set. All candidates
are checked against every original top generator. Preserve failures and choose
the largest verified lower bound deterministically.

Every original variable field equation supplies its square in H. Compute rank r
of only the original linear top generators over F2 and retain each echelon row
as an XOR of original equation IDs. In characteristic2 a linear coordinate
change preserves the ideal of all variable squares. Thus the quotient by
squares and these r linear forms is a square-zero ring on nvars-r variables;
R/H is its graded quotient, proving dreg<=nvars-r+1. This strengthens the prior
nvars+1 upper bound without changing H. Exact values are claimed only if a
verified lower and upper bound coincide. No interval ordering establishes a
base improvement when the intervals overlap.
