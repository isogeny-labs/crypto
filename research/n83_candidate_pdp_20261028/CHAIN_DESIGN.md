# Larger-m binary original-top chain certificates

This phase binds84 existing systems: original/baseline/spike40 times
Koblitz/binary times m3..9 times planted/natural. It creates no new target,
point or polynomial presentation. Original planted targets differ from paired
ones; repeated natural sources are provenance identities, not independent draws.
The aim is stronger certified intervals, not exact larger-m degrees or a base win.

## Object and unchanged source checks

Over F2, H is generated by the highest homogeneous part of each saved original
polynomial, including every selector and intermediate-coordinate field equation.
The quotient is square-zero in N=27m+83(m-2) variables. Its first vanishing degree
is the regularity convention used here. No affine consequences are added.

The original S3 chain has m-1 groups of83 coordinate equations. After Boolean
descent, the first highest form is coordinates of X0*X1*U0; internal forms are
coordinates of U(k-1)*X(k+1)*Uk; the last quadratic is coordinates of
(Ulast*Xlast)^[2]+t*(Ulast*Xlast), where [2] squares coefficients, NOT variables.
All X blocks have27 selector bits, all U blocks83 unrestricted field-coordinate
bits. The full source and input hashes are pinned. Native parsing checks the
ring, each equation ID, every canonical monomial, every top coefficient and
complete expected support; every field equation and exact witness suffix is
checked. Repeated/out-of-order monomials, extra terms, missing terms, wrong field
or suffix fail closed. Lower-degree coefficients are not rederived here; their
unchanged bytes are hash-bound. This is sufficient for the highest-form object.

## Cubic component bound

Work over K=F2[z]/(z^83+z^45+z^2+z+1). In every variable block, use coefficient
Frobenius linear forms L_k=sum(w_i^(2^k)*v_i). The full83-by83 transform and both
27-by27 selector transforms have explicit inverses, checked in both orders.
Characteristic two preserves the ideal of squares under these changes.

The kth coefficient-Frobenius combination of product coordinates equals the
product of the kth transformed forms. Verify every coefficient for k0..26 on
both first-cubic shapes (27,27,83) and middle-cubic shapes (83,27,83), for both
selector domains. This supplies27 disjoint cubic monomial relations. Each such
slot permits at most two of its three variables in a surviving monomial. With
squares on all other variables, the component's socle degree is at most its
variable count minus27. Projection killing a27-variable block attains this
bound for the isolated cubic component. That isolated-component fact is not an
exact-degree claim for the full chain.

## Final quadratic component bound

Let P_k=U_k*Y_k denote embedded product coordinates, where U is a full83-variable
block and Y a27-variable selector block. With t=target_x, transformed original
quadratics are D_k=P_(k+1)+t^(2^k)*P_k, cyclically indexed modulo83. Explicit
linear combinations of the ORIGINAL coordinate quadratics are verified on all83
abstract product-basis elements. They show P_k=c_k*P_0 with c_k=t^(2^k-1) for
nonzero t. Every c_k is checked nonzero. No additional affine equation is used.

Modulo squares and Q, h=P_0 is annihilated by every U_k and Y_k because
P_k*U_k=P_k*Y_k=0 and P_k=c_k*h. The checked transforms span all local variables,
so (Q,P_0)/Q has no elements above degree2. Moreover (Q,P_0) is precisely the
full product-coordinate ideal: all P_k lie in it and the full83-coordinate
Frobenius transform is invertible. The full product ideal has27 disjoint pair
relations, hence vanishes above degree83 in110 variables. Since83>2, Q itself
vanishes above degree83. Setting Y=0 retains all83 square-zero U variables,
so the isolated final quadratic component has first vanishing degree84.
For t=0, coefficient Frobenius is invertible and Q already equals the product
ideal; the same result follows. Both zero/nonzero branches have explicit controls.

## Disjoint blocks and lower projection

Select chain equations k=0,2,4,... up to m-2. Their original variable blocks are
disjoint; the native checker verifies this variable by variable. There are
floor(m/2) selected components, each reducing the square-zero socle bound by27.
Their tensor product, together with unselected free square-zero variables,
therefore vanishes in degree

  upper = N - 27*floor(m/2) + 1.

The original top ideal contains this selected subideal, so its quotient cannot
have a larger top degree. Separately retain only the first27 base selectors and
ALL83(m-2) intermediate variables. Every non-field highest monomial vanishes
under this projection, checked against the actual source. This supplies

  lower = 28 + 83*(m-2).

These are full-system BOUNDS. Any gap stays unresolved. Extending F2 to K
preserves graded dimensions; the component bounds descend. No full Hilbert
function, solving degree, LFD, speed, elite-base or universal FFD/LFD claim.

## Controls and retention

Native explicit squarefree Macaulay matrices compute complete Hilbert functions
for24 quadratic and6 cubic cases over F8/F32. All quadratic targets0,1,2,3 are
included. These computations do not use the structural shortcut to obtain the
Hilbert coefficients. Additional controls reject altered coefficients, duplicate
monomials, singular/corrupted inverses and non-Boolean witnesses.128 n83 field
multiplications are cross-checked by carryless multiplication and long reduction;
Rabin certificates check the prime extension degrees and every Frobenius square.
The disjoint-block selection is exhaustively checked for m3..12.

Lean covers only elementary slot-sum arithmetic. The field, source, ideal,
annihilator, tensor-product and scalar-extension bridges remain written/native
proofs, not a fully formalized theorem. Independent scientific review is pending.

Commit/freeze source and protocol before production; no retries or reused output

errors/partial summaries and continue remaining cases. Upload periodically and

Archive every raw input/output/executable/log with hashes, restore from S3,
