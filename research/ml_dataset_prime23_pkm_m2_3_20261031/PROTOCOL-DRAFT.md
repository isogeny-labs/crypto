## Why this block

Merged `research/ml_dataset_coverage_20261031/coverage-v7.json` records 224 retained receipts and **zero PKM receipts**. Its prime-method rows record root and enumerate at n61, and BSGS at prior sizes; the separate prime n23 F4 and prime n47 enumerate documents are unlaunched drafts. This four-cell block adds a different, implemented algebraic solver and an alternative factor-base population. It does not replace any of the requested n23–31/n47–61, m2–15, curve-seed, generator, key, or twist axes of the full dataset plan.

The source capability record in `src/ml_dataset/solvers.rs::capabilities` declares PKM for prime fields, m2–3 and p<2^31. `pkm_solve` constructs `PrimeKummerBase` using the smallest nonzero abscissa in the ordinary public factor base as coset representative `g`, then saves `pkm-factor-base.json`, `pkm-prepared-system.json`, and `pkm-algebra.json` when those stages finish. `src/cryptanalysis/ic_framework/prime_tower.rs` defines the alternative Kummer x-domain `g·mu_(2^t)`, both point signs, and an abscissa column map. The PKM oracle saves prepared equations and bounded F4/grid extraction. A returned decomposition must pass the original group-sum verifier. Native F4 does not expose every private pivot or rejected pair, and the finite-grid fallback is separately counted.

The native prime constructor starts at 2^23−1 and descends through odd candidates. Independent arithmetic suggests p=8,388,593, whose p−1 is divisible by 16 but not 32. **This is a candidate, not a bound run input.** Read back the actual native modulus and both quadratic-twist models and exact curve/subgroup/cofactor certificates before freezing. If that modulus is selected, the current `t=min(v2(p−1),4)` rule yields t=4, 16 Kummer x-values, and at most 16^2=256 or 16^3=4,096 finite-grid tuples for m2/m3. Those are grid cardinalities, **not total native F4, setup, trace, or instruction costs**. `work_limit=20,000` admits both grids in the inspected source, while the native oracle's five-second budget and outer process cap are separate. A missing nonzero ordinary-base x, empty Kummer lift, failed twist/order construction, unsupported source revision, or resource stop is retained as that cell's outcome; no seed or coset is substituted after seeing it.

Require 8 GiB free on the declared output filesystem before each new shard. These are operational ceilings, not estimated observed runtimes or a monetary grant. Outer time/memory/trace limits remain authoritative because `work_limit` is not a bound on every native matrix allocation, tower setup operation, serialization, or S3 request.
