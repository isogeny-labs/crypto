## Native validator

[`../prototypes/r00_r16.rs`](../prototypes/r00_r16.rs) is a dependency-free
Rust program for the flat JSONL emitted by this standalone comparator. It
does not parse or compare `wall_s`. It checks:

- one bound instance and exactly twelve rho records;
- successful known-answer recovery in every rho record;
- five counted/production width pairs and the legacy-setup pair;
- zero field/group counters in all production rows;
- the complete paired execution tuple;
- the exact 24-element composed `strong-b8` phase vector; and
- the eight-element legacy setup vector.

Its `--selftest` mode mutates a trajectory field, authority flag, and field
counter in memory and requires all three corrupted copies to fail. This is an
additional producer-side differential oracle, not an independent validator
`[pcc:unverified]`.
