## Executed deterministic screens

The native standalone executable deliberately shares no arithmetic
implementation with `strong_rho.rs`. It uses a nine-product convolution as the
mul5/square oracle, exponentiation as the cubic-inverse oracle, extended Euclid
as the binary-inverse oracle, and widened remainder as the reduction oracle.

Exact command:

```sh
rustfmt --edition 2021 research/quartic_ghs_r0_r99_20261119/prototypes/r17_33.rs
rustc --edition 2021 -O research/quartic_ghs_r0_r99_20261119/prototypes/r17_33.rs \
  -o /tmp/ghs-r17-r33
/tmp/ghs-r17-r33
```

Observed exit status: `0` `[pcc:unverified]`. The receipt records:

- 768,394 exhaustive mul5 operand pairs over `q=5,7` and every nonzero `c`;
- 180,000 deterministic larger-field mul5 pairs over 18 `(q,c)` cells;
- 2,558 exhaustive specialized-square cells;
- 342 exhaustive nonzero values in `F_(7^3)` for direct inversion;
- 169,839 binary base-inverse comparisons;
- 80,056 Barrett reduction comparisons; and
- 80,000 branchless/u32/Montgomery comparisons.

The mul5 core used five general base-field products and four explicitly
counted small divisions in every tested case `[pcc:unverified]`. This is an
algebraic operation-count contract, not an elapsed-time reduction.

## Screened constraint

At `q=2^31-1`, only four maximal values `(q-1)^2` fit in `u64`. Therefore a
five- or nine-product lazy accumulator cannot use an unrestricted `u64`
sum. R29 remains open only for a proved `u128` or staged-reduction design
`[pcc:unverified]`.
