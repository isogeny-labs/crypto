## Signed-state invariant exercised

For a canonical quotient representative `R`, the lazy state stores an
orientation `s in {+1,-1}` and coefficients satisfying

```text
a*G + b*Q = s*R.
```

When the eager quotient walk adds canonical jump `J`, the lazy walk adds
`s*J`. If canonicalizing `R+J` contributes flip `t`, the next lazy state keeps
the already updated coefficients and replaces `(R,s)` by
`(canon(R+J), s*t)`. Therefore its coefficients remain `s*t` times the eager
coefficients without eagerly negating them. The standalone oracle checks this
identity after every transition.

For two records with the same quotient representative and orientations
`s1,s2`, the collision solver normalizes both coefficient equations before
solving:

```text
k = (s2*a2 - s1*a1) / (s1*b1 - s2*b2) mod n.
```

The candidate is still required to satisfy `[k]G=Q`; zero/noninvertible
denominators remain sterile. The cyclic oracle exercised equal and opposite
orientations. This does not substitute for the elliptic-curve replay required
by the registry `[pcc:unverified]`.
