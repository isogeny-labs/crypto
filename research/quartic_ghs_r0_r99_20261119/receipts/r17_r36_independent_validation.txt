Validator independence
----------------------

Provenance limitation
---------------------
The record is internally consistent under the checks above, but it is not yet
source-bound evidence. Its instance says source_revision=worktree-r17-r36,
while main.rs and strong_rho.rs were modified relative to checked-out commit
67e251c8c60bf8e3a9d5bea5da3fc6c480a16bd8. At validation time their working
tree SHA-256 values were:

d4ea3e3318f47ce797e6474f03947ce7efdb2cb13524ce692ed3568e090c5ec6  research/quartic_ghs_strong_rho_20261114/main.rs
30178eaf990ff84671565893c1cd32b11a64a055a864ddc8c07c728da47a60d3  research/quartic_ghs_strong_rho_20261114/strong_rho.rs

The JSONL does not carry those source hashes or a binary hash. Therefore this
receipt validates the deterministic record contents only. It is not a replay
certificate, execution proof, source-binding certificate, validation-gate
artifact, timing result, end-to-end ratio, or frontier result. A rerun from a
pushed commit with commit/source/binary hashes remains required before
promotion.
