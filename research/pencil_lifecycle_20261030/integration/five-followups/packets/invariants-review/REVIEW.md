For shared_zero_q, substituting dq=0 into B=0 gives -(dr)^2=0. A field has no nonzero square-zero element, hence dr=0 and equal support. Distinct-support exclusion follows by contradiction. Source gate.rs performs the same-support test first, then computes dq/dr and the identical B expression before the zero-dq error. This is a correct field-level exclusion, even in characteristic two. The machine guard requires canonical equality and arithmetic correspondence; the theorem does not silently discharge those obligations. Weight validity or smoothness is not needed for this particular guard exclusion.

These demonstrate that the assumptions have the advertised role, and that the distinct-support branch hypothesis is not inherently vacuous.
