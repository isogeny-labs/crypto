\section*{Precise arithmetic statement}
For canonical words $a,b<p$ and $p<2^{31}$, $ab<2^{64}$. Consequently Lean's actual UInt64 multiplication followed by UInt64 remainder has natural value $(ab)\bmod p$. Casting that value to $\mathbf Z/p\mathbf Z$ equals the product of the decoded inputs. Squaring is the specialization $b=a$. Remainder preserves canonicality for positive modulus.
