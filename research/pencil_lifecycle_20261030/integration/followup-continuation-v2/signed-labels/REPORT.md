# Saved candidate group-label verification

Verified all 731 recorded candidate equations across the six saved signed-collector streams. For each candidate, the checker reconstructed D=aG'+bQ' with the existing affine Cantor law and compared the exact divisor. Three recorded successful scalars also passed kG=Q on the source curve. No collector was rerun. The per-candidate labels and divisor coordinates are in equations.csv and summary.json.

Concrete controls. The streams come from one public synthetic instance, p=739, generator seed 23, target 2G and walk seed 47. Affine, projective and projective target-rank streams have caps 64 and 2048. Checked candidate counts are 64,64,64,180,180,179. The records contain 728 degree-two candidates and 3 degree-one candidates. No restart event occurs in these streams, so the restart-equation handler has no positive coverage here. The 1,260-target scientific cohort remains a separate obligation.

Source and transfer checks. Reconstruct the Scholten short source curve and destination polynomial from p; compare exact header model identities, canonical source points and transferred generator/target. Check source curve membership, nonzero points, r-torsion in source and destination, and each recorded divisor's Jacobian membership. Prime domains are checked by the preceding strict replay; the label checker restricts p to the explicit Scholten constructor domain with p no greater than i32::MAX. The source curve's total order is not independently recounted.

Corruption results. A canonical change to the label a of a nonsplit candidate was accepted by graph-only replay, because that candidate supplies no graph row. The new equation check rejected it at sequence 5. A coherently altered transferred-target header, matching the stream's own header, also passed graph-only replay but failed reconstruction from the source point. Both rejection controls passed. These are deliberate corruptions of saved software fixtures, not observed collector failures.

Arithmetic scope. The verifier uses the native affine Cantor implementation, shared with the affine producer and projective fallback. It reconstructs labels separately from the recorded walk but is not an independent arithmetic implementation or a universal proof of transfer correctness.
