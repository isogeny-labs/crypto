## Exact criterion

Let J be a finite abelian group, r a prime, and G an element. There exists an additive map ell:J -> F_r with ell(G)=1 if and only if G is not in rJ.

Proof. Every additive map to F_r kills rJ. Conversely, J/rJ is an F_r-vector space. If the image of G is nonzero, extend that vector to a basis and take its coordinate functional, then compose with the quotient map. This is a mathematical existence proof; it does not provide an efficient way to evaluate ell.

For a collector it suffices to apply this criterion to the subgroup K generated by G and every point class used in its retained relations. A map on K need not extend to all of J. The choice of K must cover the entire retained stream, not one row at a time.

## Explicit sufficient construction

Assume |J|=c*r, gcd(c,r)=1, and G has exact order r. Let u*c=1 modulo r and e=u*c. The quotient J/<G> has order c, so [c]X lies in <G> for every X (Lagrange in the quotient). Thus [e]X lies in <G>, and [e]G=G. Multiplication by e is an additive retraction. Define ell(X) to be the unique scalar t modulo r with [e]X=[t]G. Uniqueness and addition in <G> prove ell is additive and ell(G)=1. This derives a sufficient condition, not a necessary coprime-cofactor requirement.

## Why the current diagnostic is insufficient by itself

In J=Z/49Z, r=7, c=7 and G=7, G has order seven. Every X satisfies [7]([7]X)=0, but any additive ell:J -> F_7 has ell(G)=7*ell(1)=0. Consequently even an exhaustive version of the torsion diagnostic would not prove ell(G)=1. This is an abstract group counterexample, not a claim about a cohort curve.

Conversely J=F_7 x F_7 and G=(1,0) has ell(x,y)=x, despite |J|/r=7 not being coprime to r. Thus rejecting every noncoprime cofactor would discard valid cases. In the first counterexample K=<G> does admit a coordinate; whether other factor-base classes enlarge K is material.

## Relation and sign binding

Assume tau is additive on the source subgroup, H=[k]P, G=tau(P), ell(G)=1, and every retained relation satisfies D + sum_i Q_i=0 with D=tau([a]P+[b]H). Applying ell yields sum_i ell(Q_i)+b*k=-a modulo r. Under the hyperelliptic point-class negation identity, low-y representatives give signed coefficients +1/-1; an order-two class maps to zero for odd r. These are precisely the semantics required by the stored augmented row [A,b,-a]. The prior homogeneous adapter negates only this final right-hand-side column. Source verification of a returned scalar remains a separate check.
