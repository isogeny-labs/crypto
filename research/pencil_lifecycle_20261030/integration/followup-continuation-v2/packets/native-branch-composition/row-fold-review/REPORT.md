Source fidelity. The bound walk source evaluates four left-fold steps as add(accumulator, mul(matrix[row][column], input[column])). The Lean definitions use that order and the same unsigned modular operations. Canonical operands and p at most 2^32 bound each product below 2^64 and each modular accumulation below 2^64. Four unreduced products sum below 2^66 and therefore fit a 128-bit accumulator. Decoding and nonzero comparison are connected to the existing field and comparator proofs. Counter increments and compiler behavior remain separate.

Square-scalar bridge. The final theorem assumes the sixteen explicit dot-product remainder equations, canonical matrix/input entries, prime p and a canonical nonzero scalar c. It derives the field square-scalar identity and then nonzero preservation of the actual word fold. It does not assume nonzero output. This supplies the arithmetic interface to the earlier matrix certificates; this review does not instantiate all 105 historical records as Lean terms or prove the geometric interpretation of those actions.
