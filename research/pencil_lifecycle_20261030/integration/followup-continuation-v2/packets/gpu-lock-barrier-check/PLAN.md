# Bounded inherited-descriptor mechanism check

Before execution, freeze this source and plan by SHA256. One deterministic trial, local Linux flock only, no benchmark or repository changes. Outer timeout 60 seconds, internal alarm 15 seconds. Child is forked once and coordinates with two pipes. Positive control: parent closes its acquired lock descriptor while the child confirms its inherited descriptor remains open; a fresh descriptor's LOCK_EX|LOCK_NB must fail and exact errno is recorded. Negative control: parent releases barrier, child closes inherited descriptor and exits, parent reaps it, then fresh-descriptor acquisition must succeed. Separate no-child baseline acquires/closes/reacquires successfully. Record FD_CLOEXEC to distinguish fork inheritance from exec lifetime. No actual exec or reproduction of the original concurrent Rust test is claimed. Unexpected outcomes fail the checker; preserve all output and do not retry. Cleanup/reap child on failure.
