# Three source points leave a binary-linear norm line

For the binary curve Y²+XY=X³+AX²+B, use F=X³+rX²+sX+t and G=hX+k. Vanishing at a point (x,y) gives the field-linear equation r x²+s x+t+hxy+ky=x³. Impose the fixed target and three source points. Four distinct x coordinates give rank4 in the five unknown coefficients: if rank dropped, the unique dependence among evaluations of1,X,X² would also annihilate y and xy. Lagrange interpolation then makes all four y values affine in x. A line meets the monic cubic curve in at most three distinct x coordinates, a contradiction. Hence the coefficient solutions form an affine field line v0+lambda*v1. The implementation still retains any unexpected exceptional dimension instead of rejecting it.

The norm N=F²+XFG+(X³+AX²+B)G² is monic degree6 and divisible by X+z at the target. H=N/(X+z) is monic quintic. Along the affine line each coefficient is a+blambda+clambda². In characteristic two this is affine F2-linear in the n binary coordinates of lambda. Thus all coefficient-span conditions are one exact binary linear system of at most5n rows and n variables. Every residual lambda is retained; supported norms are checked with the multiplicity-safe predicate.

Enumeration over source point triples covers supported norms admitting three distinct rational source x coordinates. Repeated-only, zero/pole and nonrational-lift branches require their own charts; no general all-roots completeness is asserted. The actual k=0 certified distinct-nonzero family is included, while the interpolation itself allows any h,k. Triple enumeration still costs up to order2^(3d) and this experiment does not claim a global cost reduction or baseline gain.

In odd characteristic the same affine parameterization gives quadratic coefficient polynomials, but lambda² is not additive. Binary Gaussian elimination cannot be reused. Prime-field interval support also cannot be replaced by a proper prime-subfield span. These require separate solvers. Interpolation, norm maps and linearized polynomials are established tools; novelty of their combination remains to be reviewed.
