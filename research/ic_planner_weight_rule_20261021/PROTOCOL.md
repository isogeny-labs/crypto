# Protocol: a weight-aware factor-base size rule for the `ic` planner

Frozen 2026-10-21, before any recorded session of this study. The planner is
at `ic-planner/1` (`src/cryptanalysis/ic_framework/planner.rs`) and `ecbench`
has `compare --weight` (this change). Changes after data go under
"Deviations" in the README, never edited in here.

## Question

`research/ic_planner_validation_20261020` found that the planner's prime
factor-base size, `N₀ = round(#E^{1/3}/2)` abscissae, is the cheapest of
`{N₀/2, N₀, 2N₀}` in counted `S` (w = 0), but that `2N₀` is cheaper by
2–9% when the five unpriced native counters are charged at 1 GAE each
(AGENTS.md §5a). A larger base needs fewer trials, and each trial is one
lookup and one guard probe.

Can the planner take a declared native weight `w` (GAE per unpriced native
operation) and choose a base multiplier `m*(w)`, so that `N = round(m*(w)·N₀)`
is cheaper at that weight than `N₀`, on curves the rule was not fitted on?

weights; `ecbench verify` with replays.

### The derivation rule (fixed now)

For each weight `w ∈ W = {0, 0.03, 0.1, 0.3, 1}` and multiplier `m`, let
`G(m, w)` be the geometric mean over the three curves of
`mean S_w(m) / mean S_w(1)` (band means). Then
`m*(w) = argmin_m G(m, w)`; ties go to the smaller `m` (smaller table).

`ic-planner/2` takes `--native-weight w` (default 0):
- `N = round(m*(w_k) · N₀)` with `w_k` the largest element of `W` not above `w`;
- `w > 1` uses `m*(1)`, with support `extrapolated`;
- the memory cap of `ic-planner/1` still applies after the multiplier.

If `m*(0) ≠ 1`, the default (w = 0) plan changes too, and the README says so.

## Predictions

- P1. `m*(w)` is non-decreasing in `w`.
- P2. `m*(0) = 1` (the earlier study's counted optimum) and `m*(1) ∈ {1.41, 2, 2.83}`.
- P3. Held out, at each `w` with `m*(w) ≠ 1`, `S_w(v2) / S_w(v1) < 1` with
  the paired cluster 95% interval below 1 on at least 3 of the 4 curves.
- P4. Held out, at w = 0, every `v2` arm costs at least as much as `v1`
  (the rule trades counted `S` for native work, it does not lower both).

## Decision rule

- `ic-planner/2` is adopted if P3 holds at `w = 1`. Otherwise the planner
  keeps `ic-planner/1`'s size, and the study is reported as a negative result.
- The weight is a declared pricing assumption, not a measurement. A plan for
  `w > 0` is cheaper only at that weight, so it is reported at that weight
  and never as a band-robust ranking (AGENTS.md §5a).
- Class: engineering (a weight-conditional configuration choice). No counted
  `S` improves on a measured baseline, so no leaderboard row of tables A–C moves.
  The gap to the matched rho is reported at each weight as the distance remaining.
- Failures, timeouts and unverified runs are reported, never dropped.

## Inadmissible

Changing curves, multipliers, weights, targets or seeds after seeing a
session of the same phase; dropping a run; editing the planner between
sessions; fitting Phase A's rule on Phase B data.
