# Exact quadratic lifting contract
Let K=F2[T]/M be the declared field and sources a,b,c lie in the degree<d polynomial prefix, with u arbitrary in K. The original equations are S3(a,b,u)=S3(u,c,z)=0, where S3(x,y,t)=(xy)^2+(xt)^2+(yt)^2+xyt+B. Introduce the RAW polynomial products P=a*b of width2d-1 and W=u*c of widthn+d-1. For each raw coefficient impose its XOR convolution equation. These rows contain one new bit with coefficient1, so every original assignment has exactly one auxiliary extension, including zero and repeated inputs. No field inverse or squarefree assumption is used.

In characteristic2 squaring is F2-linear. The first field equation is (au)^2+(bu)^2+P^2+Pu+B=0. The second is W^2+zW+z^2u^2+z^2c^2+B=0. Coordinate expansion in the specified basis gives exactly2n quadratic/linear rows; the2d-1+n+d-1 gadget rows also have degree<=2. Substitution proves both directions and injectivity of original projection. Thus all ordered original polynomial roots, all finite u, and all zero/repeated/ghost branches are retained. There is no rational-point filtering. Multiplicity of algebraic schemes is not claimed; the contract is exact distinct finite root tuples.

For n83,d18 this is137+35+100=272 variables and166+35+100=301 equations. Stored coordinate expansion uses O(n^2d+nd^2) term occurrences in the worst dense basis, plus O(d^2+nd) product gadgets; it does not remove the general search cost. All-root recovery projects to low137 bits, checks the deterministic extension and literal original equations, and returns every tuple without truncation. This encoding alone is not an all-roots algorithm or a speed gain. The preregistered representation prediction is fewer than half124560 original term occurrences. Canonical original-root matching and charged lifting/serialization are mandatory for later end-to-end comparisons.
