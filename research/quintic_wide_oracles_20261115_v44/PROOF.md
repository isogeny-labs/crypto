The quintic discriminant proof is independent of extension degree and basis: it uses characteristic two, polynomial division and the algebraic closure. This panel checks the implementation in exact Rabin-validated binary fields of degrees 83,163,233,283,409,571. Known root bags include zero, repeated sources, two repeated pairs, distinct supported roots and an outside-domain root. Their full products give an independent exact support expectation, while a Euclidean derivative GCD verifies repeated-root classification. This is arithmetic/support coverage, not ordinary-curve or Koblitz system coverage and not a hard benchmark. The odd-characteristic derivative is different, so this dispatch must never be used for NIST prime or odd extension fields.
