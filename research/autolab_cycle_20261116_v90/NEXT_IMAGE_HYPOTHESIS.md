# New conditional elimination variant: image-subspace annihilator

For binary S3(x,y,u)=0 with x constrained to F2-subspace D,basis b1..bd,write a=(y+u)^2,b=yu,c=(yu)^2+B. The equation is g(x)=c with g(x)=ax^2+bx,F2-linear. Let V=g(D),whose spanning vectors are g(bi). Then there exists x in D iff L_V(c)=0,where L_V is the exact subspace annihilator constructed by the tested recurrence. This is an exact separator factor,not a probabilistic residue filter. If a,b both0,handle c=0 explicitly;otherwise ker(g) has at most2elements,so dimV=d or d-1. Output every affine preimage fiber when reconstructing roots;an existential predicate alone is not an all-roots solver.

The proposed variant changes representation of the existing consistency test:use image-subspace annihilator rather than a fresh83x18augmented matrix solve at every conditional separator. It is mathematically equivalent to matrix membership and does not claim new pruning.
