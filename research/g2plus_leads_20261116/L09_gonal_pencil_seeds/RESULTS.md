# L09 RESULTS: free gonal-pencil relations as large-prime graph seeds  [pcc:unverified]

Numbers only. Not a benchmark: no leaderboard row, no `benchmarks/` row, no speed claim, no wall time.
Synthetic random (3,3) curves on P^1 x P^1 over F_p only. One producer, one shared 4-core host, no
independent replay. Seeds 0..19 for every cell (not extended). Everything is `[pcc:unverified]`.
Source of every number: `raw/*.jsonl` (hashes in `raw/SHA256SUMS`, commands in `raw/COMMANDS.log`),
aggregated by `code/analyze.py` into `raw/summary.json`; tables below generated by `code/gen_results_tables.py`.
Unit: FPM = field multiplications + 10 x inversions (squarings as multiplications), counted in the field type.
`r_B = 1 - T_B/T_A` per seed (T = line trials at the first moment the union-find yield proxy `Y >= n_cols - 1`);
`f_B` likewise with `FPM_rel = FPM_line + FPM_pencil`. N = median |r_AA| from the A/A arm A2.
