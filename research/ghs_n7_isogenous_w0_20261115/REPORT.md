## Family and bound

[Menezes–Teske, Sections 5.1–5.2](https://cacr.uwaterloo.ca/techreports/2004/corr2004-25.pdf) define W0 by absolute `Tr(a)=0` and both factors of `b=(gamma1 gamma2)^2` in one cubic Frobenius kernel; the direct descent genus is seven. W1 requires `Tr(a)=1` and a cubic-times-linear second factor; the direct descent genus is fourteen. The prior 112-row census paired 56 eligible coefficient values with both twists. At q=2, exactly 56 rows have a=1 and are W1. Its genus-seven hyperelliptic isogeny-class screen passes 42 of those 56 and excludes 14. This is a bound on the **hyperelliptic isogeny-class route** in that finite census, not on every GHS transfer or on attack cost.

For the W0 product construction, the two cubic kernels yield 49 distinct coefficients in `F2[z]/(z^7+z+1)`. Two independent point counters agree on all 127 nonzero elliptic coefficients. The W0 trace distribution is `{-15:14, +1:7, +9:21, +17:7}`. In particular, this W0 locus has no trace −9 source at q=2. The coefficient b=8 has witness `gamma1=z` (mask 2), `gamma2=z^64` (mask 18), with `(gamma1 gamma2)^2=z^3=8`. This makes `E_+: y²+xy=x³+z³`, trace +9 and order 120, a published W0 representative. Its twist `E_-: y²+xy=x³+x²+z³` has trace −9 and order 138 and is W1 at q=2.

There is a stronger **published isogeny barrier** than this finite nonintersection: [Hess, Section 6](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/315278965D8D277A85812D9498236A88/S146115700000108Xa.pdf/generalising-the-ghs-attack-on-the-elliptic-curve-discrete-logarithm-problem.pdf) records that ordinary binary curves with absolute `Tr(a)=0` have group order divisible by four, whereas those with absolute `Tr(a)=1` have order congruent to two modulo four. Isogenous elliptic curves over the same finite field have the same group order. Therefore **no W1 source can be carried by an isogeny to a W0 curve over that same field**, for any field size. The q2 orders 138 and 120 exhibit the two congruence classes. This closes the proposed W1-to-W0 isogenous-source shortcut globally, while leaving other cover or correspondence constructions open.

## Direct map control

Magma returned `WeilDescentGenus(E_+,F2,z)=7`, a genus-seven plane curve, and an evaluated `DivCrvElt` for `P=(z^36:z^82:1)` of order five. The output is **not identified** with the separate hyperelliptic `Y_+` from the trace census. The independent [kernel calculation](kernel_bound.txt) finds minimal Frobenius annihilator `T³+T+1` for both `c=z` and `r/c=z^64`, where `r=sqrt(b)=z^65`; their least common multiple has degree three. [Hess, Theorem 14](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/315278965D8D277A85812D9498236A88/S146115700000108Xa.pdf/generalising-the-ghs-attack-on-the-elliptic-curve-discrete-logarithm-problem.pdf) bounds the kernel of the published conorm/norm homomorphism by `2^(3−1)=4` torsion for this non-subfield curve. Thus the map is injective on the source subgroup of order five as a theorem-level consequence. The response evaluates a divisor expression but does not perform Jacobian-class arithmetic or target-log recovery.

## Novelty and limits

The +9 direct genus-seven route is a rediscovery of the published W0 construction. The −9 W1 source has a genus-seven **hyperelliptic isogeny-class carrier**, but its direct Magma descent census has minimum genus fourteen. A point-count match to a hyperelliptic Jacobian does not construct the intervening isogeny or make the published W1 map land there. No new eligible curve family, new attack method, or measured GHS advantage follows from these results. The family label correction removes the earlier ±9 W1 novelty claim. An exact −9 curve equation or its hyperelliptic carrier may still be a new recorded witness, subject to wider prior-work review.

The genus-seven lower bound for nonzero algebraic transfer from the simple seven-dimensional Weil restriction holds for these nonexceptional traces. It is a geometric dimension bound. A rigorous vulnerability bound also needs the total relation, decomposition, linear-algebra, descent, map-evaluation, and same-subgroup rho costs. Those quantities are not measured here.
