Question

Binary Koblitz curves, Weil descent of Semaev's S4 (m = 3) and S3 (m = 2) over a factor base of F2-dimension ℓ = n/3. Lever: write the system in the translation invariants of the rational 2-torsion point (u = 1/(x+1), w = u²+u, s = Σu; base {P : u(P) ∈ V}, 1 ∈ V). At m = 3 that gives degree-4 equations in 3ℓ−2 unknowns, against the production chain (degree 3, 3ℓ+n unknowns) and the direct S4 (degree 6, 3ℓ unknowns). Metric: HKY last fall degree dF (least c with 1 in the degree-c mutant closure), and its slope in n.

Reading

Every frame falls at input degree + 1 (chain 4, direct 7, sym 5), far below the semi-regular degree of the same shapes: the known low fall degree of the binary Weil descent. The symmetrised system is the most nearly square (n equations, n − 2 unknowns), so its excess over its input degree appears first. The frame trades size for degree; it shrinks the matrix by a constant that decreases with n and does not change the growth. At m = 2 all frames read dF ∈ {2, 3}; the window base is one degree lower than a random base in the x frame at n = 18 and 21, and in the sym frame only at n = 21.

Limits

Toy range (n ≤ 18 for the paired comparison). One step in four n values carries the slope, so the bootstrap intervals (zero draw variance) understate the uncertainty. dF only, no F4/F5 solving degree.
