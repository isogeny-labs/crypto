Readable proof: apply the inverse cyclic-group isomorphism to the point relation;
additivity and natural-scalar compatibility give the scalar equation. In a
prime-cardinality group the subgroup generated by a nonzero P cannot be trivial,
so it is the full group. For recovery, distribute a certified weighted sum of
valid original rows and evaluate the resulting target unit row. For ambiguity,
linearity preserves every equation after adding a kernel vector, while the
target coordinate changes by one.
