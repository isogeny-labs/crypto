## New local correctness work and bounds

These are bounded native exact proof-development units, not runtime
benchmarks. No EKS, paid resource, public CAS or old failed cell is launched.

1. One new depth5 graph lift: read the cached structured footprint, adjoin
   its four degree4 standard monomials as unique coordinates, and run the
   existing exact BM/S-pair/rank checker on this new embedding only. At most
   30s/2GiB and one fresh-directory byte replay with the same bound. No
   parameter search, no rerunning the previous twelve membership cells.
2. One deterministic depth8 construction/certificate and complete-law
   correctness invocation, <=180s/2GiB, with at most one 180s byte replay
   after success. Export base construction/identity and retained point data.
   Do not run BM on the larger base under this allowance. Verify the dense
   complete-intersection identity if applicable and a spanning/rank bound
   for the structured input. Record a failed hypothesis rather than changing
   the fixture. Prove no rational2torsion by a polynomial gcd certificate.
3. Implement the published complete projective law; exact GMP/OpenSSL
   controls include identity, inverses, doubling, nonunit projective scales
   and balanced m=2--7 planted sums. Replay every reported relation. A
   finite checked control set does not itself prove the law universally;
   cite the published law and certify its hypotheses independently.
4. Bound a deterministic target-line search by64 slopes per fixed target.
   No line found means that cell remains open. The discriminant nonsquare
   certificate proves the line isolates the target on rational points.
5. Construct and review complete-system upper/lower bound proofs. An upper
   bound for each arm alone is insufficient. Explicitly check Gorenstein
   duality shifts, empty/nonempty accepted sets, H-torsion and degree bounds.
   Only a proved theorem with all concrete hypotheses certified may produce
   a complete-system inequality. Unexecuted sketches stay conditional.

Do not expand exponentially large m-input polynomial lists or enumerate the
relation table to obtain a favorable degree. Report a circuit as a circuit,
not a materialized list; expansion/runtime remain charged and unmeasured.
Keep the original depth8 and matched m=2--7 obligations explicit. No F4 or
end-to-end speed claim follows from a regularity bound.
