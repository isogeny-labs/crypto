## Proposed finite-point algebra bound

Let A=S/I(T) be the homogeneous coordinate ring of a finite rational point
set T, all in H!=0, with H regular and graded free K[H] basis of maximum
degree r. Let F be generated by homogeneous polynomials of degrees<=e,
with e>=1, **in this same ring and without new free auxiliary variables**.
For the complete ideal J=I(T)+F, the proposed bound is

    reg(S/J) <= r+e-1,       reg(J) <= r+e.

Argument to formalize/check: all point functions have homogeneous degree-r
Lagrange representatives L_t. If t is rejected by F, choose f_i(t)!=0;
then L_t*f_i equals f_i(t)*L_t*H^deg(f_i) in A. At degree r+e, F therefore
contains every rejected-point coordinate function. In all later degrees
S/J is exactly the accepted-point function space, and multiplication by H
is an isomorphism. H-torsion ends by r+e-1. The saturation is the reduced
accepted-point algebra, with quotient regularity<=r; the torsion exact
sequence gives the bound. Empty accepted sets need the Artinian case.
This is an unsigned derivation, not a checked Lean theorem.

For a Cartesian product of m membership blocks with a common H, disjoint
H-free leading monomials give a free tensor-product basis, so r is the
sum of block quotient regularities. The dense/chain depth5 blocks give
r_dense=15m and r_chain=4m. These are **input ring** regularities, not
complete relation values. Comparing two upper bounds cannot establish2x.

3. Certify complete-ideal Hilbert/H-torsion data or another exact graded

   alongside a candidate upper bound; local point degrees are insufficient.
4. Apply the same group encoding and normalization to both paired arms,
   preserve m=2–7/depth8, freeze resources before executing, and retain all
   censored cells. No new CAS run or extra budget is launched in this note.
