## Question and falsifier

The candidate avoids the inverse cubic for each raw destination point. It does not claim extra relation support: if transport is correct, the projected source columns should equal those in the frozen inverse arm.

The leading falsifier is that dual-kernel discovery, map evaluation and one `[153]` multiplication per column cost as much as or more than inverse-Vélu setup, or that the paired cold total remains above the same-target rho/BSGS controls. A failed dual identity, mismatched projected base, relation row, rank, recovered scalar, or checker replay invalidates the candidate run. A complete primitive price and isolated wall claim require separate gates below.
