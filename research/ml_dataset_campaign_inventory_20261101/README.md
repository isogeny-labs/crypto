This is a launch inventory for the requested n23–31 and n47–61 Koblitz, ordinary-binary, and prime-curve campaign, with both twists, m2–15, multiple generators/keys, and every supported native method. It records selected *non-overlapping, checked* cohorts; it is not a claim that the requested matrix is complete. The native `crypto dataset` CLI and its strict structural exporter are documented in `docs/ml-dataset/`. The authoritative [stratified campaign design](../../docs/ml-dataset/STRATIFIED-CAMPAIGN.md) retains 165,150,720 method cells at full width and explicit unsupported, timed-out, and unattempted cells rather than silently narrowing the denominator. The prospective [training contract](../../docs/ml-dataset/training-protocol-20261101.md) governs any fit or evaluation.

These seven blocks total 124 *selected terminal method cells* under different protocols, seeds, methods and resource caps. The count is a coverage inventory, not a pooled estimate or a training set. Separately, the [252-cell compatibility matrix](../../docs/ml-dataset/training-protocol-20261101.md) spans n23/47/61, every family and twist, and m2–15; its overridden build identity makes it development-only, and it is not added to the 124. The local n24–31 restricted payloads have verified custody manifests and restores but have not been uploaded to S3. The older n23/n47/prime61 S3 claims are exactly those in their linked producer checkpoints and independent readback samples; this inventory does not perform a new remote readback.

The available labels are mostly method-completion, bounded candidate counts, factor-base outcomes, and censored statuses. The n24–31 strict structural exports provide public features with restricted targets and whole-field-size split components. They do not create exact first/last fall-degree labels or complete instruction-to-solve labels. Existing n23 and n47 raw calling-thread PMU readings do not verify whole-worker coverage. The n26–31 strict split has 30 train and six validation occurrences, with no test component by itself. The n24–25 split adds a test-size component, but those separate exports have not been combined and audited as one training corpus. A model result must therefore use an explicit joined manifest, group-aware split audit, eligible target definition, and held-out evaluation before a training claim.

For the requested model, useful *prospective* targets are (1) whether a public factor-base choice yields a verified decomposition or relation under a fixed budget, with planted positives and independently sampled natural controls kept distinct; (2) causal next-step predictions on public prefix traces and relation/rank growth, with later solver state and known scalar withheld; (3) invariant representations of Frobenius/symmetry orbits and curve/twist metadata, with orbit members kept in one split component; (4) checked polynomial-consequence or certificate prediction for F4/F5/F6/root and SAT/Weil/PKM traces. An exact first- or last-fall-degree claim requires its own finite-instance certificate and independent checker. A new SAT solver proposal needs a frozen controlled baseline and exact solution/certificate replay, not just a faster model score. Unknown labels remain null; timed-out cells remain censored.

The next data wave should first build a checker-clean *combined manifest* for the already saved cohorts and verify split components and feature/label eligibility without copying restricted witnesses into public model inputs. Then freeze a prospective planted-positive/control protocol that yields nondegenerate PDP labels across m3–15 and the prime n23 gap, followed by stratified F4/F5/F6/root/SAT/Weil/PKM blocks at all requested n and twists. Keep the full requested grid as a manifest with method support and work limits per cell; increase generator/key diversity only under a priced, approved envelope. The currently proposed Conductor task T-1257 for full-run S3 transport needs an approved grant-bound worker and cumulative budget before cloud submission. [PR #3037](https://github.com/isogeny-labs/crypto/pull/3037) merged preflight/launch-gate code, but that is not itself a worker grant or a completed S3 publication. Do not repeat the Diem-managed generator or existing EKS jobs while pursuing this plan.

The mathematical scope uses [Semaev 2004](https://eprint.iacr.org/2004/031.pdf) for prime-field summation-polynomial context and the [SAC 2020 subfield-curve study](https://sacworkshop.org/SAC20/files/preproceedings/18-IndexCalculus.pdf) for the conditions behind Frobenius-invariant factor bases. The source records above distinguish Koblitz from ordinary-binary coefficient fields and preserve twist identities; the inventory makes no inference that a relation on one curve transports a logarithm to its twist.
