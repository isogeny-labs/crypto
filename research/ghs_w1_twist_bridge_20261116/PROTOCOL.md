# W0-to-W1 twist bridge: decisive geometry check

Frozen 16 November 2026 before the public Magma call.

The known W0 source `E_+: y²+xy=x³+z³` over `K=F2[z]/(z⁷+z+1)` has trace `+9`, order 120 and a published genus-seven descent to a plane curve `C_+`. Its quadratic twist `E_-: y²+xy=x³+x²+z³` has trace `-9`, order 138 and W1 membership. The finite hyperelliptic census exhibits `Y_+: y²+v(x)y=u_+(x)` and `Y_-: y²+v(x)y=u_-(x)` over F2 with `v=0x8f`, `u_+=0x802f`, `u_-=0xc07a`. A direct bit-polynomial check must verify `u_-+u_+=v²`, the exact constant quadratic-twist identity.

This identity alone does **not** twist the W0 descent: the published target is `C_+`, which has not been identified with `Y_+`. Twisting a homomorphism `Res(E_+)→J(C_+)` by the sign character gives a map to the sign twist of `J(C_+)`. For it to arise from a quadratic twist of the **same curve**, a curve involution must induce `[-1]` on its Jacobian. In genus at least two this is the hyperelliptic involution. The decisive first falsifier is therefore geometric hyperellipticity of the exact Magma `C_+` returned by `WeilDescent(E_+,F2,z)`. The source calls the documented `IsHyperelliptic` and `IsGeometricallyHyperelliptic` predicates and records the complete response. A negative result closes this direct curve-twist bridge, while leaving an explicit isogeny to the separate hyperelliptic carrier open.
