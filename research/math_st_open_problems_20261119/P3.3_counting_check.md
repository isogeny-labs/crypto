# P3.3 / Q099: upstream's "pure-power penalty" is the counting bound

Triage row: `triage.json`, P3.3 (attack later). Kill criterion, preregistered
in `README.md` (commit `5ea6c7e`): "$\min\ell^e/p$ roughly constant across
sizes means the 'structural gap' is counting".

## The restatement

The problem (math-st P3.3) asks for ideals equivalent to a given left
$\mathcal O$-ideal $I$ of norm about $\sqrt p$.

- **Unconstrained norms (easy).** The norm form on $I$ is a positive-definite
  quaternary form, and Minkowski gives a nonzero element of reduced norm
  $O(\sqrt p)\,N(I)$. A shortest vector in dimension 4 is found exactly in
  polynomial time. So reaching $\sqrt p$ is easy and has nothing to do with
  KLPT.
- **$\ell$-power norms (impossible below about $p$).** There are
  $(\ell+1)\ell^{e-1}$ left ideals of norm $\ell^e$, and about $p/12$ ideal
  classes. Reaching a prescribed class therefore needs
  $(\ell+1)\ell^{e-1}\gtrsim p/12$, so the smallest $\ell$-power norm is of
  order $p$, not $\sqrt p$.

The meaningful open question is the gap between this counting optimum (about
$p$) and what KLPT returns (about $p^{3}$ to $p^{7/2}$). Upstream calls it the
"exact shape gap" and treats it as structural.

## Check on upstream's own recorded data

Source: upstream
`problems/P3.3-ideal-to-isogeny-norms/data/measure_power_targets_p2203-245000047x18_t6_s33032028_20260713_raw.csv`
at math-st `f631bcc`. It holds 108 rows; 3 rows with empty norm fields were
skipped. The table gives medians of upstream's recorded norms, per size of $p$.

| $p$ bits | instances | median $N_{2^e}/p$ | median $N_{3^e}/p$ | median $N_{\text{unconstrained}}/\sqrt p$ | range of $N_{2^e}/p$ |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 12 | 36 | 0.091 | 0.092 | 0.369 | 0.004 – 0.591 |
| 20 | 35 | 0.117 | 0.082 | 0.389 | 0.005 – 0.728 |
| 28 | 34 | 0.063 | 0.078 | 0.369 | 0.004 – 0.231 |

Both normalized medians are flat in $p$. The pure-power optimum sits at
$N\approx p/12$; the class count is $\approx p/12$ and $1/12\approx0.083$.
The unconstrained optimum sits at $N\approx0.37\sqrt p$.

Upstream's headline "roughly 2500× penalty in the 28-bit band" is therefore
$\approx(0.07\,p)/(0.37\sqrt p)\approx0.2\sqrt p\approx3\times10^3$ at
$p\approx2^{28}$. That is the counting bound.

**Verdict.** The preregistered kill criterion is met on upstream's data: the
"structural gap" between pure-power and unconstrained optima is counting.
P3.3 should be restated as the counting-optimum ($\approx p$) versus KLPT
question. Q099 (validated KLPT on matched ideals) remains the only open part.
It is an implementation task and is not attacked here.

**Method caveat.** These medians were taken by a short Python read of
upstream's recorded CSV values, not by a native run of ours. The lab's no-Python
rule covers our own result generation. Before this check is cited as a lab
measurement, rerun it natively, or regenerate the norms with our own code.
