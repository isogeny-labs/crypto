## Cost and decision

Membership is materially relevant: genus4H11shuffle succeeds with3824–4672principal outputs, while the same mode atH22needs688–832. Thus its smaller matrix trades for substantially more generation. Atgenus3H18shuffle needs1224–1768outputs versus800–1024atH36. Genus2H7never recovers across the five frozen modes; full-family failures cannot be repaired by pivot reorder within their admitted row space.

No membership mode supplies an all-genus half-core recovery or complete tenfold cost premise. Target-support selection also sometimes exceeds the strict small-core budget; silently enlarging H would change the experiment. The already-computed source/target equations should be reused in an implementation that pays to obtain them, but that is ordinary reuse/accounting and the existing Compact front end already does so.

A successor needs a structural cheap-constraint mechanism with a complete charged cost case against the best existing solver, including anchor selection, failed preparatory descents, scan/shifts, all principal outputs/tails, roots, graph/LA, allocations, setup and verification. Selected counters and smaller dimensions do not provide that case. The full mathematical10xobjective remains active. No timing campaign or performance promotion follows from these counts.
