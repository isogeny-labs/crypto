# Frozen degree-seven quadratic-square denominator screen

16 November 2026 UTC. This is a **partial** search for K-defined degree-seven maps from the fixed genus-five `Y/F3` to each of the ten exact trace-`+26` elliptic curves. It covers `B=H²` and `B=Qi H²` for every monic quadratic `H=x²+ux+v` coprime to f, excluding `H=(x-c)²` already covered in `DEGREE7_LINEAR_SQUARE_PROTOCOL.md`. It does not cover cubic H, two quadratic branch poles plus a rational square, or three quadratic branch poles.

The branch CRT includes `L=x+1` at the unique rational elliptic 2-torsion x-coordinate and all unpoled `Qj` at their three Galois-compatible choices. For a given assignment h and modulus M, the only possible A of degree at most seven is `(B*h) mod M`. Write `H²=x^4+2u x^3+(u²+2v)x²+2uv x+v²`. The coefficients of `x^d` in A are explicit quadratic polynomials `F_d(u,v)` obtained from the five precomputed residues `(base*x^i*h) mod M`, where base is 1 or Qi. For `B=H²`, M has degree11 and `F_8=F_9=F_10=0` is necessary for `deg A≤7`. For `B=QiH²`, M has degree9 and `F_8=0` is necessary.

Solve the first system by a nonzero pairwise resultant in v, then specialize and take a univariate gcd; if every pairwise resultant is zero, enumerate all u. If a specialized gcd is zero, enumerate all v. Solve the second system by enumerating all u and taking the v-roots of its specialized polynomial, enumerating all v if that polynomial is zero. Every root is reconstructed and checked against all coefficient equations. Reject H sharing a factor with f or equal to `(x-c)²`; require `deg A=7`, `gcd(A,B)=1`, and exact `f*C²=B*(A³+2A²B+bB³)`. A positive hit records A,B,C and branch choices.

For each target, there are 58,559 allowed H, 243 branch assignments for `H²`, and 405 for `QiH²`. The source is committed before execution; every target is run separately with source-bound stdout and timing. A zero-hit result applies only to these two shapes and is not a complete degree-seven exclusion.
