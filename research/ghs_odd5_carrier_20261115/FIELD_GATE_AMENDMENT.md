# Field of definition of the Howe pullback

15 November 2026. This note fixes the destination field for the four cover-induced subgroup maps in `THEOREM.md`. [pcc:unverified]

For `t=-14,-10,+22,+26`, Howe's theorem gives a curve map `φ:Y_t,K→E_t` over `K=F243` whose degree is smaller than the selected source subgroup prime. Push-pull therefore makes `φ*:E_t[r]→J(Y_t)(K)[r]` injective. **This argument lands in the Jacobian over `K`; it does not by itself give an element of `J(Y_t)(F3)`**, the smaller group needed for the intended GHS transfer. The target field is explicit in the formula in `THEOREM.md`. The separate integral Tate argument gives existence of an `F3`-isogeny `Res_(K/F3) E_t→J(Y_t)` of degree coprime to `r` in seven traces, but supplies no evaluation formula. Neither existence proof presently gives the requested executable small-field map. [pcc:unverified]

This distinction remains in the four-page `REPORT.pdf` and should be preserved in any use of the cover-degree table or PR summary. An explicit `F3`-correspondence, its action on a selected source subgroup, and the complete same-target attack cost are the next constructive tests. [pcc:unverified]
