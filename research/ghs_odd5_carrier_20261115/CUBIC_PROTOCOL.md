For a genus-five hyperelliptic curve with rational Weierstrass point at infinity, a morphism to an elliptic curve can be translated so infinity maps to the origin. It then intertwines the hyperelliptic involution with negation. A degree-three morphism gives a degree-three rational function `u=A/B` on the two x-lines. The twelve distinct branch points of the hyperelliptic map must lie above the four elliptic 2-torsion branch points; there are at most three points in each fiber, so all four fibers consist of three distinct branch points. In particular infinity is a simple pole of `u` and the other two poles are finite roots of `f`. Thus `B` is a monic squarefree degree-two divisor of `f` and `A` can be taken monic cubic and coprime to `B`. A target equation `v²=u³+c2*u²+c1*u+c0` yields a coefficient identity `f=B*(A³+c2*A²*B+c1*A*B²+c0*B³)` after normalizing the target y coordinate. The current source models have `c1=0`, but the search must allow `c1` because a quotient need not equal the selected model. [pcc:unverified]

For each degree-two divisor `B` of each fixed `f`, the congruence `(f/B) ≡ A³ (mod B)` determines `A mod B` uniquely, since cubing is a field automorphism of `K[x]/(B)`.
