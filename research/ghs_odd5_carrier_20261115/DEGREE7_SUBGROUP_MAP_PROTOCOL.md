# Frozen subgroup-map verification for degree-seven cover hits

16 November 2026 UTC. The exploratory b-mask 30 check found that a degree-seven cover from the quadratic-square screen gives a nonzero map from its order-109 source subgroup into `J(Y)(F3)` after the correct pole-fiber subtraction and Galois trace. The first attempt subtracted `7*infinity` instead of the full fiber over the elliptic origin and produced a class with a 2-torsion offset; this is retained here as a diagnostic, not accepted as a transfer. The frozen verifier below repeats the corrected construction on every exact hit.

For a retained `B=Qi*H²` cover `phi:(x,y)->(A/B,y*C/B²)`, verify `deg A=7`, `deg B=6`, `gcd(A,B)=1`, and `f*C²=B*(A³+2A²B+bB³)`. For an order-109 point `P=(u,v)` on `E_b(K)`, let `U=A-uB`, made monic, and `V=v B²/C mod U`. When U is squarefree and C is invertible mod U, `(U,V)` represents `[phi^{-1}(P)-7 infinity]`. The fiber over the elliptic origin consists of the two branch points over Qi, the four points over H, and infinity; the conjugate pairs over H cancel in the Jacobian. Its class relative to `7 infinity` is exactly the 2-torsion class `(Qi,0)`. Therefore the corrected pullback is `D(P)=(U,V)-(Qi,0)`.

The small-field transfer is `T(P)=sum_(i=0)^4 Frob_3^i D(P)`. Verify `[109]D(P)=0`, `[109]T(P)=0`, `T(P)!=0`, and `Frob_3(T(P))=T(P)`. For an independently chosen scalar `k=37`, verify `D([k]P)=[k]D(P)` and `T([k]P)=[k]T(P)` using independently computed preimage divisors. Retain exact P, kP, source map polynomials, corrected Mumford representatives and traced Mumford representatives. These are finite-point checks of the explicit rational map, supplemented by the exact function-field identity and standard divisor pullback functoriality; Lean does not formalize the geometric functoriality.

This protocol does not claim a faster discrete-logarithm attack. A full decomposition, factor-log, target-recovery and same-target rho comparison remains required, particularly after base change to larger subgroup sizes.

The first frozen run reached all assertions but failed when Python JSON encoding encountered Sage's Integer type in the output. Commit `0ade70b35bd3` retains that attempt. The amended source converts Sage integers during serialization; no mathematical check was relaxed.
