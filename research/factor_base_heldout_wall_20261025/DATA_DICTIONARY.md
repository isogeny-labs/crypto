# N12 paired wall-cost data dictionary

This packet is a separate wall-time experiment on the frozen 420-target factor-base held-out cohort. It does not change or blend the operation-count pricing in `research/factor_base_heldout_replay_20261025`.

`PROTOCOL.md` gives the prespecified target set, 12-process A/A plus five-round ABAB schedule, CPU isolation, time boundary, and validation gates. `results/status.json` is the compact machine-readable no-run status and null wall ratio. `results/host.json` records the local hardware and Rust compiler. `results/binaries.sha256` records the timed binary hashes if a timed schedule begins. `results/isolation.jsonl` is the output of `tools/isolated_bench.py run`, with `run.contended`, `reserved_cpus`, quiet-host preflight and resource counters. If the lock or preflight blocks the schedule, the wall ratio is null; a failed preflight without a tool receipt is recorded in `results/preflight.txt`.

`results/timed/processes.jsonl` has one row per scheduled arm process. It records `slot`, `round`, `arm`, `start_unix_ns`, full process `wall_ns`, exit code, raw JSONL path, row count, verified count, and I/O pressure before and after the arm. Slots `aa1,aa2` are independent rho copies. Slots `ab1a` through `ab5b` alternate candidate/rho order. Each arm process includes startup, input parsing, all target construction, 420 source-verified solves and output serialization. One process runs at a time on the reserved SMT pair.

`results/timed/raw/*.jsonl` contains 420 native target rows per completed process. `wall_ns` here is the inner `solve + independent source verification` interval; `counts`, `native`, `phases`, `source_add`, target derivation and scalar verification are source fields. `results/validated/wall-targets.csv` has exactly 5,040 expected slot-target rows and is stored by hash in `results/validated/.artifacts.json`; `python3 tools/artifacts/artifacts.py pull research/factor_base_heldout_wall_20261025/results/validated` hydrates it. `status=not_run` means the observation is absent, `invalid` means a row or process failed validation, and `valid` means all frozen checks passed; missing and invalid timings have no headline ratio. `results/validated/wall-processes.csv` has 12 process rows. `results/validated/validation.json` reports all gates and a ratio only if the full schedule and isolation pass.

The A/A statistic divides the two full rho process times. The candidate/rho statistic divides the candidate and rho full process times in each paired round; the report gives all five ratios, their median and minimum. Inner target times remain diagnostic and are not used to claim cold process wall cost. All wall ratios are null if the full schedule or isolation is invalid. Neither wall ratio nor operation-count ratio is a security claim.
