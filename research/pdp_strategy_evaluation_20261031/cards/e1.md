Verdict: REVISE (narrow to one stage diagnostic; the lead cannot improve S as written)

I recomputed from `results/m2.jsonl`+`geomtrace_m2.jsonl`: 656 distinct bases, 7 families, n = 13..47, MAE 0.059: reproduced. But the fit is m = 2, direct formulation, D in {2,3,4}. m = 3 has a different fit (309 bases, `D = 4 + [e<22.5] + [e<3.5] + [e<-39.5]`, MAE 0.157, 0.222 leave-one-n-out). There is no m = 4 fit. The committed closure draws all use `random_subspace_basis` (`examples/dreg_ladder.rs:440`): zero structured-base cells exist for the descent cells. Closure-degree-predictor (ml-cryptanalysis) confirms the features are only m, n, l, N, S.
Prediction: (1) e is two-valued: over 63 (n,l) cells the median number of distinct e is 2 (max 3). Top-e = {prefix, geometric, geomtrace}; median-e = random. "Rank subspaces by e" has no gradient and its argmax is analytic. (2) The baseline already is top-e: `koblitz-standard-subspace` = span{1,z,..} (`src/bin/ecbench/pdp3_koblitz.rs:64`); ladder d = 5,7,8,10,12 at n = 13..37 vs (n+2)/3 = 5,7,8.3,11,13. So gain over baseline from this lever = 0. (3) Versus random bases, measured: m = 2 cells where the rule predicts a 1-degree gap, ops/attempt 19.3x (2.8-100), else 1.5x; m = 3 (31 paired cells, my recompute) mean dD +0.245, geo-mean ops/attempt 1.42x, 7 cells at dD = +1 (all l = 4-5, n >= 29, N = 12-15) with 2.1x. (4) Descent cells, derived from the committed ECC2K-130 model: one closure degree at m = 4 is worth 2.3-2.7 bits (w = 1: 5->4 moves +10.32 to +7.62) or 12-13 bits (w = 2, the exponent the closure measures); best case D = 3: +5.34 (w=1), +19.84 (w=2). No scenario crosses rho. (5) End-to-end: see appendix; ceiling 1.00x at w = 0.
Decisive experiment: re-draw the committed ell = 7 cells (m = 3; n = 19, 21; N = 40, 42) with the prefix base `v_basis = [1,2,4,..,64]` and a random geometric progression, 8 UNSAT draws each, plus fresh random A/A and the ell = 6 cells as sanity (all arms must refute at 4). Instrument: `examples/dreg_closure --to 4 --max-cols 130000`; about 40 draws, 5-8 GB, 1-2 h on the 16 GB container, negligible disk. Frozen: committed random arm refutes at degree 4 on 0/8; success if prefix+GP refute on >= 12/16 and A/A <= 2/16; kill if <= 3/16 (one-sided Fisher p > 0.05 against 0/16), which closes it as m = 2-specific.
Risks and contradictions: Success only re-prices an extrapolation (stage diagnostic, not an improvement, AGENTS section 6). Yield cost is 1.5-5% (P(dec) ratio 0.95, 0.985). Kosters-Yeo give first fall degree 2 for descended S_3; the repo's chain reads 3, so D has a floor the base cannot move. The brief's "+7.62 floor" is the D = 4, w = 1 row; the free-oracle floor at m = 4 is 2^56.46 (-4.35 bits).
Confidence: high that baseline = top-e (code-checked); low on any ell = 7 effect.
