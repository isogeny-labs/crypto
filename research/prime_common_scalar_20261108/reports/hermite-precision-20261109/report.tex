\textbf{Actual multiplier bound by pairing.}
Exclude $0,t$ and let $m(s)$ be the actual centered representative of $t-s$.
Lean proves the involution, both exclusions, congruence modulo $2L$ and exact
valuation preservation. Put $v_s=v_2(s)$, $w_s=v_2(t-s)=v_2(m(s))$, $i=v_2(t)$.
For the actual product at zero,
\[
 V=v_2(E_t(0))=\sum_s q(s)(v_s-w_s),\qquad
 2V=\sum_s(q(s)-q(m(s)))(v_s-w_s).
\]
The decreasing natural taper is $h$-Lipschitz, giving
$2V\geq-h\sum_s(v_s-w_s)^2$. The actual ultrametric relation gives
\[
 (v_s-w_s)^2=(v_s-i)_+^2+(w_s-i)_+^2.
\]
Pairing makes these two sums equal. Both excluded terms have zero excess.
Actual level counts, including the lone endpoint at top level $a$, imply
\[
 \sum_s(v_s-i)_+^2
   =\sum_{j=0}^a2^{a-j}(j-i)_+^2\leq6\,2^{a-i}.
\]
The final bound uses the finite identity
$\sum_{k=0}^n k^2/2^k=6-(n^2+4n+6)/2^n$.
Thus $V\geq-6hD_i$, $D_i=2^{a-i}$, from the actual grid rather than an
assumed moment estimate or random distribution.
