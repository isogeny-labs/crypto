# Broad goal status and the exact missing theorem

The requested goal is a faithful Lean proof establishing or ruling out a novel
classical sub-exponential algorithm for general prime-field ECDLP. The current
work does not complete either side of that goal.

## What is actually established here

There are source-connected algebraic proofs of node preservation, actual
integral local charts and their formal inverses, actual polynomial degree,
finite coefficient/ideal precision equivalence, and an exact finite modular
inverse-buffer specification. The frozen scalar panel and all its losses are
retained separately. See the proof maps and producer receipts; signed independent
standing remains absent. [pcc:unverified]

## The mathematical blocker

The inverse buffer takes the denominator's coefficient vector as input. Its
correctness does not construct or bound the full relation/partition evaluator.
No completed theorem in this work gives the actual contraction/recovery program
and a uniform all-level bound on every construction, enumeration, child-entry,
precision, bit-arithmetic, failed-attempt and verification cost for general
prime-field ECDLP. In particular, there is no established complete recurrence
T(n)=exp(o(n)), n=ceil(log2 p), for such a program here.

The finite tuple-count comparisons do not fill that gap. A local kernel
identity, a finite cardinality, and a conservative precision proxy cannot be
substituted for a uniform asymptotic algorithm and its correctness/cost proof.

The other direction would require an appropriate lower bound over the allowed
non-generic algorithm class. The origin-preservation obstruction applies to
the stated node-preserving repair family. It is not such a lower bound and does
not imply unrestricted nonexistence of sub-exponential ECDLP algorithms.

## Continuation contract

The next meaningful step is an explicit actual contraction/recovery evaluator
with a quantitative all-level mode and child-call bound, followed by a faithful
bit-cost recurrence. Native refinement of the finite buffer and full Hermite
precision remain supporting obligations. PLAN.md gives their scopes.

No permission, credential, machine-isolation or missing-tool change is needed
to alter this status: the outstanding requirement is the mathematical algorithm
and bound. The goal must not be marked complete from the current component
proofs, nor replaced by an assumed-bound theorem or a claim of global
impossibility. Research can resume from these precise obligations without
rerunning or relabelling the completed frozen panel.
