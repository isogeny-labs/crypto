## The outstanding algorithm theorem

The inverse buffer takes the denominator's coefficient vector as input. Its
correctness does not construct or bound the full relation/partition evaluator.
No completed theorem in this work gives the actual contraction/recovery program
and a uniform all-level bound on every construction, enumeration, child-entry,
precision, bit-arithmetic, failed-attempt and verification cost for general
prime-field ECDLP. In particular, there is no established complete recurrence
T(n)=exp(o(n)), n=ceil(log2 p), for such a program here.

The finite tuple-count comparisons do not fill that gap. A local kernel
identity, a finite cardinality, and a conservative precision proxy cannot be
substituted for a uniform asymptotic algorithm and its correctness/cost proof.

The other direction would require an appropriate lower bound over the allowed
non-generic algorithm class. The origin-preservation obstruction applies to
the stated node-preserving repair family. It is not such a lower bound and does
not imply unrestricted nonexistence of sub-exponential ECDLP algorithms.
