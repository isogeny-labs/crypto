QUESTION
  Does choosing a sparse / BKK-minimal presentation of the prime-field Semaev decomposition system
  lower the cost of an F4 (msolve) decomposition attempt by a factor that GROWS with p, or only by a
  p-independent constant (or not at all)?

SYSTEM (one decomposition attempt)
  p prime of b bits, b in {12,14,16,18,20}; E: y^2 = x^3 + a x + b random over F_p.
  m in {3,4} summands. Factor base V = L random distinct elements of F_p (roots of a dense degree-L
  polynomial f), L = round(2^(b/m)) (balanced choice |V|^m ~ p).
  Target x_R = x(R), R a random point of E(F_p).
  Base constraints f(x_i) = 0 for every summand i; identical in every presentation.

PRESENTATIONS (arms)
  D   dense:     S_{m+1}(x_1..x_m, x_R)                              vars x_1..x_m
  m=3:
  C3  chain:     S_3(x1,x2,u), S_3(u,x3,x_R)                          vars x1,x2,x3,u (u last in DRL)
  C3u chain:     same equations, u first in DRL
  m=4:
  C4  chain:     S_3(x1,x2,u1), S_3(u1,x3,u2), S_3(u2,x4,x_R)          6 vars
  B4  balanced:  S_3(x1,x2,u), S_3(x3,x4,v), S_3(u,v,x_R)              6 vars
  H4a hybrid:    S_4(x1,x2,x3,u), S_3(u,x4,x_R)                       5 vars
  H4b hybrid:    S_3(x1,x2,u), S_4(u,x3,x4,x_R)                       5 vars
  BKK pick: the arm minimising the BKK mixed volume of its square subsystem (base equation of the
  last summand dropped); ties broken by fewer variables, then by arm order as listed.
  Oracle pick (more generous to the route): per instance, the alternative arm with the lowest
  measured value of the metric.

INSTRUMENT
  msolve (system binary /usr/bin/msolve), 1 thread, default solve mode (GB + parametrisation),
  -v 2 to log every F4 step (degree, matrix rows x cols). Per call: timeout 900 s, address space
  limit 6 GB (ulimit -v). Metrics per run:
    T     wall time of the msolve call (s)
    Dmax  largest F4 step degree printed
    Msum  sum over F4 steps of rows*cols (matrix work proxy; deterministic)
    Mmax  largest rows*cols of a single step
  Instances: 3 per (m,b) cell (seeds derived from (m,b,i)). Arms run interleaved per instance.
  Positive control per (m, arm): one planted instance per m at the smallest b (V contains the
  x-coordinates of P_1..P_m and R = P_1+...+P_m); the arm must return a solution. An arm that fails
  its positive control is excluded and reported.
  Correctness check of S_4, S_5: they vanish on x-coordinates of 20 random sums on a test curve.
