## Each scheduled iteration

1. Read current repository instructions, the PR head/state and the active
   requirements/problem record. Stop on merge, close or user stop. Inspect CI
   at the repository cadence; do not wait on it in the foreground.
2. Check the lead atlas and prior protocols before selecting an untested
   hypothesis or unresolved proof. Choose one focused step from the queue
   below. Do not spend another iteration merely restating known evidence.
3. Record the hypothesis, exact objects, inputs, comparison, controls, budgets
   and interpretation of each possible outcome **before** execution. Deferred
   concrete experiments get a validated `backlog/` card under the repository's
   rules. A workstream label below is not a preregistered experiment.
4. Execute the feasible bounded step. Use native research arithmetic and
   independent checks, or an actual Lean proof attempt with pinned dependencies
   and axiom auditing. Retain failures, nulls, counterexamples, caps and exact
   commands. Default native command caps remain600 seconds; any different
   envelope must be justified before execution. Use existing local resources;
   do not provision paid compute or purchase external access.
5. Commit finished work to the existing PR normally, with the required
   source-linked report/visual/PDF for a new research result. Update the
   requirements and next work item. Report what actually ran and what it
   establishes; identify remaining hypotheses and missing validations.
6. Preserve one later check about four hours out while the PR remains open.
   A new executed research attempt/result or external PR activity resets the
   unchanged counter. Unchanged CI alone does not mean research made no
   progress. Timestamp edits or repeated plans do not count as progress.
   Keep the existing stop after three genuinely unchanged checks; report a
   concrete access/resource blocker once if it prevents feasible progress.
