## Formal proof map

The Lean theorem
[`KummerCost.pooled_loss_of_target_losses`](../../lean/CostAggregation.lean)
proves this aggregation step: for nonempty aligned natural-number candidate
and rho price lists, if every paired rho price is strictly below its
candidate price, the pooled rho sum is strictly below the candidate sum.
Its companion lemma proves the weak inequality for any such list. The
[Lean gate audit](../../lean/evidence/2026-10-22/audit.log) passed for three
declarations with no trusted native axiom. The 45,360 full-cold target-level
price losses supply empirical motivation for the premise, but the CSV's
floating-point costs are not imported into Lean as exact natural-number
prices. Neither theorem certifies the price ratios or the Kummer group laws;
the [proof map](../../lean/README.md) records that boundary.

## Scope and limits

The candidate starts from recorded public-point coordinate witnesses and
charges their cold setup in the analyzer. This replay verifies that the
saved implementation, inputs, outputs, and price computation are
reproducible on this host. It does not prove the Kummer formulas for every
curve, establish a cryptographic attack at practical sizes, or establish a
wall-speed advantage. The independently replayed theta and coordinate
validators separately report 21/21 models and 1,260/1,260 public targets,
with zero coordinate or source failures; their scope remains the finite
recorded cohort.

The [PDF run report](run-report.pdf) embeds the graph and these execution
facts. The [results PDF](../../KUMMER_PAIRED.pdf) remains the study's
five-page price-model report.
