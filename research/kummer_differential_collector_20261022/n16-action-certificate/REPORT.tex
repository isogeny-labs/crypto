\noindent For $u=x^2+a_1x+a_0$ and $v=b_1x+b_0$, the constant coefficient
of $v^2=f\bmod u$ gives $b_0^2=a_0b_1^2+R_0$. Write
$x^n\bmod u=C_n+L_nx$. Since $C_{n+1}=-a_0L_n$ and $f(0)=0$,
\[
 R_0/a_0=-\sum_{n\ge2}f_nL_{n-1}
\]
is a polynomial even at $a_0=0$. Every generic projection numerator
$a_0T_i-b_0^2$ therefore has the direct continuation
$T_i-b_1^2-R_0/a_0$. This cancels the common factor that caused the
source's 5,599 zero vectors; it does not use a torsion action to set its
expected value. The finite replay found every extended output nonzero.
