\noindent For $u=x^2+a_1x+a_0$ and $v=b_1x+b_0$, the constant coefficient
of $v^2=f\bmod u$ gives $b_0^2=a_0b_1^2+R_0$. Write
$x^n\bmod u=C_n+L_nx$. Since $C_{n+1}=-a_0L_n$ and $f(0)=0$,
\[
 R_0/a_0=-\sum_{n\ge2}f_nL_{n-1}
\]
is a polynomial even at $a_0=0$. Every generic projection numerator
$a_0T_i-b_0^2$ therefore has the direct continuation
$T_i-b_1^2-R_0/a_0$. This cancels the common factor that caused the
source's 5,599 zero vectors; it does not use a torsion action to set its
expected value. More strongly, the four coordinates equal the recorded
conversion matrix $C$ applied to
$(1,-a_1,-a_0,a_0a_1+b_1^2+R_0/a_0)^T$ in the direct $C_2$ basis. Its first entry is 1 and
the exactly checked $\det C_2$ is nonzero on every frozen model, so the
continued chart is nonzero for every degree-two polynomial input, including
$a_0=0$. The degree-one candidate chart $C_1(0,1,x,x^2)^T$ is likewise
nonzero for every $x$. These are all-state chart statements, not an
all-state geometric translation theorem.

\noindent The source $C_1$ is also an alternate degree-two basis:
with $\sigma=1+\lambda+\mu+\nu$ and $q_3=a_0a_1+b_1^2+R_0/a_0$,
$C_2(1,-a_1,-a_0,q_3)^T=C_1(1,-a_1,a_0,q_3+\sigma a_0)^T$.
Thus N15's $C_1$ inverse correctly recovers $a_0$. The earlier claim
