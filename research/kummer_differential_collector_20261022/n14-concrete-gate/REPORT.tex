\noindent All selected theta inverse constants and all four coordinates of
$K(0)$ and $K(G)$ were nonzero in the 18 frozen models. The final 62
no-divisor lifts split into 56 general-conversion nulls, five zero $\Delta$
denominators, and one zero $b_1$ coefficient. The source fallback did not
enumerate a Jacobian candidate in this sample. Every model has nulls at
$k=0,1,r-1$; two 20-bit models have eight further sampled nulls. Exact
scalar IDs and complete operation counters are in the raw CSV.

\noindent The M/A/I counts include the signed-recovery module after one-time
cached inverse setup. They exclude labels, collector work, relation
processing, final target verification, and elapsed time. Rho was not run;
no matched cost ratio follows. All-state exceptions and the full 1,260-target
cohort remain open. The native validator recomputes row continuity, counts,
costs, and source and artifact hashes.
