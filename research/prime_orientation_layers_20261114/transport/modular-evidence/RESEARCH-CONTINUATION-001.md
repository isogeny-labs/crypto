# Research continuation contract

User correction, 2026-11-15: a quiet PR watcher must not replace the active
research task. Repeated maintenance-only final messages are a failure mode.

- Keep PR maintenance subordinate and at its existing cadence. Its three
  quiet checks stop that watcher only. Protected integration conflicts do not
  prevent independent local mathematics.
- After maintenance, a research continuation reads the latest journal and the
  saved proof output, then executes the next available research obligation.
  Never restart an unchanged run or publish separate drafts from maintenance.
- Preserve failures. Repair the diagnosed issue in a new version, record the
  actual source/log/exit and scope, and continue to the next dependency.
- End a research work interval with a concrete artifact, diagnosed failure or
  specific research-blocking external dependency. A future PR check time is
  not such a dependency. Do not repeat final responses without new work.
- An ongoing goal does not guarantee execution between turns or guarantee a
  breakthrough. A continuation must do useful authorized work when invoked;
  do not claim mathematical success from activity or component acceptance.
