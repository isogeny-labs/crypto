\[

\[
 \mathrm{VBound}(1,\det M-1),\qquad \det M\ne0,\qquad

\textbf{Any approximate rational factors.} Suppose rational matrices $P,Q$

\[

\]

\[
 2^u=\operatorname{rank}(P_{\rm res}Q_{\rm res})
 \le\operatorname{rank}(P_{\rm res})\le k.

No integrality assumption on $P$ or $Q$ is needed. Dividing an actual
unnormalized approximation of residual weight $w+1$ by the derived nonzero
