# Constructing nP in local coordinates

Write z=X-x for a nonbranch place P=(x,y). The truncated square root W of f(x+z) at y is unique since 2y is invertible. Its kth coefficient is the kth shifted coefficient of f minus the inner convolution of the previous coefficients, divided by 2y. Compute it once to degree g+1. For n=g+1 and g+2 take W modulo z^n. The function Y-W has contact of order at least n at P. Its norm leaves H=(f(x+z)-W²)/z^n of degree at most g. Conjugating the residual gives nP, with monic support H/lc(H) and ordinate -W at each residual root. A residual zero at z=0 preserves excess contact. If the leading coefficient drops, use the actual degree. Branch places are omitted as in the generic baseline.
