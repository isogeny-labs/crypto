# Literature used by the explicit-quartic construction

Access note: arXiv and publisher sites were not reachable from this session (DNS blocked).
The `research-kb` server was not available to this subagent. Statements below are
classical results cited from memory, from the in-repo notes named, or identified
numerically here. Each row says which. Nothing in this study rests on a formula that was
only remembered: every formula used was re-identified or checked numerically (see
`RESULTS.md`, `data/tables_check.json`).

| # | Statement used | Source | How it is used / checked here |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Thomae's formula for hyperelliptic theta-nulls; duplication formula `theta[a;b1](tau/2) theta[a;b2](tau/2) = sum_beta theta[a+beta; b1+b2](tau) theta[beta; b1-b2](tau)` (half-integer characteristics, same top row) | Mumford, *Tata Lectures on Theta* I (duplication) and II (Thomae); in-repo `../g3_affine_twist_20261121/derive.py`, `LITERATURE.md` | `derive2.py` writes all 106 same-row products; checked against direct theta series (rel. dev. 4.2e-12). |
| L2 | The 28 bitangents of the canonical model of a non-hyperelliptic genus-3 curve are the lines `sum_k d theta[n]/dz_k(0) * omega_k = 0`, `n` odd | Riemann (1876); Dolgachev, *Classical Algebraic Geometry* (CUP 2012), ch. 5-6; Grushevsky-Salvati Manni, "Gradients of odd theta functions" (Crelle 2004) | `tables.py`: for 5 period matrices and 3 Aronhold sets each, all 28 gradient lines are bitangents of the quartic built from 7 of them (`max defect <= 1.1e-4` vs `>= 0.034` for random lines; double roots are found to sqrt(eps) accuracy). |
| L3 | Aronhold sets: 7 odd characteristics with every triple azygetic (sum even); 288 of them; an Aronhold set of bitangents determines the quartic | Dolgachev ch. 6.1; Riemann (1876) | `fpquartic.all_aronhold_sets` finds 288. |
| L4 | Riemann's model: with `u1..u4 = x1, x2, x3, x1+x2+x3`, `u_{5+j} = a_j . x`, linear forms `xi_i` with `xi1+xi2+xi3+u4 = 0`, `sum_i xi_i / a_ji + k_j u_{5+j} = 0`, the quartic is `(x1 xi1 + x2 xi2 - x3 xi3)^2 - 4 x1 x2 xi1 xi2 = 0` | Riemann (1876); Dolgachev Thm 6.1.9 (from memory); Milio 2019, `hal-01589683`, Thm 14 (via `../g3_affine_twist_20261121/LITERATURE_ADDENDUM.md`); implementation ported from in-repo `research/cubic_extension_transfer_20261025/historical/extension-field-experiments/research_lead/quotient_theta.py::riemann_quartic` | The bitangency check of L2 tests it numerically. |
| L5 | Jacobi's derivative formula, genus 3: for an azygetic triple of odd `n1, n2, n3`, `det(grad theta[n_i](0)) = +- pi^3 prod of 5 even theta-nulls` (the 5 complete a fundamental system with the triple) | Igusa, "On Jacobi's derivative formula and its generalizations", Amer. J. Math. 102 (1980); Grushevsky-Salvati Manni (2004); Guardia, "Jacobian nullwerte and algebraic equations", J. Algebra 253 (2002) | Not taken from the papers: the 5-set and sign were **identified numerically** for all 2,016 triples (unique match each) and re-checked on 4 more period matrices (worst rel. dev. 1.3e-12): `formulas/jacobi.json`. In every case the 8 characteristics sum to 0. |
| L6 | Weber's formula (the Aronhold coefficients `a_ij` as quotients of theta constants) | Weber, *Theorie der Abel'schen Functionen vom Geschlecht 3* (1876); Fiorentino, arXiv 1612.02049; Nart-Ritzenthaler (2017); Milio Prop. 13 | **Not used.** Replaced by L5: `a_{k,1} = D(m_k, m_2, m_3) / D(m_4, m_2, m_3)` (Cramer), which is a quotient of two 5-products of even theta-nulls (10 factors on 8 distinct characteristics for the set checked). Numerically equal to the gradient-based `a_ij` (<= 1e-12). A 2/2-theta Weber quotient was searched for and not found for the Aronhold set tried; Weber's normalisation may differ. |
| L7 | Riemann's theta formula -> quartic relations `M1 +- M2 +- M3 = 0` among products of 4 even theta-nulls over cosets of 2-dimensional subspaces | Mumford Tata I; Igusa, *Theta Functions* | 315 relations **identified numerically** (all with +-1 coefficients) and re-checked on 4 more period matrices (worst residual 2.1e-15): `formulas/riemann_rel.json`. |
| L8 | Galois conjugates of a theta-null point are theta-null points of the same ppav for another level structure (theta-null moduli defined over `Q(zeta8)`); so arbitrary fourth roots `rho_ij` may be used | Mumford, "On the equations defining abelian varieties" (Invent. Math. 1966-67); used the same way in the twist study | Consequence tested in the run: different roots/`zeta8`/relabellings give the same point counts (gate G1). |
| L9 | A smooth plane quartic with trivial automorphism group descends to the field of moduli uniquely (no twists); Hilbert 90 for `GL_3` gives the descent matrix | Serre, *Local Fields* ch. X / *Galois Cohomology*; Lachaud-Ritzenthaler-Zykin (MRL 2010) for the Jacobian-vs-twist obstruction | `fpquartic.descend`. Then `Jac(X)` is `B` or `B^tw`, and the point counts decide which. |
| L10 | Twist label `s = chi(-1) chi(f(t)) chi(S_L)` | `../g3_affine_twist_20261121/RESULTS.md`, `ERRATA.md` | The label being tested; the construction never reads it. |
