\noindent\textbf{Question.} Does the measured Rust arithmetic path implement
the published complete, inversion-free weighted genus-2 law? This is a source
audit of an existing arm, not a fresh arithmetic or DLP experiment.

\noindent\textbf{Source.} E. Ozbay Gurler and H. Hisil,
\href{https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13389-024-00351-7}
{\emph{Complete group law for genus 2 Jacobians on Jacobian coordinates}},
J. Cryptogr. Eng. 14 (2024), sections 3--4. The printed law covers
$y^2=x^5+a_3x^3+a_2x^2+a_1x+a_0$ in odd characteristic. Its section 4
prints weighted Eqs. (13)--(21), and refers to Hisil--Costello and
Hu--Lin--Zhao for several common and earlier degenerate branches.

\noindent\textbf{Observed implementation.} The source-audited dispatcher
\texttt{projective\_law\_gate.rs} implements identity, 1+1, 1+2, and 2+2
branches in weighted coordinates. Its SHA-256 is\\
\texttt{5976e7f30b3e9c5c49a5fc0f4095404b7322832e4b02f467bc3cb7e0d9dcc8c0}.
The attached CSV records each predicate, function, equation, output type,
and exact source line. The weighted arithmetic functions do not call field
inversion; setup and optional affine normalization do. The wrapper collector
normalizes every addition, whereas the retained-state collector normalizes
at the split relation boundary. Their frozen costs include those inversions.

\noindent\textbf{Finite checks and costs.} The 21-model arithmetic gate has
zero unresolved cases in its exercised weighted branch sets. An additional
42-model proof audit compares 1,224,000 weighted additions to independent
Cantor arithmetic with zero mismatches, plus exact branch polynomials and
written divisor arguments. The frozen 1,260-target collector arms each
verified all source scalars. At the report's representative operation price,
wrapper/rho is 3.7938 and retained-state/rho is 4.1793. These are operation
price ratios, not isolated wall-time ratios.

\noindent\textbf{Corrected N6 source attribution.} The pinned author
Magma file at commit \texttt{ec98e115} (full hash in the companion audit)
sets \texttt{WW1:=W1\string^2} before its \texttt{N6} assignment multiplies
by \texttt{WW1}. The prior audit mistook this alias for \texttt{W1}.
Thus the author code and Rust both use $W^2$ in this intermediate.
The exact $p=739$, $W=3$ fixture tests a synthetic $W^1$ mutation,
which disagrees with independent Cantor; its historical raw field names
are retained. The article defines the same weighted coordinates in
Section 4 and its affine doubling coefficients in Eq.~(7), but does not
print this particular $N6$ line. Full compiled Rust-to-published-law
semantics and the projective/Pic$^0$ bridge remain open. Frozen costs
are unchanged.
