# N6 weighted-exponent proof boundary

The [published complete-law article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13389-024-00351-7)
defines the weighted coordinate action in §4; its companion
[author-code repository](https://github.com/ozbayelif/jac-on-jac) is the source
of the transcribed `N6` expression. The frozen Rust runtime uses
`(2R - 3Q² - a₃Z⁴)W²`, whereas the transcribed author-code term uses one
power of `W`. The exact `p=739` regression in
[`../results/n6-w-power-regression.json`](../results/n6-w-power-regression.json)
records a valid weighted input where the `W²` variant agrees with independent
Cantor arithmetic and the `W¹` variant does not. This packet proves the
homogeneity distinction for the intermediate polynomials; it does not prove
the complete group law.

The weighted action holds `Q,R,Z` fixed and scales `S,T,W` by a nonzero
`μ`, leaving the represented affine divisor unchanged. In the runtime
doubling branch, `N1=QS−T` and `N2=RS` have weight 1, while
`N5=S²−HW²` has weight 2. With `N6=DW²`, both
`AA=N1·N5−N2·N6` and `CC=S·N5−T·N6` have weight 3.
With the literal `N6=DW`, their scaling defects are exactly
`μ²(μ−1)RSDW` and `μ²(μ−1)TDW`, respectively. These four
polynomial equalities and an integer counterexample are kernel-checked in
[`N6Homogeneity.lean`](N6Homogeneity.lean). The retained
[`build.log`](build.log) lists only Lean foundational axioms (`propext`,
`Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`), with no project-specific axioms.
The four polynomial claims also have native
[`identity.certificate/v1` records](identities.json), each replayed at 32
sealed and 32 checker-chosen points in [`identity-check.log`](identity-check.log):
`IDC1h92ee5de5110623fd` (`AA`, `W²`),
`IDC1h7679e312ee733fcb` (`CC`, `W²`),
`IDC1h87fbf5497ad4d6e0` (`AA`, `W¹` defect), and
`IDC1he881917e142d1cb5` (`CC`, `W¹` defect). The certificates point to the
separately checked Lean source; they are not a substitute for its proof.
A deliberate mutation of the first certificate's seed was rejected as a
digest mismatch (exit 101); the [`tamper-check.log`](tamper-check.log)
preserves that fail-closed control.

For a concrete illustration, fix `Q=T=H=0` and `R=S=D=W=1`.
At `μ=3`, the same affine divisor produces `AA=-27` with `W²` and
`AA=-9` with `W¹`. The five exact integer rows are in
[`scaling.csv`](scaling.csv), generated by [`scaling.rs`](scaling.rs),
and drawn in the [graph](scaling.svg) embedded in the [PDF](REPORT.pdf).
This graph is a coordinate-scaling witness, not a speed measurement.
