The author code and Rust both use `N6=(2R-3Q²-a₃Z⁴)W²`. The earlier report mistook `WW1` for `W1`; that author-code discrepancy claim is withdrawn. The frozen `p=739` [regression](../results/n6-w-power-regression.json) compares production W² with a **synthetic W¹ mutation**; its historical `transcribed_*` keys do not denote the author code. The [source correction](source-correction/README.md) checks the pinned alias and proves the weighted A/B/C coefficients reduce to the article's printed Eq. (7).
