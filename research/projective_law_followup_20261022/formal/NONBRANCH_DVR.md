# Rational nonbranch point: principal ideal and actual DVR

The checked Lean source proves that, for any field `K`, monic quintic `f=X^5+a3 X^3+a2 X^2+a1 X+a0`, rational point `y²=f(a)`, separability of `f`, and the **pointwise condition `2*y≠0`**, the affine localization and its actual weighted-Proj point stalk are discrete valuation rings. The localized maximal ideal is generated by `x-a`. The factor witness from separability is supplied by [Common22SeparableAffineDomain.lean](Common22SeparableAffineDomain.lean).

Review artifacts: [PDF note](NONBRANCH_DVR.pdf), [SVG dependency graph](nonbranch-dvr.svg), [standardized CSV status table](nonbranch-dvr.csv), [Lean source](WeightedProjNonbranchDVR.lean), [direct compiler and axiom log](nonbranch-dvr-lean.log), [TeX source](NONBRANCH_DVR.tex), and [vector figure PDF](nonbranch-dvr-figure.pdf). Lean source SHA-256: `0eb48894992f0c1e412c20cdec44acc71692ea2b165b2d7afc3d72b6b204ce3f`.

The condition `2*y≠0` cannot be replaced by `y≠0` in characteristic two. Over `F₂`, `f=X⁵+X³+1` is separable, and `(0,1)` lies on the curve, but `f'(0)=2y=0`; the point is singular. The alternate pointwise case `f'(a)≠0` is not formalized here. The checked DVR does not yet transport the common 2+2 numerator or norm factors into the stalk. Global principal-divisor/Pic0 transport and compiled Rust semantics also remain open. This packet contains no target experiment or cost comparison.

The local DVR criterion follows the [Stacks Project, Tag 00PD](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00PD). The projective stalk equivalence is the checked `WeightedProjNonbranchZeroOrder.pointRingEquiv`, based on the open-immersion stalk principle in [Stacks Project, Tag 01HD](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01HD). The proof also reuses pinned Mathlib's `IsDiscreteValuationRing.TFAE` and `IsLocalization.AtPrime.map_eq_maximalIdeal`.
