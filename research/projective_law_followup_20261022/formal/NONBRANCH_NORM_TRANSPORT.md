# Source-style common 2+2 norm in the actual rational-point stalk

The checked Lean bridge maps the actual chart quintic to the square of the ordinate in the weighted-Proj rational-point stalk. It transports a polynomial identity `n²-u1*u2*support=b²*f` into `(n-bY)(n+bY)=u1*u2*support` there when `f` is the actual monic quintic. `source_style_projective_norm_identity` supplies that polynomial identity from the repository's checked common 2+2 coefficient theorem under valid Mumford congruences and a nonzero determinant.

The separate checked `projective_norm_order` gives the order of a **chosen** vanishing factor as the sum of the three norm-factor orders under separability, `y²=f(a)`, `2*y≠0`, `b≠0`, the polynomial norm identity, and a nonunit premise for `n-bY`. It derives the actual stalk domain and DVR, the unit conditions for `2`, `b`, and `Y`, and the norm identity. It does not infer which conjugate factor vanishes. In characteristic two, `n-bY=n+bY`; this allocation argument does not apply even at derivative-smooth points.
