# Rational derivative-point local DVR

For the actual quintic curve `y²=f(x)`, the checked Lean source proves `q_a(a)=f'(a)`. At any rational point `y²=f(a)` with `f'(a)≠0`, the difference quotient `q_a` is a unit in the local ring; the point maximal ideal is generated by `y-y0`. With separability of the monic quintic to supply the domain, the affine localization and the actual weighted-Proj point stalk are DVRs. This pointwise derivative condition covers the complementary smooth-point case in characteristic two without assuming `2*y≠0`.
