Let $A_X$ and $B_z$ be the separately presented localized affine and infinity-chart coordinate rings. Pinned Lean previously checked an algebra equivalence $A_X\simeq B_z$. Applying the contravariant spectrum functor now gives a checked scheme isomorphism $\operatorname{Spec}(A_X)\simeq\operatorname{Spec}(B_z)$. The localization morphisms into $\operatorname{Spec}(A)$ and $\operatorname{Spec}(B)$ are checked open immersions. Their ranges are precisely the basic opens $D(X)$ and $D(z)$. All five new declaration audits use only \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, and \texttt{Quot.sound}.
