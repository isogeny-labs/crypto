# Weighted sextic: exact ordinary `Z = 1` quotient

The pinned Lean file `Common22WeightedProjKernel.lean` extends the checked
specialization in `Common22WeightedProjQuotient.lean`. For any field `K` and
coefficients `a3,a2,a1,a0`, it constructs algebra maps both ways between

`(K[X,Y,Z]/(F))/(class(Z)−1)` and `K[x,y]/(y²−f(x))`,

where `F = Y²−(X⁵Z+a3 X³Z³+a2 X²Z⁴+a1 XZ⁵+a0 Z⁶)` and
`f = x⁵+a3 x³+a2 x²+a1 x+a0`. It proves both compositions are identity,
packages the maps as `chartEquivAffine`, and proves the kernel of the earlier
surjective `toAffine` is exactly the principal ideal generated by `class(Z)−1`.
This is an **ordinary-ring quotient equivalence**. It does not construct the
weighted grading, identify the degree-zero localization defining `D₊(Z)` in
weighted `Proj`, prove its infinity chart, or transport to `Pic⁰`. It changes
no end-to-end operation or cost count.
