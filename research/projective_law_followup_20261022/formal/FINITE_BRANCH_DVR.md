# Simple finite branch: DVR and local orders on actual weighted Proj

This packet records a conditional formal geometry result, not a new target experiment or cost comparison. The [diagram](finite-branch-dvr.svg), [gate CSV](finite-branch-dvr.csv), [Lean source](WeightedProjFiniteBranchDVR.lean), [compiler log](finite-branch-dvr-lean.log), and [PDF](FINITE_BRANCH_DVR.pdf) are provided for independent review.

For any field `K`, coefficients `a3,a2,a1,a0`, and rational point `a` satisfying `f(a)=0` and `f'(a)≠0`, the actual weighted-Proj structure-sheaf stalk at that finite branch point is a discrete valuation ring. `projectiveY_order_one` proves the pullback of affine `y` is a uniformizer with order one; `projectiveXSubA_order_two` proves the pullback of `x-a` has order two. The proof uses the separately checked [finite-branch stalk isomorphism](FINITE_BRANCH_STALK.md), the actual affine-branch domain/DVR proof in `Common22BranchDomain.lean`, and the checked local relation `y²=(x-a)q` with `q` a unit. It follows the repository's existing infinity-stalk transport pattern in `WeightedProjInfinityDVR.lean`. The mathematical DVR characterization is described in [Stacks Project Lemma 10.119.7](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00PD).

This establishes the projective-curve local orders needed by the written Weierstrass finite-zero argument. It does not prove that every law numerator has the factorization needed by [the conditional order-one theorem](BRANCH_ZERO_ORDER.md), account for every nonbranch zero, construct a global principal divisor, prove equality in `Pic⁰`, or connect the compiled Rust law to the hypotheses. It changes no frozen target row, matched-rho control, or cost ratio.
