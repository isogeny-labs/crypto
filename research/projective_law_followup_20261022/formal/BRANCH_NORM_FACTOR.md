# Branch factor forced by a norm identity

This is a conditional polynomial and actual projective-stalk proof, not a target experiment or cost comparison. The [PDF](BRANCH_NORM_FACTOR.pdf), [diagram](branch-norm-factor.svg), [gate CSV](branch-norm-factor.csv), [polynomial Lean source](Common22BranchFactorization.lean), [projective Lean source](WeightedProjBranchPolynomialEval.lean), and their [polynomial](branch-factorization-lean.log) and [projective](projective-branch-eval-lean.log) compiler logs preserve the exact checked boundary.

Let `f,u1,u2,n,support` be polynomials over a field, and let `a` be rational. If `f(a)=0`, the polynomial norm identity `n²-u1·u2·support=b²f` holds, and `u1(a)u2(a)support(a)=0`, then the checked theorem proves `(X-a) ∣ n`. Its proof specializes the already checked branch-overlap theorem at ordinate zero and applies the polynomial factor theorem. The repository's [source-style norm theorem](COORDINATE_RING_BRIDGE.md) is a separate checked input with concrete coefficient hypotheses; this packet does not assert those hypotheses for every runtime path.

`WeightedProjBranchPolynomialEval.lean` defines the coefficient map and `x` germ in the **actual weighted-Proj branch stalk** through the checked stalk isomorphism. It proves that the image of `X-a` is the already defined projective `x-a` germ, then proves `projective_norm_branch_factor`: under the norm and support-root hypotheses above and a rational branch point of the actual curve, the mapped polynomial `n` has a local factor `x-a`. This is a checked composition of the polynomial factor and projective evaluation maps; it does not identify `f` with the curve's concrete quintic or `n` with a compiled runtime value.

The result supplies a local factorization for the mapped polynomial in the zero-order argument. It does not yet identify `n` with the compiled numerator in every runtime branch, establish the required unit `B` in every branch, count nonbranch and infinity orders, or prove a global principal divisor and `Pic⁰` class equation. Frozen 1,260-target rows and matched-rho ratios are unchanged.
