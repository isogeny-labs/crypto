# Weighted quotient: checked `Z = 1` specialization

The Lean file `Common22WeightedProjQuotient.lean` constructs an algebra map
from the ordinary quotient of `K[X,Y,Z]` by the weighted sextic to the affine
coordinate ring `K[x,y]/(y²-f(x))`, for any field `K` and coefficients
`a3,a2,a1,a0`. It proves that the sextic maps to zero, the descended map sends
`X` to `x`, `Y` to `y`, and `Z` to `1`, and the map is surjective. It also proves
that `Z-1` lies in its kernel. The definitions reproduce the existing affine
coordinate-ring presentation; they are local here so this proof can import the
already-built weighted head without recompiling the earlier long import chain.

This packet checks the ordinary-ring specialization and surjectivity. The
subsequent [exact-kernel packet](WEIGHTED_PROJ_KERNEL.md) proves that its
kernel is `(Z-1)` and constructs the corresponding ordinary quotient algebra
equivalence. Neither packet constructs the quotient's weighted grading,
identifies the degree-zero ring after localizing at `Z`, or proves a `Proj`
chart isomorphism. The packets change no end-to-end operation or cost count.

## Earlier exact-kernel attempt, superseded

The next target is the equality between the kernel of `toAffine` and the
principal ideal generated by the class of `Z-1` in `WeightedRing`. A bounded
Lean attempt formed the additional quotient `WeightedRing/(Z-1)` and checked
that the image of `Z` is one and the sextic remains zero there. It then tried
to descend an inverse map from the affine coordinate ring. The inverse map
was **not** constructed: normalization of the affine relation against the
three-variable relation reached Lean's deterministic `whnf` limit of 200,000
heartbeats, and the resulting quotient-lift proof retained the unsolved goal
`0 * affineToChartPoly q = 0`. The attempt exited **1**. These errors identify
a proof-engineering obstruction in that attempt, not a counterexample to the
kernel equality. No exact-kernel or chart-isomorphism theorem was added by
that attempt. The later [checked proof](WEIGHTED_PROJ_KERNEL.md) establishes
the ordinary exact kernel and algebra equivalence using generator-level
equalities; the weighted-`Proj` chart remains open.
