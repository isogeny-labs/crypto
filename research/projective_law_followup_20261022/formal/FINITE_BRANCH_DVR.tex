\section*{Checked statement}
For a field $K$, quintic $f$, and rational $a$ with $f(a)=0$ and $f'(a)\ne0$, the structure-sheaf stalk of the actual weighted-Proj curve at the finite branch point is a discrete valuation ring. Under the checked stalk isomorphism with the affine branch localization, the pullback of $y$ has order one and the pullback of $x-a$ has order two.
