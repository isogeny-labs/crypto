\section*{Checked statement}
For polynomials $f,u_1,u_2,n,U$ over a field and a rational point $a$, assume
\[
f(a)=0,\qquad n^2-u_1u_2U=b^2f,\qquad u_1(a)u_2(a)U(a)=0.
\]
The checked polynomial theorem specializes the norm identity at ordinate zero and uses the factor theorem to conclude
\[
(X-a)\mid n.
\]
The second checked theorem defines the coefficient map and $x$ germ through the actual weighted-Proj branch-stalk isomorphism. It proves that the image of $X-a$ equals the projective $x-a$ germ and composes this with the norm theorem: the mapped polynomial $n(x)$ has a local factor $x-a$.
