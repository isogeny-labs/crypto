\section*{Statement and mechanism}
Let $K$ be a field and let the affine chart be $K[x,y]/(y^2-f(x))$. The checked nested polynomial equivalence maps the defining relation to a polynomial of degree two in $y$. An $x$-only polynomial has degree zero in $y$. The new affine lemmas prove $y\ne p(x)$ and $p(x)-B y\ne0$ for $B\ne0$.
\par The common 2+2 numerator $N$ is $x$-only. A checked specialization gives $N-B y\ne0$. The existing injection of this chart into the function field of the actual weighted-Proj curve gives $N/B-y\ne0$ when the quintic is separable and $B\ne0$. The affine degree argument needs no separability or characteristic restriction.
