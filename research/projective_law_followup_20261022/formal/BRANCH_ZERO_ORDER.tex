\section*{Checked statement}
At a rational simple root $a$ of the cubic $f$, the actual local stalk has $y^2=(x-a)q$, with $q$ a unit and $\operatorname{ord}(y)=1$. The new theorem \texttt{actual\_branch\_x\_factor\_zero\_order} assumes $N=(x-a)t$ and $B$ is a unit. It proves
\[
N-By=y\bigl(yq^{-1}t-B\bigr),\qquad \operatorname{ord}(N-By)=1.
\]
The second factor is a unit because $yt$ is a nonunit and $B$ is a unit in the local ring. The result is also recorded as a generic discrete-valuation-ring lemma and an actual-stalk specialization.

The discrete-valuation-ring characterization and unit-times-uniformizer valuation are recorded in the Stacks Project, \href{https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00PD}{Lemma 10.119.7}. The present packet imports the already checked actual-stalk DVR theorem and proves the conditional order statement; the Stacks lemma alone does not establish the genus-2 divisor-class equation.
