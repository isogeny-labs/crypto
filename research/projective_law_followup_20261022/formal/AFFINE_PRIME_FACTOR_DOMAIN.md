# Prime-factor sufficient condition for the affine quintic domain

This packet records a checked sufficient condition, not an unconditional separable-quintic theorem or a target experiment. The [PDF report](AFFINE_PRIME_FACTOR_DOMAIN.pdf), [editable SVG dependency graph](affine-prime-factor-domain.svg), [standardized CSV gate table](affine-prime-factor-domain.csv), [Lean source](Common22AffineOddDomain.lean), [compiler log](affine-prime-factor-domain-lean.log), and [vector figure used in the PDF](affine-prime-factor-domain-figure.pdf) are available for independent review.

For a field `K` and the monic quintic `f(x)=x⁵+a₃x³+a₂x²+a₁x+a₀`, the checked Lean theorem assumes an **explicit** factorization `f=t*u` in the one-variable polynomial ring, `Prime t`, and `¬t∣u`. `nested_equation_irreducible_of_prime_factor` applies the existing Eisenstein lemma to `Y²−f(X)`. `affine_ring_domain_of_prime_factor` concludes that the actual affine coordinate ring is a domain. `point_local_domain_of_prime_factor` transports the domain to localization at any rational curve point; `projective_point_domain_of_prime_factor` uses the checked projective-stalk equivalence to obtain the actual weighted-Proj stalk domain. These four statements were directly compiled.

The factor witness has not been derived from separability in Lean. A precise next path is to use pinned Mathlib's `Separable.squarefree`, extract an irreducible factor of the nonconstant quintic, and use squarefreeness to prove that factor does not divide its cofactor. The required transport across `MvPolynomial.uniqueAlgEquiv` must also be checked. Odd-degree nonsquareness in `K(x)` is another direct route; it was not formalized in this packet. Neither route should be treated as checked from this source.

The principal nonbranch maximal ideal, local DVR, proof-side norm transport, global divisor and `Pic⁰` class equation, and compiled Rust correspondence remain open. The [previous nonbranch packet](NONBRANCH_STALK_ORDER.md) proves the stalk equivalence and (y)-unit statement but leaves the domain and DVR hypotheses visible. This packet discharges the **domain** hypothesis when the factor witness is supplied. No frozen target, matched-rho cost, or performance row changes.
