# Weighted quotient grading and degree-zero chart ring

This packet records a pinned Lean proof for the weight `(1,3,1)` sextic over an arbitrary field `K` and arbitrary coefficients `a3,a2,a1,a0`. It changes no collector run, rho control, or cost ratio. The [PDF](WEIGHTED_QUOTIENT_COMPONENTS.pdf), [dependency graph](weighted-quotient-components.svg), and [standardized CSV](weighted-quotient-components.csv) expose the checked and open boundaries for independent review.

`WeightedRingComponent.lean` proves that weighted-degree projection descends to the sextic quotient as additive maps. The projections are orthogonal and idempotent, reconstruct every quotient element from finitely many components, separate quotient elements, and give direct finite sums. Products of homogeneous quotient lifts have the summed degree. `WeightedRingGraded.lean` packages these facts into degree submodules, an internal direct sum, and an actual `GradedRing` instance.

`WeightedChartKernelRec.lean` proves a general saturation theorem: in a nonnegative graded ring, if homogeneous `h` belongs to `(z−1)` for homogeneous degree-one `z`, then `z^k h=0` for some `k`. `WeightedChartRoute.lean` constructs a map from homogeneous localization away from `z` to any ring where `z` maps to `1`, proves its finite-component surjectivity when the original ring map is surjective, and proves injectivity under the saturation hypothesis. `WeightedChartIso.lean` applies those lemmas to the sextic quotient and proves a ring equivalence between its degree-zero homogeneous localization away from `Z` and the earlier affine quotient `K[x,y]/(y²−f(x))`. `WeightedProjSchemeChart.lean` applies Mathlib's basic-open isomorphism to prove the scheme isomorphism `D₊(Z) ≅ Spec(K[x,y]/(y²−f(x)))`. `WeightedProjCover.lean` proves `D₊(X) ∪ D₊(Z) = Proj` and that both opens are affine; it uses the sextic relation to place `D₊(Y)` inside `D₊(Z)`.

`WeightedChartDehomog.lean` proves a generic equivalence between degree-zero localization away from a degree-one element and quotient by that element minus one. `WeightedInfinityOrdinary.lean` specializes `X=1` and proves the resulting quotient is equivalent to the previously defined explicit infinity chart `K[z,w]/(w²−z(1+a3z²+a2z³+a1z⁴+a0z⁵))`. `WeightedProjInfinityChart.lean` proves the resulting scheme isomorphism `D₊(X) ≅ Spec` of that chart ring. Independently, `WeightedProjProper.lean` proves finite type over the degree-zero subring; `WeightedProjBase.lean` identifies that subring with `K` and proves the weighted `Proj` proper over `Spec K`.

`WeightedProjSmoothPoints.lean` checks two bounded Jacobian facts: over a perfect field of characteristic other than two, a squarefree quintic gives a nonzero partial derivative at every affine `K`-point; every point of the infinity boundary `z=0` has nonzero `z`-partial without extra assumptions. `WeightedJacobianIdeal.lean` proves a stronger ring-global affine certificate: for a perfect field of characteristic other than two and squarefree quintic, the ideal generated by `2y` and `f′(x)` in the affine coordinate ring is the unit ideal. This does not itself assert a scheme-level smoothness instance. `WeightedOverlapScratch.lean` maps the chart generators to the common homogeneous localization away from `XZ`, checks that both chart equivalences send their coordinate fractions to the expected generators, and proves `x·z=1` and `w·x³=y` in the common ring. These identities match the existing transition formulas on generators. `WeightedOverlapFull.lean` constructs both localized projective chart rings, identifies each with its separately defined overlap ring and with the common homogeneous localization away from `XZ`, and composes these equivalences into a canonical overlap-ring transition. `WeightedOverlapEquality.lean` proves that the canonical overlap transition sends the affine generators `x` and `y` to `1/z` and `w/z³` in the infinity overlap ring. These match the separately defined transition formulas on the two generators. `WeightedOverlapMapEquality.lean` proves equality of the full ring maps conditional on preservation of every `K`-scalar by the canonical transition; it closes the localization extension from the two generator theorems. `WeightedOverlapScalar.lean` proves that scalar preservation for each chart map and both maps into the common homogeneous localization, then discharges the condition. Its theorem `projTransition_eq_separate` states equality of the canonical projective overlap-ring transition and the separately presented overlap-ring equivalence for every field `K` and all four coefficients. `WeightedOverlapScheme.lean` applies `Spec` to that equality and proves that the resulting overlap isomorphism is the transition morphism used by the separately glued two-chart scheme. This does not identify the two chart embeddings into weighted Proj with the embeddings into the glued scheme, or transport local stalks.

`WeightedInfinityJacobian.lean` now checks a ring-global infinity-boundary
certificate for any field and coefficients: in the explicit infinity-chart
coordinate ring, the ideal generated by `z` and the image of the `z`-partial
of its defining equation is the unit ideal. The proof writes that partial as
`-1-z·h`, an exact Bézout witness. Thus every prime containing `z` avoids
the derivative. This is stronger than the earlier check at `K`-rational
boundary points, but it does not construct a `Smooth` instance or settle
the other chart's local presentations.

`WeightedOverlapEmbedding.lean` checks both localized chart embeddings into
weighted Proj. Its `zLocalized_awayι` and `xLocalized_awayι` theorems equate
the Spec map from each localized chart followed by the actual `Proj.awayι`
with the canonical immersion from the common `D₊(XZ)` localization. They use
Mathlib's `Proj.SpecMap_awayMap_awayι` and two checked localization-naturality
lemmas. These equalities concern maps into weighted Proj; the theorem that
the separately glued two-chart scheme is globally isomorphic to weighted
Proj, with compatible local stalks, remains open.

`WeightedProjSmoothness.lean` proves the scheme-level local-on-source
reduction `smooth_of_two_basic_opens`: for any morphism from the actual
weighted Proj to a scheme, Smooth on both the `X` and `Z` basic-open
restrictions implies Smooth globally. It uses the checked two-open cover.
Both explicit affine coordinate-ring structure maps are now Smooth under
the stated squarefree hypotheses. Transport to the actual two Proj chart
restrictions and the global conclusion remain open.
`WeightedProjGlobalGlue.lean` defines both actual
chart morphisms into weighted Proj by composing the checked chart
isomorphisms with the basic-open immersions. `WeightedProjChartNaturality.lean`
checks the two ring-map and `Spec.map` localization squares.
`WeightedProjChartIntoProj.lean` composes these with both actual chart
isomorphism inverses and Proj immersions: each separately glued
principal-open chart enters Proj through the canonical `D₊(XZ)` map.
`WeightedProjGlueCompatibility.lean` proves that the separately glued
overlap transition commutes with both maps to the common `D₊(XZ)`
coordinate Spec. `WeightedProjGluedMorphism.lean` checks compatibility after
the actual Proj immersion. `WeightedProjGlueCone.lean` constructs a morphism
from the separately glued two-chart scheme to actual weighted Proj and checks
its restriction to each chart. `WeightedProjGlueIso.lean` proves this morphism
surjective on scheme points using the checked two-open cover. An open
immersion, global inverse, and isomorphism remain open.

`WeightedChartRingSmooth.lean` constructs an explicit submersive
presentation of the infinity quotient and proves that its structure map
from any field is Smooth after localization away from the `z`-partial.
`WeightedInfinityOpenSmooth.lean` conditionally transports Smooth from the
affine `D(x)` ring to the infinity `D(z)` ring through the checked overlap
equivalence. `WeightedAffineXPartial.lean` proves both genus-2 partial
identities in the affine quotient. `WeightedAffineSquarefreeFinal.lean`
combines these with the squarefree Jacobian certificate and generic
plane-quotient criterion to prove `RingHom.Smooth` for the full affine chart
over a perfect field of characteristic other than two with a squarefree
quintic. `WeightedInfinitySquarefreeSmooth.lean` combines the derivative-open
and overlap results to prove `RingHom.Smooth` for the full explicit infinity
coordinate ring under the same hypotheses.
`WeightedProjGlobalSmooth.lean` proves the exact conditional global step:
the actual Proj structure morphism is Smooth if each basic-open restriction
equals the canonical chart isomorphism followed by the corresponding
`Spec.map` of the field-to-chart-ring algebra map. `WeightedProjChartStructure.lean`
checks the corresponding chart structure maps. `WeightedProjSmoothFinal.lean`
proves both required precomposition equalities and applies the two-open
reduction to establish `Smooth` for the actual weighted-Proj structure map
over a perfect field of characteristic other than two with a squarefree
quintic. Its direct pinned Lean compile exited zero and its axiom audit
lists only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`.

`WeightedAffineRingSmooth.lean` proves a generic plane-quotient criterion:
if the quotient images of both partial derivatives span the unit ideal,
the affine coordinate-ring map from `K` is `RingHom.Smooth`. It also checks
the `Y`-partial identity for this genus-2 equation. The separate
`WeightedAffineSquarefreeSmooth.lean` proves that the existing squarefree
Jacobian unit-ideal theorem implies the derivative-range premise,
**conditional on both explicit derivative identities**. The two new affine
files above discharge that condition and establish the specialization.
`WeightedPic0Bridge.lean` identifies the actual weighted-Proj stalk at the
infinity-chart origin with `Localization.AtPrime` of the explicit chart ring.
`WeightedProjInfinityDVR.lean` transports the local DVR structure and proves
that the transported `w` germ is a uniformizer and the transported `z` germ
has valuation order two. These local facts do not establish a global
principal divisor or equality in `Pic⁰`.
`Common22FiniteZeroOrder.lean` proves a conditional nonbranch valuation
identity for the norm factors in a DVR. `Common22BranchStalk.lean` constructs
an actual finite branch-point stalk and its localization.
`Common22BranchParameter.lean` proves the branch factor identity and that
its complementary factor is a unit when the quintic derivative is nonzero.
`Common22BranchValuation.lean` proves `ord(x-a)=2 ord(y)` in that actual
localization, explicitly assuming it is a domain and a DVR. The affine
domain, branch DVR instance, `ord(y)=1`, and global divisor/Picard transport
are not proved by these files.

This identifies both scheme charts, establishes their finite affine cover and properness over `K`, proves the actual weighted-Proj structure map Smooth under the stated hypotheses, constructs a surjective glued-curve-to-Proj morphism, and proves the actual Proj stalk at infinity is a DVR with the stated local orders. An open-immersion/inverse theorem for the glued morphism, global divisor-class transport, the `Pic⁰` class equation, and the Rust-to-Lean execution bridge remain open. The finite-zero multiplicity argument in [PROOF_AUDIT.md](../PROOF_AUDIT.md) is written mathematics, not a Lean divisor theorem.
