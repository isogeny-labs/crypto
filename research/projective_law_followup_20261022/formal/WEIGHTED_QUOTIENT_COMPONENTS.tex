\section*{Open boundary}
The weighted quotient has no packaged \texttt{GradedRing} instance in this packet. The implication that a homogeneous element in $(Z-1)$ is killed by some power of $Z$ is still a proposed degree-by-degree telescoping proof. The degree-zero chart isomorphism, projective completion, Picard-class identity, and Rust-to-Lean semantics bridge remain open.
