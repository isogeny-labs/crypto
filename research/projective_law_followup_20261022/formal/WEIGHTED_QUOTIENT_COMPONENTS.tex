\section*{Open boundary}
Global weighted-Proj Smooth is checked under the stated squarefree hypotheses. An unconditional open-immersion/inverse theorem for the glued-to-Proj morphism, global divisor-class transport, the Picard-class identity, and the Rust-to-Lean semantics bridge remain open. The infinity stalk is a checked DVR with local valuation two for $z$. The global morphism is surjective on points but has no checked inverse.
The actual branch localization is now a checked DVR at each simple root, with $\operatorname{ord}(y)=1$ and $\operatorname{ord}(x-a)=2$. The global principal-divisor equation has not been proved. Both cross-chart opposite-open preimages and their image-to-overlap entries are checked, but equality of overlap lifts and the global glued-to-Proj inverse remain open.
