Pinned Lean constructs a \texttt{Scheme.GlueData} from the affine chart, the infinity chart, their localized overlap spectra, the checked overlap isomorphism, and the two open immersions. It defines the glued scheme and checks that its two chart maps are open immersions and jointly cover the underlying space. All five audited new declarations use only \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, and \texttt{Quot.sound}.

The construction uses \texttt{CategoryTheory.GlueData'} indexed by \texttt{Bool}. Mathlib supplies diagonal overlaps through \texttt{GlueData.ofGlueData'}. The triple-index transition and cocycle obligations in \texttt{GlueData'} require three pairwise-distinct Boolean indices and are discharged by contradiction. The off-diagonal transitions are the inverse maps of the previously checked overlap isomorphism.
