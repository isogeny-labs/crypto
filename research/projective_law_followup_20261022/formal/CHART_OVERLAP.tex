Let $K$ be any field. The affine equation is
\[y^2=x^5+a_3x^3+a_2x^2+a_1x+a_0,\]
and the infinity-chart equation is
\[w^2=z(1+a_3z^2+a_2z^3+a_1z^4+a_0z^5).\]
On $x\ne0$ put $z=x^{-1}$ and $w=yx^{-3}$. Lean proves the two equations are equivalent. On $z\ne0$, the inverse substitution $x=z^{-1}$, $y=wz^{-3}$ satisfies the affine equation; the coordinate round trips hold in both directions. These are field-level identities for arbitrary characteristic.
