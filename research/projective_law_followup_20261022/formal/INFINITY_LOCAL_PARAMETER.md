# Infinity-chart derivative and conditional local parameter

Later work in the [explicit-chart packet](EXPLICIT_CHART.md) constructs the
affine quotient and origin localization, discharging the supplied
coordinate-ideal and unit hypotheses for that particular local ring. The
projective infinity stalk and DVR identification remain open.

Status: the pinned Lean packet checks the chart's transverse derivative and
a conditional local-ring implication. It does not construct the smooth
projective completion or its infinity stalk.

For the normalized quintic, the chart is
`F(z,w)=w²-z(1+a3 z²+a2 z³+a1 z⁴+a0 z⁵)`.
`Common22InfinityChart.lean` proves `F(0,0)=0`,
`∂F/∂z(0,0)=-1≠0` over every field, and root multiplicity one for the
univariate section `F(z,0)` at zero.

`Common22LocalParameter.lean` starts with a **supplied** local ring `R`,
chart coordinates `z,w`, a unit `u`, `w²=z*u`, and the additional hypothesis
`maximalIdeal R=span{z,w}`. It proves `z∈span{w}` and
`maximalIdeal R=span{w}`. If `R` is also a domain and `w≠0`, it proves
`Irreducible w`. The derivative theorem does not automatically discharge
the maximal-ideal hypothesis in Lean.

The open geometric step is to construct the smooth projective completion,
identify `(0,0)` with its unique infinity point, identify its local ring
with the supplied `R`, prove its maximal ideal is generated by the chart
coordinates, and establish the Noetherian one-dimensional domain/DVR
instance. Only then does `w` become the actual normalized uniformizer in
the [conditional DVR](LOCAL_VALUATION.md) and
[fraction-field](FRACTION_POLE.md) packets. The principal divisor,
Mumford reduction, `Pic⁰` transport, and Rust semantic correspondence
remain open. No target solve or rho ratio changes.
