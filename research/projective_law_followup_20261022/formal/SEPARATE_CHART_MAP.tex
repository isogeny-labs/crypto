Let $A=K[X,Y]/(Y^2-X^5-a_3X^3-a_2X^2-a_1X-a_0)$ and let $B$ be the separately
presented infinity-chart coordinate ring. The principal opens are $A_X$ and
$B_z$. Pinned Lean checks a map $A_X\to B_z$ with $X\mapsto z^{-1}$ and
$Y\mapsto wz^{-3}$: the affine equation maps to zero, the map descends to the
quotient, and the image of $X$ is a unit. It also checks the reverse map
$B_z\to A_X$ with $z\mapsto X^{-1}$ and $w\mapsto YX^{-3}$, using the
analogous equation, quotient, and unit proofs. The theorem axiom audits list
only \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, and \texttt{Quot.sound}.

identities, first on quotient generators and then by localization
extensionality. Constructing maps in both directions alone does not prove an
isomorphism. The prior same-ambient quotient theorem does not identify these
separately presented rings. See
