Let $A=K[X,Y]/(Y^2-X^5-a_3X^3-a_2X^2-a_1X-a_0)$ and let $B$ be the separately
presented infinity-chart coordinate ring. The principal opens are $A_X$ and
$B_z$. Pinned Lean checks a map $A_X\to B_z$ with $X\mapsto z^{-1}$ and
$Y\mapsto wz^{-3}$: the affine equation maps to zero, the map descends to the
quotient, and the image of $X$ is a unit. It also checks the reverse map
$B_z\to A_X$ with $z\mapsto X^{-1}$ and $w\mapsto YX^{-3}$, using the
analogous equation, quotient, and unit proofs. Generator round trips and
quotient/localization extensionality prove both full composition identities;
\texttt{separateChartOpenEquiv} is an equivalence $A_X\simeq B_z$ of $K$-algebras.
The theorem axiom audits list only \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice},
and \texttt{Quot.sound}.

\noindent This identifies the separately presented localized coordinate rings.
It does not yet identify them with distinguished open subschemes of a smooth
projective curve, transport its infinity stalk, or prove divisor/$\operatorname{Pic}^0$
or Rust-law semantics. The prior same-ambient quotient theorem alone did not
identify these rings; the checked inverse maps do. See
\texttt{Common22SeparateChartMap.lean}, \texttt{separate-chart-map.csv}, and
\texttt{separate-chart-map.svg}. No empirical cost follows.
