## Question and scope

Can an irreducible quadratic Mumford support be represented as a point on the actual weighted-Proj genus-2 curve? The search covered the pinned Mathlib projective-scheme, adjoined-root, and DVR dimension lemmas and the repository's rational-point constructors. The curve is the weighted Proj of `degreeSubmodule a3 a2 a1 a0` for the odd-characteristic quintic model `y² = x⁵ + a3 x³ + a2 x² + a1 x + a0`. This round did not execute targets or alter any cloud job.

## Derived proof boundary

For any scheme point `z`, if its stalk is a domain and a discrete valuation ring, then `coheight z = 1`. [Mathlib's stalk dimension lemma](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/d13f23b723b8a846827a245b89c10fc7d3f11612/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Properties.lean#L376) identifies the stalk Krull dimension with coheight; [its DVR dimension lemma](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/d13f23b723b8a846827a245b89c10fc7d3f11612/Mathlib/RingTheory/DiscreteValuationRing/TFAE.lean#L244) makes that dimension one. `WeightedProjCoheight.lean` states the composition. The compiler receipt below governs whether the local declaration is independently checked.

## Quadratic-support chart point

`WeightedProjQuadraticPoint.lean` now defines field-extension evaluation on the affine quotient and transports its prime-spectrum point into the actual weighted-Proj `z` chart. For irreducible `u` satisfying `u ∣ v²−f`, `root_curve_of_dvd` proves that `(root u, v(root u))` lies on the curve. `rootEvalAlgHom_surjective` proves the evaluation reaches all of `AdjoinRoot u` because its image contains `root u`, and `rootPointIdeal_maximal` proves the chart ideal maximal. `rootProjStalkIso` identifies the projective stalk with localization at that ideal. The theorem is valid for any irreducible `u`; a degree-two `u` is the intended Mumford case. This proves a maximal affine-chart point and its projective image, without yet proving a global closed-point theorem.

## Local-ring bridge

`WeightedProjQuadraticLocal.lean` now specializes the already-proved whole-curve integrality and local Noetherianity to the irreducible-support point. Under separability of `f`, its actual weighted-Proj stalk is a domain; without that extra condition it is Noetherian. The local-ring structure is intrinsic to a scheme stalk. Pinned Mathlib's DVR criterion then proves the stalk is a DVR **if** its cotangent space over its residue field has dimension one. The module does not prove that dimension. The exact hypotheses and source SHA are recorded in [status.json](status.json).

`WeightedProjQuadraticJacobian.lean` adds the residue-field Jacobian condition at the same support point. For `2 ≠ 0` and separable `f`, it proves `2v(root u) ≠ 0` or `f'(root u) ≠ 0` in `AdjoinRoot u`. The proof uses the curve equation `v(root u)²=f(root u)`: if `v(root u)=0`, separability makes the derivative nonzero; otherwise `2v(root u)` is nonzero. It then defines the one-row linearized affine equation `−f'(root u)·dx + 2v(root u)·dy` and proves its kernel has dimension one over `AdjoinRoot u`. Both statements compile in the default Lake target and depend only on the printed foundational axioms. The kernel is a formal tangent model; the module does **not** identify it with the dual of the actual projective-stalk cotangent space or establish that cotangent space's dimension.

## Open source-specific boundary

The next proof must identify the compiled tangent kernel with the dual of the cotangent space at this quadratic-support stalk, or instead prove an equivalent principal-maximal-ideal criterion. The searched pinned Mathlib `AlgebraicGeometry`, `RingTheory/RegularLocalRing`, and `RingTheory/Extension/Cotangent` paths did not supply a directly applicable quotient/localization theorem for this curve; that search is a proof-engineering gap, not a claim of absence from all mathematics or libraries. Only after the missing bridge does the conditional DVR theorem and generic coheight lemma apply. A global closed-point theorem, the global principal divisor, and Pic⁰ class equation also remain open. No runtime-law or cost claim follows from these local-ring lemmas.

A concrete, **unproved** local-parameter route is to identify the evaluation kernel with the ideal generated by `u(x)` and `y−v(x)`. If `v(root u) ≠ 0` in odd characteristic, the relation `(y−v)(y+v)=f−v²` suggests `u(x)` as a local parameter because `y+v` is a unit. The branch case needs a separate parameter argument. Neither the kernel-generators equality nor either parameter argument is in the compiled proof.
