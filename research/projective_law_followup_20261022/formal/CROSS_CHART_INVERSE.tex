\section*{Scope and checked theorem}
For every field $K$ and coefficients $a_3,a_2,a_1,a_0\in K$, the existing weight-$(1,3,1)$ sextic model has two basic-open charts. In \texttt{WeightedProjCrossInjective.lean}, the theorem \texttt{cross} identifies affine- and infinity-chart points in the separately glued scheme whenever their images in the actual weighted $\operatorname{Proj}$ coincide. The theorem \texttt{gluedToProj\_isIso} then proves that the canonical glued-to-$\operatorname{Proj}$ morphism is a scheme isomorphism. Neither statement requires smoothness or squarefreeness.
