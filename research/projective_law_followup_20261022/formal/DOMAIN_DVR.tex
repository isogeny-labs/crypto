\noindent\textbf{Object.} For every field $K$ and every $a_3,a_2,a_1,a_0\in K$, put
\[
A=K[z,w]/(w^2-zU(z)),\qquad
U(z)=1+a_3z^2+a_2z^3+a_1z^4+a_0z^5,
\]
and let $\mathfrak m$ be the kernel of evaluation at $(0,0)$. The local ring studied here is the explicitly constructed $A_{\mathfrak m}$.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Checked results.} The pinned Lean sources prove that $W^2-zU(z)$ is Eisenstein at $(z)$ as a polynomial over $K[z]$. An explicit variable swap and the multivariable polynomial equivalence transport irreducibility to $K[z,w]$. Hence $A$ is a domain. A degree argument proves that $w$ survives in $A$; injectivity of localization gives $w\neq0$ in $A_{\mathfrak m}$. The earlier chart proof established that $A_{\mathfrak m}$ is Noetherian and local with maximal ideal $(w)$. Mathlib's Noetherian local domain criterion now proves that $A_{\mathfrak m}$ is a discrete valuation ring and $w$ is an irreducible uniformizer there.

\noindent\textbf{Scope.} The DVR is the localization of the affine equation. A projective completion and stalk isomorphism remain to be proved before transferring this uniformizer to the smooth projective curve. The principal divisor, Mumford reduction, and $\operatorname{Pic}^0$ steps, as well as Rust semantic correspondence, remain open. This packet changes no target counts, operation costs, or rho ratio.
