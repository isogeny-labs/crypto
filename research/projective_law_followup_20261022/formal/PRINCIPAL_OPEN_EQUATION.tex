\[F=y^2-(x^5+a_3x^3+a_2x^2+a_1x+a_0),\quad
G=(ys^3)^2-s(1+a_3s^2+a_2s^3+a_1s^4+a_0s^5).\]

It constructs $R/(G)\simeq R/(F)$, then instantiates $R$ as the localization of $K[X,Y]$ away from $X$, with $s=X^{-1}$.
