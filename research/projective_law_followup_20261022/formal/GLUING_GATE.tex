The prior pinned Lean theorem identifies the two principal-open overlap spectra and their open immersions into the separate chart spectra. This packet checks the pinned Mathlib scheme-gluing interfaces. It does not construct a glued curve.

\texttt{Scheme.GlueData} requires chart schemes $U_i$, overlap schemes $V_{ij}$, open immersions $f_{ij}$, transition maps $t_{ij}$, maps $t'_{ijk}$ between pullbacks, diagonal isomorphisms and identity transitions, factorization, and a triple cocycle. The checked overlap gives off-diagonal candidates; it does not fill all gluing fields. A constructed glued scheme would still need an independent isomorphism to a chosen smooth projective genus-2 completion.
