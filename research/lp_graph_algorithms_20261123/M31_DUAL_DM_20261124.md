## Decision

Do not prioritize native DM block extraction for the frozen ten-query stream. A maximum matching alone is a structural rank upper bound; it does not prove a chosen square minor is nonsingular over F_r. The earlier exact full-column-rank certificates are separate algebraic evidence. Nor does dependence alone imply a target is pinned: for a point relation matrix M and target coefficient b, a target witness exists iff b is outside col(M), equivalently some w in ker(M^T) has b^T w nonzero. The planted M61 historical pin-at-first-dependence observation must not be generalized to natural M31 coefficient streams.

For a future admitted stream, prefer exact rank/target tests and native same-stream forward/transpose tape validation under #5076/#5120. If selecting a square minor for Krylov, certify actual nonsingularity or use justified preconditioning with verified original-system residuals; do not silently select the structural matching minor. A production Hopcroft–Karp matching costs O(Z sqrt(R+C)) plus O(R+C+Z) coarse reachability and SCC, and memory O(R+C+Z). The existing Kuhn example can cost O(R Z) and its probes omit other bookkeeping. No reason to pay either solely for the block shortcut on these fixed data.
