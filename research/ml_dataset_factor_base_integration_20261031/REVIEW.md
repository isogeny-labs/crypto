## Original identity and construction

`curves::verify_factor_base_config` checks requested size and public family/n/m/twist, requires absent policy with v1 versus explicit policy with v2, compares strict typed recipe and derived sampling seed, then replays the complete construction. `verify_and_store_worker_originals` invokes this before writing original artifacts or minting its token. Central verification and the native Koblitz/prime original readers use this stronger check. The replay computes its own membership decisions and compares the entire v2 Value, including counters/status; acceptance bits and private logs do not drive it. Generic reference group arithmetic is independent, but candidate enumeration/lift/Frobenius remain shared implementation, as notes disclose.

All four policies are retained. Explicit legacy keeps the mathematical domain while selecting v2 identity. Sampled control draws with replacement independently of target/key/generator indices and projects into the subgroup. Literal binary subspace performs an intersection, not projection; it does not truncate to requested point count. Frobenius union first checks model-preserving action and subgroup stability, processes whole orbits and sign pairs, and reports capped prefixes without claiming domain completion. Prime-family binary recipes return explicit unsupported construction rather than fallback. Repeated-prime order uses bounded cyclic-membership diagnostics rather than r-torsion alone. Private diagnostic logs remain outside public model features.

## Compatibility and exporter

CaseConfig's absent optional field skips serialization; plan/v1's absent recipe axis also skips serialization and preserves mixed-radix ordering. Explicit legacy is a distinct plan/v2 identity. Present empty axes, duplicate recipes, unknown recipe fields, dependent/out-of-range bases and excessive limits reject. Plan cell generation selects solver then recipe then existing axes; cell counts include the axis with checked multiplication.

Exporter verifies sealed cells and checks exact expected plan/config before projection. Strict policy_projection emits only recipe kind, declared basis and candidate limit. Sampling seed, candidate records, membership logs, construction status and counters are absent from model inputs; construction outcomes go to restricted targets/report measurements. `source_factor_base_policy` remains alongside solver-specific replacement factor bases for PKM and double-large-prime. Existing prime-chain prefix/algebra projection receives the same allowlisted source inputs. Unsupported recipes emit no model feature row. v1 export keeps v6 policy marker; v2 selects v7.

## Explicit remaining integration boundary

Its authenticated original_math call will gain config-recipe validation from this patch, so wrong-recipe sources are rejected; however, equal mathematical bases from different declared policies produce equal native group feature rows. The current exporter does not invoke project_koblitz_m3_prefixes, so this is not a bypass in the wired static/prime-prefix exporter. Do not claim native Koblitz causal recipe coverage from these 19 files.
