=== per-gadget cost at 61 bits (patched encoder)
modular_multiply   9247 edges   degree 520
modular_add         717 edges   degree 5
less_than           122 edges   degree 3
gated_equal         241 edges   degree 3
hash_member       40712 edges   degree 520
point_add        188704 edges   degree 520

Within point_add, the two curve-validity checks account for 112,192 used
signals (59%); the paper's addition constraints and the multiply gadget are the
rest. modular_multiply is therefore NOT the bottleneck.

Conclusion: the remaining floor is structural. Two Ai validity checks are
required by the language (interface points may be off-curve), each costing
x^2, x^3, y^2. Removing them or folding validity into membership is unsound.
Going below this needs either a different validity encoding or dropping the
low-degree requirement in favour of a single program vertex.

=== derived-parameter decomposition (structural)
A2 = T - A1 instead of a free second point. For cofactor-one curves, this
parameterizes the same decompositions with a smaller interface. With a
nontrivial cofactor, A1 is projected into the certified subgroup; projection
may give multiple witness assignments for a decomposition.

Cumulative structural change: 493124 -> 31865 edges (15.5x smaller).
--check-fast: 16/16 combinations agree with brute force on cofactor-one
fixtures. A separate cofactor-two regression checks the projected derived
encoding and its expected non-parsimonious witness count.

=== scalar-parameter decomposition (structurally removes validity)
Interface is k with A1 = k*G by a double-and-add programme circuit, A2 = T - A1.
k -> k*G is a bijection on the prime-order subgroup, so the exact count is
unchanged. A1 is on-curve by construction; A2 is on-curve by the group law.
No curve-validity check remains.
