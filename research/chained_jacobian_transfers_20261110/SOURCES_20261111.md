## Read before implementation

- PR #3877 at `4ff4da410d6b10834a59e843455a79a12b1baa30`, its eleven
  study files, and 2026-11-10 validation receipt. The split-only `push` returns
  `None` both for unsupported inputs and the elliptic identity. The new API
  must distinguish errors from infinity and must include closed degree-two
  support. The one-infinity coordinate chart must be retained.
- `papers/ecdlp-index-calculus-treatise/survey/transfer.md`, sections 1 and
  11–13, and `refs.bib` (GHS 2002 bibliographic entry). These are internal
  summaries, not universal closures of computational transfer mechanisms.
- `.agents/skills/ecdlp-surfaces/references/higher-genus.md` and
  `docs/curves/HYPERELLIPTIC_INFRASTRUCTURE.md`. Existing odd sextic support
  does not supply full split-infinity arithmetic. Existing binary GF(8) norm
  support must not be generalized to arbitrary fields.
- `src/prime_hyperelliptic/{curve,fp_poly}.rs` and
  `src/cryptanalysis/hyperelliptic_word.rs`: pre-existing native Cantor
  implementations on odd-degree models, suitable differential references.
- Steven Galbraith, *Mathematics of Public Key Cryptography*, corrected
  version 2.0, [chapter 8](https://www.math.auckland.ac.nz/~sgal018/crypto-book/ch8.pdf),
  section 8.3, especially Theorem 8.3.8(5) and Corollary 8.3.10. Pullback and
  norm descend to Picard groups and norm-after-pullback multiplies by degree;
  these statements supply the geometric bridge, not the mere point map.
- Galbraith, [chapter 10](https://www.math.auckland.ac.nz/~sgal018/crypto-book/ch10.pdf),
  sections 10.3–10.4: Lemma 10.3.5 (multiplicity/Hensel conditions),
  Theorem 10.3.14 (composition), Theorem 10.3.21 and section 10.4.1
  (ramified model), Exercise 10.4.4 (the four forms, including irreducible
  quadratic support), section 10.4.2 (split-infinity caveat). This determines
  the chart, validation invariants and nonsplit/ramified controls.

The chapter PDFs were fetched from the author's site and read as full-text
excerpts in this session. No novelty is claimed for these constructions.
