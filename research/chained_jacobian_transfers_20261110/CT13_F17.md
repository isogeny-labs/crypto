## Explicit, independently recomputed finite-field witnesses

The sextic has factorization

```text
F(X)=(X³+6X²+X+7)(X³+11X²+X+10)  (mod 17).
```

The two cubics are irreducible. Work in
`L=F17[t]/(t³+6t²+t+7)`; take `β_i=t^(17^i)`, i=0,1,2.
For k=0,1,2 define `G_i(X)=(X-β_i)(X+β_(i+k mod 3))`.
Then `∏_i G_i=F` and Frobenius cyclically permutes each triple.
There are exactly **three** Frobenius-stable perfect matchings of the
six branch roots. Their coefficient determinants are

```text
k=0: Δ=0   (opposite-root, decomposable)
k=1: Δ=11  (nondegenerate)
k=2: Δ=6   (nondegenerate)
```

With the precisely specified raw bracket convention
`H_i=G_j' G_k-G_j G_k'` (i,j,k cyclic),
`R=H_0 H_1 H_2 / Δ` has *ascending* coefficients:

```text
k=1: [14,12,6, 4,10, 3,16]
k=2: [14, 5,6,13,10,14,16]
```

Both vectors lie in F17 despite being constructed from quadratic factors
over F17³. The second polynomial is exactly `R_1(-X)`; the two
candidate codomain curves are F17-isomorphic by `X↦-X`. They must
be **deduplicated as destination isomorphism classes**, while keeping their
different kernel data. Both polynomials are irreducible sextics over F17,
proved by exhaustive trial division by every monic polynomial of degrees
1,2,3. In contrast, the source branch roots have Frobenius type (3,3).
An F17-isomorphism between the source and either target curve would induce
an F17-defined PGL2 map of their hyperelliptic branch sets and preserve
Frobenius orbit lengths; (3,3) versus (6) excludes such an isomorphism.

Exact character sums over F17 and F17² give, for the source and **both**
candidate targets,

```text
#C(F17)=16, #C(F17²)=348, #J(F17)=285;
L(J,T)=1-2T+31T²-34T³+289T⁴.
```

The #J count agrees independently with `#E19 × #E15 = 285`.
**These are necessary isogeny-class/zeta checks, not a direct
verification of the normalized Richelot correspondence or dual.**
Unlike F11, `-1=4² mod 17`, so replacing R by -R is an
F17-coordinate isomorphism `y↦4y`, *not* a quadratic-twist test. This
field cannot independently settle the F11 normalization sign convention.

## A bounded second-hop obstruction

A degree-six irreducible polynomial over F17 has a Frobenius **6-cycle**
on its branch roots. A six-cycle preserves exactly **one** perfect
matching: each root paired with the one three Frobenius steps away.
Thus each candidate target here has at most one F17-rational
maximal-isotropic (2,2) kernel of the six-branch-root pairing type.
Conditional on the explicit Richelot correspondence being correctly
normalized, its F17-rational dual supplies that kernel, leaving **no
nonbacktracking rational Richelot second hop from this target**.

This is deliberately local: other isogeny degrees, non-Richelot
correspondences, larger fields, different curves and chains remain open.
Even if the candidate isogeny is established, `g_* f^*` and any
specific order-19 image **still require actual divisor-map evaluation**.
