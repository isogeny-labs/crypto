# Known logic-synthesis boundary

[Boyar and Peralta, SEA2010, primary NIST publication page](https://www.nist.gov/publications/new-combinational-logic-minimization-technique-applications-cryptology), reviewed during C112, describes optimization of nonlinear gates followed by linear components. C113uses a deterministic greedy shared-pair heuristic with an incremental membership index; it is not claimed to implement that paper's full method or establish algorithmic novelty. The known category is linear common-subexpression synthesis.

The useful new measured result here is a concrete exact graph for this pinned necessary root family, below its source-control formulation counts. It says nothing by itself about solving time, genuine GB ratios or cryptographic-scale feasibility. No published S-box result is imported as a rootfinding measurement.
