# Three-point norm lines and characteristic-specific parameter kernels

A new derivation removes fixed-h/fixed-k assumptions for conditional binary norm search. For F=X³+rX²+sX+t and G=hX+k, the target and three source points impose four field-linear equations in five coefficients. Four distinct x coordinates have rank4: a rank drop would put their y values on a line, contradicting four intersections with a monic cubic. The coefficient family is an affine field line. Every norm coefficient on it is a+blambda+clambda², hence affine F2-linear in binary fields. Coefficient-span admission is therefore one exact linear system in the83 coordinates of lambda; no outer h or k loop is required for that point triple.

Complete literal enumeration of98,304 coefficient assignments over GF8/GF16 verified nine point-lift contexts,80 norm-line evaluations and25 positive supported norms. The residual small-field constraint fibers had dimensions2 or3 and were retained in full. All16 frozen actual GF(2^83) point-triple/lift cells were rejected by exact linear systems while allowing arbitrary h,k. This is conditional cell closure; it does not enumerate all triples or cover every repeated/nonrational-lift/exceptional branch. Full original roots and qualified speed ratios remain open.

For odd extensions, squaring the parameter is not additive. A separate exact kernel substitutes quadratic parameter maps into coefficient-subfield constraints M(lambda)^p-M(lambda)=0. Their GCD has degree at most2p, and modular exponentiation intersects it with lambda^|K|-lambda without constructing a huge dense polynomial. Identically zero constraints return a whole-field symbol. All36 frozen GF9/GF25 map cases passed complete independent parameter enumeration, including empty, positive and whole-field cases. This kernel supplies the exact parameter constraint polynomial; general large-field root extraction and integration with odd curve interpolation remain open. For prime fields, Frobenius admission does not enforce interval support.
