# Exact characteristic-two support and the failure of odd traces

Let K have characteristic two, H be monic degree five, A=K[X]/(H), and R the multiplication-by-L_D(X) endomorphism. The finite domain D is a binary linear subspace, and its squarefree annihilator L_D is linearized. Every algebraic root of H belongs to D, with arbitrary multiplicities, iff R is nilpotent, equivalently R^8=0. Repeated factors must remain present; no squarefree shortcut is used.

Write det(tI-R)=t^5+e1*t^4+e2*t^3+e3*t^2+e4*t+e5 and sj=Tr(R^j). Newton identities in characteristic two give s1=e1, s2=s1², s4=s2², s3=e1*s2+e2*s1+e3 and s5=e1*s4+e2*s3+e3*s2+e4*s1+e5. Consequently s1=s3=s5=0 implies e1=e3=e5=0, but imposes no requirement e2=e4=0. The characteristic polynomial is then t*(t^4+e2*t²+e4), or t*(t²+a*t+b)² over the perfect finite field with a²=e2,b²=e4. This is a parity constraint on nonzero eigenvalues, not nilpotence.

If s1=0 is already known, the FOUR field residuals (e2,s3,e4,s5) vanish iff the characteristic polynomial is t^5, hence iff R is nilpotent.
