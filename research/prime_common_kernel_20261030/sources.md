# Sources and scope

The starting sources were read before selecting these constructions.
Exact primary PDF pins and assumptions remain in
[the original source study](../prime_one_shot_20261029/continuation/tensor-review/README.md).

- *The Exponential Time Hypothesis is False*, Oct25,2026, pinned
  [primary manuscript](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/7ef5c0cc5ca4b0e072b7a050fca09ab24bc6fa3a/preprints/The-Exponential-Time-Hypothesis-is-False-October-25-2026/paper.pdf),
  SHA256 `5b1612dc2eebb2cad5b4cbe62d1fed2982f01ab1908c26f70010a651487e8123`.
  D.4.1–D.4.7 define the actual Boolean interpolation, packet/mode identities,
  tapered precision and immutable implicit-entry descriptions. Those
  identities do not automatically apply to a different exterior kernel.
- Akshima et al., *A Subexponential Time Algorithm for Elliptic Curve
  Discrete Logarithm*, v0.2 Oct29,2026,
  [pinned primary](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bochum-crypto/subexponential-ecdl/1ee44e562b1b9a50ea339f3b8f81112b5a24b96f/subexponential-ecdl.pdf),
  SHA256 `82e273629b437660607117d5e99f400141ed7aa78b25c6749b03651f42deaa11`.
  Its claimed prime-field result is existing work, not a result of this study.
- Al-Bashabsheh–Mao, *Normal Factor Graphs and Holographic Transformations*,
  [arXiv1004.3833v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1004.3833), §2.3–2.4/Theorem1:
  inverse finite-index gauges are established prior art.
- Mortier et al., *Fermionic tensor network methods*,
  [arXiv2404.14611v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.14611v2), §2.1–2.4:
  mode ordering and occupation-dependent signs must be retained.
- Epple–Fries–Hinrichsen, *Implementing the sine transform of fermionic
  modes as a tensor network*,
  [arXiv1705.10186v1](https://arxiv.org/html/1705.10186v1), §V:
  lifting one-particle factorizations is prior work.
- Bravyi, *Contraction of matchgate tensor networks on non-planar graphs*,
  [arXiv0801.2989](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0801.2989), §1/Theorem1:
  arbitrary endpoint predicates do not become matchgates by adjoining a
  fermionic kernel; vertex identities and genus remain assumptions.
- Knill–Laflamme, *A Theory of Quantum Error-Correcting Codes*,
  [arXivquant-ph/9604034v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/9604034), §2–3:
  projected-error/syndrome identities are established prior art. The proposed
  common kernel here uses those identities in classical coefficient algebra.
- The existing [matched-level analysis](../prime_tensor_variants_20261030/reviews/matched-level.md),
  [independent recovery proof](../prime_tensor_variants_20261030/reviews/matched-recovery-independent.md),
  [counterfamily](../prime_tensor_variants_20261030/reviews/levelwise-binomial-counterexample.md),
  and [problem record](../../problems/correlated-packet-common-kernel.md).
- Bounded lead-atlas search in `crypto-autoresearcher/knowledge/leads/ecdlp/`:
  tensor, kernel, basis, gauge, Fourier, Cauchy and fermionic/matchgate terms.
  The algebraic-transform corpus includes a spinor/matchgate lead with
  unsupplied constructors. That is neither a failed measured experiment nor
  evidence of impossibility. Private ML/RL content is not exported here.

The linked source locations provide provenance. Neither an organization
namespace nor an unsuccessful search certifies a theorem or novelty.
