# Horizontal fibre admission

The exact census found 36 completely split fibres at nonzero ordinates up to sign. All 36 occurred in genus two. None had at most two large places under the registered catalogue ordering. The genus-three and genus-four curves yielded zero complete fibres, including multiplicities. The proposed horizontal startup pass therefore adds zero initially admissible rows on all eleven curves.

Distinct nonzero ordinates up to sign have distinct squares. Their rational abscissa sets are disjoint because f(x) has a single value at each x. Thus these 36 rows also cannot cancel one another's large-place coefficients. Genus-two rows could become eligible after joins from another relation family. That would require paying for creation and retention and measuring the eventual effect. For genus three and four, reordering the factor base cannot create a missing complete fibre.

The census was reproduced byte for byte from the frozen Ruby source. Counts are algebraic; no wall-time observation is attributed to this screen.
