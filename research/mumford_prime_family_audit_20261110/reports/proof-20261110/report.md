# Unbounded prime orders in the checked arithmetic family

For every bound B, Lean proves an actual parameter t with g=12t+2>B
and prime n=24t+5>B. The reduced-pair arithmetic group over F₃ for
f=Xⁿ+1 has cardinality n, actual generator (X,1) of exact order n,
certified normalized Cantor sums for every ordered pair, and every
member generated by that point. [pcc:unverified]

All members are smooth, fraction 1. The full finite universe of valid
reduced pairs has strictly smaller fraction. Such prime-order disparities
exist beyond every bound; Lean directly negates universal equality for
the stated prime-order arithmetic family. [pcc:unverified]
