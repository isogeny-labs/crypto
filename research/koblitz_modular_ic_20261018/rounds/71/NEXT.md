# R72 local lengths and ramification

In each R71 fiber, compute the Frobenius-fixed subspace of the stable
image. Enumerate its complete Boolean set of idempotents: three
nonzero possibilities over `z=0` and 15 over `z=1`. Test each against
the full specialized 528-product multiplication table. Identify the
primitive orthogonal idempotents, verify they sum to the source unit,
then compute the rank of multiplication by each primitive idempotent.
Because R70 proves the affine order normal over the DVR at each finite
prime, each local rank is `e*f`; R71 gives `f=2`. Check the sum of local
ranks is 32. This will give the separate ramification indices.

Then compute a uniformizer and valuations for each primitive local
factor, especially `b,c,lambda,nu` and the registered denominators.
Repeat the complete residue/idempotent/local-length calculation at
`f_C`, and construct the infinity chart before claiming proper divisors.
