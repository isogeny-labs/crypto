\section*{R90 rank-32 transport: bound-61 prefix and dimension failure}
The single frozen attempt checked forward column 1 at its first
pole-clearing power 61. Its full R50 module product and corrupted-coordinate
control passed. Forward column 2 failed when Singular converted a
32-component vector with a trailing zero to a 31-by-1 matrix. The driver
rejected the attempt; the reverse direction was not reached.
