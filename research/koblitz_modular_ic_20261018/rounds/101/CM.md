# R101: exact x=1 fiber of the marked-23 y/lambda order

The accepted R51 degree-112 triangular donor has a source-checked additive
lambda exponent grid `{16,8,4,1,0}`. R101 exhaustively specialized all 28
power-of-x rational coefficients of `G51` and every term of `f51` at x=1.
The resulting equations are

    f51(1,y) = y^7+y^3 = y^3(y+1)^4,
    G51(1,y,l) = l^16+l^8+1 = (l^2+l+1)^8.

The specialized lambda derivative is zero as a polynomial in y and l.
Both y=0 and y=1 lambda fibers have the same irreducible quadratic factor
`l^2+l+1`, exponent 8. Singular checked both exact products and changed
products. The source-bound native run exited zero without `?` errors and
printed `FIBER101_ALL_CHECKS_PASS`. A separate process rebound the donor,
repeated Python specialization and Singular factorization, and reproduced
all exported bytes. The independently committed GF(2) checker passed the
two power identities, irreducibility of the quadratic over F2, and
dimensions 48+64=112. Lean 4.34.1 compiled concrete F2 polynomial identity
and component-length checks; the source-binding remains Python/CAS evidence.

For the finite monogenic order `F2[x,y,l]/(f51,G51)`, the x=1 fiber has
two maximal ideals, each with residue field F4. Its two local components
have F2-vector-space dimensions 48 and 64. These are **source-order**
lengths, not normalized branch counts or ramification indices. The linear
`N51` coordinate and its denominators were not used in this order's fiber
calculation. Normalization of the local rings is the next obligation.
