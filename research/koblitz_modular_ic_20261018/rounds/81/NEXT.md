# Test whether the auxiliary split survives in the source field

Recover the candidate auxiliary generators as fractions of the
original R50 `x,y,p` source field. For each denominator, compute its
local order at `p=0` and test integrality of the corresponding
fraction over F2[[p]] on both source-model components. A verified
nonintegral generator rejects the R81 split as branch data; a complete
integral basis with certified source-field equality permits comparing
its four residual maps to an independent local closure. Only after
that comparison should the degree-2 factors enter the `e*f` budgets
or the divisors of `b,c,lambda,nu` and registered denominators.
