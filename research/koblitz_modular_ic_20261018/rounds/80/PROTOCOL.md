the retained 22-variable/400-relation ring, recheck characteristic

corresponding R78 source factor. Compute the radical of the complete

two-component summary. The prime degrees are **candidate residue
degrees**, not actual normalized infinity branches; promotion requires
independent local integral closure/source-field certification.

a time, seed 80, 1200 seconds and 4 GiB virtual per program. Copy
source/protocol/driver bytes from committed Git and verify all donor
hashes. Accept only exit 0, exact marker, zero native
whitespace-question-mark errors and all assertions. Retain failures
and timeouts; freeze any repair before a fresh ID. No statistical or
comparative timing claim.
