# Certify or reject the four candidate factors

Extract explicit generators for each of the four `minAssGTZ` ideals,
their multiplication tables, and the source-coordinate images. First
check whether the candidate algebra has the same fraction field as
the R50 relative source equation and whether its proposed integral
basis closes under multiplication. Then compute the local integral
closure over F2[[u]] independently on each 16-dimensional component.
Compare its primitive idempotents, residue degrees and `e*f` budgets
with R80. A failed source-field or integrality check rejects these
candidate factors; a successful certificate promotes them to actual
branches and permits individual valuations of `b,c,lambda,nu` and
registered denominators. Those complete divisors feed the level-77
Riemann--Roch construction and the order-11 action test.
