# R39 Lean workflow, frozen before compilation

Lean 4.34.1, mathlib d13f23b723b8a846827a245b89c10fc7d3f11612;
one thread, 4096 MiB Lean limit, 1200-second compile and audit limits.
Use the retained dependency roots and audit tool. No native arrays are imported.

Prove exact universal negative-CM-root identities and their consequences when
a squared minus a plus 2 vanishes. Prove the twist fixed-line equivalence given
d to the fifth equals minus one. Prove recovery of an element killed by 11
from its ninefold multiple with scalar 5, including preservation of nonzero
elements and a conditional operator-value interpretation.

Acceptance requires compilation and axiom audit exit zero, no failing or
trusted declarations. Retain every rejected attempt. These are algebraic and
group-theoretic implications; they do not prove the modular decomposition,
source-model binding, polarization, saturation, or an evaluated Koblitz map.
