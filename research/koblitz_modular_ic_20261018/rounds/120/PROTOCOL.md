For each of the two F4 residue roots, determine the unique F4 leading correction `beta` satisfying `beta^8 =` the recorded translated constant's leading coefficient. Substitute `Z=s^m(beta+W)`, divide by the edge order `8m`, and combine all five coefficient supports with explicit cutoffs. Report only exact orders below cutoffs and a lower hull only if all five resolve. Compare conjugate inputs, byte-replay, changed-input rejection and independent bit-plane evaluation; bind finite polygon arithmetic to Lean if it resolves.

The three places and both roots are fixed. There is no sampling, timing, performance comparison, or cloud spend. Source and protocol must be committed before data.
