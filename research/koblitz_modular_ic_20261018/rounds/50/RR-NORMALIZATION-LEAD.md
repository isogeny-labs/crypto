# Complete RR spaces as generators of affine integral closure

This is a written construction lead, with explicit hypotheses. No native
integral-closure output is asserted here.

Let C/k be a smooth geometrically integral proper curve of genus g, and
pi:C -> P1 a finite map of degree n, with affine coordinate z on the base.
Let D be an effective k-divisor supported in pi^-1(infinity), with
degree(D)>2g-2+n. A complete k-basis of L(D) generates

    Gamma(C minus pi^-1(infinity), O_C)

as a k[z]-module.

Proof. Work first over an algebraic closure. For each finite base point a,
the complete scheme-theoretic fiber F_a is a Cartier divisor of degree n.
Both D and D-F_a have degree greater than 2g-2, so RR gives
l(D)-l(D-F_a)=n. Restriction to the fiber has kernel L(D-F_a), and its
target has dimension n because D has no support there. It is therefore
surjective, including ramified and nonreduced fibers. The full affine
ring is finite over k[z], by finiteness of pi; hence so is its quotient by
the module generated by the RR sections. Every geometric finite fiber of
that quotient vanishes. Local Nakayama and faithful base change force the
quotient to vanish. This argument concerns module generation; it does not
require a projective-normality assertion or a search over coefficient boxes.
