# A pole-independent differential filter alongside the cubic search

## Proposed construction, not measured data
For the ordinary target y²+xy=x³+a x²+1, its invariant differential is
omega_E=dx/x. A separable source map pulls it back to a nonzero holomorphic
logarithmic differential omega=d(x_source)/x_source. Cartier fixes logarithmic
differentials. If the map has sigma(x)=x, omega is also sigma-fixed. Thus
compute the JOINT Cartier-fixed and sigma-fixed holomorphic differentials
on the proper genus61 source before increasing every function pole bound.
A zero joint space would exclude separable sigma-compatible ordinary maps
at every pole bound. A nonzero space supplies differential directions to
constrain the rational-function reconstruction. Neither outcome identifies
the intended order11 factor; keep that as a separate action obligation.

This is an inference applying a standard necessary condition to our source,
not a new Cartier theorem or an experiment result. For inseparable maps,
first remove Frobenius factors and justify the target/pole transport over
F32; do not use a zero pullback to reject an arbitrary inseparable map.

## Concrete exact obligations
1. Derive the COMPLETE divisor of a separating differential du, including
   every ramified and exceptional branch, not only the four infinity points.
   Build61 holomorphic differentials with complete local/field identities
   and independence. A selected differential subspace cannot certify a zero
   global joint space. Certify the separating parameter and constant field.
2. Implement Cartier in the degree32 field using the exact decomposition
   f=g²+u*h², C(f du)=h du. Prove the square decomposition complete and
   unique, verify every whole-field reconstruction and local regularity.
   Construct the actual61x61 Cartier and sigma matrices; verify square
   and commutation identities in their proper semilinear meanings.
3. Over F32 Cartier acts on coefficients by inverse squaring c->c^16.
   Expand all61 coefficients into305 binary coordinates. Solve C(omega)=omega
   together with sigma(omega)=omega by complete exact kernels. The joint
   fixed set is an F2 vector space, not generally an F32 vector space.
   Retain full splitting and source-bound separator/preimage certificates.
4. For any nonzero direction, solve d(x)=x*omega in the source field modulo
   squares, then recover the required divisors and AS relation with exact
   whole-source checks. Logarithmic directions alone are not elliptic maps.
   Preserve both trace parities and all coefficient conjugates. Compare
   with known n3Klein/samefieldn5 map controls where the differential data
   can be transported exactly.

This filter complements, rather than substitutes for, the complete324-cubic
ambient and818-pattern larger-pole construction in NEXT.md. Direct modular
relations, integral Hecke projectors and order11 action still need their
own exact data. R51/CM.md already warns that an F2 differential matrix
cannot separate integral sign eigenspaces or certify an11-torsion scalar;
that warning applies here.

## Primary source checked, 2026-11-01
Friedlander, Garton, Malmskog, Pries and Weir, *The a-numbers of Jacobians
of Suzuki curves*, section3.3, lists the characteristic2 Cartier rules,
including preservation of logarithmic forms, inverse-Frobenius scalar
behavior and the square decomposition. The paper studies Suzuki curves;
its curve-specific basis and matrices are NOT transferred to this source.
Only the standard operator rules motivate the proposed exact computation.
