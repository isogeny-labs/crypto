## 1. Port the QR certificate rather than search another carrier box

Already implemented in R51: the actual universal R22 matrices clear to
polynomial identities,and their quadratic twist descends explicitly. Their
common denominator is exactly (1+c)^5*Delta^3. This makes other marked bases
accessible through a source-bound specialization and inverse certificate.
The marked23 run is active;generic primality and selected991 action follow
only after the actual algebra is constructed. Preserve the level161 sign
constraint from R15. Avoid adding the old order11 constraints to the23base.

## 2. Choose a valid connectedness test for the degree16 QR cover

FIBER51 exhaustively checks the sixteen nonzero F7-vector classes modulo
{1,2,4}. Its queued1008-matrix test asks whether an ordinary Frobenius action
can ever give a16-cycle. If not,an irreducible degree16 ordinary fiber is an
unsuitable connectedness criterion. Then use symbolic primality or an actual
monodromy/moduli identification,not a finite-fiber search that cannot succeed.
The finite-group result and its application to the modular cover are separate.

## 3. Construct a genuine Hecke-3 correspondence on the normal carrier

The CM-prime filter matters: 3 is inert in Q(sqrt(-7)),so T3 is an
annihilator/relation probe,not a candidate nonzero map on the CM factor.
The primary [49.2.a.a record](https://www.lmfdb.org/ModularForm/GL2/Q/holomorphic/49/2/a/a/)
records CM discriminant-7 and a3=0. For the direct one-prime CM characters,
vanishing at the good inert prime follows from the absence of an ideal of
norm3;this is a mathematical inference from the retained character construction.
The queued native prime filter finds the first good odd split candidates:
23 for level77 and11 for level161. Their actual target residual eigenvalues
must still be checked. Keep this distinction in any relation/rank experiment.

This is a proposed carrier construction,not a computed source correspondence. The
new explicit chart algebra makes a four-branch3-isogeny correspondence worth
trying after nu's source-coordinate meaning and transport have been checked.
For the Tate model Y²+(1+c)XY+bY=X³+bX²,derive and verify the3-division
quartic natively,then use each cyclic order3 kernel to construct its quotient
map. Normalize the image of the marked level11 point back to Tate form.
Recover the actual QR7 packet through the isogeny before claiming formulas
on lambda/nu;do not infer their transformation from b,c alone.

Acceptance sequence: full function-field identities and kernel checks;all
four kernel branches and all denominator/exceptional cases;compatibility
with the actual QR source equations;normal-chart regularity and descent;
then action on the order11 target and new relation rank after removing
Frobenius/deck dependencies. This directly adds a correspondence beyond the
already checked diamond action. Nonzero generic degree alone is insufficient
for the selected target action.

A primary implementation reference supplies a concrete characteristic-two

## 4. Use the zero Hecke eigenvalue as a projector constraint

T3's vanishing on the CM factor need not discard the correspondence. Once
its actual action matrix on the retained anti-invariant differential/Jacobian
module is constructed,compute its zero-eigenspace and the other factors
exactly. A polynomial in T3 can project onto its zero eigenspace with an
integral scalar denominator determined by a Bezout/Smith calculation. This
could isolate the CM block from some larger carrier components. It does not
automatically isolate the selected Frobenius sign or prove the zero eigenspace
has only CM constituents;both require actual matrix/factor checks.

The decisive test is an integral source-bound projector certificate and its
scalar on the order11 target. If the scalar is divisible by11,record the
exact lattice obstruction and investigate joint Hecke/Frobenius/diamond
saturation. If it is a unit mod11,compose with the required factor/sign
selection and test actual divisor images. This gives T3 a useful role while
keeping a3=0 explicit. No coefficient-box or inferred nonzero-action claim.
