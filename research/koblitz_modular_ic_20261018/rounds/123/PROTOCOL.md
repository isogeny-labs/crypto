# R123: normalized x=0 branch census

Claim class: exact computation plus a stated application of the characteristic-p Newton polygon theorem. Bind R116 and R119–R122 result files by SHA-256. For each of the four F2-rational base places, use the first fully split translated polynomial: R119 y1/e1, R120 y1/e2, R121 y0/e1, R122 y0/e3. Check both F4 residue roots, all coefficient orders/cutoffs, the positive edges of horizontal lengths 1 and 7, nonzero leading coefficients, and the seven-edge residual relation `W^7=1`. Derive the local factor degrees, ramification indices and residue degrees over F2; check the x=0 divisor degree sum against the source extension degree 112. Preserve the distinction between Newton polygon theorem input and finite checks. Replay byte-identically, reject changed inputs, independently recompute the degree total, and bind finite arithmetic to Lean. No timing, sampling, comparative claim or cloud cost. Commit protocol/source before output.

Prior work: R116 base places and R119–R122 translated polygons. Sources: [Stacks Henselian local rings](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/04GE) for simple-root lifting and [Stoll's Newton polygon notes](https://www.mathe2.uni-bayreuth.de/stoll/teaching/pAdicAnalysis-WS2015/Skript-pAdicAnalysis-pub-print.pdf) for slopes and root valuations in characteristic p.
