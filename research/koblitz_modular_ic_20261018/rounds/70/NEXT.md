# After affine normality: explicit proper divisors

Use the accepted R70 rank-32 normal `A=F2[z]` order directly. At each
finite omitted prime `z`, `z+1`, and
`f_C=z^5+z^3+z^2+z+1`, reduce the complete multiplication table modulo
the prime and factor the resulting finite algebra into residue fields.
For each branch, compute a local uniformizer and exact valuations of
`b,c,lambda,nu`, the R51/R52/R53 registered denominators, and the
Tate/QR incidence coordinates. Record residue degrees and the sum of
ramification-degree products as a degree-32 control. Do not infer
branches from the semigroup pattern alone.

At infinity, transport the accepted R64 bridge `z=1/u`, construct the
second normal chart and its overlap with this affine order, then compute
the same valuations and leading coefficients. The union of finite and
infinity valuations gives actual pole divisors. Use those divisors to
build a complete prescribed Riemann--Roch space, then search for a
nonconstant level-77 map or correspondence. A candidate must pass
whole-function-field identities, all descent conjugates, and a nonzero
action on the order-11 subgroup. The proper polarized embedding and
projective saturation remain independent checks.

For scaling, reuse the complete-quotient Frobenius method on the
accepted degree-112 marked-23 source only after constructing its own
finite free order, trace Gram, field embedding and conductor over-lattice;
R70's rank-32 normality does not transfer automatically to degree 112.
