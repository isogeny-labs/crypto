# After rational ramification

For each of the six primitive local factors, construct the maximal
ideal as the kernel of its map to the `F4` residue field. Find a
uniformizer of valuation one in the normal local ring and verify its
powers have the expected successive module lengths. Use the local
factor projection to compute valuations and leading coefficients of
`b,c,lambda,nu` and every R51/R52/R53 registered denominator.

The degree-five `f_C=z^5+z^3+z^2+z+1` fiber needs the same complete
finite algebra, idempotent, local-length and valuation pipeline over
`F32`. Construct the second normal infinity chart and overlap to
finish the pole divisors. Only then freeze a complete prescribed
Riemann--Roch space for a level-77 map and test its order-11 action.

The six rational branches already yield a useful leading check:
the `z+1` discriminant exponent is exactly the tame contribution from
the two `e=7` branches, while `z=0` has wild excess 48. A conductor
and different calculation at the wild branches may expose convenient
uniformizers or a smaller basis for the prescribed space.
