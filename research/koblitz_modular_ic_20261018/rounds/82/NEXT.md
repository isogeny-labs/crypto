# Use direct positive membership

Freeze a new protocol and fresh attempt. Reuse the saved `norid`
generators without computing `std(norid)`. For each denominator-cleared
fraction identity, require a zero reduction and, preferably, export
the actual reduction/lift coefficients. Check the source denominator
nonzero modulo the selected source prime with its smaller standard
basis. If a direct reduction stalls, test the 19 identities in small
fixed batches and retain the first exact failure. Do not interpret a
nonzero remainder against a non-Gröbner generating set as a failed
identity.
