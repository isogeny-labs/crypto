# Candidate reduced fibers, then normalization proof

On each accepted R79 candidate fiber, compute the absolute Frobenius
stable image, its dimension, and all idempotents from the full
multiplication table of the 16-dimensional quotient. Rebind each
factor to the R50 source equation and retain an explicit bad-factor
control. This can expose candidate residue degrees and nilpotency
lengths without repeating the timed-out global `norTest`.

To promote those factors to actual degree-32 infinity branches,
independently certify the local integral closure over `F2[[u]]` or
complete the global normality/source-field verifier. Then compute
`e,f` with weighted sum 16 above each quadratic place and individual
orders of `b,c,lambda,nu` and all registered denominators. The
proper divisors feed the level-77 Riemann--Roch map and order-11
action test.
