# Post-seal scope clarification:polar support versus all zeros

This note adds an explicit obligation;it does not change the sealed55-member
archive,its source frame or the reviewed PDF. The exceptional table is complete
only above SS5 and Z5.

The five rational base supports are the remaining known polar supports in the
base functions and generic recovery denominators. Resolving their lifts is the
next step toward a complete prescribed pole divisor for RR. It does not by
itself enumerate every zero of lambda,nu or the registered denominator
polynomials on the proper source curve. The original request for actual zero
and pole divisors remains in force.

A complete principal-divisor calculation must also compute the exact field
norms/resultants of those functions,identify any additional base zero supports,
and resolve their full source fibers with multiplicity and degree checks.
No statement here proves that there are no further ordinary zero supports.
Existing ordinary460/367 fibers/images and collector-divisor obligations are
not removed by the SS5/Z5 calculation. A pole-containment argument for an RR
space is a different claim from a complete zero-and-pole divisor table.

The proposed next protocol should therefore record both:the five rational
polar charts,and exact norm/zero-support accounting for the remaining functions.
Only a complete L(D) or certified dimension may replace the old ansatz spaces.
The final map still requires source identities,F2 descent and nonzero order11
subgroup action. This clarification adds no new native trial or IC cost claim.
