# Normalize the two sixteen-dimensional u=0 components

Start from the retained R50 finite-`u` equation, not its chart named
infinity. Complete the local integral closure over `F2[[u]]` on each
of the `v=0` and `v=1` components. Compute a certified local basis,
the reduction algebra, all primitive idempotents, residue degrees and
ramification indices; require the weighted `e*f` sums to be 16 on
each quadratic place. Rebind any normalized basis to the full source
function field and verify its multiplication, not just a plane
factorization.

Use the resulting branches to distribute R77's `b,c` orders and
compute `lambda,nu` and registered-denominator valuations and leading
residues. Separately lift R75 finite uniformizers. Then assemble the
complete proper divisors, prescribed Riemann--Roch space, candidate
level-77 correspondence, descent compatibility and order-11 action.
