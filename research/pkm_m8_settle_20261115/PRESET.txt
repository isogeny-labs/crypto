B (PC-degree value, secondary but frozen here): D_PC(cell) = min c such that 1 is in V_c, where V_c is the
  smallest subspace of F_p[x]_{<=c} containing the generators and closed under (w in V_c, deg w <= c-1 =>
  x_i w in V_c) (HKY closure; order-independent).
  Upper bound U: 1 in V_c if (i) msolve finishes with max round degree c, or (ii) Singular std with
  degBound = c returns 1 (every polynomial formed has degree <= c under dp, so the refutation lies in V_c).
  Lower bound L: 1 not in V_c certified by I3 (a)+(b)+(c) on G = std(I) at degBound = c: if all pairs with
  deg lcm <= c reduce to 0 and all generators reduce to 0, span{t*g : deg <= c} = V_c and has leading
  monomials <LT(G)>_{<=c}; then 1 in V_c iff 1 in G. The certificate value is the rank deficit
  delta_c = dim F_p[x]_{<=c} - dim V_c = #standard monomials of degree <= c (delta_c > 0 iff 1 not in V_c).
  Supporting, not certifying: msolve -v 2 trace enters a round of degree c+1 before any 1 is found.
  D_PC is a value when L = U - 1 certificate holds (1 not in V_{U-1} and 1 in V_U).
