No concrete composition error found in inspected source. This review does not compile or rerun the assembled source operation.

The new worker consumes the authenticated OriginalOwner into_boolean_search and then calls publish_capture before transferring custody. Its early admit, map-build and map-publication failures retain the original owner/sink via worker::Custody and the dedicated boolean_search_report_unavailable flag. Successful returned owner custody also retains that flag and returns a null public outcome. No completed capture or signed candidate is promoted into a full solve report.

The adapter's real pre-Matrix checkpoint is after normalize and before finish_native/begin_native(true). It has original checked expansion and equations available while Construction remains active and original reserves can still succeed. If checkpoint admission fails, the adapter returns with the failed publication and original stop retained. Mid-continuation state is not automatically checkpointed. Final publication does not create fresh allowance for a stopped owner.

The encoder's disjoint with_parts lends original Budget and CaptureEmitter separately from immutable evidence fields, excluding Publication; the bridge temporarily moves Publication and restores it before consuming ordinary errors. The encoder consumes that accessor for retained diagnostic state; normalized equations remain derived from the same authenticated expansion.
