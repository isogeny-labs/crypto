\noindent\textbf{Linked subgroup inventory.} The 700 linked EC1
representations comprise 141 binary degree-three triples and 559
odd-characteristic degree-two triples. The binary profile records bounded
generator-level typed identities; the odd-characteristic profile does not
supply compatible induced maps.

\noindent\textbf{Provenance and verification.} The catalog adds 13,219
prime-field models relative to the previous 2,771-row inventory. The dated
registry audit records source bindings, order certificates, and their stated
limits. At the catalog commit,
\texttt{curve\_cover\_check --check} returned
\texttt{verified=15990}, \texttt{invalid\_input=0}, and
\texttt{unsupported=0}. The generated assessment is byte-exact under
\texttt{build\_cover\_transfer\_assessments.mjs --check}; the independent
\texttt{verify\_assessment.mjs} reconstructs the model, map, cover, field,
and Jacobian joins, replays the declared cover equations, and SHA-binds this
report and its figures.

\noindent\textbf{Boundary.} The 186 binary maps have bounded typed
point-class operations. The 15,804 odd-characteristic maps have cover geometry
without a compatible balanced two-infinity Jacobian transfer. No complete
catalog transfer pipeline, discrete-log cost improvement, or wall-speed result
is established. The conditional Lean group lemma does not import this catalog.
The older packaged release replay covers a smaller bundled registry and is not
a replay of all 15,990 current inputs.
