Simple-root series lifting and coprime Hensel factor lifting are established techniques. Previously reviewed primary abstracts: Neiger,Rosenkilde,Schost2017 https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.10658 DOI10.1145/3087604.3087642 and Roth-Ruckenstein2000 DOI10.1109/18.817522. No fresh full-text novelty review or general novelty certification is claimed. The proposed unknown-affine-coefficient-family application still needs independently tested correctness, measured total search costs and a broader primary-source review. A given-polynomial lifting bound does not solve coefficient search.
