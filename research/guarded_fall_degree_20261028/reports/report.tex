\begin{center}
{\Large Guarded summation-polynomial fall degrees}\par
{\small Study report, 28--29 October 2026. Derived bounds and development checks; no performance claim.}
\end{center}

Let $S$ be $B$ distinct rational affine-point abscissas, $g(T)=\prod_{s\in S}(T-s)$,
and $r=x(R)$ for an affine rational target. The ordinary polynomial-ring inputs are
\[
g(x),\quad g(y),\quad g(z),\quad S_3(x,y,u),\quad S_3(u,z,r),\quad v(x-y)-1.
\]
Both point signs are allowed. The guard requires distinct first abscissas.
Every degree below is ordinary total degree with explicit membership equations.
The first-fall convention is homogeneous-top syzygies modulo Koszul syzygies.

The binary curve is $Y^2+XY=X^3+a_2X^2+a_6$, $a_6\ne0$.
Its cube-orbit exception requires even extension degree, so the pair is $(4,5)$
for all three-abscissa unsatisfiable instances over $\mathbb F_{2^n}$ with odd $n$.
The growing lower bound depends on the degree-$B$ membership input and gives
no lower bound in the variable count of a fixed-degree Boolean encoding.

\textbf{Direct Boolean upper bound.} For the binary two-$S_3$ chain with one
shared $u$, three linear $\ell$-bit base embeddings, and every Boolean bit
equation explicit, $d_F\le3\ell+1$ for $\ell\ge1$, independently of $n$.
This holds with or without the inverse guard, without rational-lift assumptions.
Fixing the base bits leaves an affine auxiliary system; staged assignment
indicators localize its contradiction. Proof size may be exponential.

\textbf{Method.} Staged multiplication and cubic reduction normalize two
quadratic fibres, then derive their scalar resultant within degree five.
Finite-grid inversion yields $1$. Characteristic two uses a Frobenius identity
instead of division by two. Closed separating superspaces give the lower bounds.
An exact triple-selector partition lifts local proofs to growing cardinality.

\textbf{Verification boundary.} Native sparse replay checks the compact historical
proof DAGs against separately supplied inputs. Native binary-field arithmetic and
the pinned cube-orbit fixture have development unit checks; no new field/base/target
sweep or timing measurement was run. Lean checks only the declarations and
hypotheses listed in \texttt{formal/PROOF\_MAP.json}. Ordinary closure and the
exact prime-field guarded three-abscissa pair $(4,5)$ are now formalized,
including the degree-five construction, rational-root/unit-ideal bridge,
uniform lower obstruction and graded top-syzygy/Koszul definition.

\textbf{Prior work.} The checked sources are Huang--Kosters--Yang--Yeo,
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.02532}{1505.02532}; Caminata--Gorla,
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.05579}{2112.05579}; Semaev,
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.01175}{1504.01175}; and Kosters--Yeo,
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.08001}{1503.08001}. Assumptions and repository

\begin{center}
{\Large Prime-field bit restrictions: a size-independent upper bound}
\end{center}

Let $p>3$, let the curve be nonsingular, and use three blocks of $\ell\ge1$
Boolean-constrained base variables. Each coordinate lookup has degree at most
$\ell$ and takes every Boolean assignment to a rational point abscissa.
Encode $u$ and optional $v$ by independent $n$-bit integer maps onto
$\mathbb F_p$, with $2^n\ge p$. The coefficient field remains $\mathbb F_p$.
Include every bit equation $W^2-W$ and supply the multilinear normal forms of
the two summation equations and optional inverse guard.

\[
\boxed{\text{UNSAT}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad d_F\le3\ell+2.}
\]

The cap is independent of the modulus size and of $n$. It depends on the
exact generating presentation; it is not a theorem for a $\mathbb F_2$
carry-circuit implementation of prime-field arithmetic.

\textbf{Degree accounting.} Put $L=3\ell$. Each normalized equation has
base support $s_i\le2\ell$ and auxiliary degree at most two. Multiply by
assignment-indicator factors on its support, reducing Boolean powers after
each step. Every reduced row has degree at most $s_i+2$; a next factor costs
at most $s_i+3\le L+2$. The complementary factors then give the fully
localized equation within $L+2$.

After fixing the base assignment, rational point lifts make the first
quadratic's roots rational. The equal-abscissa case is linear or a nonzero
constant; an active inverse guard excludes it directly. Unsatisfiability
therefore gives coprime univariate polynomials of degree at most two.
Their constant refutation needs degree at most two: a monic difference gives
$u-\alpha$, and subtracting its linear multiples from the quadratic leaves
its nonzero value at $\alpha$. Carrying the assignment indicator throughout
costs at most $L+2$. Summing all indicators gives the exact identity $1$.

\textbf{Raw inputs.} If the supplied substitutions have maximum degree
$d_{\rm raw}$, normalizing them first gives
\[
d_F\le\max(3\ell+2,d_{\rm raw}).
\]
Arbitrary multilinear lookups have $d_{\rm raw}\le4\ell$; affine lookups
have $d_{\rm raw}\le4$ and retain the $3\ell+2$ bound.

\textbf{Limits.} Lookup tables and the proof may have exponentially many
terms or rows. No first-fall equality, growing lower bound, exact degree,
runtime gain, general ceiling five, or E12 certification follows.
Eight local algebraic dependencies pass Lean build and fresh kernel replay,
including the explicit discriminant/diagonal branch and quadratic cancellation.
The complete filtered-closure theorem is not Lean formalized.
The full statement and all edge cases are in
\texttt{extensions/large\_curves/prime\_bit\_restricted\_upper.txt}.
