## 4. Reading (*derived* from the table; read with PROTOCOL.md section 2)

1. **No specialised run needed degree >= 8, so by the pre-registered rule none counts as H0 lower-bound evidence.**
   The largest specialised degree is 6 (M1, M2 at N = 21 and 24). Specialised runs finishing at <= 7 say nothing
   about H1 and nothing about the unspecialised `D`.
2. **Controls.** At N = 15 (D = 7) the specialisations lower the degree by 2 (H1, M1, M2: 7 -> 5) and by 3 (H2: 7 -> 4);
   the choice of root of unity and of fixed level (head vs mid) changed nothing. Msolve 0.9.5 reproduces the
   recorded unspecialised N = 15 answer (`[-1]`, max degree 7), so the instrument agrees with 0.6.5 there.
3. **Within the specialised families the degree is not flat in N for the M arms:** 5 (N = 15) -> 6 (N = 21) -> 6
   (N = 24) for M1/M2, while H1 stays 5 and H2 stays 4. That is growth between N = 15 and N = 21 in the family that
   keeps coupled free chain levels, then a plateau at 6 through N = 24 (one target at N = 24, M2 only). It is a
   statement about the specialised ideals; no inference to I (frozen rule, PROTOCOL.md section 6).
4. **Unit-ideal requirement.** The unit-ideal status of the two N = 21 targets is *not established*: target 0 had
   degree 7 exhausted without 1 in the basis (A1 run), target 1 stopped by memory inside degree 7. Even a >= 8
   specialised result would have been conditional on that. Specialised ideals all being the unit ideal does not
   transfer to the originals.
5. **Memory.** Specialisation made N = 21 cheap (<= 1.6 GiB, M1 1,287 CPU-s) where the unspecialised system exceeds
   16 GiB inside degree 7 (target 1) and 99.6 GiB in degree 8 (target 0, A1). The tested specialisations remove
   too much to approach the degree-8 regime; 12-16 GiB is enough for them and not enough for the unspecialised system.
6. **Verdict.** H0 lower-bound evidence from this study: **none at >= 8**. The only movement is a +1 in the
   specialised M family from N = 15 to N = 21 (5 -> 6), with a plateau to N = 24. H1 is not supported or refuted.
   The existing H0 evidence remains the A1 target-0 trace (degree 7 exhausted without 1), still lacking unit-ideal
   confirmation. What would discriminate next: a specialisation that keeps more levels free (fix a deeper
   chain variable at larger t, so the drop at N = 15 is 1 rather than 2), calibrated against N = 15 and the m = 4
   cell; the m = 4 M1/M2/H1 controls need a finished run (they were interrupted).
