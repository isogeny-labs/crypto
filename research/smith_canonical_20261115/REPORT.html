What was built

psi(t; b) = T(t) (b0 : b1 : b2) with the symmetric matrix T_ik = [x^2]( x^(i+k) D adj(F mod G) mod G ) in F_p[t]. The three coordinates are the traces along Smith’s correspondence R of the differentials x^i dx / y of H (derivation in the module doc). No singularity analysis of the plane model and no Riemann-Roch computation is used. C is the unique quartic through the images of the points of X over U. Psi to Pic^0(C) uses plane_quartic_pic unchanged; the class of a conjugate pair is built from the F_p-conics through the pair and the base point.

This is not the route suggested in the brief (adjoints of Phi(t, rho) = 0). The plane model is still supported as an input (psi_t_rho), by lifting (t, rho) to X.

Reading the columns:

  • “DLP verified”: Psi([k]D) = [k]Psi(D) with [k]D computed on H and moved to C independently of D, and the BSGS logarithm on C equal to k modulo ord Psi(D). “k recovered exactly”: the logarithm equals k itself. The four curves of cell A where it does not are those where ord Psi(D) = ord D / 2: the transfer loses the 2-part that lies in the kernel, as it must. Orders of Psi(D) ranged 832 to 32202 (A), 43176 to 576044 (B), 138376 to 5032808 (C).
  • “2-torsion”: every nonzero rational 2-torsion class (u, 0) of J_H. 112 classes in S all map to 0; 134 classes outside S all map to a nonzero class. Membership in S is decided from N, D by polynomial arithmetic, without psi.
  • The order ratio is 1 or 2 on all 500 classes, never 4 or 8, as it must be for a kernel of exponent 2.

Checks that did not run

  • Cells D, E (deg F = 8): checks 7-9 are not implemented (no J_H arithmetic for the even-degree model). Stated in the protocol.
  • Relations: on 1 curve of cell A (i = 19) and 7 curves of cell D no pair of split functions was found within the search budget (few usable points at p = 37; the 0/0 rows). Those curves have no relation evidence. For the cell A curve the class checks 7-9 ran and passed; the 7 cell D curves have no evidence for C2 at all; only C1 is supported there.
  • Where fewer than 10 relation pairs are listed for a curve, the two functions drawn were identical and the pair was skipped.
  • Cell C: #C(F_p^3) not counted (protocol).

Proven by code, and assumed

Checked by exact computation on every curve of the table:

  • the three coordinate functions are linearly independent on X, the image lies on no conic or cubic and on exactly one quartic, which is smooth;
  • that quartic has the point counts of H over the listed fields (so, with k = 1, 2, 3, the same zeta function);
  • for deg F = 7: Psi is well defined on the tested classes (independent representations agree), additive, annihilated by #J_H(F_p), kills the rational part of S and no other rational 2-torsion, and carries a planted logarithm.

Follows from those by a standard argument, not separately computed: psi is birational onto C (a non-constant map from a genus-3 curve to a smooth plane quartic not through a conic has degree 1), hence C is the canonical model of X.

Assumed, not checked:

  • Smith’s theorem that R induces an isogeny J_H -> J_X with kernel S (used to derive T; only its consequences above are tested);
  • that the fitted quartic equals the canonical image at the points of X not evaluated: the fibres over t = infinity and over the t outside U. The zeta match covers them in aggregate only;
  • the kernel beyond rational 2-torsion: classes of S that are rational as a set but whose elements are not rational are not tested one by one;
  • correctness of plane_quartic_pic (its own study) and of smith_trigonal::image_points;
  • nothing is claimed for primes beyond 211.
