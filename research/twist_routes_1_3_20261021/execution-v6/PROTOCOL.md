# Twist generator order and finite prefixes: pre-data protocol

Follow the user's generator/progression suggestion. The completed full-universe
overlap census does not explain underfilled bases from a chosen twist orbit.
Test a precise target-blind constructor change; no scalar map is assumed.

Use all ten original fixtures and all 60 archived twist-orbit constructions
from execution-v4/runs/geometry-v1/ROWS.json. Native arithmetic must reproduce
the original first point and all 60 complete old bases exactly. Select a second
seed as the lexicographically first x, with smaller nonzero y, having the full
known twist order. Scan deterministically, without target/shift/B/yield input.
Fail explicitly if no full-order seed exists; do not substitute another rule.

For both seeds, all shifts 0,1,3 and B8/B16 give exactly 120 constructions.
Keep every underfilled set; the 40 zero-shift constructions must remain empty.
Record source/twist cardinalities, original source generator order, both twist
seed orders, gcd of rational group orders, x trials/order tests in seed discovery,
all sorted coordinates and attempted terms. Full discovery, cardinality checks
and construction occur in the actual native process. These counters are not a
complete primitive-cost unit, measured time, solver work or GAE.

Independent PARI recomputes both cardinalities, the source generator order,
canonical first/full-order seed selection, both seed orders and every complete
base. Require exact equality, actual exit/output hashes and clean GP diagnostics.
The known source generator and a separate p7 unit case are positive controls;
all zero-shift sets and a nonmultiple-order unit failure are negative controls.
They are embedded checks, not fabricated separate execution arms.

The native producer and independent GP replay are two real complete-panel runs
on one common panel instance. No time comparison or statistical inference. The
primary result is the exact construction/order census; different seeds yield
different answer sets and cannot establish same-output solving speedups. Choosing
a full-order seed is standard group theory, not a novelty claim. Coprime group
orders supply no proposed source-to-rational-twist scalar transport.

Literature first: reviewed the completed predecessor reports, the atlas's
generic-table and structured-base coincidence leads, existing twist field/group
implementation and public constructor source, and local primary PARI help for
ellorder/ellcard. ellorder's optional order argument is a multiple, not evidence
that a point generates the whole group. Both actual cardinalities and actual
point orders are independently checked. No broader literature novelty audit
or new universal group theorem is claimed.

Freeze source/binaries/input hashes, protocol and analysis before panel data.

no paid resource, funding or IAM change. Push Setec checkpoint/gate before run.
