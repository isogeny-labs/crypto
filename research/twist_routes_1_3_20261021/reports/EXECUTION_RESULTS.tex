\section{Bounded execution of twist routes
1--3}\label{bounded-execution-of-twist-routes-13}

Runs: 2026-10-21--22. PR:
\href{https://github.com/aburan28/crypto/pull/2380}{\#2380}.

The bounded panels produced executable trace, cover, divisor-class,
equivalent-system, isogeny and rational-lift checks. The combined native
IC panel recorded \textbf{1,920 executions: 1,704 verified logarithms
and 216 empty-base errors}. All 1,704 verified records reproduced
exactly in the native audit. \textbf{A calibrated IC speedup, a novel
algorithm and a NIST-size benefit are not established.}

The original eighteen-experiment program remains broader than these
executions. Three-gluing construction, complete higher-degree divisor
factorization, broader solver grids, full accounting/calibration and
independent-host replay remain open. These are explicit partial results,
not a completed full campaign.

\subsection{Inputs and execution}\label{inputs-and-execution}

The \href{../execution-v1/fixtures.json}{frozen manifest} contains two
structural models, eight public-seeded A=-3 ordinary prime-order ladder
models at field sizes 8, 10, 12 and 14 bits, and three even/composite
controls. It records exact orders, generators, quadratic/cubic/sextic
fields, tower conversions and rejected ladder candidates. Its SHA-256 is
\texttt{8d490a36d65ebf906b93e04eeeb21ac5802f2a9382ef03271a4648fd4b78a4ed}.
The canonical Rust identity constructor independently checked all 67
manifest models, including existing entries, against the registry and
alias map. The registry gained 65 models; 34 of them are extension
models.

All experiments used native Rust or native PARI/GP, Singular and msolve
on the same Debian 13 x86\_64 managed host. The host has a four-CPU
quota and 16 GiB memory limit. Individual CAS limits and source/input
freezes are in the versioned protocols and
\href{../execution-v1/}{execution receipts}. Runs are L0. No wall-clock
comparison is admitted. NIST P-192/P-224/P-256/P-384/P-521 appear only
in public-parameter applicability checks; no NIST DLP was executed.

\subsection{Recorded results}\label{recorded-results}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Trace and rational-only controls:} nine exhaustive
  quadratic-extension censuses and 30 exact quartic/extension
  witness-set comparisons passed. For \texttt{icv1-fp6-tm7-fc20da94},
  the 3,551 extension points give 67 trace images, with 53 lifts per
  trace. Repeated lifts do not become independent rows. There are 1,179
  configured relation-census rows: 678 verified and 501 unsupported
  empty/undersized bases; identical bases are explicitly reused.
\item
  \textbf{Covers and transfer:} exhaustive search of 27,140 translate
  parameters on ten fixtures found 11 actual twist complements, with at
  least one per fixture. Matching j or opposite trace alone was
  rejected. All ten selected covers passed executable pullback/norm
  tests, including all 3,551 rational classes on the smallest Jacobian
  and 256 sampled classes on each larger fixture: 5,855 classes total,
  plus 1,280 additivity/associativity samples. PARI independently
  reproduced all ten characteristic polynomials.
\item
  \textbf{Three-gluing module filter:} a separately frozen PARI
  calculation on the public orders reproduces all ten toy E11 counts. Of
  the five NIST P-curves, only P-521 passes, with four
  determinant-minus-one anti-intertwiners over F3. P-192, P-224, P-256
  and P-384 have zero. This necessary companion-module test supplies a
  construction candidate; the actual torsion/pairing bridge, polarized
  quotient, genus-two curve and divisor maps remain open. See the
  \href{../execution-v1/runs/public3-20261022T0132Z/pari.stdout}{raw
  result} and
  \href{execution-20261022T0032Z/public3-20261022T0132Z/REPORT.pdf}{report}.
\item
  \textbf{Low-degree isogenies:} of 30 degree/fixture cells, three
  degree-seven maps were constructed and 27 cells failed the stated
  applicability filters. The first run supplied two algebraic duals. A
  separately frozen amendment also samples independent torsion with a
  repeated eigenvalue and requires a nonzero forward image. The new run
  supplies all three explicit duals; composition {[}7{]} is checked on
  every rational source point, with 24 scalar return controls. The
  finite lookup inverses remain separate toy controls.
\item
  \textbf{Rational lifts:} 254 standard-model-height searches found
  points in 25 cases and three-term relations in five. The separate
  equal-original-height control found points in 76 cases and triples in
  seven. Square-parameter reductions and every projected relation passed
  the identical-base ordinary group census. No projected row lies
  outside that census. One rank interval remains unresolved; these are
  not certified full Mordell-Weil bases.
\item
  \textbf{Conditional pairing control:} a separate registered
  supersingular model with r=11 and embedding degree two passed ten
  native Miller/bilinearity and independent PARI/source-log checks. This
  is a deliberately known-scalar positive control, with no ordinary/NIST
  transfer or speed claim.
\end{itemize}

These equivalence checks validate the implementations and remove
duplicate or incorrect projection explanations. They do not measure
whether an auxiliary family could find the same useful rational
relations more cheaply.

\subsection{Formal checks and
validation}\label{formal-checks-and-validation}

The \href{../lean/README.md}{proof map} pins Lean 4.34.1 and Mathlib.
Five exact cleared polynomial identities and four conditional
trace/eigenspace statements on Mathlib's actual Weierstrass point group
build successfully. The repository auditor checks 13 declarations,
including four generated defining equations: zero failures, zero
compiler-trusted proofs, standard foundations only.

The concrete finite-field Frobenius/twist embedding, odd subgroup,
field/tower/chart/Jacobian maps and cost/complexity bridges remain
formally open. The GP characteristic-polynomial check does not
independently replay arbitrary-divisor norm code. None of these gaps is
replaced with an axiom.

The final root library suite passed \textbf{4,479 tests}, with zero
failures and 119 existing ignored tests, including the new
scalar-Frobenius regression. The standalone suite passed 128 supporting
tests; the repeated-eigenvalue amendment additionally executes
every-point dual and scalar-return checks. Earlier sparse-fixture/socket
failures, the stale 1,324-model roster assertion and invalid
CAS/printing attempts are retained. The roster now expects 1,358
extension models, preserving every per-model field/subgroup check. The
three backlog cards validate and remain blocked for the full campaign's
unmet criteria. Conductor was unavailable locally.
