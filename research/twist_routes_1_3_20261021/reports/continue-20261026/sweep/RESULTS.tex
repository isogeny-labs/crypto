managed Linux x86\_64, L0. Public synthetic/known-answer fixtures only.
No admitted wall time.

\subsection{Frozen task and
accounting}\label{frozen-task-and-accounting}

All ten original curves; B=8,12,16; arities 2 and 3; public target label
0. Sixty correlated configurations and ten distinct fixture-target
pairs. Eight worker arms per configuration, 480 requested processes.

Startup, parsing, symbolic/F4 work, all branches and serialization are
charged. Construction, roots/signs, exceptions, certification, relation
algebra and full recovery are excluded. Each worker has a 10,000 ms
cooperative budget, 12-second hard timeout plus one-second grace, 4 GiB
and 32 MiB/file. The batch cap is 7,200 seconds and 1 GiB.

\subsection{Validation, limits and raw
preservation}\label{validation-limits-and-raw-preservation}

The 104 incomplete cells are retained, including cooperative/hard-budget
and incomplete-output attempts. Their raw exit, signal, solver status
and logs remain in RECORDS.json and the archive. Missing pairs are not
zero-cost successes. The 50 completed direct comparisons are correlated
settings of ten target pairs, not fifty independent targets.

This basis-worker interval matches complete branch outputs and counters
to the earlier independent Singular complete-basis certificates. It has
no new full-root or PARI witness replay.

Measured instructions are simulated guest instructions Ir, not CPU
cycles, seconds, field operations or GAE. Ratios are native-audited
candidate/control values. Counts repeat correlated configurations; A/A
repeats check only the frozen five-percent within-input gate. No
confidence interval or independent-host replication is claimed. Missing
pairs stay missing. No matched rho or validation-gated benchmark row is
added.

Raw bytes, failed/capped cells and loose replay metadata are preserved
by \href{../../../execution-v3/continue-20261026/RAW_ARTIFACTS.json}{the
deterministic archive manifest}. The user-selected remote is
\textbf{s3://setec-astronomy-artifacts/store}, logical name is recorded
in the manifest.
