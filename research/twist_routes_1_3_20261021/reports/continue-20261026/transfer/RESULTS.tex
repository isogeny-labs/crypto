\section{Given-map genus-two transfer
cost}\label{given-map-genus-two-transfer-cost}

Run \textbf{transfer-cost-20261026-v1}, 2026-10-26. Intel Xeon Platinum
8573C, managed Linux x86\_64, L0. Public synthetic/known-answer fixtures
only. No admitted wall time.

\subsection{Frozen task and
accounting}\label{frozen-task-and-accounting}

The existing 24 public toy targets: eight each on
icv1-fp8-tm21-71e3bcb7, icv1-fp10-tm21-b87c79a9 and
icv1-fp14-t33-d98086ae, subgroup orders 233,773,10079. Four arms per
target: source/transfer BSGS and their A/A repeats; 96 requested
processes.

Both groups use the same ceil-sqrt(n) BSGS. Transfer includes reference
chart setup, pulling both G and Q, subgroup checks, Jacobian BSGS,
norm/inverse-three recovery and source verification. Map/parameter
discovery, polarization identification and destination IC are excluded.
Limits: 60 seconds/process, 4 GiB, 32 MiB/file; 2,400 seconds/1 GiB
globally.

\subsection{Validation, limits and raw
preservation}\label{validation-limits-and-raw-preservation}

The reference uses Fp6 chart arithmetic, often in an Fp3 subfield,
rather than optimized balanced Fp Jacobian arithmetic. This experiment
does not rule out another destination algorithm, optimized arithmetic or
another cover, and is not the strongest matched-rho comparison.

Independent PARI positive and negative checks each: \textbf{96}. The
native gate reopens script, stdout, stderr and actual-exit hashes; only
the documented maximum-stack warning is allowed.

Measured instructions are simulated guest instructions Ir, not CPU
cycles, seconds, field operations or GAE. Ratios are native-audited
candidate/control values. Counts repeat correlated configurations; A/A
repeats check only the frozen five-percent within-input gate. No
confidence interval or independent-host replication is claimed. Missing
pairs stay missing. No matched rho or validation-gated benchmark row is
added.

Raw bytes, failed/capped cells and loose replay metadata are preserved
by \href{../../../execution-v3/continue-20261026/RAW_ARTIFACTS.json}{the
deterministic archive manifest}. The user-selected remote is
\textbf{s3://setec-astronomy-artifacts/store}, logical name is recorded
in the manifest.
