# Independent given-map and decomposition interval checks

The separate native auditor reopens all plan cells in order, task and output
hashes, raw Ir profiles, frozen expected-output records and the fresh PARI
script/output/exit receipt. A worker does not receive its expected answer.
For decomposition require every branch's complete root verdict, exact full
projected tuple equality, complete signed-witness coverage and matching base
and target. For transfer require the old independently checked scalar and
target, and explicit norm/inverse-three recovery for transferred arms.

Count all positive and wrong-sign/wrong-scalar PARI checks independently and
require exact marker coverage. Reject nonzero exits, changed hashes and real
stderr errors; retain only the already documented maximum-stack-size warning.
Keep failed/incomplete outputs and their raw partial instruction totals, but
exclude them from completed costs. Recompute direct/source A/A gates, plus
the transferred A/A gate, before comparisons. Report every missing pair,
descriptive medians/ranges, configuration counts and distinct target counts.
These are exact measured worker intervals, not complete IC or fastest-tool
results. No rank denominator, calibrated GAE or independent-host claim.
