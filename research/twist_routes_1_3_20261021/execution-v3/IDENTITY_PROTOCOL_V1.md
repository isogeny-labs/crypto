# Classical F3 identity certificate

Freeze the identity-runner source before issuing one identity.certificate/v1
for the unconditional integer-polynomial numerator-square identity in five
variables x,y,z,a,b, total degree at most six. Use the existing native issuer
with 32 reproducible probes and the native checker with 64 extra domain-separated
probes over 2^61-1. Attach the SHA-256 of lean-v3/TwistPairLift.lean. Reject a
deliberately false right-hand side plus one. This certificate supplements the
exact Lean proof; randomized evaluation alone is not a universal proof.

Use one process, 4 GiB address space and 60 seconds, capture exit, stdout,
stderr, source and binary/protocol hashes. No performance or novelty claim.
Prior four certificates and their statements/receipts remain unchanged.
