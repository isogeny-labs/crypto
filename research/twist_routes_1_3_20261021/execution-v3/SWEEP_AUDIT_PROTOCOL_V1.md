Freeze the separate Rust auditor before running its aggregation. It reopens
every planned cell and enforces all 480 records in order. It hashes capsules,
raw profiles, outputs and stdout/stderr independently, recomputes Ir from the
single-event raw footer, checks every payload against the frozen catalog and
reopens earlier native inputs/results and complete Singular certificate
scripts/stdout/stderr. An old accepted flag alone is insufficient: hashes,
positive marker, clean errors and expected vdim must agree.

Check all branch outputs for exact basis and row-counter equality, source
job IDs, missing branches, timeouts and degree truncation. Recompute acceptance
and primary aggregate counts. Missing/failed cells stay outside admitted costs.
The two raw-event parser tests reject absent totals and multi-event traces.

For each configuration independently recompute direct A/A spread and enforce
the frozen 5-percent gate. Report every candidate/control pair, its missingness
and exact candidate/control ratio; group medians and ranges are descriptive
statistics of correlated training configurations, not independent-target
confidence intervals. Report all ten target pairs separately. No ranks are
used as affine-solver denominators. This does not independently rerun the
complete mathematical computation or supply an independent-host result.
