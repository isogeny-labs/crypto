# Next discriminating experiments after v14

V14 passes the exact finite relation gate. It does not meet the complete-cost
or scale gates. These are concrete follow-ups, not frozen/runnable protocols,
leases, schedules or new scientific results. Existing original cards retain
their full acceptance criteria and zero-funding status.

## 1. Complete cold cost, with useful target recovery as the output

Continue [maximal-seed cold cost](../../../backlog/XP-20261027-twist-maximal-seed-cold-cost/README.md)
and the [complete-cost card](../../../backlog/XP-20261022-twist-complete-cost-accounting/README.md).
Retain all ten original fixtures, B8/B16, shifts1/3 and public development
target labels; do not substitute the favorable p827 examples for that panel.

Implement native counters and the independent verifier before freezing a new
protocol. The interval starts with curve/twist inputs, not supplied discovered
seeds or maps. Charge cardinality/order/seed and torsion/map discovery; rejected
candidates; lifting, map construction/evaluation, signs and exceptions; complete
query collection and deduplication; matrix construction/elimination and original
source point verification. Record cold one-target totals first. Any reusable
map/catalog setup must also appear in an explicit amortized workload.

Include ordinary low-x, curve progression and target-blind random controls;
first/maximal twist seeds; matched alpha augmentation and equations where a
certified map exists. Preserve unsupported maps and underfills instead of
replacing their inputs. Give every ordinary arm the same known scalar edges.
Do not attribute their rank to sampled query information.

Same-base group MITM and full-root solvers must return the identical complete
witness equations. Different-base arms must finish the same original public DLP;
more witnesses or smaller algebraic degree is insufficient. Include strongest
available external tools, matched rho and applicable accounting bands under the
repository gates. Price a direct generator/scalar-action implementation as well
as rational-map evaluation: the map must not get an artificial implementation
advantage over the same known endomorphism used by the baseline.

Freeze source, binaries, inputs, caps, counters/units, interleaving and controls
before any new outputs, then checkpoint to Setec. Preserve failures/caps and
validate every scalar on the original curve. A claim requires a complete admitted
cost interval below the best frozen verified IC baseline. A p827 diagnostic
may size the instrument but cannot discharge the ten-fixture card.

## 2. Compress known alpha pairs before solving

As a separately declared ablation of the cost panel, compare the exact same
equations represented with both variables and known alpha-edge constraints
versus substituting the known edge multipliers before elimination. Both must
have identical target fibers, including ambiguity witnesses. Retain cycles,
canonical-sign collisions, noninvertible edge cases and disconnected components.
Measure all preprocessing and reconstruction; give ordinary bases the identical
compression. This tests an engineering benefit of using known information,
not extra relations or a new mathematical attack.

## 3. Larger held-out toys after the cost instrument is fixed

Freeze curve identities, generator-bound targets, candidate bases and an allowed
public-feature selection rule before collecting their outcomes. Include several
sizes and independent curves at each size, ordinary controls and unsupported
cases. A cheap map available on p827 must be independently constructed and
priced on each new curve; it cannot be assumed for NIST curves. Separate
structure-specific families from broad applicability.

Keep the original [E28 final labels32–63](../../../backlog/XP-20261022-twist-heldout-selection/README.md)
unopened until its complete-counter/selector prerequisites are satisfied.
V14 supplies neither those counters nor a frozen selector. Evaluate every
planned held-out case once, with no outcome-dependent refill or replacement.
Require useful rank/target recovery and complete one-target cost, not relation
counts alone. Report finite scale evidence and independent-host replay with
their actual scope; do not extrapolate toy behavior to NIST security.
