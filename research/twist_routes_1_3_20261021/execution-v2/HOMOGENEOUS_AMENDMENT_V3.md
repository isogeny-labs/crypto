batch finish. Large homogenized bases exceed that checker stage's 30-second

Let G=std(I) be Singular's independently computed complete reference basis.
Verify B⊂I by reduction against G, and I⊂(B) by zero division remainders.
Then compare the two monomial ideals generated by lead(B) and lead(G), in
both directions. Monomial generating sets are themselves Gröbner bases, so
these reductions certify equality of leading ideals. Together these checks
certify B is a complete Gröbner basis of I; this is stronger than equality
of generated ideals alone. Record this as an independent leading-ideal
certificate, never as an all-S-pair replay. Missing reference completion,
parser errors, resource caps or any failed inclusion/equality remains
unaccepted. Re-run the small positive/truncated/wrong-field controls under
the same alternative gate. Lex signature orders remain unsupported.
