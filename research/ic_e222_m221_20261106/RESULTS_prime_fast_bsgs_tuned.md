# Tuned multi-target BSGS as the reference arm for multi-target IC

Status: [pcc:unverified] producer's own measurement; same machine, 1 thread, wall clock, toy sizes; the tuned arm was run after the frozen IC evaluation (not part of that freeze), on the identical planted targets (same seeds, curves and instances).

Change: `examples/prime_fast_bsgs_multi.rs` gained a `tuned` mode: baby steps are generated by batched additions over 1024 lanes (one inversion per round) and stored in an open-addressing table (`Vec<(x, j)>`, linear probing), replacing the per-step inversion and `HashMap`. Giant steps are unchanged (all T targets stepped together with one batched inversion). Every one of the 1000 targets of each run is verified `k G = Q_t`.

Results (T = 1000; rows are the 14 instance-rows of the frozen evaluation; medians of per-row ratios; ranges in brackets):

| bits | rows | tuned BSGS ms | untuned / tuned | table2 IC / tuned BSGS | sweep3 IC / tuned BSGS |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 | 4 | 14 to 21 | 2.1 | 5.7 (4.6 to 8.8) | 9.4 |
| 27 | 4 | 22 to 35 | 2.6 | 10.0 (7.2 to 11.9) | 21.8 |
| 29 | 4 | 42 to 64 | 3.3 | 11.7 (9.3 to 13.2) | 19.4 |
| 32 | 2 | 177 to 198 | 2.7 | 13.4 (11.7 to 15.0) | 21.5 |

This confirms the validator's probe (untuned/tuned 2.6x to 3.9x there; 2.1x to 3.3x here) and replaces "2.9x to 5.0x behind BSGS" with the figures above: **the best multi-target IC variant (table collector) is 4.6x to 15x slower than a tuned multi-target BSGS at 25 to 32 bits, and the gap grows with size.** The improvements of the multi-target IC this campaign (134x to 570x over repeated single-target `ic_solve`, then 1.5x to 2.8x for the table collector) are real but do not approach the best multi-target attack at these sizes.

Limits: the tuned BSGS has not been separately validated (the validator confirmed the untuned arm and measured a tuned probe of its own); not frozen before the data; 1 thread; T = 1000 only; no §5a band and no memory pricing (table arm 169 MB, tuned BSGS about 36 MB at 32 bits); not independently replayed.
