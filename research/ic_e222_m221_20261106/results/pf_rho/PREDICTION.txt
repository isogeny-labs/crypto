Multi-target distinguished-point rho on the native engine (examples/prime_fast_rho_multi.rs): r-adding walks (32 steps, shared table of multiples of G), start points u*G + Q_t, DP collisions between walks of different targets link the logs (k1 = +-k2 + d); an anchor target Q_0 = G (log 1) fixes a component; signed union-find; stop when all T targets are determined; all logs verified k G = Q.
Prediction written before the build/run: at 27 bits and T = 1000 the multi-target rho takes between 1x and 3x of the tuned multi-target BSGS time (no negation map; one thread). If it is slower than 3x or fails to resolve all targets, the rho reference is weak and I say so.
