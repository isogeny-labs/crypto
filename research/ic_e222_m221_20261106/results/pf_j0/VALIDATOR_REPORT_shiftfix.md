2. Provenance in the rows: no fix flag, commit, argv, env (`SHIFTFIX`, `CSKIP`, `NOPLAIN2`, `C3`) or host; bound here by deterministic rerun. `var_os("SHIFTFIX").is_some()` also enables the fix for `SHIFTFIX=0`; the fix should be the default or recorded in the row.
3. Curves: the two curves per size are sixth-power twists of one group (CSKIP=11 gives the same primes as the producer's), so statistical support is about one group per size; the CSKIP=1000 different-prime runs agree.
4. Cells are 4 rows; repeat noise about +-10%; no A/A interval or isolation.
5. No pre-registered threshold for the corrected claim (the original freeze predates the fix; the fix was found by code reading, not tuned on these curves).
6. Reporting contract: wall-clock ms only, no S column, no §5a band, no bench.record, pcc id or `VALIDATION.json`: cannot enter `docs/ic/leaderboard.json` or `benchmarks/README.md`. The gap to rho (about 5x-8x at 28 bits, 12x-16x at 30, 27x-33x at 32, 42x-65x at 34) is against negation-only rho, not the repository's x6-folded rho, so the true matched-rho gap is larger.
