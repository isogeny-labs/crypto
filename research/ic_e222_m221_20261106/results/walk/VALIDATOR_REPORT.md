Analysis of whether "at least 25x to 53x" is sound, too high or too low (all values GAE, priced work only unless stated; rho side = claimed S x 2^(bits/2)):

* **Sound as an order-of-magnitude floor for this implementation.** The priced terms are a subset of the real work. Linear algebra, factor-base construction, hash probes and hashing are left out. The priced work is estimated, not counted by an instrumented harness. The 20-bit and 24-bit entries hold at the best-time fb and at the op-minimising fb (20 bits: fb 64 is the op-minimum; 24 bits: the op-minimum over the fb grid is within 5% of fb 128, 227.9k at fb 256 for the median-time instance vs 238.4k, and 241.9k vs 238.4k when the median is taken over the three instances).
* **Too high at 22 bits.** Over the fb grid the op-minimising fb at 22 bits is 128 (priced ops 86.6k median over three instances, 84.4k for the median-time instance), not 64. Using the file's rho operations that is **33x, not 41x**. The best-time fb is a time-optimum; at fb 128 the unpriced hash and table work is what makes it slower in time. The 22-bit rho reference is also uncertain (0.925 to 1.28), so the 22-bit ratio spans about 33x to 57x.
* **Too low once the unpriced work is priced (§5a).** Pricing the unpriced native operations (one hash probe per trial, one table insert per pair-sum, the elimination's `(fb+1)^3/3` modular multiplications, about `3*fb*bits_p` field multiplications for the factor base) at 0.1 GAE gives IC/rho 33x / 46x / 70x at the best-time fb; at 1 GAE per operation 104x / 93x / 232x (op-minimising fb: 56x / 93x / 141x). The IC arm is larger than the rho arm at every price, so the order holds across the band; rho's own unpriced work (canonicalisation, distinguished-point storage) is small by comparison and does not flip it. The report did not apply the band; this is the first computation of it.
* **A floor for this walk only, not for walk-IC in general.** 47% / 60% / 59% of the priced cost is the restart policy (two scalar multiplications every 32 steps, plus the 33 arm points). With free restarts the IC cost is 19.5k / 42.6k / 98.5k, i.e. **13.5x / 16.3x / 21.6x** (S = 19 to 24). So the distance to rho is at least 13x even if restarts were free, and the 25x to 53x belongs to the as-implemented restart rule.
* **Trend.** IC ops grow as 2^(0.68 per bit) from 20 to 24 bits (rho 2^0.5), from three instances per size and one seed: "consistent with an exponent above 1/2" is the right strength; the 22-bit point is not reliable enough to call the widening monotone (25x, 33 to 41x, 52x).
* **Conclusion:** the claimed range "25x to 53x" is a fair statement of the distance for the implemented walk at the best-time fb, with the 22-bit value too high as a floor (33x) and the whole range low once §5a pricing is applied. No claim of parity with rho is supported.
