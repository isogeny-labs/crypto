# Protocol: replication of the binary-field k = 4 IC vs rho crossover

Frozen 2026-10-27, before any claim data. It executes `research/gaudry_k4_char2_redteam_20261026/REPORT.md` §3 (merged in #2628), as amended by the four review rounds (`REVIEW-1.md` to `REVIEW-3.md`, round-4 verification). Branch `claude/k4-char2-replication-20261026`. Conductor T-3.

## 0. What is being decided

Whether k = 4 index calculus (Gaudry/Diem, FGHR D₄ system, binary-field port, the claim's own `gaudry-d4-bin` oracle and parameters) costs less than the strongest legitimate Pollard rho on the claim's curve family `f2m4_torsion(m, seed, max_cofactor = 2)` at m = 14 to 17. The claim under audit put the crossover near m = 17.

## 1. Evidence already in hand (not claim data)

- **Step 1, replay.** The claim binary reproduces the claim's clean `d-ladder` session exactly: 18/18 records, 0 problems (`audits/replay-d-ladder.json`).
- **Instruments.** These were built and adversarially reviewed in four rounds (`REVIEW-*.md`):
  - fully priced rho arms, including a batched-inversion rho;
  - full pricing of every native counter on both arms;
  - a reference arithmetic-cost model (§3);
  - a one-pass native price table measured on one host;
  - a unit-price command with per-phase CPU timers.
- **Legacy counts.** Legacy method ids count exactly as at `e403626cb`. The m = 17 rho walk is byte-identical under a capped diff.
- **Calibration findings.** Inline, IC's measured CPU per priced unit is about 1.26 times rho's (median of 36 comparisons, range 0.70 to 1.70, no trend in m; `unit-price/CPU_GAP.md`). An earlier apparent 2.5× gap was a thread hand-off artefact.

## 2. Arms (identical on every target)

| arm | method id | role |
|---|---|---|
| `ic` | `ic.pipeline_f2m4_refmm`, with the claim's `ic-wied` parameters unchanged (`factor_base=bin-t-line`, `oracle=gaudry-d4-bin:macaulay=11,frobenius=1,sparsest=1,replay=1,charge_unsolved=1`, `linalg=wiedemann`, `max_trials=50000000`) | candidate |
| `rho-batched` | `rho.negation_batched_refmm` (negation map, distinguished points, Montgomery batched inversion, canonicalisation charged) | reference |
| `rho-neg` | `rho.negation_f2m4_refmm` (the claim's walk, fully priced) | reference |

- **Best rho.** On each m, best rho is whichever of the two rho arms has the lower mean cost. It is chosen per unit and per pricing model, never per target.
- **Decision-grade records.** The `*_refmm` ids are decision-grade: their mod-r units are the minimum of the code kernel and Montgomery from one pass on one host.
- **Reported beside them.** The `*_ref` (code kernel) totals and the polynomial-basis totals are in every record, and are reported beside the decision-grade totals.

## 3. Units

1. **Counted unit (primary).** Counted F_q operations plus native work in FME, divided by the reference cost of one affine addition. That cost is 42 at odd m and 64 at even m (log-table tower; δ = u at odd m). Every record also carries:
   - the polynomial-basis model, at 42 for every m;
   - the §5a band, with unpriced native work at 0, 0.1 and 1 GAE per rule application.

   No record may be a lower bound; a `cost.lower_bound = true` record is an execution failure.
2. **Hardware unit (secondary).** One-thread CPU time of complete solves, run inline: `ecbench unit-price-f2m4 --closure --closure-variants refmm`, one job per m at m = 14, 15, 16 and 17. This avoids the pool hand-off artefact.
   - The ecbench sessions' own wall and CPU times are recorded, but they run under the global pool, so they are descriptive only.
   - The host is a shared cloud container, isolation L0. That is a stated limitation of the hardware unit.

## 4. Workloads and seeds

- **Sizes.** m ∈ {14, 15, 16, 17}; r is about 2^{4m−1} (cofactor 2). r ≥ 2^64 at m = 17 runs the u128 paths.
- **Targets.** 8 targets per m. Targets 0–3 are on curve seed 1 (the claim's curve) and targets 4–7 on curve seed 2.
- **Seeds.** Target and algorithm seeds are the first 63 bits of sha256 of `gaudry-k4-redteam-v2|target|m|i` and `…|alg-<arm>|m|i` (`make_specs.py`). None of the claim's targets are reused.
- **Sessions.** One ecbench session per (m, target, arm): 96 specs in `specs/`, generated by `make_specs.py` and committed with this file. The three arms of a target share a workload id and are paired by it.

## 5. Order, resilience and preflight

- **Queue order** (`jobs.txt`, `run_queue.sh`): the m = 14 calibration closure first, then the m = 14 sessions alongside it on the other workers, then each larger m's sessions followed by that m's closure job. Ascending m, and within an m in target order. 4 workers, each with its own ecbench lock, `--cpus none --allow-busy`.
- **Calibration.** `unit-price-f2m4 --m 14 --closure --closure-variants refmm --reps 1` runs complete inline solves of both arms at m = 14. It settles whether the about 1.26 inline CPU level holds on complete solves before the m ≥ 16 sessions, which are the long ones.
- **Restarts.** `run_queue.sh` skips sessions whose `session.json` says `complete`. It renames an interrupted session directory to `<name>.attemptN-killed` and restarts it. Killed attempts are never evidence. Every completed session is committed and pushed.
- **Audit.** `ecbench verify --replay 1` on every completed session, and `--replay-all` on one session per m.

## 6. Decision rules (frozen)

For each m with all 8 targets complete, let ratio_i = IC_i / best-rho_i on paired target i, in the counted unit. Report the geometric mean, a 95% t interval on log ratio_i, and a 95% bootstrap interval (10,000 resamples, seed 202610270001).

1. **Survives at m.** The upper 95% bound of the mean ratio is below 1 in the counted unit, under the log-table tower model and under the polynomial-basis model, at all three §5a band points. And the hardware-unit point estimate (IC CPU ÷ best-rho CPU from the m closure job) is below 1.
2. **Refuted at m.** The lower 95% bound is above 1 in the counted unit under either model.
3. **Inconclusive at m otherwise.** Add 8 fresh targets once (indices 8–15, same seed scheme). If it is still inconclusive, report it as inconclusive.
4. **The claim survives** if rule 1 holds at m = 17, or at m = 16 with m = 17 not refuted.
5. **Early stop.** If at m = 16 the lower 95% bound of the counted ratio exceeds 2 under both models, skip m = 17 and report "crossover not within reach of m ≤ 17".
6. **Slope.** Fit log(mean ratio) against m on the completed m values. Report the slope with its interval against the red team's −0.93 per unit m, and the interpolated m* with its interval. This is reported whatever rules 1–5 say.
7. **Drops.** A target is dropped only for a verification failure, never for wall time. Every drop is reported.
8. **Extension.** m = 18–20 with large primes runs only if the claim survives under rule 4, under a separate frozen amendment.

## 7. Known limitations, stated in advance

- **Isolation.** L0 shared host, so the hardware unit is noisy (±25% seen in calibration). A positive verdict must be re-run on an isolated host before it is called a claim.
- **u128 coverage.** The u128 IC and batched-rho paths are unit-tested only at small m. The first m = 17 session is checked by `--replay 1` before the rest are trusted.
- **S3 checkpoints.** These are unavailable in this container (no token, no AWS CLI). The resume note and every finished session go to git instead.
