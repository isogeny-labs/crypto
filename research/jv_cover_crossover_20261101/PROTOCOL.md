# Protocol: does the Joux-Vitse cover cross measured rho at p = 1300-1600? (frozen 2026-11-01, before any main-run data)

Labels used everywhere: **measured** (read from a run record), **derived** (computed from measured values by `fit/`, Rust), **model** (fit extrapolation or preregistered prediction).

## Scope
This weak class only: the `jv_cover` genus-3 cover of E(F_{p^6}) over F_{p^2}, full rational 2-torsion, never prime order, built by `generate_spec(p, seed)`. Lower-bound comparison. No statement about prime-order or deployed curves. The study reports verdicts by the rules below and nothing else.

## Question and what changes from the predecessor
Predecessor: `research/jv_cover_p_scaling_20261024/` (slope of ln(S/rho) on ln p = -2.05, 95% [-2.28, -1.83]; S/rho = 1.59-1.98 at p = 1009; p* about 1330, 95% [1170, 1540]). Its rho was measured (stored-table `rho_e`) only at p <= 251; at 503 and 1009 it assumed the pooled value 1.344. This study measures rho on the same kind of instances at the new primes with two memory-light walks.

## Primes (probed before freezing)
`generate_spec(p, seed)` succeeds for seeds 1..3 at **p = 1301, 1451, 1601** (probe: `--exp ccov --residuals 0 --constructed 0`, all nine instances returned |F| = 634/651/662, 735/729/703, 764/820/824; l = 2^60.1, 2^61.0, 2^61.9). These are the primes.

## Arms (same (p, seed) instances, seeds 1..3)
* **Cover arm.** `dlp --rho-runs 0 --rho-ref 1.3 --check-every 64`, one invocation per (p, seed). S = `total_muls` / (`c_add_e` sqrt(l)) (**measured**; units: group additions on E(F_{p^3}) per sqrt(l); the binary's `s`). `solved`, `correct`, `mismatches == 0` required, else a recorded failure, no silent replacement. The 1301 seed 1 run was made before freezing as the timing probe (same binary, same command, 843 s) and is reused as data, as the predecessor did with its probe.
* **rhodp (primary reference).** `--exp rhodp` (`run_rho_dp`: the 32-adding walk of `rho_e`, distinguished points shared between walkers). S = group_ops / sqrt(l), every group operation of every walker charged, the multipliers once per walker. `--threads 4`, `--dp-bits 18` at the new primes (DP overhead about 4 * 2^18 = 1e6 steps against 1.1e9-2.1e9, below 0.1%; stored points about 4e3-8e3, tens of kB). One process per instance, `--rho-runs 3` (runs 0,1,2; the run index fixes the walk seed). **3 runs per instance, 9 per prime** (see Budget: this is below the requested minimum of 4 per instance, and is a declared deviation).
* **rhostrong (secondary reference).** `--exp rhostrong --neg 1 --batch 16 --r 256 --dp-bits 16` (the `run_strong_rho` of `research/jv_sieve_strong_rho_20261019`, whose 2026-10-20 review qualification withheld comparative conclusions because that study's controls lacked numeric rules; this study states its rules below), seeds 1..3 as three parallel single-thread jobs, `--run 0` and `--run 1`: **2 runs per instance, 6 per prime**. S is the binary's `s_harness` (F_p multiplications / (c_add_e sqrt(l))), `s_tuned` is recorded for information only. The current source charges the distinguished-point and history hashes that the 2026-10-20 review found missing in the old rows.

## Controls (numeric rules, stated before data)
1. **Calibration (positive control).** `rhodp` at p = 251, seeds 1 and 2, **8 runs per seed (16 total)**, `--dp-bits 12`, 4 threads (DP overhead about 0.2%, matching the main arm's about 0.1%). Reference: the predecessor's measured `rho_e` samples at p = 251, seeds 1 and 2 (32 runs, mean 1.264, single-run sd about 0.62), read from its `dlp_p251_s1/s2.json`. **Agreement rule: the Welch 95% interval (Satterthwaite df, t quantile) of (rhodp mean - rho_e mean) contains 0.** The tool also prints whether |difference| < 0.3 (information only; with n = 16 and 32 the interval half-width is about 0.4, so this is a one-sided-looser check). If the rule fails, the rhodp arm is reported as not shown to measure the same quantity and its verdicts carry that qualification.
2. **Reproduction control.** `dlp` at p = 1009, seed 1, same command as the predecessor (`--rho-runs 0 --rho-ref 1.3 --check-every 64`). `total_muls` must be **bit-identical** to `research/jv_cover_p_scaling_20261024/results/dlp_p1009_s1.json` (452313823812). The predecessor binary had sha256 `a045cb80...e34d`; this study's binary is built from the current working tree (sha256 recorded in `results/versions.txt`) and differs, so identity is a real test, not a tautology. A mismatch is reported as a finding that the instrument changed and the cover S of this study is then not comparable to the predecessor's points.
3. Every correct/capped flag is checked; a run with `correct = false` or a capped walk is excluded and listed.

## Preregistered predictions (model, from the predecessor fit S/rho = (p/1329)^-2.05, pooled rho 1.344)
1301: **1.04**; 1451: **0.84**; 1601: **0.69**. With this study's instances the measured seed spread is expected to be about +-15%.

## Decision rules (frozen)
For each new prime p: G(p) = geometric mean over seeds of S_seed(p) / R(p), where R(p) is that prime's mean S of all correct runs of the reference arm (all instances pooled). 95% **percentile bootstrap, 20000 resamples**, resampling jointly (a) the cover seeds with replacement and (b) the reference runs with replacement within each instance (independently of (a)). Verdict on the interval [lo, hi]:
* "**below measured rho at p**" only if hi < 1;
* "**above measured rho at p**" only if lo > 1;
* otherwise "**not resolved**".
The same is done against rhodp and against rhostrong separately. With 3 seeds the seed resampling has only 10 distinct multisets, so the interval is coarse in the seed direction; this is stated, not corrected.
**Refit.** All per-seed points, ln(S/rho) on ln p, OLS slope with t interval, cluster bootstrap (20000, seeds within prime, rho runs at new primes) for slope and p* = exp(-a/b) (**model** extrapolation). Primary variant A: predecessor points with their own measured rho_e mean (p <= 251; 2 seeds at 251), predecessor 503 and 1009 points with the pooled 1.344 **still an assumption**, new primes with their measured rhodp mean. Variants, all reported: B (503, 1009 priced at the new-prime pooled rhodp mean), C (only points with measured rho), D (new primes only, rhodp), E (new primes only, rhostrong). Report how p* moves from 1330 [1170, 1540].

## Section 5a handling
* rhodp and rhostrong report native counts: rhostrong emits `canon_cmp + hash + dp_check + scalar_ops + table_ops + cycle_cmp` per run; rhodp does not emit counts, so its native work is **derived** as one walk-index hash per step plus about steps/2^dp_bits DP table operations (stated as derived, not measured).
* The cover arm reports only F_p multiplications (`total_muls` = setup + base + stream + weil + F4 + linear + post + verify muls, group-addition equivalent via c_add_e). It does **not** emit its F_p additions, memory traffic, row reductions or Wiedemann vector work. So **no arm pair has counts on both sides and no band ranking is possible**, as in the predecessor. As a sensitivity only, S/rho is also printed with each rho arm repriced at w = 0, 0.1, 1 GAE per native operation and the cover left at 0; this is one-sided and **not a valid band**, it only shows how far the denominator can move.

## Budget (timed before freezing) and the cut
Host: 4 vCPU, otherwise idle, free memory 12 GB, free disk 5.8 GB (logs are tens of kB; no stored walks). Timings: `dlp` p = 1301 seed 1: **843 s** wall on 4 cores (measured; scaled by the predecessor's 620 s at 1009: about 1000 s at 1451, 1200 s at 1601 **model**); `rhodp` p = 1301, 4 threads: **4.3e6 steps/s** (read from the walk's progress lines: 4.03e8 steps at 93 s), so about 340 s, 460 s, 620 s per run at 1301, 1451, 1601 for the expected 1.3 sqrt(l) steps; `rhostrong` p = 503 three instances in parallel, 24 s for 0.2-1.1 sqrt(l) steps (about 2.3e6 steps/s per thread), projected 350 / 480 / 650 s per batch at 1301 / 1451 / 1601 for 0.9 sqrt(l) steps (**model**).
Projection (single job group at a time, at most 4 cores busy): cover 8250 s (9 runs less the probe), reproduction 620 s, calibration under 1 min, rhostrong 6 batches 2960 s, rhodp at 4 runs per instance 17000 s, at 3 runs per instance 12750 s. Total with 4 runs per instance: about 8.5 h from now plus 0.55 h already spent, **over the 8 h limit**; with 3 runs per instance about 6.9 h (plus 0.55 h spent = 7.4 h). **Cut applied, as the brief specifies: runs per instance for rhodp (4 to 3), no prime dropped, rhostrong kept at 2.** No job launches after T0 + 6.5 h. Each job has a `timeout` (cover 3600 s; rhostrong 3600 s; rhodp 2400 / 3000 / 3900 s per instance at 1301 / 1451 / 1601; reproduction 2400 s; calibration 600 s). Every failure (timeout, OOM, wrong answer) is recorded in `results/walls.txt` and the README with its reason; nothing is silently rerun. Consequence stated in advance: with 9 rhodp runs per prime the reference mean has a relative standard error of about 0.62/sqrt(9)/1.3 = 16%, so a verdict is likely to be "not resolved" unless S/rho is far from 1.
