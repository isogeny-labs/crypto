# Recover the bidegree-(3,3) equation and count rational ruling fibres

Use only the committed 120 labelled planes and the split quadric basis. In the affine chart of the Segre grid, X=e+s g-t h+ts f. A tritangent restricts to a (1,1) form. For each Steiner difference in ascending order, the 28 products span a six-dimensional subspace of (2,2) forms on the quadric (rank seven before removing Q). On the canonical curve this space identifies with Sym² H⁰(K+α) by multiplication: dimension six follows from the measured restriction rank and injection of (2,2) forms into sections on the (3,3) curve. Each product is projectively the square of a section. The 28 points in this six-space are therefore on a quadratic Veronese surface.

If the fitted space of quadratic equations of the 28 points has dimension six, it equals the full quadratic ideal in degree two of that Veronese. Compute the tangent spaces as nullspaces of gradients. For two distinct points corresponding to v² and w², their tangent spaces are vV and wV. Their common annihilator is the line generated by a square in the dual six-space, since it corresponds to symmetric forms annihilating both v and w. Fit quadrics to all 378 such dual points. If this space again has dimension six, it is exactly the degree-two ideal of the Veronese of squares of linear functionals. Skip differences with insufficient interpolation ranks. At a curve point, evaluation of products is a square of an evaluation functional (possibly zero), hence these dual quadrics pull back to (4,4) forms vanishing on the curve.

All these forms must be multiples of the true (3,3) equation by (1,1) forms. Stop at rank four, where they span its full multiple space, with no further base points. Evaluate on all 1024 rational pairs and fit the unique (3,3) form if possible. Require equality of its full four-dimensional multiple space with the measured span as a polynomial identity. This last certificate is independent of the size and interpolation quality of the rational point set. Report normalized prime-field coefficients, rational curve points and distinct rational points per ruling fibre for both axes. Code 31 denotes infinity. Distinct rational multiplicities do not separate ramification from higher-degree residual factors.

This construction adapts Steiner-pair reconstruction to the recovered split quadric; see [Lehavi](https://academic.oup.com/imrn/article-abstract/2015/19/9472/910121) and [Hanselman–Pieper–Schiavone](https://math.mit.edu/~sschiavo/articles/reconstruction-20240205.pdf).
