# Rational plane divisors reach rank 30

The two trigonal rulings each have five fully rational degree-three fibres including multiplicities. Their principal differences have combined rank eight. Of 30784 rational plane sections, 81 split completely over the 32 curve points: 25 reducible (1,1) forms and 56 irreducible forms. Among the 81 sections, 34 contained six distinct points. Degree-six differences alone had rank 30 modulo 239753. Adding the two pencil relation sets kept rank 30.

The 32 columns therefore leave two null directions, one of which is the constant degree direction. The other supplies relative factor-base logarithms up to scale if the points have nonzero projection onto the target subgroup. The result establishes a complete relation span on this model conditional on that projection. The original target still needs transport and decomposition, and the exhaustive screen was not costed as a full solver.
