# Open case O3: index calculus with relations from all of J_C(F_p): protocol (2026-11-13)

Frozen by `frozen.sha256` before the first run of `full_jacobian_ic` (the unit
tests at p = 211 and one smoke run of the binary at p = 211 and 419 with unit
class times and output outside the repository ran earlier, only to check the
code; their numbers are not used anywhere below). Every prediction and every number named here is `[pcc:unverified]`.

## Question

`research/chained_transfer_proof_20261112/PROOF.md` T5 and T7 show that an
index calculus whose relations lie in the transferred subgroup
`S = c^*(E(F_p))` is a collision search on `E`. Case O3 (section 5) is the
index calculus that draws relations from **all** of `J_C(F_p)`, a group of order
about `p^2` with the transferred order-`r` subgroup inside. Its known cost is
`O~(p^{2-2/g}) = O~(p)` for `g = 2`, against rho on `E` at about `sqrt(r) <= sqrt(p)`:
a loss that should grow like `sqrt(p)`. This run measures that loss, so that O3
is closed by data and not by expectation (no proof is claimed).

## Instances

The bielliptic cover curves of `cover_pushforward` (`cover_select(p, i)`, the
first candidate that passes the admissibility checks), `p = 211, 419, 821,
1619, 3203, 6421, 12841` (stage 1), then `25601, 51203, 102407` (stage 2, run
in that order while the total compute stays under about 60 minutes; a prime
that does not finish is reported as not run, never extrapolated). Per prime 3
DLP instances (`k = 0, 1, 2`: `seed = seed0 xor (k 0x1000)`, `x = (seed *
2246822519 + 7) mod r`), the pulled-back pair `(phi^*G, phi^*[x]G)` on `J_C`.
`r` is the largest prime with `r || #E1(F_p)`.

**Curve selection and the projection to the r-part.** `#J = n1 n2 = h r` with
`h = #J / r`. The selection rejects `r | n2` and `r^2 | #J`, so `r` does not
divide `h` and the `r`-part of `J(F_p)` is cyclic, generated by `G' = phi^*G`
(`r > 50` odd, so `phi^*` is injective on it). Hence `h D` lies in `<G'>` for
every `D in J(F_p)`, and `l_j = log_{G'}(h P_j)` (mod `r`) is the logarithm of a
factor-base place projected to the `r`-part. Every skipped candidate before the
selected one is counted by reason (`bielliptic_rejected`, `r_below_50`,
`r_divides_e2_order`, `r_squared_divides_jac_order`, other) and recorded.

## The algorithm (arms A, B)

A random `Z in J` with `h Z != 0` is drawn once per instance; `zeta =
log_{G'}(hZ)` is one more unknown. A walk collects `R = a G' + b T' + k Z`
(16 jumps `alpha G' + beta T' + gamma Z`, `alpha, beta < min(r, 2^12)`, `gamma <
2^12`; a restart every 4096 steps at a uniform `a, b < r` and `k < #J`), so `R`
ranges over `<G', Z>`, which is all of `J` up to the index of `<Z>` (reported as
`ord(Z) / #J`). A smooth `R = sum eps_j P_j` gives the row
`sum eps_j l_j - (h b) t - k zeta = h a (mod r)`. Linear algebra: graph reduction
on the large primes, peeling, counted Wiedemann mod `r` (`ic_control`'s own
`peel`, `wiedemann`, `combine`, `Dsu`; `build_rows` copied with the `zeta`
column). Certified by `[t]G' == T'` on the word Jacobian (counted and
right-to-left), then independently `[t]phi^*G == phi^*T` on `J_C` (library
`Genus2Curve::mul`) and `[t]G == T` on `E1` (`Curve::mul`).

- **A**: factor base = every degree-1 place up to sign (`~p/2` columns), only
  fully smooth `u` are relations.
- **B**: double large primes, `ceil(2 sqrt(p))` factor-base columns
  (`fb_size_g(DoubleLP, 2, p, 2.0)`), relations with at most two places outside
  the base, large-prime graph, cycles through the vertex "1".
- 4 algorithm seeds `700 + s` per instance (12 runs per arm per prime).
- **Controls (the T7 regime, same instances, same word Jacobian)**: `A_S`, `B_S`
  = `ic_on_rung` arms `A` (full) and `C` (double large prime) whose walk stays
  in `<G'>`; 4 seeds `500 + s` per instance. They are the recorded baseline of
  `negation_remeasure_20261113` and show what relations from `S` cost.
- **Matched references, same process**: negation-map rho on `E1` (`rho_full_ec`,
  16 seeds `1000 + s` per instance, primary reference); plain rho on `E1` for
  context; negation-map rho on the order-`r` subgroup of `J_C` (`rho_full` on
  the word Jacobian, 4 seeds per instance), for context only.

## Pricing and calibrated reading

Unit: one group operation of the arm's own group, 1 GAE (a Cantor step costs
more than an elliptic addition; ratios of genus-2 counts over elliptic counts
understate the gap and are labelled so). Native counters of every arm, rho
included, priced at 0, 0.1, 1 GAE per operation (AGENTS.md 5a): IC `rel.mul +
la.mul`, rho `canonicalisations + partition_hashes + dp_lookups + dp_inserts +
word_ops`. Calibrated reading: `w_cal(class) = time(native operation) / time(one
group addition of the arm's own group)`, best of N (25 batches of 20,000),
shared unisolated VM, applied to both sides at 0, 1 and 10 `w_cal`, exactly as
`negation_remeasure` does. Order classes: `above` / `below` / `unresolved` by
`negation_remeasure::classify`. The transfer (pull-back, cover) is one-time and
in no per-instance total. Additions are listed as a sensitivity.

## Decision rule

For arms A and B separately, over the stage-1 primes (stage 2 primes are added to
the fit when they complete, reported separately):

- **O3 closed by data (loss grows)**: every cell `above` at the band and at
  the calibrated reading, **and** the lower end of the 95% interval of the
  ratio slope (at `w = 0.1`, group operations plus priced native work) is above
  0.2.
- **O3 open: advantage seen**: any cell `below` at all three band prices (an
  accounting change is not an advantage: the cell is re-read with every native
  counter listed before the word is used).
- **Otherwise unresolved**, reported with the cells that block the rule.

Both arms must meet the first condition for the verdict "closed"; a mixed
result is "unresolved". The result is a measured gap to the matched rho, not
a pass/fail gate for the success criterion: the distance remaining is reported
for every row, and arms are not called improvements over the index-calculus
baseline unless their emitted-counter total is below the subgroup control's at
all three prices (the scorecard says which). All prose claims in RESULTS.md
carry `[pcc:unverified]`.
