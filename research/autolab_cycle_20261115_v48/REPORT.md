A new native scanner eliminates the38-dimensional constant v image once, packs its45-dimensional quotient into uint64, and solves35-direction point systems. It pays batch inversions and a validated linear half-trace table, retains both curve-point lifts and rank-deficient residuals, and updates columns by precomputed Gray XOR increments.
