# Regular-prime polarized quotients on the cyclotomic genus-three lane

[pcc:unverified] Proposed general deduction for independent mathematical review. This is not an assertion of a speedup, independent verification, or new priority. It strengthens the *scope of a conditional exclusion* beyond the bounded primes in KERNEL-NEXT-GATES.md.

## Statement with premises

Let J be the canonically principally polarized Jacobian of a smooth C:y²=x^7+c over F_p, p odd, p=1 mod7, c!=0. Let sigma be a rational order7 rotation. Assume the established faithful rank-one cyclotomic Tate action and the Frobenius identity pi=P(sigma) from frobenius-certificate/DERIVATION.md. Put O=Z[zeta7], I=[O:Z[pi]], assumed finite. For every prime l not dividing 7*p*I, any rational maximal isotropic H in J[l] has a quotient canonically isomorphic to J as a principally polarized variety over F_p.

This rules out changing to a non-hyperelliptic principally polarized genus-three Jacobian through one of THESE regular-prime isogenies. It does not exclude exceptional-index primes, ramified7, characteristic-p isogenies, different polarizations, extension fields, other input curves, or a chain that first uses an exceptional edge.

## 1. The cyclotomic class number is one

For K=Q(zeta7), O is the full ring of integers, |disc(K)|=7^5, degree6, three complex places. Minkowski gives every ideal class an integral ideal of norm at most
(6!/6^6)*(4/pi)^3*sqrt(7^5).
It is strictly less than5: use pi>3 and sqrt(7^5)<130; the resulting rational upper bound is less than5. The residue degrees of2 and3 are ord_7(2)=3 and ord_7(3)=6. Thus no prime ideal has norm2,3 or4, nor can a composite ideal of norm4 exist without a norm2 prime ideal. Only norm1 remains. Hence every ideal is principal.

## 2. Every totally positive real unit is a square

Let F=Q(t), t=zeta7+zeta7^-1, with f(t)=t^3+t^2-2t-1=0. The elements t and s=t²-2 are conjugate units of norm1; -1 is a unit. Rational root intervals (6/5,5/4), (-1/2,-2/5), (-2,-3/2) give signatures t=(+,-,-), s=(-,-,+), -1=(-,-,-). These three sign vectors are independent over F2.

Dirichlet gives O_F^*/(O_F^*)² dimension3. Its signature map is therefore an isomorphism, so its totally positive kernel is exactly the squares. This uses no assumption that the displayed units constitute a fundamental system: independence modulo squares plus the rank theorem suffices.

## 3. Rational kernels are O-stable outside the index

Because l does not divide I, Z_l[pi]=O tensor Z_l. A rational subgroup of J[l] is Frobenius-stable, hence O-stable. The integral Tate module is locally free rank1 over O tensor Z_l: it is an O-stable lattice in a rank-one rational module, and O tensor Z_l is a product of DVRs. Thus J[l] is the regular O/l module. The canonical Rosati involution restricts to conjugation, since a curve automorphism preserves the canonical polarization and sigma dagger=sigma^-1.

At l!=7 the algebra O/l is a product of fields. Adjointness pairs a field factor only with its conjugate factor. A self-conjugate factor is a nondegenerate summand, so an O-stable isotropic subgroup cannot contain it. For each pair of distinct conjugate factors, maximal isotropy selects exactly one of the two.

For l mod7 in{1,2,4} this gives8,2,2 choices respectively; for residues{3,5,6} it gives none. This count only applies outside the Frobenius index. It is not a classification of exceptional-prime kernels.

## 4. A polarized self-isogeny realizes every such kernel

Let L be the preimage of H in O under O->O/l. Complementary conjugate factor selection gives L*bar(L)=(l). The annihilator ideal of H is A=bar(L), with A*bar(A)=(l). By class number one choose eta with A=(eta). Then eta*bar(eta)=l*u for a totally positive real unit u. By the previous step u=v² for a real unit v. Replacing eta by eta/v preserves its integral ideal and gives eta*bar(eta)=l.

The endomorphism eta/v is rational over F_p because O is generated by the rational sigma, and its kernel on J[l] is exactly H. Its full degree is l³ (norm of its ideal), and eta dagger eta=[l], so its entire kernel is H and its pullback of the canonical polarization is l times that polarization. The induced map from the principally polarized quotient J/H to J is a rational polarized isomorphism.

The annihilator/preimage distinction is essential: taking a generator of L itself interchanges the chosen kernel with its conjugate. Principal ideals alone would prove only an unpolarized statement without the totally positive unit correction.
