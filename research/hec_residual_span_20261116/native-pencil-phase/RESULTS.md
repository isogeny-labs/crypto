## Next design constraint

The cubic experiment's admitted-row gain and this partition together reject relation-yield-only reasoning. A prospective mechanism should specify (1) how it reduces signed-row production/anchor work, (2) how it avoids expanding LP payloads or reduces final matrix work, and (3) how it preserves successful public-target validation/final verification at the claimed total cost. A graph/forest with deferred payload expansion is a classical candidate for item2, but does not by itself satisfy1or3 or establish novelty/10x. Review existing implementations and derive fullcostcase before another prototype.
