# Why genus2 needs a different general route

The current Gaussian reconstruction/integration controls are genus4. A straightforward genus2 C4 specialization cannot be promoted as coverage of absolutely simple genus2 Jacobians.

For every smooth genus2curve C:y²=x5+a*x3+b*x over an algebraically closed field of characteristic !=2, b!=0. Choose u with u4=b and define

 tau(x,y)=(u²/x,u³*y/x³).

Direct substitution gives f(u²/x)=u6*f(x)/x6, and tau²=1. This map is different from the hyperelliptic involution iota. Its fixedxcoordinates are±u. Atx=u the yfactor is1; atx=-u it is-1. Smoothness implies f(±u)!=0 (otherwise a²=4b produces multiple roots). Thus tau has exactlytwofixedpoints, both abovex=u. TameRiemannHurwitz gives 2=2(2g(C/tau)-2)+2, hence g(C/tau)=1. The complementary involution iota*tau has its twofixedpoints overx=-u and another genus1quotient.

Therefore J(C) has a positive-dimensional proper elliptic quotient over the algebraic closure and is not absolutelysimple. The argument is geometric: it does NOT assert either quotient or the maps descend toFp, nor that a particularFpDLP has been solved or spedup. It does NOT apply to a general genus2curve without this C4form. It also does not invalidate the independentlyqualified genus4models, where the generic odddegree9 coefficients lack this forced reciprocal symmetry.

This is a classical scope obstruction, not new mathematics.
