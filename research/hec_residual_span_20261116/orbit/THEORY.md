This is an adaptation of classical endomorphism factor-base reduction, not a novel index-calculus algorithm. The current positive rank fixture has substantial special structure; do not generalize to arbitrary g2/3/4 curves.

For C:y²=x^9+2 over F19 and zeta in F19 of exact order9, phi(x,y)=(zeta*x,y) preserves f and infinity, hence induces a group automorphism on the Jacobian. For a reduced divisor (u,v) of degree d its pushforward is

 u_phi(X)=zeta^d*u(X/zeta), v_phi(X)=v(X/zeta).

These formulas preserve monicity and the Mumford relation; the map has ninth power identity. Let D=13P0 have prime order8623. If phi(D) lies in <D>, write phi(D)=lambda D. Then lambda^9=1 modulo8623. Exactly one match certifies the subgroup action; no match is ineligibility, not permission to assume stability.
