## Exact price model

Let oldbase/subgroup REDCs be A,B, candidate counts C,D. With one subgroup reduction priced r>0 times one base reduction, the proxy ratio is

    R(r)=(A+rB)/(C+rD).

This is a weighted average of A/C and B/D with weights C and rD. Therefore min(A/C,B/D)<=R(r)<=max(A/C,B/D). Tenfold requires

    (A-10C)+r(B-10D)>=0.

ENVELOPES.tsv records the exact rational price cutoff or all/no-positive-price status for every actual controlarm, rather than choosing separate per-field best controls and pretending they form one implementation. derive.rb checks the formula independently at three rational sample prices; the written algebra, not those finite samples, proves the general bound.

This model includes REDCs only. It is NOT a runtime model, a measured relative price, a bound on other operations, or a mathematical lowerbound on other algorithms. No runtime claim can be concluded by picking a convenient r. Countervalues were fullyintegrated, but maps/lookups/allocations/integerwork and non-REDC primitives are not priced here.
