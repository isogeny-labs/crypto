# Rational Frobenius cannot supply a nontrivial GLV eigenvalue here

[pcc:unverified]. Author-derived elementary obstruction, classical facts, no novelty claim or exclusion of all endomorphisms. Scope: J/Fp and D in J(Fp) of primeorderell, including the genericmixedterm initialg2/3/4panel. It doesnot covertracezero pointsoutsideJ(Fp), proper-subfield Frobenius on extension inputs, automorphism-derived operators, or twistedcompositions.

The p-power Frobenius pi fixes EVERY Fp-rational Jacobianpoint, notjustdivisors supported onrationalcurvepoints. In Mumford form its coefficients areinFp and unchanged bypthpowers. Hence pi^i(D)=D and

    [k]D = sum_i [a_i] pi^(e_i)(D)

is equivalent to sum_i a_i = k modell, for ANYnonnegative exponents. With m integercoefficients and s=min(k,ell-k), the triangleinequality implies

    m*max_i|a_i| >= |sum_i a_i| >= s.

The secondinequality uses the leastabsolute integer representative ofkmodell; everyother representative has magnitude atleast s. For k near ell/2 and fixedm this gives max|a_i|>=ell/(2m) up to integerrounding. Thus powersofidentityFrobenius cannot give the usual fixed-dimensional O(ell^(1/m)) GLVcoefficients uniformly over allscalars. It also gives no newfactorbaseorbit: Frobeniuscopies ofrationalinputrows repeat thesamecolumns/relations. This is not a lowerbound againstordinary window scalar multiplication, otheradditionchains, orverified nontrivialoperators.

Extending the coefficientfield alone doesnotchange pi(D)=D for the SAME originallyFp-rational D. An embedding into a differentfactor/tracezerosubgroup orisogeny/twist-derivedoperator would require an explicit homomorphism, actioneigenvalue, applicability/domain proof, successfulsame-target transport andchargedconstruction/verification. No such genericmap is constructedbythisnote.
