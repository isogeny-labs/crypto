## Necessary Frobenius obstruction

All primes are 1 modulo 3. If Frobenius on J[3] preserves a maximal isotropic three-dimensional W, then V/W is the dual W, so

    characteristic_Frob(T) = h(T) h*(T),
    h*(T) = T³ h(1/T) / h(0),

for a monic cubic with nonzero constant over F3. This necessary condition does not assume semisimplicity. We enumerated all 18 such cubics and compared products exactly with reversed L modulo 3.

The three larger-field sources have NO such factorization. Consequently the current rational (3,3,3)-isogeny route cannot apply to these three particular sources, despite valid large prime subgroups. This does not exclude other isogeny degrees, extension fields or attacks. The two F127 cases pass only this necessary condition; the new F127/c33 case still has no constructed kernel or map.
