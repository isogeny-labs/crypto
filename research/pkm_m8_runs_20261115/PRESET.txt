- sigma_hat(m) = OLS slope of D_cell on N over the exact rungs. Needs >= 3 exact rungs spanning
  Delta N >= ceil(m/2); otherwise INCONCLUSIVE(m).
- KILL(m): sigma_hat >= alpha*(m,1).
- WEAK-KILL(m): alpha*(m,2) <= sigma_hat < alpha*(m,1).
- OPEN-LOW(m): sigma_hat < alpha*(m,2).

  (D_lb(r0) - D_cell(first rung)) / (N_cens - N_first); implied exponent e = 2/(m+1 - w*m*H(sigma)) for
  w = 1, 2 (infinite if the denominator <= 0), the parent's cost model.

8. WHAT THIS CANNOT SHOW
- Toy N <= 18; one presentation; one engine; D is msolve's F4 degree, not an algorithm-independent bound.
- Mixed-depth rungs use unequal per-summand bases; they measure D(N) at finer N resolution, they are not a
  different attack. The cost model's N is the total number of tower bits.
- OLS slope on small-N integer step data is not an asymptotic statement.
