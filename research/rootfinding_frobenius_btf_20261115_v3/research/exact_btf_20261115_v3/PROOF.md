# Exact matched block triangular audit

The algorithm implements the arbitrary finite-field set-completeness theorem in research/sparse_block_theory_20261115_v2/PROOF.md. It combines equal monomials over the field before incidence. Every remaining positive exponent, including pth powers, contributes a dependency. It matches equation rows to variables, orients each nonpivot dependency toward the matched variable, removes unmatched variables as an explicit outer assignment, and obtains topologically ordered strongly connected components by two graph traversals. Every equation, including unmatched rows, is checked as soon as its support has been assigned. No inversion or nonsingular-pivot hypothesis is used by the block enumerator. Consequently vanishing leading coefficients, identically zero specialized equations and free coordinates retain all values. Independent dense enumeration evaluates the original uncanonicalized equations with repeated multiplication; block evaluation uses canonical equations and binary powering.

Every original root chooses exactly one outer assignment and component tuple at each level. It passes all ready equation checks and appears at the leaf. Conversely every leaf has all labeled variables assigned and passes every original-equivalent canonical equation. Thus emitted tuples equal the complete finite-domain root set. This is a root-set theorem, not scheme multiplicity computation. Small controls are exhaustive; large actual matrices receive a structural audit and recorded family replay only, not a new all-roots search or independent rank recomputation.

The self-contained finite fields are polynomial quotients: GF2 and GF3 by X; GF4 by X²+X+1 over2; GF8 by X³+X+1 over2; GF9 by X²+1 over3. The degree2/3 moduli have no roots in their prime fields, hence are irreducible. Arithmetic is schoolbook convolution and monic reduction with canonical base-p digits. Exhaustive distributivity, Frobenius and nonzero inverse controls accompany the all-roots oracles; these controls do not replace the irreducibility argument.

The actual input is frozen raw evidence from the preceding regular-double branch, exact SHA in INPUT_RULES.json. Its 166 rows encode 83 binary k variables, with zero rows retained. For each of three recorded repetitions the worker builds exact nonzero incidence, runs matching/SCC, reports unmatched variables/rows and component sizes, and replays the recorded particular/kernel vectors against all rows. This is graph analysis of existing evidence, not a rerun of the old norm or rootfinding search. Recorded rank/nullity are explicitly attributed; they are not independently rederived here. The original339/332 encoder, parameter-neighbor differences and Schur fill are UNOBSERVED.

Matching and SCC are classical (Dulmage–Mendelsohn1958, Tarjan1972); no novelty or speedup follows from this audit. With n variables, m rows and e incidences, the simple augmenting matching costs O(m e) worst case, SCC O(n+e), incidence storage O(n+m+e); recursive depth is at most n. Exact component enumeration remains exponential and explicit tuple output costs its output size.
