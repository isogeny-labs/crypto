# G1 card: cheapest member of the Gaudry/Diem/Joux-Vitse family on EcGFp5 / EcMasFp5

Desk model, independent derivation; not a measurement or improvement claim. Scripts and outputs: `.../eval2/g1/`.

**Verdicts.** (1) Cheapest member: REVISE, see below. (2) LEAD-ICP-gfp5-symmetrized-gaudry: REVISE. The "≥2^142 floor" is not a floor, and D-drop value is bounded (about 3-4 bits). (3) C5, JV (n-1): CLOSE with named obstruction (memory); arithmetic alone closes it only against 2^128, not against rho.

## 1. Formulas (n=5, q=p≈2^64; JV = JOUX-VITSE paper_fulltext.md)
- Full base: relations n!·c·q/2 (l.353) + sparse LA Õ(nq²) (l.361) = 60·c·p + κ(p/2)².
- Large-prime rebalance: n!·c·q^{2-2/n} (l.365, 370).
- JV (n-1): (n-1)!·c̃·q²/2 (l.418, 435; Thm 1 l.492-506) = 12·c̃·p² = 2^{131.6}·c̃, so above 2^128 for any c̃ ≥ 1.
- Hybrid (guess one variable): n!·q systems per relation, degree 2^{n-1} (l.526-575) = 60·c_h·p², at least 5× JV because c_h > c̃ (degree 16 vs 8).
- Pornin l.138-141 counts p^{2-2/5}=2^{102.4} systems **without** the 5! (JV l.365 carries it: ×2^{6.9}); l.143-149 times D²=2^{40} gives 2^{142.4}. It has no LA term and fixes B≈p^{0.8}.

## 2. f(B) with a subset base (derived)
L=p/2=2^63 x-values carry a point. All F_p-rational roots are all-E for generic R. A subset base of size B needs 120/(B/L)^5 attempts per relation and ≈B relations:
R(B)=120·c·L^5/B^4, LA(B)=κB², κ≈960 words (Wiedemann, normal equations, ±1 entries).
Optimum R=LA/2: **B\*=2^{52.5+log2(c)/6}, T=2^{115.5+log2(c)/3} words** (valid to log2 c≈62.7, where B\*=L; then T=2^{71.9}·c).
Single or double large primes change T by at most -1.5 bits here, at the price of graph memory (single LP stores 2^{61.3} relations at c=2^{40}). I re-derived the exponents p^{5/3} (none) and p^{8/5} (double); only 2-2/g was checked, against Diem's 2006 abstract (arXiv math/0606607).
Pornin's product fails because T scales as c^{1/3}, not c: at c=2^{40} his B≈2^{50} is far below the optimum.

## 3. Units
- c is in F_p multiply-adds (4 words each; range 3-12). LA uses mod-n adds (12 words) and mulmods (200 words).
- F_{p^5} mult = 29 mul + 20 add (Pornin l.249-266, 49 cycles). Group add is 10M complete (Pornin l.88); I derive affine with batch inversion at 5.7M = 165 F_p mults + 114 adds ≈ 800 words (range 500-1830).
- Rho = 2^{159.33}×800 = **2^{169.0} words** (168.3-170.2).
- Sensitivity: T ∝ u^{1/3}κ^{2/3}. Over u 3-12 and κ 540-1800, T(2^{40}) spans 128.1-130.0 and the 2^128 crossing spans log2 c = 34-40; rho moves ±1 bit. If "2^128" means group operations (=2^{137.6} words), the crossing is at log2 c≈65.

## 4. Memory
- No-LP optimum: LA needs ≈280 B per column (matrix, vectors mod n, Berlekamp-Massey), so 2^{67.3}-2^{71} B. Relation storage equals the matrix at 40 B per column, 2^{64.5} B.
- Rho with DPs: 2^{30}-2^{40} points × 40 B = 2^{35}-2^{45} B, so IC needs 2^{22}-2^{36} times more.
- Memory-capped (no LP), IC beats rho only above 2^{56.8} B (c=2^{40}), 2^{59.3} B (2^{50}) or 2^{61.8} B (2^{60}); light-row double LP gains ≤4.5 bits. At 1 PiB, IC is about 2^{27} above rho.

## 5. Crossovers and the D map
- T<2^128 iff log2 c<37.5 (single LP 40.8; band 34-40).
- T<rho iff log2 c<94-97, beyond any c≤5D³≤2^{83}: in raw time the family is below rho for every D, raw included. Memory is the barrier, and nothing here is a validated improvement.
- D map: D=2^16 gives T 2^{126.2}-2^{132.3} (straddles 128). D=2^20 gives 2^{128.8}-2^{136.3}. D=2^26.9 gives 2^{133.4}-2^{154.4}.
- EcMasFp5 (no 2-torsion) gets the D=2^20 band.
- Symmetrization is bounded: D 2^{20}→2^{16} buys 2.7-4 bits. The free-c floor is 2^{115.5}.
- F4 dominance: the dense proxy gives c_F4≈2^{64.5} for S_5 (above FGLM 2^{62.3}) and 2^{51-54} for an assumed quotient shape (5 equations of degree 8 plus a quadric; **assumed, not committed**), so T≈2^{132.5-133.6}, above 128. Measured partial bound: 836 s × 1-4 threads × 2.8 GHz = 2^{41.1-43.1} cycles before OOM at degree 33 (EV-GFPN-fc5950).
- JV (n-1) c̃: JV's 8.5 s on a 2.6 GHz Core 2 (l.589-591, 706) is 2^{34.4} cycles. The repo's msolve 25-bit anchor, 5.88 s at 2.1 GHz, is 2^{33.5}. Full base then costs 2^{166.5-167.5} words, 1.4-2.4 bits **below** rho, at 2^{71.2} B. Break-even is c̃=2^{35.4} (34.7-36.6). For B≤2^{62.5} it loses to rho (T=1.5c̃p^5/B³). The arithmetic does not close C5 against rho; memory does.

## 6. Decisive experiment
Measure c (not D) for one complete m=5 solve (F4 + parametrisation) of the F_p-rational quotient (KN-OPEN-9b4a2b), validated first at n=m=3 (D=16 vs 64).
- Envelope: msolve 0.6.5 at p'∈{4111, 65551}, 512 GiB RAM, 32 threads, ≤72 h per cell, RLIMIT_AS ≥480 GiB. No S_5 at m=5.
- c_meas by callgrind instruction count at a toy prime, or the F4 matrix proxy (K6, within 2×), divided by 4. PMU is likely absent on cloud hosts.
- RAM, dense echelon bound from the Hilbert function of a complete intersection (not measured):

  | system | RAM at degree |
  |---|---|
  | S_5 (5×deg 16) | 7.4 GiB at d=33, 10.0 at d=34 (matches the 11 GiB death), 30 at d=38, 143 at d=45, 754 at d=55, 9.3 TiB at d=76 |
  | quotient proxy | 31 GiB at d=36 (5 variables), ≈125 GiB with the extra variable |

  At an assumed 2^{37} mult-adds/s on 32 threads, a dense quotient run takes 4.5-36 h.
- **Frozen criteria.**
  - PROMOTE (go to memory-priced study): D≤2^{17} and c_meas≤2^{40}.
  - CLOSE ("symmetrization cannot put EcGFp5 below 2^128 at free memory"): c_meas≥2^{44} or D≥2^{19}.
  - Else, or on OOM: not_measured, with RSS-at-death.
- Expected outcome: CLOSE (T≈2^{132-134}).

## Risks, confidence, sources
- Risks: model unvalidated (no positive or A/A control); LP row weight and relation independence assumed; no 64-bit F4 exists, so the 32-bit c̃ is carried over; the repo's §5a 0/0.1/1 GAE band is not in the frozen checkout, so I used word-ops with the sensitivity above.
- Confidence: structure (T∝c^{1/3}, B\*≈p^{0.92}) 0.8; absolute bits ±3, 0.7; sign of JV-versus-rho 0.5.
- Sources: frozen JV 2010/157, Pornin 2022/274, ECMASFP5 note, EXP-GFPN-05ff43/analysis.md, EV-GFPN-fc5950, KN-OPEN-9b4a2b; web: Diem math/0606607 abstract. Not retrieved: Gaudry 2009 (paywalled), eprint 2004/153 (403), Thériault/GHT exponents.

**Summary.** (1) Cheapest member is the rebalanced subset-base m=5 Gaudry/Diem, T≈2^{115.5+log2(c)/3} words, not ≥2^142. (2) It is at or just above 2^128 for D≤2^20 (0-8 bits), far below rho in raw time, but needs ≥2^{67} B (rho: 2^{35-45} B). (3) JV (n-1) is within ±2.4 bits of rho at full base with ~2^{71} B, so C5 closes by memory, not arithmetic; next measure c on the quotient with ≥512 GiB.
