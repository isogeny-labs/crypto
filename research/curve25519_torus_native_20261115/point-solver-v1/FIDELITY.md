# Comparator domain conditions

The original TargetCircuit constrains the sum of the eightfold member images
to Q. It does not constrain their uncofactored sum to the supplied R. The
adapter validates Q=[8]R to identify the same public target used by the section
attempt, but preserves the original equations without adding a raw-R sector.

The section formulation fixes a section through its raw pretarget R. Thus
matching Q alone does not establish identical search spaces. A future paired
cost protocol must retain or price that sector distinction and every original
guard, root/projection condition and setup cost. No such paired performance
comparison is performed in this fixed correctness component. [pcc:unverified]

Source fidelity: original aggregation.rs composes member eightfold image
polynomials and decode_row replays their canonical image points; original
section-compiler constructs its norm from public R and section coefficients.
Those sources stay unchanged. No general compiler/group/sector theorem is
claimed. Both public point and section attempts receive no known unknowns.

Poly's stored modulus is private in the original library. The public adapter
accepts Spec/R/Q and uses the original trusted constructors, not arbitrary
caller-built Systems. Observable sparse rows/indices/coefficients are checked;
complete model canonical range and original equations/group replay are checked
again. No old source was edited to add accessors or change its validation.
