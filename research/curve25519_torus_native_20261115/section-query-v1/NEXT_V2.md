## A concrete successor: compile unknown coefficients through the product circuit

For each rational [8]-pretarget R of a new public query Q, normalize c4=1 in
the finite chart, leaving five unknown field coefficients. Form the monic
norm sextic S_c exactly as in section-search-v1. Compile the *same* binary
Chebyshev, homothety and scalar Karatsuba/chirp recurrences over those unknown
coefficients, retaining the monic-remainder relation and every inverse guard.
Require the three sixth-power remainders to vanish. Do not supply roots,
labels, signs, point lists, selected subsets or witness values to this solver.

This produces a target-conditioned algebraic system/straight-line circuit
rather than enumerating B^3 signed triples. Its size alone gives no rootfinding
bound: inverses need real nonzero guards, lifting introduces auxiliary
variables, and algebraic degree/regularity can grow with k,p,q. Low input
count or quadratic gates do not establish low solving degree. Existing
polynomial residual IDs and partial unit-cancellation Lean endpoint do not
prove the compiler or a solver theorem. [pcc:unverified]

First discriminating obligation: an extension/projection contract between
solutions of the compiled unknown-coefficient system and admitted section
coefficients, followed by native fixed-field controls against this frozen
predicate and original row checks. Then bound/measure *all* coefficient
solving, failed attempts, field work, memory and useful-row yield under an
admitted growing-family protocol. Native counters must be fully priced in
bitop/v1 where applicable. No solver campaign, paid job or full-base search
is admitted or scheduled by this document.

## What would move the exponent

Let alpha=log2(3)/2 and k=B^theta. Explicit C_seed=B^chi attempted sections
have the conservative seed-test upper bound O(B^(chi+alpha*theta)) up to
stated scalar/log factors. chi+alpha*theta<3 is a sufficient budget target
for that phase at the earlier conditional six-sum model point. It is not a
necessary lower bound, a route disproof or a complete attack criterion.
Original single-target setup, public query generation/[8]-fibre, subgroup
indexing, useful-yield/rank, family, selection/private-tag, kernel, recovery,
row storage and memory constraints still apply.

A compact bulk coefficient solver can be assessed under its own complete
cost. Merely lifting every candidate into a dimension-D algebra and paying
Omega(D) representation/materialization work is not a free search over D
candidates. An actual bulk advantage needs a specified representation,
zero-selection/extraction algorithm and a bound for every materialized
coefficient, algebra multiplication and original witness.

Uniform explicit chart trials remain rejected only by the old written
finite-divisor model and its exact assumptions; biased construction and
bulk algebraic solving are not excluded. No new novelty or impossibility
claim is made. Primary literature/KB review precedes any new implementation.
