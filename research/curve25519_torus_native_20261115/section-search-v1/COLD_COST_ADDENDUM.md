# Cost boundaries for target-dependent queries

DERIVATION.md is pinned historical derivation. Its C_seed log-p term must
charge public query construction and inverse-eight transport for every new
decomposition target, including failed targets. Only fixed kernel/fibre data
may be shared in W_setup, charged in full to the single external DLP target.
If a particular fibre routine costs more than that term, add its actual cost;
do not price it once across unrelated targets. Relation rows, index/sign
decoding, validation and all private selection/kernel/recovery phases remain
charged. The formal API must expose this boundary. [pcc:unverified]

The uniform-seed rejection concerns coefficients uniform conditional on the
supplied target-bearing basis. It does not apply to a constructor correlated
with useful divisors or to choosing a public target after selecting roots.
That latter strategy must still satisfy the original hidden-target/tag
contract; no free relation generation is inferred. [pcc:unverified]
