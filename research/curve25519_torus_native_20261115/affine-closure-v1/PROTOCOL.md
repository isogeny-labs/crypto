## Compiler, certificate and public boundary

Input is a public canonical System and public protected semantic-variable
indices. No labels, signs, target scalar, point list or witness enter it.
Compute a sparse reduced linear basis with constant lineage coefficients
over the ORIGINAL linear equations. Prefer descending variable indices;
protected semantic inputs are not pivots. Keep every original variable and
equation as literal prefixes. This is ONE static pass, not iterative or
per-node Gaussian elimination, a reduced ring or F4.

For pivot rows B_v=x_v-E_v, substitute affine E_v into every original
quadratic polynomial f. Append nonzero changed residuals and basis rows.
Keep a full certificate of B_v=sum a_e f_e and
f-f(E)=sum B_v Q_v. For c*x*y, use
c*(x-E_x)*y+c*E_x*(y-E_y); linear terms use their constant cofactor.
RREF replacements contain no pivot variable. A separate coefficient checker
must reconstruct these identities with its own polynomial arithmetic before
the output reaches a solver. Keep all original guards; no gauge assumption,
exceptional chart, root or alias may be discarded. A checked linear conflict
is recorded at this finite input, not called a general route disproof.

Strict canonical parser and independent original-assignment evaluator use
direct BigUint modular arithmetic, not old Field/Poly evaluation. Reject
duplicate terms/roles, unsorted/out-of-range monomials, noncanonical values
and malformed shapes. Prime-field validity remains external for solving.
