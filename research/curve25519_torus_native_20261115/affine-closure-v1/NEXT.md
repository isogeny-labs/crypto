fixed curve controls show unchanged node counts and outcome classes, so simply
adding these consequences is not evidence of branch pruning. Additional
equations can increase other work, but the current partial counters do not
establish total cost. Preserve this null result and its complete raw outputs.

affine reduction**: specialize public equations with assignments made by the
solver, derive newly linear consequences, retain exact row lineage and
substitution certificates, and undo both the basis and derived constraints on
backtracking. First reproduce the coupled and protected-input controls with
assignment/rollback corruption refusals. Then compare the same fixed public
curve inputs, root order and unchanged limits. Charge work moved from the queue
into the reducer as part of its entire cost.

existing propagation already makes them redundant and bookkeeping increases

trace-projected compiler are distinct comparators, not claimed consequences
