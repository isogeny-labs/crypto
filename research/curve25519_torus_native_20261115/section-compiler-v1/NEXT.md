# Next discriminating scientific step

Root discovery is still the missing algorithm. V1 evaluates and checks known
coefficients; exporting millions of quadratic gate equations supplies no
solving-degree bound. Raising generic sparse-solver caps is not an exponent
argument. [pcc:unverified]

Before a new implementation, compare two concrete representations of the same
public predicate: the current acyclic gate program and a structured finite-free
module representation that keeps S, Chebyshev and geometric-product recurrences
as operators. Derive how a proposed elimination step acts on the five semantic
coefficients and both localizations without dropping an original guard.

The first acceptance gate is a genuinely unknown-coefficient public-input
solve on a declared finite domain, with every returned tuple projected and
replayed by the unchanged original extractor. Finite exhaustion, unsupported
extraction and resource refusal must be separate. No known section, raw labels,
scalar tag, candidate list or auxiliary assignment may enter its query API.

For an exponent claim, retain the original ROUTE_CRITERIA_V3 and separately
charge compiler/public-constant setup, root solving, field filtering, useful
relation yield, row selection/rank, indexing, sparse algebra, recovery and peak
memory on a declared growing family. A sufficient phase budget is not a lower
bound or universal route disproof. The classical guard simplification reduces
one local condition; the large membership degree and solver obligations remain.

Formal gate induction and rational-torsion/resultant certificates are separate
proof work. They should bind the actual native compiler rather than just cite
generic polynomial laws. No new solver/proof campaign is scheduled by the
CI-only checker. Keep that checker and the active scientific goal separate.
