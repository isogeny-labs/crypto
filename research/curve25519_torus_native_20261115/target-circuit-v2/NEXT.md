# Next gate: discover roots and price the complete route

The v2 compiler/adapter supplies a relation interface. A solver must receive
only public parameters and a fresh target, return original-relation
witnesses or an explicit failure, and retain every attempt. It must not
receive the labels, signs, points or fixture scalar used by these controls.

Before a run, freeze a bounded protocol with competing approaches, controls,
operation accounting, memory caps and original-ideal replay. Existing runner
admission/budget rules apply; this plan launches no campaign.

1. Direct/meet-in-the-middle enumeration supplies a correctness/cost
   reference, not an anticipated exponent improvement.
2. A lifted quadratic solver must retain range guards, both Fp2 coordinates,
   distinct raw indices and original point equations. Low degree/fewer terms
   alone do not pass.
3. Structure-specific elimination needs an explicit original-root
   correspondence, charged lifting/indexing and checks for eliminated
   nonzero polynomials/denominators.

Register falsifiable per-route hypotheses. A bounded timeout/OOM/degree
blow-up is censored evidence, not disproof. The unchanged consumer criteria
govern tested-domain rejection, family claims and full costs:

attempts, successful row checks, BSGS indexing, selection, kernel work and
