{\Large\bfseries\color{ink} Six section agreements versus a folded certificate}\par
{\small 23 November 2026 \quad Conditional integer result and original toy-base audit}

\textbf{Result.} The standard linear-algebraic folded-RS certificate cannot
meet both its dimension and root-count guards at original length $B\ge42$
with only six unfolded agreements. Core Lean checks this implication.
It restricts a published sufficient certificate; it does not prove actual
decoder failure outside that guarantee. [pcc:PCC-20261123-243ecb]

\textbf{Explicit premises.} Write $B=mN+r$, $r<m$, $1\le s\le m$ and
$L=m-s+1$. If $a$ complete disjoint blocks agree and at most $T$ unfolded
positions agree, then $am\le T$. In the optimistic five-dimensional
polynomial-message model, the standard guards are
\[
 NL<(D+1)(s+1)+4,\qquad D+4<aL .
\]
The formal statement allows any $h\ge2$ in place of 4. These two guards imply
$N<a(s+1)$. Since $a\ge1$ gives $m\le T$, including the discarded tail gives
\[
 B<m(N+1)\le ma(s+1)\le T(T+1).
\]
Thus $T=6$ requires $B<42$. This argument proves no geometric, code-applicability
or algorithm-cost bridge. [pcc:PCC-20261123-243ecb]

\textbf{Five local formal endpoints.} The source proves the general support
bound, its six-support corollary, the implication from explicit guards to the
floor predicate, their six-support incompatibility, and only the numeric fact
$213156431\cdot20633\ge42$. All five exact declaration hashes are bound;
the focused gate passes five endpoints with zero trusted-native endpoints.
Core Lean uses standard propext/Quot.sound axioms; the numeric endpoint has
none. This is not a proof of actual base cardinality or a generic divisor
bound. [pcc:PCC-20261123-243ecb]

\textbf{Original toy bridge.} Rust reconstructs the p101/A95/q3/K8 base
through original membership/index witnesses, retaining both signs and
excluding two raw labels with identity eightfold image. All 44 signed geometric
positions are distinct; cofactor aliases remain separate raw columns. A
supplied normalized section has exactly six matches with the correct $R$-pole
and $-R$ removable fibres. Original TargetCircuit, raw-$R$ and independent
numeric section replay agree. [pcc:PCC-20261123-1b51f3]

\textbf{Scope.} The polynomial-message/Artin model is optimistic and has
not been instantiated for this elliptic section code. Five normalized
coefficients alone do not make Reed--Solomon messages; cyclic torus labels
alone do not supply a function-field automorphism. Aliases require their actual
agreement budget $T$, rather than assuming six from six raw labels.
[pcc:unverified]

Failure of a sufficient guarantee is not an algorithmic lower bound.
Rank-dependent interpolation, different degree allocations, partial-block/list
recovery, overlapping windows and larger support budgets remain separate
routes. A nonzero interpolant can exist despite a failed generic dimension
count. [pcc:unverified]

\textbf{Next discriminator.} The same root guard and six-match budget imply
$D\le1$. The usual low-degree interpolation spaces then have at most 18
unknown coefficients for $1\le s\le m\le6$. Test exact rank on genuine
original large-$q$ membership rows with a prospective separate protocol.
Retain a full-rank minor and independent inverse, or a nullspace candidate and
additional-row residuals. This would discriminate one defined ordering and
space, not all decoders. It is not implemented or scheduled here.
An exponent-facing algorithm still needs non-enumerating section selection,
$q,K$-dependent cold cost, original projection and full useful-yield/rank/
selection/recovery accounting. [pcc:unverified]

\textbf{Reproducibility.} One final native call exits 0, with zero backend
invocations and zero new roots. Three release unit tests, fmt and strict
all-target Clippy pass locally. The 101-input source context consists of four
archives, not a monolithic archive. Compiled source hashes, binary pre/post
identity, write-once build/precall/postcall and actual shell transport
positive/collision controls are retained. The complete capture is a 6584-byte
lossless archive; source delta is 7732 bytes. [pcc:PCC-20261123-1b51f3]
