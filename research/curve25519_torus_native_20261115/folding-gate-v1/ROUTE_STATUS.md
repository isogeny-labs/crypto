# Which invocation is closed, and what stays open

Closed conditional invocation: transfer the standard monomial-dimension and
root-count certificate with h>=2, complete disjoint folds, at most six
unfolded matches and original length B>=42. Its premises cannot hold together.
This statement is an integer implication, conditional on the support and
partition hypotheses. [pcc:PCC-20261123-243ecb]

This does not disprove actual decoding outside a sufficient guarantee. A
rank-deficient interpolation matrix can admit a nonzero polynomial even when
generic dimension counting does not guarantee one. Different degree
allocations, partial-block/list recovery, overlapping windows and larger
agreement budgets also require separate analysis. The current code invokes
none of those decoders. [pcc:unverified]

The optimistic five-dimensional polynomial-message model is an assumption
for this compatibility audit. Five normalized section coefficients alone do
not instantiate a Reed-Solomon message space or an Artin automorphism.
Torus labels being cyclic supplies neither bridge. Guruswami1106.0436
sections3.1--3.2 gives the tested guards; Guruswami0811.4139 sections1.2/3/6
states the additional function-field structure used for AG folding.

Six raw labels can produce more than six evaluation agreements in the
presence of aliases. Use actual T in the general theorem; never silently
deduplicate. The native toy has44 distinct geometric positions, retaining
cofactor aliases as separate raw columns, and exactly six supplied matches.
No actual huge-base T6/cardinality bridge is supplied by that toy.
