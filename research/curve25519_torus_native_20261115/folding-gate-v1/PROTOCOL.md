## Exact mathematical inputs

Let a fold have width m>0 and N complete disjoint blocks, with r<m discarded
tail positions and covered original length B=m*N+r. Let a blocks agree in
ALL m entries and s<=m be the interpolation parameter. Six unfolded matches
give a*m<=6, assuming duplicate-free geometric evaluation positions. If raw
aliases inflate the number of matching positions, use their actual budget T;
do not infer T=6 from six raw labels without its bridge.

For the five-dimensional normalized coefficient coset, write k-1=4 and
L=m-s+1>0. Standard construction's sufficient certificate uses BOTH

    N*L < (D+1)*(s+1)+4       [dimension-count interpolation]
    D+4 < a*L               [root-count forcing]

with D>=0,1<=s<=m. Derive and check in core Lean that these imply
N<a*(s+1), then that this floor predicate plus a*m<=T implies
B<T*(T+1), including the discarded tail. In particular T=6 gives B<42.
This is incompatibility of these explicit certificate premises, not an
algorithm lower bound or a conversion of a sufficient condition to necessary
decoding success. Rank-dependent interpolation, another degree allocation,
partial-block/list recovery, repeated local windows, growing support budgets
and other algorithms remain separate routes needing their own proofs/costs.
