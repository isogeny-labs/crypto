# Consequences of the fixed-control result

The conditional coefficient/lifetime bridge works on the declared controls,
including drop-before-undo and exact disabled-baseline identity. The
recompute-every-quiescence variant fails the desired curve pruning gate at
this fixed budget: two baseline roots become caps, all13 enabled roots keep
their old node count, and all13 original toy inputs cap rather than returning
a root. This closes that implementation's fixed-control claim, not branch-local
Gaussian reasoning in general or an asymptotic index-calculus route.
[pcc:unverified]

The cap is cumulative modeled reservation, not observed live memory. A smaller
node number on a cap is an incomplete search prefix. Keep all15 such outcomes;
do not raise the cap, omit them, or treat the synthetic3to2node result as a curve
result. No matched cold-work result follows from this stage. [pcc:unverified]

The next implementation should avoid repeated full-System/certificate/cache
construction: share the immutable original prefix, lease conditional rows to
their premise trail, and update/undo a sparse basis or a bounded active linear
component. Record allocation, basis/checker work and row lifetimes before
comparison. Soos et al. already motivate depth-limited/incremental Gaussian
reasoning over GF2; applying a cutoff here needs its own prior frozen schedule,
same public targets/order/limits and complete outcome reporting. The cipher
paper's cutoff is not an empirically justified parameter for these equations.

First discriminating gate: account for original/dependency/row/certificate
storage and checker arithmetic; implement a bounded reversible component and
rerun the same33 controls with an exact disabled comparator. A root alone is
not a cost win. Nonlinear elimination or trace-projected relations remain
separate hypotheses with no general obstruction established here.

Original huge-base discovery, useful-yield/growing-family/selection, rank/kernel
and recovery obligations remain open under the original
[route criteria V3](https://github.com/isogeny-labs/cryptanalysis/blob/2dee266203bedb19e302d2af64240815c679f085/research/curve25519_torus_native_20261115/ROUTE_CRITERIA_V3.md).
No successor experiment, paid job or solver campaign is scheduled by the
maintenance checker. Canonical performance/exponent graphs and benchmarks
stay unchanged because no admitted performance datum is added.
