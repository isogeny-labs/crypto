## Why four roots give one row here

Negating a torus parameter swaps its two raw x branches. Inverting it keeps
its real coordinate. For h,h², sigma'=h^-3*sigma^-1 swaps the inverses of
the two shifted parameters. The known-target native control verifies the same
geometric row at sigma1,-1,h^-3. All actual cases retain4distinct sigma^q
parameter-base keys but1row; those keys do not normalize encoding/cofactor
aliases. SYMMETRY.md scopes the explanation to pair rows, not equality of the
entire large parameter bases. No relation-rank computation was run here.
[pcc:unverified]
