# Route rejection criteria applied to this mechanism

Original ROUTE_CRITERIA.md/V3 and all earlier evidence remain byte-identical.
This assessment narrows a route; it does not rewrite the original criteria.
[pcc:unverified]

**Conditionally rejected as an exponent-moving route:** a fixed finite number
of relative templates, the admitted original map/cofactor/prime-group
hypotheses, fresh iid private-tagged targets, and recovery solely through
physical equality/negation, known anchors and tagged row kernels. The written
ALL/RAW bound makes constant-probability recovery require square-root-order
queries in a growing family satisfying these contracts, even granting every
root for free. Four generic owner laws have focused kernel/audit receipts;
the full curve/probability application remains a written conditional theorem.
[pcc:unverified]

This verdict is stronger than a finite solver timeout or a fixed-panel rank
observation, but has a precisely delimited model. Reopen it when an assumption
actually changes: a growing template family with accounted selection/setup,
nonuniform/adaptive target policy with a new distribution analysis, or an
explicit extra logarithm transport with original replay and cold costs. A
counterexample to the claimed map/encoding bound would invalidate that
application; it would not refute Mathlib's finite-union law. [pcc:unverified]

**Open:** original full factor-base root discovery, useful-yield/rank/private-
selection/kernel recovery on growing actual families, total memory/setup,
complete cold one-target cost and independent validation. Anchored inversion
is a written successor reduction. It currently lacks a qualified selector
and useful-cycle cost analysis. The exponent thresholds in NEXT are
conditional gates, not measured values or universal lower bounds.
[pcc:unverified]

**Never a disproof:** fewer terms, a forced refusal, unsupported extraction,
bounded root-solver failure, a selected easy fixture, a larger kernel with
zero target coefficient, or an upper cost bound exceeding a desired budget.
No such observation is promoted to an all-index-calculus verdict here.
[pcc:unverified]
