# Fixed templates, shared columns, and the next selector

For the original admitted map, under the stated rational cofactor/kernel
hypotheses, a physical signed column has at most32torus encodings. Fixing
one of m relative roles fixes the common multiplier; at most2^m signed tuples
then remain. Consequently that column appears in at most C_m=32*m*2^m public
targets, with C_2=256. This is a written conditional map argument, not a new
native group/map refinement. [pcc:unverified]

The independent uniform-query model now allows every decomposition per
query. Let N=ell-1, a known anchor columns and t independent nonzero query
targets. There are N/2 signed physical columns. The probability of ANY
cross-query support overlap or anchor hit is at most

    C_m²*t*(t-1)/(4N) + a*C_m*t/N.

This is a union of Cartesian squares of column-incidence fibres. On its
complement, all rows separate into query blocks. Projecting a zero column
combination onto a block and evaluating its hidden logs forces that block's
row coefficients to sum to0, because its target has prime order. Both private
target tags therefore vanish on every kernel vector. Internal cycles or
many roots do not bypass the argument. Hidden logs are used in the proof,
never supplied to a query algorithm. [pcc:unverified]

With one online retained row/query, at most m*(i-1)+a prior columns can be
hit at query i. The sharper adaptive bound is

    C_m*(m*t*(t-1)/2+a*t)/N;

for m2,a2 this is256*t*(t+1)/N. Raw uniform label draws must also charge zero
targets: add n/ell and substitute n for the nonzero-query count. Failed root
attempts are included. Success-conditioned sampling does not inherit the
unconditional distribution for free. These fixed-family statements require
square-root-order queries for constant success in their explicit row model;
they do not cover arbitrary target policies or extra logarithm transport.
[pcc:unverified]

For a predeclared D-template family, ONE replaces C_m by C_m*D. Under an
actually tight per-query Theta(D^alpha) cost and an attained useful-collision
scale, the query-phase exponent would be1/2+delta*(alpha-1/2), D=ell^delta.
Alpha<1/2 is a prospective gate in that model, not sufficiency. An upper
bound with alpha>=1/2 cannot disprove a different implementation. ALL instead
has a D² collision bound and must pay for any explicit multirow output.
No bound here is an achieved collision rate or a measured attack exponent.
[pcc:unverified]
