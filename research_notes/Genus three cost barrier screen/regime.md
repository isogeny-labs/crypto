# Genus three complete cost screen

## Do plane quartic and hyperelliptic doubleLP both have exponent 4/3?

### Takeaway
No. Generic genus-3 doubleLP has exponent 4/3 on either model, but Diem's specialized plane-quartic line algorithm has exponent 1. This is an established potential exponent gain conditional on an efficient, applicable transfer and a scalable destination solver; increasing p alone does not give the present implementation that gain. Desk-study conclusions below are static analysis/inference, not independently validated performance claims. No experiment or novelty claim.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- With q=p, ℓ the target subgroup order, a suitable cold model is C_transfer(p)+A(p)p versus B(p)p^(4/3); A,B include arithmetic, logarithms and implementation constants. The necessary **full 10×** condition is C_transfer(p)+A(p)p ≤ B(p)p^(4/3)/10. Ignoring overhead and logs, constant A,B would give p ≥ (10A/B)^3. This is an algebraic condition, not an estimated crossover. For ℓ much smaller than p³, also compare the strongest admitted native method and √ℓ generic cost; p^(3/2) rho presumes ℓ of order p³.
- If both arms actually implement exponent 4/3, their asymptotic ratio is controlled by constants/logarithms and transfer overhead. Larger p alone supplies no polynomial transfer advantage in that case. Efficient fixed-degree isogeny evaluation itself preserves scalar multiplication; the gain comes from enabling a different relation algorithm.

## What would establish full 10×, and does it extend to genus 2 or 4?

### Takeaway
The present fixture requires changes across construction, transfer and destination solving. Genus-3 admission cannot stand in for the full genus-2+ objective.

### Cited Findings
- [coldcost13 RESULTS](/Users/adamburan/pq-work/hec-followups/research/hec_coldcost13_20261115/RESULTS.md) reports cold median 1780.974708 ms, selected native Compact 0.797166 ms: cold/native≈2234.13. The 10× budget is consequently 0.0797166 ms, requiring ≈22,341× candidate reduction on this case. The reported per-cell no-map residual median is 240.507208 ms, already ≈3017× that budget. Relations+descent+verification alone have median 52.211917 ms. These are recorded L0/unisolated, producer-unvalidated observations from five repeats of one pair per arm; phase medians are not additive and do not yield confidence intervals.
- Smith, *Isogenies and the Discrete Logarithm Problem in Jacobians of Genus 3 Hyperelliptic Curves*, §§3, 8–9, describes the established genus-3 transfer mechanism; its tractable (2,2,2) construction is distinct from this implementation's order-27 (3,3,3) kernel. Its approximately 18.57% prediction is conditional and does not estimate this implementation's admission rate. Proposition 5 and Remark 6 explain that a principally polarized quotient is not automatically a Jacobian in genus ≥4. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/0806.2995#S9).

### Inferences
- **Genus 2:** smooth curves are hyperelliptic, so changing to another genus-2 Jacobian cannot unlock the non-hyperelliptic genus-3 quartic mechanism. The general fixed-genus exponent 2−2/g evaluates to 1, equal to full-order genus-2 rho's exponent; this does not promise 10×. A special family or a separate constant-cost construction needs its own complete case. (Structural deduction; general exponent source: Diem 2012, introduction.)
- **Genus 4:** even an isotropic quotient needs a separate Jacobian-locus test/construction. A smooth plane quartic has genus 3; a genus-4 plane model must have degree at least 5. If a suitable degree-5 model and split-line hypothesis are established, Diem's degree formula gives 4/3 versus hyperelliptic doubleLP's 3/2. That is a conditional possibility, not an available transfer. No general claim about the existing genus-4 pipeline follows.
- **Concrete desk-study falsifier before any timing:** reject a proposed linear-cost successor if it retains the Θ(p²) source enumeration; reject its claimed polynomial advantage over optimal hyperelliptic doubleLP if its charged surviving phase has exponent ≥4/3. Require a bound on all charged phases, admission/failure probability, rank/known-log coverage and map/descent attempts. For a specified p and matched inputs, reject the 10× proposal whenever an unavoidable phase lower bound exceeds S_best-native(p)/10. At p=127, keeping the recorded non-map remainder unchanged already fails this test. For genus 2 and 4 require separate admissibility and cost certificates; a genus-3 component result cannot discharge them.
