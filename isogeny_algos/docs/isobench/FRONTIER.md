Generated by `isobench frontier build` from `docs/isobench/bounds/records/`; do not edit. A method is on the frontier when no other bound for the same problem and parameters dominates it: a bound dominates another when its mean-cost interval at the largest common size lies entirely below the other's and its alpha interval does not lie entirely above (a cheaper bound with a worse exponent is a crossover, and both stand).
