## Problem

The three leads now have executable conformance components and checked
conditional proofs, but none has a complete priced one-query/branch-set
comparison or a complete algebraic cofactor-quotient relation oracle.

The component controls are public synthetic E0 fixtures over F32/F64/F128
and the existing F(2^22) order991/cofactor4232 fixture. They do not replace
the frozen ell8/ell9/m4 programme or its original execution. Their operation
counts are not complete query prices; unit-test durations are not benchmarks.

## Evidence and uncertainty

- **Observed:** six native component tests, strict all-target Clippy and seven
  Lean lemmas pass; exact source/evidence in the linked study. The F32
  prime-subgroup case has two certified degree-at-most-four pullbacks and a
  seven-row common input block; three refuted leaves and one positive leaf
  are retained. Full-kernel checks annihilate all4232 points; fourteen Vélu
  comparisons agree with the explicit endomorphism up to the recorded sign.
- **Derived or proved:** the checked resultant identity certifies the chain
  bridge. The complementary-map identity gives `Psi Phi=[4232]` under the
  stated Frobenius/commutation assumptions. The kernel theorem is conditional
  on independent h-kernel coverage, not an assumed Phi-kernel. Actual curve
  geometry, cardinality, implementation and a cost improvement are not fully
  formalized by those lemmas.
- **Suspected explanation:** none is promoted. Preprocessing/proof replay,
  degree/support growth and projected factor-base geometry may erase savings;
  those possibilities require complete measurements.
- **Missing capability:** direct/symmetric/transfer phase-accounted query
  adapters; cost-accounted shared elimination on a full new branch set;
  algebraic encoding of the quotient image with exceptional-case lifting and
  independent relation rank; external independent replay and matched tools.
  The current session's existing AWS CLI returned NoCredentials on STS; no
  EKS or workflow-dispatch tool is connected. The inspected main workflow
  builds ecbench, not this standalone component crate. No image or accepted
  request for these new workloads has been created. This is not a quota or
  mathematical impossibility claim.
- **What this does not establish:** a bounded-degree law, global guessing
  minimum, cheaper verified queries, scaling, faster-than-external result,
  general isogeny yield increase or new ECDLP crossover. The original16Cargo
  checks remain tied to their historical source.

## What would unblock it

- [ ] Freeze matched direct/symmetric/transfer full-query arms, counting
  encoding/closure/translation, failed roots, signed lifting, subgroup checks,
  duplicate removal, peak memory and every verified relation.
- [ ] Price independent versus shared branch-set solving, including all
  compilation, common-block discovery, per-leaf certificates and replay. Keep
  every branch and positive control; do not assume post-solve intersection
  costs zero or infer transport from equal ranks.
- [ ] Implement the implicit algebraic quotient oracle. Validate distinct
  projected factor-base images, every physical relation, exceptional points,
  subgroup transport and relation rank; compare against explicit kernel
  correction with map construction/evaluation included.
- [ ] Supply the existing authorized EKS identity/submission capability and a
  tested commit-bound image containing those actual adapters, within unchanged
  protocol/budget/deadline. No replacement identity, pool increase, deployed
  fixture or smaller substitute for an original frozen workload.
- [ ] Independent replay and matched references, complete cold pricing and
  the applicable0/0.1/1GAE band before ranking; proper L2/A/A and external-tool
  comparisons before runtime/fastest claims.

These conditions permit an assessment in the tested regime, not a general
sub-rho conclusion or transfer to prime-degree challenge fields. Current
components remain deliverable without pretending these conditions are met.
