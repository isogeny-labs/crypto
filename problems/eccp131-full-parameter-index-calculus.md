# ECCp-131 full-parameter index-calculus relation pipeline

- **Status:** `open`
- **Kind:** exact-curve implementation and validation gap; method-specific small-x support obstruction
- **Affected work:** [`ic prime --curve eccp-131`](../src/bin/ic/prime.rs), [`icx estimate eccp-131`](../src/bin/icx.rs), [`ca-ic prime --curve eccp-131`](https://github.com/aburan28/cryptanalysis/blob/main/suite/src/cli/tools/ic/prime.rs), and the requested ECCp-131 index-calculus ECDLP workflow
- **Next action owner:** index-calculus implementation lane, unassigned
- **Last updated:** 2026-10-23

## Problem

The published Certicom ECCp-131 point does not yet have a validated full-parameter index-calculus solution. The `ic prime` command now has bounded exact-curve two- and three-point relation/rank/descent probes, while the `ca-ic prime` command in `cryptanalysis` still executes its relation pipeline on a generated smaller curve. The exact group is the short-Weierstrass curve and subgroup in [Certicom's curve list](https://www.certicom.com/en/curves-list), with cofactor one, generator and target bound by their coordinates. The requested end state is a scalar for the published target independently checked as `[x]G = Q` with a complete cold cost record.

## Evidence and uncertainty

- **Observed:** `src/cryptanalysis/ic_engine.rs` classifies prime-field named curves as a scaled regime. The `cryptanalysis` command `ca-ic prime --curve` reports structure on the named curve and runs its relation pipeline on a generated smaller curve; its `suite/src/cli/tools/ic/prime.rs` documents that boundary. The `crypto` command `ic prime --curve eccp-131` now admits exact-curve two-sum enumeration and an optional bounded natural-relation/rank/target probe, with a [predeclared diagnostic protocol](../research/eccp131_bsgs_ic_20261020/EXACT_IC_PROBE_PROTOCOL.md). A separate [PARI/GP check](../research/eccp131_bsgs_ic_20261020/eccp131-pari-check.out) independently returned `ellcard(E)=n`, both published points on the curve, and `[n]G=[n]Q=O`; its [script](../research/eccp131_bsgs_ic_20261020/eccp131-pari-check.gp) and [resource log](../research/eccp131_bsgs_ic_20261020/eccp131-pari-check.err) are retained. A separate [PARI/GP primality check](../research/eccp131_bsgs_ic_20261020/eccp131-primes.out) returned `isprime(p)=isprime(n)=1`. An independent [PARI/GP two-sum enumeration](../research/eccp131_bsgs_ic_20261020/eccp131-pari-pair-sums.out) found `262144` distinct pair-sum abscissae from the first `512` base abscissae (`largest_small_x=1084`), and found that the published `Q` is not itself a signed two-point sum from that base; the [script](../research/eccp131_bsgs_ic_20261020/eccp131-pari-pair-sums.gp) and [resource log](../research/eccp131_bsgs_ic_20261020/eccp131-pari-pair-sums.err) are retained. The generic `ec_index_calculus_dlp` API and `icx` workflow do not have a completed ECCp-131 relation corpus. `icx run --require-named-curve` refuses a full-parameter run. The existing ECC2K-130 fixed-parameter path is a different field and curve.
- **Exact bounded run:** The [raw `ic prime` JSON](../research/eccp131_bsgs_ic_20261020/ic-prime-eccp131-b512-seed131.json) agrees with the independent `262144` distinct-sum count and records 8/8 planted decomposition controls, 128 natural setup candidates with zero relations and rank 0, and 128 published-target descent candidates with zero relations. The target column remains undetermined. The [report](../research/eccp131_bsgs_ic_20261020/REPORT.md) records command, binary hash, cold process wall, memory, positive control, failed independent-verifier attempts, and limits.
- **Benchmark follow-up:** The [frozen stage matrix and native ecbench control](../research/eccp131_bsgs_ic_20261020/reports/run-20261021T1534Z/REPORT.md) ran 20 exact ECCp-131 probe processes at B256, B512 and B1024, all with zero natural relations and rank zero, plus a separately audited 20-bit `ecbench` IC/rho control. The full-width `ic` stage timings are L0 diagnostics. The standard `ecbench` prime-field instance uses `u64` parameters and cannot run ECCp-131; its small-curve operation ratio cannot be transferred to this challenge. No full-width solver or cost claim follows.
- **Exact three-point follow-up:** The [frozen B128 run](../research/eccp131_bsgs_ic_20261020/reports/run-20261022T0523Z/REPORT.md) added a checked three-point relation oracle that subtracts one signed base point and searches the complete pair table, then uses the same modular rank and target-column solver. Two seeds and paired two-point controls each checked 32 setup and 16 published-target candidates on the exact curve. Every process passed 8/8 planted pair controls; all four recorded zero natural rows and rank zero. An exact-curve unit test and independent PARI/GP construction confirmed a three-point sum outside the B16 two-point support. The [raw JSON, command manifest and resource logs](../research/eccp131_bsgs_ic_20261020/reports/run-20261022T0523Z/raw/) are retained. This is method implementation and bounded validation, not a published-target scalar or full cost.
- **Structured-base screen:** The [frozen exact-curve multiplicative-coset screen](../research/eccp131_structured_ic_20261022/REPORT.md) tested 16 representatives each of `g·μ₅` and `g·μ₁₀`, with paired equal-column small-abscissa controls. Twelve `μ₅` and all 16 `μ₁₀` cosets lifted at least two abscissae; their sizes were two to five and three to eight. Across two seeds for each subgroup, all 896 planted three-point relations passed independent PARI/GP point-equation replay, while 56 coset arms and 56 controls yielded zero natural setup and target rows and rank zero in their respective 256+256 candidate budgets. Exact factorization `p−1 = 2·5·155003179783434785924857641481313994241`, with the final factor prime, rules out a `2^t` Kummer root tower on this field for `t≥2`. This closes only that Kummer form and these coset cells; the auxiliary-curve isogeny form is separately tested below.
- **Full-width isogeny-form tower:** The [frozen 2026-10-23 run](../research/eccp131_full_width_20261023/REPORT.md) computed 301 auxiliary-curve orders over the exact published prime field and certified a point of order 256 on candidate `b=301`. Independent Python arithmetic replayed 256 distinct coset abscissae and all eight rational Vélu maps to one terminal x-image. A separate PARI/GP check replayed the published curve order, generator, target and eight planted point equations. Of the tower abscissae, 115 lifted onto the published curve. The 256 setup and 256 published-target candidate screen had zero natural rows and rank zero. The GP and Python phases completed under 120/60-second wall and 2 GiB sampled-RSS watchdogs. This is a full-width factor-base construction, not a completed relation/rank/descent chain.
- **Derived or proved:** The in-memory BSGS table for a whole subgroup of roughly 131 bits would require on the order of `2^65` entries. For the current two-sum method with `B` distinct factor-base abscissae, there are at most `B` doublings and `2·C(B,2)` sums, hence at most `B²` distinct sum abscissae and `2B²` covered nonzero group points. A uniform nonzero candidate therefore hits with probability at most `2B²/(n−1)`. At `B=512` the independent enumeration reaches this bound exactly: `524288/(n−1) ≈ 3.3824×10^−34 = 2^−111.1875`. With the published `n = 0x048E1D43F293469E317F7ED728F6B8E6F1` and the CLI's largest admitted `B = 8192`, the upper bound is `2^−103.1875` per candidate. Even if every successful row were independent, collecting `B+1` rows would require at least `2^116.188` candidate trials in expectation under independent uniform sampling. This is a coverage bound for this factor base and two-sum oracle, not a lower bound for all index-calculus methods.
- **Three-point support bound:** The ordered signed triple list has at most `(2B)³ = 8B³` point entries before collisions, so a uniform nonzero candidate hits with probability at most `8B³/(n−1)`. At B128 this is about `2^−106.1875`; at the admitted B8192 cap it is about `2^−88.1875`. Optimistically treating every hit as independent, `B+1` rows at B8192 still take at least about `2^101.188` uniform candidate trials in expectation. The unshifted published `Q` is fixed, so this random-candidate bound is not assigned to it. This bound is specific to the signed three-point small-x oracle, not to higher-arity algebraic or different-base methods.
- **Tower support bound:** The `t=8` isogeny-form base has 115 lifted columns and 26,450 distinct nonidentity signed pair points. The implemented pair table covers at most `230×26,450=6,083,500` triple sums. Counting the omitted identity pair as well, every three-point oracle on this *same base* covers at most `230×(26,450+1)=6,083,730` group points, whether it enumerates or solves equations. Under uniform sampling the hit fraction is at most `3.925×10^−33`, and the 512-candidate union bound is `2.010×10^−30`. A faster solver alone cannot alter this support. Higher arity, a larger base, or a different relation mechanism is needed for a measurable natural-row rate.
- **Suspected explanation:** No efficient, complete relation oracle at this size has been demonstrated in these implementations; its attainable rate and cost remain unknown.
- **Missing capability:** A relation method that produces enough natural, independent exact-curve rows and a target descent within an executable resource envelope, followed by independent replay and complete cold cost. The bounded two- and three-point collectors and modular-rank path now have code, but their method-specific support bounds prevent the measured runs from supplying the necessary rows. Any `ecbench` comparison on the exact 131-bit curve additionally needs a full-width prime instance or a sealed stage adapter with matching identities and accounting; the current `u64` prime instance is not one.
- **What this evidence does not establish:** It does not show that ECCp-131 index calculus is impossible, nor does a scaled run establish a full-parameter cost or a logarithm for the published point.

## What would unblock it

- [ ] Freeze an exact ECCp-131 protocol with curve, subgroup, generator, target, factor-base digest, sign convention, relation and rank metrics, stopping rules, raw output paths, and replay controls. A bounded two-sum diagnostic is recorded separately; it does not replace a complete relation corpus.
- [ ] Implement and independently replay an exact-curve relation, linear-algebra, target-descent and scalar-reconstruction chain that verifies `[x]G = Q`; account for setup, failed candidates, verification, memory, and wall time.

These conditions would establish a completed run on this point. A speed claim would still require a matched baseline, independent verification, and the repository's progress-fidelity record.

## Next discriminating step

- **Action or proof obligation:** Construct a larger certified auxiliary tower and arity with a frozen support calculation that makes natural rows measurable, or a different relation mechanism with the same checkable property. The new `t=8` tower calibrates exact-field construction and solver correctness but its three-point support is insufficient regardless of solver speed. Charge auxiliary search and setup; retain the existing two- and three-point screens as controls. Independently replay every natural row and rank increment before target descent.
- **Inputs and controls:** Certicom ECCp-131 parameters and target; same-field random points as null controls; independent point-addition and relation checks; a known-answer construction kept separate from the published target.
- **Execution limits and stop condition:** Freeze candidate counts, solver seconds, memory watchdog, and artifact paths before execution. Stop on the protocol limits or a verified relation corpus, retaining all attempts.
- **Outcomes and next actions:** Verified independent relations permit a rank and full-cost protocol; zero or duplicate rank narrows only the tested factor base and budget; a timeout or invalid witness directs implementation repair and leaves the curve-level question open.
- **Recheck trigger:** A complete exact-curve relation oracle or a new verified transfer with subgroup and cost certificates.

## Resolution and history

Unresolved. The 2026-10-20 exact two-sum probe makes its method-specific coverage obstruction measurable on the published group. The 2026-10-21 stage matrix extends the bounded observation through B1024 without natural rows or rank and records the separate ecbench harness limit. The published-point scalar and full cold cost criteria remain open.
