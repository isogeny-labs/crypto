# Prime-field volcano layers

Browsing copy of [`research/volcano_prime_layers_20261018/`](../../research/volcano_prime_layers_20261018/). Two depth-8 ℓ = 2 volcanoes (m0 and m5), 864 verified ECbench records and eight exact replays; counts only on a busy L0 host. Fourteen of sixteen tested intervals include the null. The two that exclude 1 are both `rho.plain` comparisons involving the single m5 crater curve, so the effect cannot be separated from that curve. The remaining intervals are wide and do not establish equality to a tight margin. No isogeny maps or transfer-attack costs were measured.
