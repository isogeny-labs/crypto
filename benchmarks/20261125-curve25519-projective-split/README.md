# Curve25519 projective-split qualification

Documentary copies of the frozen `m=3` projective-intermediate experiment.
The candidate/baseline F4 multiply-add ratios are `1.001116` at `t=3` and
`1.000464` at `t=4`; both `t=5` arms timed out and both `t=6` arms were
memory-killed before one target. The candidate is not qualified for `m=4`.

This entry is outside the admitted benchmark table. It has no independent
replay, validator report, matched-rho reference, or `VALIDATION.json`, and it
does not support an exponent or total-work subexponential claim.
