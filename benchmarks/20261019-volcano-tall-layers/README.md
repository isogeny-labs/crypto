# Generic rho cost across tall-volcano levels

Browsing copy of the pre-registered study in [`research/volcano_tall_layers_20261018/`](../../research/volcano_tall_layers_20261018/). On tier a and all ten volcanoes pooled, the pre-registered level ratios and crater-to-floor slope fall within the ±5% equivalence margin; positive controls resolve the plain/negation gap. The tier-b scale check is wider than the margin and three tier-b sessions were interrupted; their verified records were retained without rerunning them. The study covers generic solvers only, on unregistered curves, at L0 with operation counts and no wall-time claim. It says nothing about attacks using explicit isogenies.
