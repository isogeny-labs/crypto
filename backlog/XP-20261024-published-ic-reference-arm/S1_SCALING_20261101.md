### 2. Metrics, eligibility, ratio
As R2W: *whole run* = ecbench child Ir + every WDSat process Ir - 3.51e7; *solver-only* = WDSat process Ir less one measured start-up per call (start-ups of this branch's new binaries are re-measured). Counted instructions only (ecbench); `[d]G = Q` is verified by ecbench on every record. Cell ratio = median over the 8 targets of (WDSat metric / best-lab whole run on the same target). A best point must be a whole solve (8/8 verified) **or** a labelled projection (4).

### 3. l grids and the stopping rule (written now; back-tested on R2W, `research/ic_s1_scaling_20261101/lrule_backtest.out`, returns l = 6, 3, 8, 8, 10 on the five R2W cells)
- Valid l: 3 <= l <= n-1. l = 3 and 4 are always evaluated for WDSat (**low-end guard**: whole solve, or a one-target trial that projects to >= 3x the best seen, or structurally invalid = base exhausts), because the UNSAT-regime minimum is a second mode (R2W K_0 n=13: l=3 was within 16 % of the best at l=8) and an outward search from the middle would not reach it.
- WDSat start l0 = n-4 (n=19: 15; n=17: 13; n=15: 11). Evaluate outward in both directions one l at a time. **A direction stops when two successive l are both dearer than the best seen so far** (failed / invalid counts as dearer); iterate until no new best appears. A cell's WDSat optimum is *interior* when this terminates. "Dearer" is on the whole-run median, per XG configuration (6) separately.
- Lab: start l0 = round((n-1)/2) (n=19: 9), same rule without the guard, and in addition every l at which WDSat was run is **not** required (R2W showed the lab curve has no late drop).
- Best WDSat config per metric and cell = lowest median at that metric. Dearer-than-3x exclusions by trial/probe follow the R2W labels ("probe-excluded"); a trial is one target, 900 s (n=19: 3600 s).

### 4. Projections
A whole-solve session projected beyond about 1.5 h wall (load ~40, bench lock shared) is not run when a one-target trial or the 32-system probe projects it to >= 3x the best whole solve seen; otherwise the best point may be a **projection** = median Ir/system from the 32-system probe x median systems/target from the nearest measured l of the same cell (labelled "projected" in every table). Projected cells enter the fit **only labelled**: the verdict is stated both with and without them.

### 5. Fit and verdict
OLS of ln(cell ratio) on n, 95 % t interval with k-2 degrees of freedom, per metric (whole run, solver-only), over **all cells of 1** (R2W five + imported two + new three, fewer if a cell is open). Sensitivity rows: without the n=19 cells (the largest n); without projected cells; R2W five alone (repeated). Per metric: *growing* if the 95 % CI of d ln(ratio)/dn lies entirely above 0, *shrinking* if entirely below 0, *flat* if the whole CI lies inside [-0.10, +0.10] per bit, otherwise *undecided*. Overall: the common per-metric verdict if the two agree, else *undecided* (point estimates described, not voted). Rows of the table are reported for every cell: best lab l and Ir, best WDSat l and Ir, ratio, solver-only ratio, measured/projected.

### 6. XG-configuration amendment (added 2026-11-01 on the coordinator's instruction, desk check S3, before any WDSat session of this branch)
S3 (`/Volumes/SSD990-2/ic-regime-review-20261024/S3_WDSAT_ANCHOR_20261101.md`): the WDSat authors (Trimoska-Ionica-Dequen, CP 2020, Table 5) recommend disabling XG for m=3; there XG-off is 13x (UNSAT) to 32x (SAT) cheaper than XG-on. Our published arm ran `-x -b`. Configurations (runtime flags of the same first-solution binary; ecbench params `xg`, `sym`):
- **A** = `-x -b` (current; R2W, earlier S1); **B** = no flag (no XG, no symmetric branching); **C** = `-x` only; **D** = `-b` only (XG off, symmetric branching; added by me beyond the three asked, because B may pay for unbroken symmetry).
- **Probe** (before the n=19 sweeps): cells K_1 n=11 and K_0 n=13, l in {4, 8} (4 and the R2W-best l), 8 targets, first-solution, configurations A, B, C, D, plus the lab arm at its R2W-best l (5, 6) as the A/A check against R2W (must agree within 2 %). A record that times out (1800 s wall) counts as failed (0/8), i.e. dearer.
- **Decision rule.** Let m_X(cell) = min over the two probed l of the whole-run median of config X. X is *materially cheaper* if m_X <= m_A / 1.5 in **both** cells. If none is, A stays the only published arm. If one or more are, the *best config* X* is the one with the lowest geometric mean of m_X/m_A over the two cells.
- **Fits.** (a) the pre-fixed fit with config A throughout, with all cells above: this is the fit R2W and the earlier verdicts refer to. (b) the best-config fit: the published arm is config X* swept by rule 3 **at the cells where budget allows**, in this order: K_1 n=11, K_0 n=13 (their probes are the start), K_0 n=19, K_1 n=19, K_0 n=15, K_1 n=15, K_1 n=17, K_0 n=9, K_1 n=9, K_0 n=13 seed 2. The imported/R2W cells have no X* data until swept; the (b) fit uses only the cells with a completed X* sweep (k reported), with the same CI rule, and says which cells. Config choice per cell for (b) = lowest of A and X* sweeps at that cell (so (b) <= (a) cell by cell).
- The report states both verdicts and which one answers the owner's question: the owner's question is decided by the fit for the **cheapest valid config** (b) if it covers at least the cells n = 13 and n = 19 of both families and at least 5 cells; otherwise by (a), with (b) given as a labelled partial.
