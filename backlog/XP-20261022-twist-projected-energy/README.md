## Question and decision value

Do coupled trace images have an additive structure that predicts more useful decompositions after cardinality and fiber multiplicity are controlled?

A robust structural difference selects a specifically frozen family for further work. An energy increase without independent-row or cost improvement is not a collection win. A known-log diagnostic is never presented as an executable DLP shortcut.

## Inputs and method

The E21 exact projected sets, existing ordinary bases and sizes 8,12,16. Use all targets on the three exhaustive pilot curves and the fixed public target split on the other fixtures. Include 32 public matched-random-base seeds per size.

Compute pair-sum multiplicities, additive energy, three-term target decompositions and independent signed-row yield using actual group operations. Optional character/Fourier coordinates may use known toy logs only inside a separate diagnostic checker; those logs must never enter the collector, selector or reported algorithm cost. Preserve raw histograms and compare identities among configurations rather than treating repetitions as independent data.

## Controls and evidence owed

Matched base cardinality, sign quotient, support, acceptance rate and construction budget; fiber-resampled copies of one base; shuffled labels; interval-like and random rational bases. Test whether observed target yield follows the full histogram rather than its mean alone.

Exact toy distributions and heldout yield/rank tables, counted diagnostic work and uncertainty across independent random bases. Record any mismatch between energy and usable target-row span.
